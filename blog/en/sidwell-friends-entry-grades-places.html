Sidwell Friends admits most of its new students in "expansion grades", the years when a class grows. Outside those years, a place exists only when a student leaves, and the school notes that its retention rate is high. Knowing where the places are is therefore the first step in choosing when to apply.

What the numbers mean

Ninth grade is the largest entry after Kindergarten, with 30 to 35 new students joining an Upper School of about 500. Grades 3 and 4, by contrast, add only 12 and 10. A family that can choose between two years, for example Grade 6 or Grade 7, or waiting for Grade 9, should weigh the places alongside the child's readiness.

The school sizes give the context: about 295 students in the Lower School, 350 in the Middle School and 500 in the Upper School. Student-teacher ratios are 10 to 1 in PreKindergarten, 12 to 1 from Kindergarten to Grade 4, and 16 to 1 from Grade 5 to Grade 12.

PreK or Kindergarten

The two earliest entries are not equal. Kindergarten takes 28 new students, more than the 20 who join in PreKindergarten. A family that misses the PreK year, or decides its child is not ready at three, still has the larger Kindergarten intake a year later, with the same group visit, teacher recommendation and parent meeting, plus a current progress report.

Families arriving mid-cycle

Sidwell Friends does not reserve spaces for families who move to the area after the admissions season. A family relocating to Washington should therefore apply in the normal cycle for the year of arrival, starting as early as possible, rather than counting on a later opening.
