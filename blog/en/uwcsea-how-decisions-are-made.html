How UWCSEA decides

UWCSEA's admission policy follows from its mission and its commitment to diversity. Places are not given on a first-come, first-served basis, and the eligible pool is usually larger than the number of places. The decision is therefore a choice among good candidates, made on several criteria at once.

Effort: the criterion families overlook

Many parents read a report for its marks. UWCSEA reads the effort grades with equal attention. A child with excellent marks and middling effort sends a weaker signal than families expect; a child with good marks and consistently high effort, and teachers' comments about collaboration and relations with classmates, sends a strong one. Those signals are built over two years, which is why reports from both years are requested.

Building a diverse cohort

Among eligible applicants, UWCSEA tries to build diversity within each campus, school section, grade and boarding community. It considers gender, ethnicity and nationality, languages and religions, countries of residence, personality, from extrovert to introvert, schooling and curricula, interests and talents, aspirations, activities, family structures and particular circumstances. The school is clear that families should answer its questions authentically, and that applicants should not try to show how diverse they are.

Context nobody controls

Some factors depend on the year rather than the child: the gender balance in the grade, the applicant's age within the grade, whether a sibling is already enrolled on the same campus, whether siblings apply together, whether the child or a parent attended UWCSEA, and whether the child applied before. A strong applicant can miss out in one year and succeed in another.

Two possible outcomes

Families receive either an offer of a place, in one of the offer rounds, or notice that the application is closed. Applications without an offer in the first round roll over automatically to the second. Detailed individual feedback is not given for learning support decisions, so families should not expect an explanation.
