Thomas Jefferson: the residency requirement

Thomas Jefferson High School for Science and Technology, Virginia. Photo ~delta, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

TJ is a regional public high school. To apply, the student must live full-time with a parent or guardian in a participating school division, at the time of application and throughout attendance.

Participating divisions

Arlington County, Fairfax County, City of Fairfax included, Falls Church City, Loudoun County and Prince William County. Fauquier County and the cities of Alexandria, Manassas and Manassas Park do not participate: their students cannot apply. Owning property without actually living there is not enough, and a false residency statement violates Virginia law.
