Part of every Concord Academy application is written by people other than the family: the current school sends grades and records, and three adults write about the student. These documents give the committee a view of the student over time, in class, which neither a test nor an interview can provide.

RecommendationThe current-year mathematics teacher

Applicants using the Finalsite application send recommendation requests to teachers by email from their account. All materials are due on 15 January.

Why English and mathematics

The two subject letters come from the teachers who see a student read, write, reason and solve problems every week. They speak to the first things CA lists in its review, academic performance and intellectual curiosity, and often to character and maturity as well: how a student responds to difficulty, asks questions or helps others.

Strong in class, strong on paper

Teachers write about what they see. I help students consolidate mathematics and English during the year of application, so the letters have strong work to describe.

Practical advice

  • Ask the three recommenders early in the autumn, politely and in person if possible.
  • Give them the deadline, 15 January, and the platform through which the request will arrive.
  • Check in early January that every letter and record has been received.
  • For schools outside the United States, make sure grades and reports can be read in English.

Is your child's current school unfamiliar with American applications? Let's prepare the requests together.

The counsellor's letter

The third letter comes from a guidance or placement counsellor, or another appropriate official. It gives the wider view: the student's place in the school, involvement beyond the classroom and conduct. Where a school has no counsellor, a head of year or principal who knows the student can fill this role.

What families cannot write

The family has no say in what teachers write. What it can influence is the year itself: steady work, engagement in class and good relationships with teachers. The capacity for positive relationships with adults is, after all, one of the qualities CA names in its review.
