Greenwich Academy: Pre-K and K

Greenwich Academy, Greenwich, Connecticut. Photo Mx. Granger, Wikimedia Commons, CC0

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

At Greenwich Academy, Pre-K is the Pre-Connecting Class and kindergarten the Connecting Class.

Ages

For Pre-Connecting, age 4 by June 1, 2027; for Connecting, age 5 by August 1, 2027.

The steps

Part I of the application by December 15, 2026. A parent interview, from September, with an optional student-led tour. Depending on age, an individual visit for the child. A small group assessment in December or January. Then, by January 15, the parent questionnaire and recommendations from the current teacher and director.

Supporting without moulding

At 4 or 5, there is no cramming. I help you approach the group assessment calmly and prepare your interview.
