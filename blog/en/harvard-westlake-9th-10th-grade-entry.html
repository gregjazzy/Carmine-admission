Entering in 9th or 10th grade

Harvard-Westlake's upper school is not a fresh start for everyone. Most of its 9th graders have already spent two years at the middle school and move up automatically. New students join them: about 90 in 9th grade, and between five and ten in 10th grade, depending on total enrolment. A later applicant is competing for a small share of a class that is already largely formed.

The doors after 7th grade

  • 8th grade: applications are considered only when a place opens through attrition, which is rare.
  • 9th grade: the second main entry, with about 90 new students.
  • 10th grade: about five to ten students a year.
  • 11th and 12th grades: only in special circumstances, such as a family relocating to Los Angeles.

The same process, a different reading

A 9th or 10th grade applicant follows the same steps as a younger one: the application form, a student questionnaire due two days before the interview, a single ISEE sitting between 1 August and 15 January, recommendations from the current English and math teachers and an administrator, and a transcript. Interviews for 9th grade applicants take place on weekdays during school hours, in person or on Zoom, and submitting the form by 13 November guarantees one.

What changes is the record. At 14 or 15, a student has two or three years of middle school grades, course choices that show how they handle challenge, and teachers who know them well. Competitive applicants have a strong academic track record, and at this stage the record is long enough to be read as a trend.

What a later applicant must show

Harvard-Westlake looks for kind, curious and engaged students eager to contribute. For a newcomer, contribution is the key word: the class has its friendships, its teams and its clubs, and an applicant who can say clearly what they would add, on stage, on a team, in a lab or in class discussion, gives the school a reason to make room. Families may also send a short video or document showing an applicant's work in the arts or athletics.

A realistic plan

Because admission is never guaranteed and places are few, Harvard-Westlake recommends that every family apply to other schools as well. For a 9th or 10th grade applicant, that advice matters even more. Applications received after the January deadline are considered only for the waitlist.
