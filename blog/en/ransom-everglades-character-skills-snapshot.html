Ransom Everglades asks every applicant to complete the Character Skills Snapshot, an online assessment from the Enrollment Management Association, in addition to the SSAT or ISEE. The school explains why: grades and test scores do not tell the whole story, and it wants to know applicants better on the qualities it tries to develop in all its students.

What it looks at

The school lists the qualities behind it: intellectual curiosity, teamwork, initiative, responsibility, resilience, self-control, open-mindedness and social awareness. These are the same qualities the admission office looks for elsewhere in the file, in the interview, the recommendations and, for the Upper School, the applicant statement. The Snapshot adds a structured view of how a student sees themselves, gathered the same way for every applicant.

How to approach it

The Snapshot is not something to cram for. Students do best when they understand the format, take it in a quiet moment rather than at the end of a long test day, and answer honestly and consistently. Because it sits beside the interview and teachers' recommendations, a student whose answers match the person their teachers describe gives the school a coherent picture. Completing it in December, after the first SSAT or ISEE sitting, keeps January free for the rest of the checklist.
