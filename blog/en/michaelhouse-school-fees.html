In 2026, board and tuition at Michaelhouse cost ZAR 417,000 a year, from E to A Block. Fees are invoiced in ten equal instalments, from December to September, by debit order.

What comes on top

  • Registration: ZAR 1,200, non-refundable.
  • Acceptance fee, non-refundable, laptop device charge included: ZAR 81,250 for a South African resident, ZAR 208,500 for a non-resident, of which R76,250 is refunded at the end of the boy's time at the school.
  • A voluntary development levy: ZAR 6,800.
  • Stationery, textbooks and uniform.

A discount of ZAR 16,680 applies to annual fees paid in advance.
