Michaelhouse fees 2026

Every boy at Michaelhouse boards, so there is only one fee: R417,000 for board and tuition in 2026, the same from E Block to A Block. What varies is how a family pays it, whether it lives in South Africa, and what it receives in scholarships or assistance.

What is paid, and when

Item, 2026AmountWhen
RegistrationSmall, non-refundableOn registration; waived for entry in 2034 and beyond
Acceptance fee, South African residentR81,250, non-refundableOn accepting the place
Acceptance fee, non-residentR208,500, of which R76,250 is refunded when the boy leavesOn accepting the place
Board and tuitionR417,000 a yearTen equal instalments, December to September
Development levyR6,800, voluntaryAnnual

Both acceptance fees include the charge for the laptop each boy uses.

Paying the annual fee

A debit order is a requirement. The ten instalments run from December, before the school year opens in January, to September. A family that pays the whole year in advance receives a discount of R16,680. An administration fee of R5,672 applies to South African residents who do not pay by debit order, and to non-residents who do not pay in advance.

The fee follows the place

At Michaelhouse, the place is won through the record, the interview with the Rector and the Head's recommendation, and a scholarship through the February weekend. I prepare boys for each of these.

On top of the fee

Stationery and textbooks, personal purchases and uniform are charged separately. Families should budget for them, along with travel to and from Balgowan for a boy who lives far away.

Scholarships

Scholarships are awarded on merit at Grade 8 entry, without regard to the family's finances, and are expressed as a percentage of board and tuition: up to 75% for the main academic, all-round and sport awards, up to 50% for music, up to 30% for closed academic scholarships, and up to 15% for drama and art exhibitions. The value is fixed for the duration of the award, which normally covers the boy's five years.

Financial assistance

Funds are limited, but Michaelhouse helps families for whom the fees are out of reach, and can top up a scholarship that does not meet a family's need. The request starts on the son's application form, by 31 January of Grade 7, and requires full financial disclosure to the Financial Assistance Committee. It must be renewed every year. Michaelhouse also reserves the right to run credit checks on applicants.

My view on cost

I work each year with around fifteen families, a number that has held for seven years, aiming at the most selective schools. At Michaelhouse, the largest saving a family can make comes from a scholarship, and the scholarship depends on preparation for the February weekend. That is where I would spend the effort.

Scholarship and assistance requests close with registration, on 31 January of Grade 7. Get in touch in Grade 6.
