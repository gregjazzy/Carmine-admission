Poly Prep's Middle School has two entry points, Grade 5 and Grade 6. Grade 6 is the larger, with about 35 to 38 new students against 15 to 18 in Grade 5. Both are main entry years, so every applicant who submits the preliminary application by November 20, 2026 is interviewed in person.

Grade 5 or Grade 6?

There are no strict age cut-offs in the Middle School: most Grade 5 entrants are ten by September 1, most Grade 6 entrants eleven. Some families consider repeating a year before the Middle School, and the school leaves that choice to them, reaching out if placement needs discussion. A child can apply to only one grade per cycle, so the choice matters. Grade 6 offers more places; Grade 5 gives a child an extra year in the school and joins a smaller group of newcomers.

The group interview and the writing

At this age, the interview happens in a small group led by a teacher, during an after-school session. A child is seen talking with peers as well as with an adult: listening, building on what others say, taking a turn without taking over. The short writing activity in the same session gives the school a sample of the child's writing produced on the spot, to set beside the ISAAGNY common essay written at home. The two should sound like the same child.
