Godolphin bursaries

Godolphin and Latymer wants girls to be able to take up a place whatever their family's finances. Its bursaries can cover more than the full fees: up to 110 %, so that costs beyond tuition do not stand in the way either.

In figures
Maximum award110 % of the fees
Girls helpedAround ten in each year group, most with fully funded places
BasisHousehold income and realisable assets; the girl's academic ability does not change the amount
Entry testThe same as for every candidate, with no higher bar
DurationNormally guaranteed to the end of her schooling, with a fresh application each year

Four examples the school gives

  • 100 % for a family on a low income in rented accommodation.
  • 100 % for a single parent working part-time, who had moved in with extended family to stay in London.
  • 50 % for a family with a self-employed father, a mother working part-time and two daughters at the school, owning a home with little equity.
  • 30 % for a single parent recently made redundant, renting, using a redundancy payment towards the balance.

The same test for all

A bursary candidate has to earn her place exactly like any other girl. I prepare her for the Consortium test and the interview at that level.

How to apply

  1. Register by the deadline; in cases of particular need, the £210 registration fee may be waived on request to the Registrar.
  2. Tick the bursary box on the Examination Entry Form sent after registration, and return it.
  3. Within seven days, receive a link to the online bursary portal.
  4. Complete the application and upload every supporting document. Allow about two weeks to gather them.

For 11+ entry in 2027, the deadline is noon on 13 November 2026; for 16+, noon on 2 November 2026. Incomplete or late applications cannot be processed.

Wondering whether your family would qualify? Let's talk before the November deadline.

After the application

Some families are invited to a short interview with the Bursar in January; these invitations are random and say nothing about the likely outcome. The decision on the bursary arrives with the offer of a place, so a family knows what it would pay before accepting.

Scholarship and bursary together

Godolphin's scholarships, in music and in art, reward talent and are worth up to 30 % of the fees. A scholar whose family has financial need can also apply for a bursary to increase the award.
