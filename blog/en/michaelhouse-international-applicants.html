Applying to Michaelhouse from abroad

Michaelhouse draws boys from all nine provinces of South Africa and from 22 countries. A family abroad goes through the same process as a family in Durban or Johannesburg, but four things deserve particular attention: the school calendar, the trip to Balgowan, the money, and the English and curriculum gap.

1. A calendar built on the southern year

The South African school year starts in January. A boy entering Grade 8, E Block, in January 2028 applies during 2026 and 2027: registration ideally by the end of Grade 6, an interview during Grade 6, and a closing date of 31 January 2027 for registration, scholarship and financial assistance applications. Offers arrive in early April 2027. For a family living on a September calendar, that means planning from what would be the middle of the child's primary school year.

2. Meeting the school

Each year, Michaelhouse holds parent information events across South Africa, elsewhere in Africa and internationally, including in Nairobi, and an online session. They are the natural first contact. The process itself, however, expects the boy and his parents to visit Michaelhouse during Grade 6 for a tour and the interview with the Rector or a Deputy Rector. A family abroad should plan that trip, and if possible combine it with Weekend@Michaelhouse, when registered boys spend a night on campus.

From abroad, at the right level

I prepare boys abroad online for the Michaelhouse interview and, where their profile allows it, for the scholarship assessments, and help the family set a calendar that runs across two hemispheres.

4. Curriculum and language

Lessons are in English, and the five years lead to the South African matric. A boy arriving from a French, Swiss, British or American school needs reports that show his level clearly, and, if English is not his first language, enough fluency to follow every lesson and to speak with ease at the interview. Academic scholarship candidates are expected to have an aggregate of 85% or more at the end of Grade 6, a figure that is harder to read from a foreign system and worth discussing with the admissions office early.

After the offer

Boys live at school during term, in one of ten Houses, with resident staff and a 24-hour medical centre. For a boy whose family is a flight away, that structure is exactly what makes the distance manageable.

Working with families abroad

A large share of the fifteen or so families who work with me each year, as for the past seven years, live far from the school they are aiming at. For Michaelhouse, I set the calendar across both school years, prepare the boy online for the Rector's interview, and work on any academic gap before the reports are read.

The deadline is 31 January of Grade 7, in the southern calendar. Get in touch early.
