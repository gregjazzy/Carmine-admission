International schools in Asia

The English UWCSEA requires

Every UWCSEA classroom works in English, and the College supports English learners in every grade through its English as an Additional Language programme. Support is not unlimited, though: places in EAL are capped, and each section sets a threshold below which an applicant is unlikely to be eligible. The usual measure is the number of years spent in an English Medium Institution, a school where lessons are taught in English.

The thresholds

SectionWhat is expectedSupport if admitted
K1 and K2Follow simple instructions and express some ideas in English without supportAll young children treated as bilingual learners; beginners supported
Grade 1Some oral and written English; no prior English-medium schooling is unlikely to qualifyEAL support focused on communication
Grades 2 to 5One to three years of English-medium schooling; in Grades 4 and 5, strong communicative EnglishSmall groups or in-class support
Grades 6 to 8Two to three years of English-medium schooling and strong oral fluency, with reading and writingIn-class support or an EAL class replacing a language lesson
Grades 9 to 11Three or more years of English-medium schooling and sufficient academic EnglishGrades 9 and 10: EAL and English for Academic Purposes; Grades 11 and 12: English A or English B

A child with similar English exposure outside school can also qualify. A child who has left an EAL programme at their current school may still receive support at UWCSEA.

Bilingual programmes

Applicants from bilingual schools may or may not meet the criteria, and UWCSEA may contact the current school for details. A bilingual label on a report says little about how much of the week is taught in English, and families should know that figure before applying.

How English is assessed

For young children, the school usually judges English during Meet and Play or from reports, without a test. From Middle School, applicants who may need EAL take a proctored online assessment. Passing it puts the child into the applicant pool; failing it closes the application, and the fee is not refunded. In the High School, the English test must be passed before the Virtual Immersion Programme. The student may apply again for a later year.

How many years of English-medium schooling does your child have? Tell me, and I will tell you where they stand.

Timing for English learners

EAL capacity is set after current students' needs are reviewed. In the Primary and Middle School, applicants needing EAL are usually considered in the second offer round, in March. In the High School, they are considered in both rounds.

Preparing

A child below the threshold is better served by a year of English-medium schooling, or intensive academic English, than by an early application. A child just above it benefits most from work on writing, which shows in the handwritten statement and in the English assessment.
