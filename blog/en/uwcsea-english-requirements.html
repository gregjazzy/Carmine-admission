At UWCSEA, English is the language of all teaching. The school welcomes students whose first language is not English, and supports them, but sets benchmarks by age.

The benchmarks

  • Kindergarten and Grade 1: follow simple instructions and communicate some ideas in English without support.
  • Primary: without one to three years of English-medium schooling, a student is unlikely to meet entry criteria.
  • Middle School, Grades 6 to 8: without two to three years of English-medium schooling, the same applies.
  • High School, Grades 9 to 11: sufficient academic English to access the programme.

About 15% of offers go to students needing English support. These places depend on current students' needs, and are often allocated in the second round.

Reaching the right level

I measure your child's English against these benchmarks, and prepare them to reach them before the assessment.
