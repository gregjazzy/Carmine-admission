Deerfield: the entrance test, what you need to know

The Main School Building, Deerfield Academy, Massachusetts. Photo John Phelan, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

US boarding schools November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

At Deerfield, testing is not optional. The school presents it as one of the ways to get to know each candidate, and requires it of all. I know the school through my students at Deerfield. For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year with applications to leading schools in New England.

What the school says

For grades 9 and 10, the SSAT or ISEE, taken between January of the previous year and January of the application year; for grade 11, ACT, PSAT, SAT or SSAT; for grade 12 and the postgraduate year, ACT, PSAT or SAT. SSAT and ISEE results, writing sample included, are sent by the testing body. The school suggests a sitting in October, November or December: a January test is still considered, but the file is only reviewed once the score arrives.

A student for whom English has not been the language of instruction for at least three years, or is not the language spoken at home, must also take the IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT or Duolingo.

Preparing for Deerfield's test

I prepare your child for the SSAT or ISEE, writing sample included, and for the English test if needed, with a timetable set to an autumn sitting.

What the school does not say

Deerfield publishes no expected score. But by receiving the SSAT writing sample with the results, it has a text written under time pressure, without help, which it can compare with the essays in the file.

Why good students fail

The extra English test

The three-year rule applies to many students at international schools. Two tests instead of one means starting earlier.

Does the English test apply to your child? Tell me about them.

The neglected writing sample

Unscored by the testing body, it is read by the school. It is often the least prepared part.

The late sitting

Registration closes well before test dates: a December sitting is booked in the autumn.
