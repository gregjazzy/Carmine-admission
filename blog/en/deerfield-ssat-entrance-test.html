Testing for Deerfield

The Main School Building, Deerfield Academy, Massachusetts. Photo John Phelan, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

US boarding schools November 2026 6 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Deerfield requires a standardized test from every candidate and describes it as one of many ways the admission team gets to know an applicant. The rules depend on the grade, the windows are precise, and one detail sets Deerfield apart: it receives the SSAT or ISEE writing sample with the scores, and reads it.

The SSAT, ISEE and TOEFL are sent directly by the testing organization, using Deerfield's codes: 2770 for the SSAT, 220685 for the ISEE, 9577 for the TOEFL. For the PSAT, SAT or ACT, the family checks whether the scores appear on the school transcript, and if not, emails a copy to the admission office.

The writing sample is read

The SSAT and ISEE include a writing sample that the testing organizations do not score. Deerfield asks for the results to be sent directly, writing sample included. In a file that also contains a short answer, a personal essay and a graded paper with teacher comments, that sample is the only piece of writing produced under time pressure with no help at all. A reader can compare it with the rest. Children often treat it as an afterthought at the end of a long test; at Deerfield it deserves real preparation.

When to sit

Deerfield suggests October, November or December, and its own calendar names November or December as the preferred time. A January sitting is still accepted for the regular cycle, but the file is reviewed only once the score arrives. Registration deadlines fall well before test dates, so a December sitting is booked in the autumn, and a family that wants room for a second attempt needs the first one by November.

The English test: who needs it

A student must add the IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT or Duolingo if English has not been their primary language of instruction for at least three years, or if English is not the main language spoken at home. The second condition catches families who do not expect it: a child at an English-medium school whose family speaks another language at home falls under the rule. A TOEFL score of 5.0 or higher is preferred. If tests are not offered in the family's country, the admission office asks to be contacted.

What it costs

For a family in the United States, the SSAT Upper Level costs $165 in its standard form, $235 at a Prometric center and $255 at home. For international candidates, every SSAT format costs $320. ISEE prices are published separately.
