How to get into Northfield Mount Hermon

Memorial Chapel, Northfield Mount Hermon, Massachusetts. Photo Daderot, Wikimedia Commons, CC0

Book a conversation

Northfield Mount Hermon is a coeducational boarding and day school of 630 students in grades 9 through 12, plus a postgraduate year, on a 746-acre campus in the Connecticut River Valley, two hours from Boston. It publishes no acceptance rate. What it does publish is a clear list of what goes into the file, and a calendar that ends on January 15 for applications and March 10 for decisions.

I work every year with families applying to NMH and to the other leading New England boarding schools. Here is how admission really works, and where files are won or lost.

A school built on three courses at a time

NMH runs what it calls a college-model academic program. Students take three core courses per semester, in 70-minute classes, and six over the year rather than the usual five. A teacher works with about thirty students in a semester, and homework is concentrated on three subjects. Motivated students can cover two years of a subject in one, French III in the autumn and French IV in the spring.

That shapes the student NMH is looking for. A seventy-minute class is built on discussion, labs and projects, not on listening. A child who likes to go deep into a subject, and who can hold their own in a long conversation with a teacher, fits the model. The interview is where that shows.

What the file contains

PieceWhat NMH asks
ApplicationStandard Application Online or Gateway to Prep Schools, the family's choice
InterviewRequired for every applicant, on campus or virtual
RecommendationsCurrent English teacher and current math teacher
TestSSAT, ISEE, ACT, PSAT or SAT, optional
English testTOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo for non-native speakers
PortfolioOptional, for athletes and artists, sent as a link

The choice between the two application platforms changes nothing in how the file is read. It matters only for families applying to several schools at once: the one that covers most of the list saves hours of copying.

Testing that only counts if it helps

A general admission test is optional this year, and the rule is explicit: a score that strengthens the application is taken into account, a score that does not is set aside. Sending a weak score does not cost a family the decision; it simply adds nothing. What matters is whether the score says something the transcript and recommendations do not, which is often the case for a child from a school NMH knows less well.

The interview and the visit

Every applicant is interviewed, either on campus or by video, and the interview is booked directly with the admission office. On campus it is usually combined with an hour-long tour led by a current student, through the dormitories, the Gilder Center for math and science and the Rhodes Arts Center. Families from China and South Korea go through InitialView instead, which records a live, unscripted interview and adds a writing sample.

Calendar and cost

Applications for fall 2027 close on January 15, 2027, as do financial aid applications. Decisions are released on March 10, and families who applied for aid learn the amount within twenty-four hours. Boarding costs $79,382 in 2026-27, with $1,625 in fees on top. More than 40 percent of families receive need-based aid, and the average boarding award is $57,472.
