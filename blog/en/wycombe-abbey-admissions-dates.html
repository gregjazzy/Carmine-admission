Wycombe Abbey admissions dates

Wycombe Abbey has one registration deadline for every entry point: 1 June of the calendar year before entry. After that, the three routes follow their own calendars, with the 16+ first, in October, and the 13+ last, in January. For 2027 entry, registration has closed; the same structure applies to 2028.

Everything takes place at Wycombe Abbey, except the online test, sat at the girl's school, a recognised UK centre or a British Council office.

Other dates to know

  • Bursary applications: by 1 September of the year before entry.
  • 11+ acceptance and deposit: early March, on a date the school confirms.
  • 16+ acceptance and deposit: by the second week of December.
  • Open Mornings: in the autumn and summer terms; Coffee Mornings each term, for about 30 families.

Starting before 1 June

I plan with families a year or more before registration, so that the online test and the day at the school are prepared well ahead of their autumn dates.

For 2028 entry

A girl entering in September 2028 registers by 1 June 2027: in Year 5 for 11+, in Year 7 for 13+, in Year 10 for 16+. The school recommends Open Mornings from Year 4 for 11+, Year 6 for 13+ and Year 10 for 16+, and gives priority on site to families seeking entry within two years. Registered families can also arrange a private visit, usually on a Friday afternoon in term time.
