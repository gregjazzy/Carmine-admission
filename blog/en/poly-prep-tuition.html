Poly Prep publishes, for each grade, tuition, mandatory fees, the Tuition Refund Plan, the total, the deposit and the balance. The figure that matters to a family is the total, since busing, activity, grade-trip and, from Grade 5, book fees are not optional.

I go through these figures with families weighing the school. Here they are for 2026-27.

New students also pay a $500 registration fee.

In Nursery, the choice between mornings and full days is also a budget decision: the gap between the two totals is $20,822 a year. Families state their preference on a form in the application, and choosing "flexible" may lead to the half-day program.

Busing, lunch and trips inside the figure

Poly Prep includes more than many New York schools do. Lunch, devices in the Lower and Middle Schools, activities and athletics on the Dyker Heights campus, the bus service to both campuses, a daily snack from Nursery to Kindergarten, athletic uniforms and grade-wide field trips are all inside the figure. In a typical year, more than 80 percent of students ride the bus, which runs at 4 pm and 6 pm from Dyker Heights so that students can stay for after-school activities. For a family comparing schools, the bus alone can make a significant difference.

Three schedules, and the long view

Families choose among three options through VC Pay: the full year in June, two halves in June and November, or ten monthly payments from June to March. For a child who enters in Nursery and stays to Grade 12, the 2026-27 totals, with a full-day Nursery, add up to more than $1 million across fifteen years, before increases; seen that way, the decision to apply for need-based aid deserves a careful look even for families with comfortable incomes.
