Moving to Nashville

Many families discover Montgomery Bell while planning a move to Nashville, often with a job offer in hand and a school year already under way elsewhere. The good news is that most of the MBA application can begin remotely. The less good news is that two steps want the boy in Nashville, and the calendar is tight.

What can be done from afar

  • The inquiry, online, which opens the admissions portal account.
  • The official application form, due January 1.
  • The transcript request, sent to the current school wherever it is.
  • The teacher evaluation, completed by a current teacher at that school.
  • The Character Skills Snapshot, completed online by the boy.
  • The ISEE, which can be sat in a test centre or at home in many places, so the boy does not need to wait for MBA's own sitting on January 30.
  • The financial aid application on Clarity, also due January 1.

What needs a trip

The shadow visit, step three, is a full school day at MBA: the boy follows a student from advisory at 7:50 to the afternoon. It cannot be done remotely. The interview is required of every applicant, and MBA holds an Interview Day on February 6. A family that can plan one well-chosen trip should try to combine the shadow day with a Thursday-morning parent tour, and ask the admissions office early how the interview can be arranged.

Getting to school once you arrive

MBA runs two morning shuttle routes, shared with families from St. Cecilia, Overbrook, Akiva and Harpeth Hall: one through LaVergne, Antioch and Brentwood, the other along Franklin and Hillsboro Road. The buses run every school morning, including exam weeks. For a family still choosing where to live, the routes are a practical factor worth knowing before signing a lease.

When the move comes mid-year

A family arriving in January faces four MBA dates in five weeks: the application on January 1, the ISEE on January 30, the supplemental materials on February 1, the interview day on February 6. Decisions arrive on March 6 and replies are due by March 10. The safest plan is to start everything that can be done remotely in the fall, so that the trip, or the arrival, is used only for what needs the boy in person.

A different school culture

For a boy coming from a co-educational school, or from another region or country, MBA's culture can be new: a boys' school, an academic day that runs into afternoon athletics, and ideals spelled out as gentleman, scholar and athlete. Talking about that before the shadow day helps him arrive ready to take part.
