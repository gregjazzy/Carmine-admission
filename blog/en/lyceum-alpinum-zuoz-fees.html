Lyceum Alpinum Zuoz fees: the real cost of a year, and the clauses to read before signing

The Lyceum Alpinum, Zuoz, Upper Engadine. Photo Asurnipal, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Zuoz is Switzerland's highest boarding school, at 1,750 metres, a quarter of an hour from St. Moritz. In 2026-27, a boarding year costs CHF 101,100 up to grade 10 and CHF 104,900 in grades 11 and 12. That is the figure everyone finds. What matters more is what comes on top in the first year, and above all three contract dates that families often discover too late.

For a grade 9 boarder in a double room, the first year comes to over CHF 107,000 before any extras, plus CHF 16,000 of deposit tied up until the student leaves. With a single room, it passes 122,000. And the supplement guarantees nothing: single rooms go first to grades 10 to 12 and to returning students, and families learn the assignment two weeks before term. In grades 7 and 8, everyone shares; in grade 12, everyone has their own room.

What is included, and what is not

The package is broad: accommodation, meals, health centre and psychologists, activities, textbooks, college counselling, community weekends, and a year-round mountain pass, skiing included. External exams are not: allow from CHF 1,000 for IGCSE or IB depending on subjects. Private lessons cost CHF 110 per forty minutes one to one. Outings and trips are recharged with a 5% handling fee and 8.1% VAT. Pocket money runs from CHF 50 to 300 a month depending on age.

Two details weigh on the bill. For a student whose parents live outside Switzerland and the European Union, the school's insurance is compulsory. And a family paying in euros is invoiced at the cantonal bank's rate of the day plus a 2% safety margin: on CHF 100,000, that is no detail.

The three dates that bind you

Zuoz's contract is precise, and that is where the real money is. First, a family can withdraw at no cost before 15 May, losing only the application fee and the Legacy Fund; between 15 May and 15 June, half the deposit is forfeited, and after 15 June, all of it. Second, leaving the school requires a registered letter to the Head before 31 October for departure at the end of the first semester, or before 28 February for the end of the year; miss those dates and the next semester is owed. Third, a disciplinary expulsion after 15 May or after 30 October leaves parents liable for the current and the following semester, whether or not the family considers the expulsion justified.

No absence is refunded, and fees are indexed to Swiss inflation each year. For a family still undecided in spring, 15 May is the real deadline.

The right reflexes

The 10% sibling discount only applies when the children are at Zuoz at the same time. Individual learning support is billed separately, from CHF 3,000 to 9,000 a year depending on intensity: for a child who will need it, it belongs in the budget from day one. And the programme shapes the total cost too: a student entering in grade 9 to finish the IB or the Matura in grade 12 commits to four years, more than CHF 400,000.
