Singapore's school year starts in January, and the AEIS calendar follows it. The main exercise runs from July to December for a January start; the supplementary exercise, the S-AEIS, runs in the first months of the year for a start in April or May. For a family planning a move, the choice between the two often depends on the moving date more than on anything else.

I plan this calendar with families every year. Here it is, with the 2026 dates as a guide.

The step before the application

For primary applicants, the Cambridge English result must be in hand when the family applies, and it must come from a sitting after a set date; for the 2026 AEIS, results from July 2025 onwards. Cambridge exam dates and result times vary by centre, so the English test needs to be booked months before the AEIS application opens. Missing it does not cost a few weeks: it costs the whole exercise.

The S-AEIS is not a full second chance at every level. It covers Primary 2 to 4 and Secondary 1 and 2 only. A child who reaches twelve in the year of entry can sit the Primary 5 test at the AEIS but only the Primary 4 test at the S-AEIS, and Secondary 3 is open only through the AEIS.

Processing and reporting

Once submitted, an application shows as pending, then as registered, which takes at least four weeks, longer if documents are missing. The ministry does not answer status queries; families watch the remarks column in their portal. After results, successful children must report to their school within a short window, for primary in 2026 from 18 to 30 December, or risk losing the place.
