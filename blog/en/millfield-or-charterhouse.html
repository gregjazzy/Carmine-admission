Millfield or Charterhouse

Families who look at Millfield and Charterhouse together usually want a co-educational British boarding school with ambition beyond the classroom. The two schools cost about the same, but they are built differently, and they choose pupils differently.

Two ways of choosing

Charterhouse's recommended 13+ route is long and structured: registration by 30 September of Year 6, the ISEB Pre-Test in the autumn, group activities, interviews and creative writing in the spring, and an offer that waits on the child's Year 8 reference. Millfield asks for less, but asks it face to face: a thirty-minute interview with a senior member of staff on the child's interests, activities and experiences, a reasoning test of just under an hour, and the school's reference. A child who performs well in tests and group tasks may find Charterhouse's process suits them; a child whose strengths come through in conversation may be read more fully at Millfield.

Two characters

Millfield is the largest co-educational boarding school for 13 to 18 year olds in the United Kingdom, with thirty sports, two hundred sports staff and facilities that include an Olympic-size pool and an equestrian centre; it is also international, with two pupils in five living abroad. Its size is a strength for a child who wants range and a weakness for one who prefers a small community, though classes average around twelve. Charterhouse, four centuries old, looks for the complete child, independent and kind, ready to try everything, and its academic selection starts earlier.

Money

Millfield's boarding fee is a little lower, and covers many items other schools bill as extras. Its scholarships are capped at 10% but open the way to means-tested bursaries of up to 100% in exceptional cases. Charterhouse offers merit scholarships and separate means-tested bursaries reviewed every year.

Applying to both

The calendars overlap without colliding. For 13+, Charterhouse comes first: registration by 30 September of Year 6 and the Pre-Test that autumn. Millfield's interview and test can follow in Year 6 or Year 7. Preparing the Pre-Test also strengthens the reasoning Millfield assesses, and preparing the Millfield interview helps with the Charterhouse assessment day.
