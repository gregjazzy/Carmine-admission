For an international student, Raffles Institution does not recruit directly. The first step is entering Singapore's mainstream system.

The AEIS

International students who wish to join a Singapore mainstream school sit the AEIS, the Admissions Exercise for International Students, run by the Ministry of Education each year around September or October. Raffles advises contacting the ministry first.

And then

Once at a mainstream school, an international boy can aim for Raffles through the Secondary 1 posting after the PSLE, the appeal, or Year 3; these routes are open to international students.
