The Phillips Exeter admission timeline

Book a conversation

On paper, Exeter's calendar comes down to two dates: the file is due on January 15 and decisions arrive on March 10. Families who stop at those two dates tend to start in December. The applications that hold up are built over about fifteen months, because several pieces have their own clock: the test, the teachers, the translations, the interview slot.

I work every year with families applying to Exeter and to the other leading American boarding schools. What follows is the calendar as it really runs, for entry in September 2027.

The year before: the test window opens

A test score counts only if the test was taken no earlier than one year before the January 15 deadline. For a September 2027 entry, the window therefore opened on January 15, 2026. A practice SSAT taken in the autumn of seventh grade is too early to count, and a child who sits the real thing then will have to sit it again.

The test itself depends on the grade. Ninth-grade applicants take the SSAT or ISEE at Upper Level. Tenth and eleventh-grade applicants may also use the PSAT, SAT, PreACT or ACT. One-year seniors and postgraduates can no longer use the SSAT or ISEE at all. A family that knows which test applies in the spring can plan a first sitting, a margin to improve, and a final score well before the winter.

A file built on time

Exeter reads an application in which every piece has had its time: the test, the essays, the interview, the two teachers. I prepare the child for each of them and keep the calendar, so that January 15 is a formality.

From July: opening the file

The application begins on Gateway to Prep Schools, with the Candidate Profile, from July. Within twenty-four hours Exeter sends the login for its own applicant portal, where the family books the interview, submits the essays and the parent statement, and follows the file until decision day. The fee is $60, and it is waived for families applying for financial aid.

Each applicant has one interview appointment, and it must take place by January 15. Families who choose the campus interview usually combine it with a tour, which means a trip to New Hampshire to plan around school holidays. Booking in the summer leaves the choice of date; booking in January leaves whatever is left.

September and October: aid and the teachers

The financial aid application opens in September, on Clarity, with its own $65 fee and the same January 15 deadline. It has to be made in the year of admission: aid that was not requested at entry may not be available later.

In October, the recommendation and transcript section opens on Gateway. The family enters the contact details; the school and each recommender then receive their own link. Four letters are needed: the principal or counselor, the current English teacher, the current math teacher, and an adult outside school who can speak to character. Teachers write dozens of letters in the autumn. Those asked in October, with a word on why Exeter, write better ones than those asked in December.

Reports must reach Exeter in English, from the school or from a licensed translator. For a child at a school that reports in another language, two years of reports need translating, and that is a task for October, not January.

November to January 15: the written file

The two essays, 200 to 500 words each, and the parent statement are submitted in the applicant portal, where the prompts appear. They are the only pieces the child and the family write themselves, and the ones that most often suffer from being written over the Christmas holidays. A first draft in November leaves time for the second and third.

The test score must also be in. For ninth grade, the SSAT or ISEE result goes directly from the testing organisation to Exeter; a score sent by the family is not accepted. A child who still hopes to improve needs a sitting early enough for the result to arrive before the deadline.

March 10, and the summer that follows

Decisions are released on March 10. For a family abroad, the next stages are concrete. The instructions for the I-20, the document needed for the student visa, are sent in late April, and the form cannot be issued more than 120 days before the start of school. International students are invited in April to an orientation held over the three days before term. Every new student completes a questionnaire and sits placement tests in mathematics and languages, which decide the courses they start in. Incoming ninth graders also have required summer reading, and dormitory assignments are published at the end of August.
