Eton College fees 2026-2027

In 2026-2027, Eton costs £21,891 per Half (term), including VAT. With three Halves in the year, that comes to about £65,700. The fee covers tuition, boarding, lodging and most games and educational materials.

What comes on top

  • Registration: £480, non-refundable.
  • Acceptance fee, for entry in September 2026: £3,840, of which £500 is refunded when the boy leaves.
  • Overseas deposit: one term's fees, paid on accepting the place and credited on the final bill.
  • Visa: £3,000 a year for a boy whose student visa Eton sponsors.
  • Extras: music lessons, insurance, boat club, school trips.

Since January 2025, 20% VAT applies to English independent school fees.

A place cannot be bought

At Eton, price never separates families: the Pre-Test, the interview and the final exam do. I prepare your son for each.

Bursaries

Eton spends over £10 million a year on financial aid. In 2024-2025, 22% of boys received support, and 89 paid nothing. Bursaries are means-tested, after a financial declaration completed by all families at the end of Year 5. Scholarships such as the King's Scholarship carry no automatic fee remission.
