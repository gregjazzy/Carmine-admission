Visa, age and grade placement at HKIS

Before any question of assessment or priority, HKIS applies practical conditions. They are simple, but families relocating to Hong Kong sometimes discover them late, and some can change the year a child enters.

Residence and visa

  • Visa or right of abode: students must hold valid proof, such as a dependant visa, a Hong Kong identity card, a Hong Kong birth certificate or a Hong Kong passport, before school starts.
  • Living with a parent: a student may not enter HKIS unless living with at least one parent who is in full-time residence in Hong Kong.
  • Health insurance: every student must have personal health insurance; HKIS does not provide medical cover for accidents during school activities.
  • Medical examination: a physical examination within the past six months is required of all new students before entry.

Proof of birth date, such as a birth certificate or passport, is required for the youngest. A student entering High School must submit records for all previous high school coursework, as graduation credit cannot be given without a full high school history.

Arriving from a different school calendar

HKIS runs from the third week of August to the second week of June. Students transferring from a country whose school year follows a different calendar generally continue in the grade they have just completed. A child in Grade 2 in Australia, for example, would remain in Grade 2 at HKIS for the rest of HKIS's academic year. Families should plan for this, especially when moving mid-year from the southern hemisphere.

Timing a move

Applications close on 1 October for the Primary School and 1 November for Grades 6 to 12; later applications are accepted only case by case. A family planning to move in the following summer should apply in the autumn before, even if the visa will only be issued later: it must simply be valid before school starts.
