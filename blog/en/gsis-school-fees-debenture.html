GSIS: fees and debenture

At GSIS, annual fees run from HKD 203,900 in kindergarten to HKD 210,800 in primary, HKD 251,300 in lower secondary and HKD 265,700 in upper secondary, paid in two instalments.

What comes on top

  • The registration fee, one month's fees, offset against the first instalment but forfeited if the student does not start.
  • A debenture or capital levy, compulsory for every student. The Standard Debenture is HKD 800,000, refunded when the student leaves, subject to its conditions.
  • Bus, PE uniform, activities, exams, trips, extra.

A place cannot be bought

Even a debenture does not exempt from the assessments: it only gives priority once they are passed. I prepare your child to pass them.
