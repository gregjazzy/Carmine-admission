The Milton admissions calendar

Milton Academy has a single admission round for its Upper School. Everything happens between September and April, and almost every deadline falls in a three-week window in January. For a student entering in September 2027, the year looks like this.

Autumn 2026: opening the file

The first step is the request for information form, which unlocks the admission portal. From there, the family books the interview, on campus with a tour or on Zoom; appointments run from September to mid-January and fill up quickly. Autumn is also the time to start the online application through Gateway to Prep Schools or the SSAT Standard Application, and to ask the three required recommenders, a school administrator and the current English and math teachers, while they still have time to write well.

Families who decide to submit test scores, which Milton does not require, should plan the SSAT or ISEE between October and January. Students whose English is not their first language, or who have spent less than two years in English-medium schooling, should also schedule TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo.

Winter: the January crunch

  • Before 15 January: the Candidate Biographic Profile, submitted well ahead of the deadline.
  • 15 January 2027: application deadline. The interview must be completed, recommendations and grades in, any test scores sent.
  • 31 January 2027: financial aid deadline. Families who meet it are considered for aid before those who apply later.

The fall term or semester grades are part of the file, so the first months of the current school year count.

Spring 2027: the answer

Decisions are released on 10 March 2027, together with the preliminary financial aid assessment for families who applied. Families then have until 10 April 2027 to reply. That month is often when families compare offers from several schools, ideally after a second visit.

The dates that cannot move

Three dates are fixed: the interview before 15 January, the application on 15 January, and aid on 31 January. Everything else, from testing to the profile answers, can be arranged around them. The most common problem is the interview: families who book late get the least convenient slots.

Grade 10 and 11 applicants

The calendar is the same for boarding applicants to Grades 10 and 11 and day applicants to Grade 10. For Grade 11, the PSAT, SAT or ACT can replace the SSAT or ISEE if a family chooses to send scores.

How I use this calendar

For seven years, I have worked with around fifteen families a year on applications to very selective schools. For Milton, I fix the interview date first, then plan the recommendations, the profile answers and, if useful, a test date backwards from 15 January.
