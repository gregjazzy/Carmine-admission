Milton's timeline converges on January 15, and ends on March 10.

The dates

September to mid-January: interviews, on campus or by Zoom, booked in the portal.

By January 15: tests, if sent, taken in the current school year; complete file.

By January 31: financial aid application.

March 10: admission and aid decisions.

The preparation timeline

Interview slots go fast: best to book from September, and prepare the interview beforehand. Teachers who will recommend the child should be asked in the fall.
