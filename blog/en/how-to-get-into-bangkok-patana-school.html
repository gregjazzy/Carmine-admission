How to get into Bangkok Patana School

Bangkok and the Chao Phraya. Photo Piyatad, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

International schools in Asia November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Bangkok Patana School is Thailand's first and largest British international school: over 2,200 students aged 2 to 18, from about 65 countries. A not-for-profit school, it follows the English National Curriculum to Year 11, then the IB in Years 12 and 13.

The steps

1. The file: reports from the last two years, identity documents, forms, and a recommendation sent by the current teacher with a writing sample. 2. The online application. 3. Review and assessments, subject to places. 4. The decision: a formal offer from the Head. 5. Enrolment, with an initial payment to hold the place.

What to know

Many year groups are full: the school advises applying early, and places files in a waiting pool.
