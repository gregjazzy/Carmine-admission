Recommendations and the parent statement

Grubbs Quadrangle, Loomis Chaffee, Windsor, Connecticut. Photo Daderot, Wikimedia Commons, CC0

US boarding schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

In a holistic, test-optional process, what other people write about a student carries real weight. Loomis Chaffee asks for three recommendations from the current school and a statement from the parents, and invites more when they help.

What is required

PieceFrom
English recommendationThe current English teacher
Mathematics recommendationThe current math teacher
Principal or counsellor recommendationUsually sent with the transcript
Parent/Guardian StatementThe parents or guardians, for every candidate

All can be submitted through the Standard Application Online or the Gateway application, and are also available as PDFs.

When to add a letter

Additional recommendations are not necessary, but can give a more complete picture. Loomis advises choosing someone who knows the student well and can share information not available elsewhere in the file. When a student does not submit test scores, the school strongly recommends an additional academic recommendation, which takes on part of what a score would have shown.

Every voice in its place

I help families plan who writes what, so that teachers, counsellor and parents each add something different, and the file reads as one coherent picture.

Book a conversation

Asking teachers well

  • Ask early, in the autumn, well before the 15 January deadline and the winter break.
  • Ask current teachers, as Loomis requires, even if a teacher from last year knew the student better; that teacher can write the additional letter.
  • Give context: a short note on the student's interests and the reasons for Loomis helps a teacher write specifically.

Not sure who should write the extra letter? Let's think it through.

The parent statement

Parents know things no teacher sees: how a child spends free time, handles setbacks, treats younger siblings, or what they talk about at dinner. For a boarding school, those details help the committee imagine the student in a residential community. The most useful statements are specific and honest, and add to the file rather than repeat it.

The transcript beside the letters

Recommendations are read next to official transcripts, which the schools send directly. They must show the current year's courses and latest grades, plus the previous year; applicants to grade 11 or a postgraduate year send all of high school. Talent material, if any, goes in as Vimeo, Hudl or YouTube links, never Google Drive or social media.
