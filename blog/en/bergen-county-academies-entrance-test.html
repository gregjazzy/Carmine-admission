The BCA entrance test

BCA writes its own entrance test; it uses neither the HSPT nor the COOP. Together with the application file, the test decides who goes on to the interview in Phase 2. Every applicant sits it on one of two days in late November.

The two parts

EnglishMath
Time45 minutes60 minutes
TaskRead a passage, write an essay in response40 multiple-choice questions
ContentComprehension, a clear thesis, organisation, support from the text, style, grammar and spellingBasic math skills and word problems
Practice materialNone publishedA 40-question sample test with answer key

Calculators, phones, smart watches, smart glasses and notes are not allowed in any part. The test is taken on Chromebooks provided by BCA. Students bring their test ticket, three #2 pencils and erasers, in a clear plastic bag; no food or drink.

The date is assigned

For entry in September 2027, testing takes place on Saturday 21 or Sunday 22 November 2026. Applicants do not choose: BCA assigns each student a date and time, visible on the studentrecs.bergen.org portal from 12 November. Families must keep both days free between 7:30 a.m. and 2:00 p.m. A student who is ill must email the admissions office on 23 November with a doctor's note to receive a make-up date.

Two very different skills

I prepare students for both halves: a structured essay in 45 minutes, and 40 questions at a pace of 90 seconds each with no calculator.

The honour pledge

Every applicant pledges to complete the test without help or devices, and never to discuss or post its questions, before or after. The same applies to interview questions. A breach can invalidate the application. That is one reason families find so little reliable information about the content beyond the official sample.

Preparation, honestly

BCA states that students who take prep courses have no advantage over those who do not, and recommends reviewing middle school English and math and working through the sample test. It also notes that students who have taken algebra and geometry in middle school typically do better. Real preparation is therefore less a course than a foundation: solid algebra, fluent mental arithmetic, and the habit of writing a clear argument about a text.

Accommodations

Students with a 504 Plan or an IEP must tell their guidance counsellor at least five days before the test to expect a request from BCA; the counsellor confirms the accommodations.
