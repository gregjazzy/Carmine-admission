The BCA test has two parts, and its result decides who goes on to interview. For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year towards the most selective schools, and I have students at Bergen County Academies.

What the school says

English: 45 minutes to read a passage and write an essay. Math: 40 multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes, on basic skills and word problems. No calculators, phones, smart watches or glasses, or notes. A 40-question sample math test with answer key is published. The school adds that students who take algebra and geometry in middle school typically fare better.

Preparing both parts

I prepare your child to write a structured essay in 45 minutes and keep pace through 40 questions without a calculator.

What the school does not say

No cut-off is published. But with far more applicants than places, every part counts.

Why strong students fail

Time

A minute and a half per math question, no calculator: mental arithmetic must be secure.
