Entering UTS in Grade 7

University of Toronto Schools admits around 96 new students into Grade 7 each year, against about 22 in Grade 9 and up to four each in Grades 10 and 11. There is no entry at all in Grade 8 or Grade 12. Grade 7 is therefore the main door, and for many families the only realistic one. Applicants for September 2027 are in Grade 6 now.

I work every year with families applying to the most selective schools, UTS among them. Here is how the Grade 7 entry works.

Who can apply

The student must be in Grade 6 in the year of application, and the family must live full-time in the Greater Toronto Area for the whole of the child's time at UTS. A gifted designation is not required: UTS takes students from gifted and non-gifted programs, and its enriched program includes course acceleration at every grade level. Reach-ahead credits are not needed either.

Admission is merit-based and decided separately from bursaries. A family that needs financial support can apply for it without affecting the admission decision.

Stage 1: the file and the test

The Stage 1 file for Grade 7 is short: report cards from the last three years, a list of interests and activities, and optionally an Individual Education Plan or an individual circumstances letter. Unlike older applicants, Grade 7 candidates submit no teacher references. The application closes on December 1, 2026, and costs $250 before November 1 or $300 after; the fee covers the test.

The test itself, on December 5, is the heart of Stage 1. Grade 7 applicants write in the morning at the University of Toronto Exam Centre, in two groups by last name. It covers English and math from the Ontario curriculum up to Grade 6: reading with written responses and a writing task, then short-answer questions and multi-step problems with full solutions, without a calculator.

Why Grade 6 content is not easy

Families sometimes relax on hearing that the test uses Grade 6 material. They should not. With roughly 96 places and hundreds of applicants, the test separates children who know Grade 6 content from children who command it: who can solve an unfamiliar problem in several steps and explain each one, and who can read a text and write about what the author is doing rather than summarize it. Grade 7 is also the only level for which UTS publishes past papers and sample questions, so every serious applicant has seen them.

Stage 2: stations and group task

Selected applicants are invited on January 8, 2027. On January 23 they come to UTS for five or six short interview stations, modelled on McMaster medical school's multiple mini-interviews, and a group task with five or six other applicants, judged on collaboration rather than results. A current progress report is also requested. Decisions are announced on February 26, 2027, with bursary decisions at the same time.

For an eleven-year-old, the stations are often the more unfamiliar part. Short scenarios, a few minutes each, with a new adult every time: children who have practised thinking aloud under time pressure arrive much calmer.
