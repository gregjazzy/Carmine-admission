Lycée International: entering seconde and preparing the BFI

Schools near Paris November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

In lycée, the Lycée International leads to the French International Baccalaureate, the BFI. Entering seconde (age 15) therefore means choosing a distinctive bac, and preparing it for three years in the section's language. Admission remains that of the whole school: a file and a section test.

How to get in

In lycée, the test has a written and an oral part, mainly in the section's language. In the American Section, the written English exam lasts up to 90 minutes, and selected candidates have a 30-minute interview. The British Section offers an entry for French speakers with advanced English, Seconde Accueil, with a €150 application fee; its lycée registration fee is €1,750.

A lycée place, and the right BFI

The BFI opens French and foreign universities. Since 2021, my students have been admitted to Columbia, Stanford, EPFL, McGill and King's College: I prepare entry into seconde, then the application that will count after the bac.

What the BFI is worth here

The American Section's class of 2026 had a 100% BFI pass rate, with 15% assez bien, 35% bien, 38% très bien and 8% with the jury's congratulations. For a student aiming at British or North American universities, the BFI of an English-speaking section is a real asset, provided written English is at the level from seconde.
