Newark Academy admits students into six different grades, from 6th to 11th. Two of them, 6th and 9th grade, are its main entry points; the others take fewer students. Choosing the right year can matter as much as preparing the application.

The six doors

GradeWhat it means
6thMain entry into the middle school
7th and 8thFewer places, depending on space
9thMain entry into the upper school
10th and 11thFewer places; 11th grade entrants arrive as the IB years begin

Every applicant, whatever the grade, completes the Online Admission Form, the SSAT or ISEE, the Candidate Conversation and teacher recommendations. There is no mid-year entry.

Read in context

An admission officer reviews each file, then the Admission Committee discusses it in the context of the grade applied for. In practice, a candidate is compared with others applying for the same year, and the committee builds a class with diverse interests and backgrounds within each grade.

The right year, then the right file

I help families decide when to apply, then prepare the SSAT or ISEE and the conversation at the level that grade expects.

6th or 9th?

Entering in 6th grade gives a student seven years at the school, with time to settle before the demands of the upper school. Entering in 9th grade brings a student into a larger group of newcomers at the start of high school, with four years ahead and the IB programme in 11th and 12th grade. Both are main entry points; the choice depends on the child's readiness and the family's plans.

Not sure whether to apply this year or later? Let's look at your child's situation.

Arriving later

A student entering in 10th or 11th grade has less time before the IB years, in which juniors and seniors choose between individual IB courses and the full Diploma. All seniors take at least one IB course. A late entrant should look carefully at the course sequence that leads into the IB subjects they want.

What does not change with the grade

The calendar is the same for every year of entry: the form by 1 December, the prior year's transcript by 15 December, the other materials by 11 January, decisions on 19 February. The financial commitment is the same too: tuition of $55,525 in 2026-27, plus required fees, and 100 % of demonstrated need met for every admitted family, whichever grade the student joins.
