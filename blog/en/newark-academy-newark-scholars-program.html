The Newark Scholars Program

Livingston, New Jersey, home of Newark Academy. Photo Scott Brody, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Newark Academy takes its name from the city of Newark, with which it has a long-standing history, even though its campus is now in Livingston. Its Newark Scholars Program, created in 2012, keeps that tie alive: each year, up to six talented students from the city receive a full scholarship and a support network designed around them.

What the scholarship covers
PlacesUp to six students a year
EligibilityLiving in, or attending school in, the city of Newark
Financial coverTuition, fees, trips and transportation
SupportA dedicated program coordinator; a mentorship scheme pairing new and returning scholars
CommunityA cohort meeting monthly; an alumni network with on-campus events

How students are considered

There is no separate form. A student who lives or studies in Newark and completes both the admission process and the financial aid process through Clarity is considered automatically. That makes the ordinary deadlines decisive: the Online Admission Form by 1 December, and the supplemental materials and Clarity application by 11 January.

The same bar for everyone

Newark Scholars are admitted through the same process as every applicant. I prepare students from Newark for the SSAT or ISEE, the form and the conversation.

Beyond the money

A full scholarship removes the financial barrier; the rest of the programme addresses the others. A coordinator is a point of contact for scholars and their families, mentoring connects new students with those who arrived before them, and the monthly cohort gives scholars a group of their own within the wider school.

Where scholars have gone

The school features several members of the class of 2016 from the programme. One earned a degree in industrial and labour relations at Cornell and now works in risk management at a major bank; another studied business at Muhlenberg while playing college football; a third became a professional basketball player in the WNBA after a master's in public policy at the University of Virginia; a fourth works for an architecture firm in Newark.

If the family is not from Newark

All other aid at Newark Academy is need-based too, and the school meets 100 % of demonstrated need for every admitted family. The Newark Scholars Program is a distinction on top of that commitment, not the only route to an affordable place.
