Franklin: getting into sixième, and what happens before

At Franklin, sixième (age 11, the first year of collège) is the main way in, and also the shortest. Applications for September 2027 opened on 15 September 2026 and closed on the 30th: two weeks, during the CM2 year, a year before entry. After that, the form closes. That is what surprises every family. What surprises them less, and matters more, is what decides afterwards.

Selection by file, then an appointment

There is no written exam. The file, submitted online with its documents, is checked and then reviewed by the director or the préfet. The family then receives either an offer of an appointment or a refusal; and the school warns that the appointment does not mean enrolment. Everything therefore rests on two things: the school reports, and that appointment.

That is why sixième at Franklin is prepared from CM1. The CM1 and early CM2 reports are the ones the school reads. A family that starts thinking about it in September of CM2 has already played the part that counts most.

A sixième place at Franklin

I prepare with you an application of excellence, submitted in the September window, and bring your child to the appointment with the director or the préfet at the top of their level. My aim: that they get into Franklin, or into the school of your choice if another suits them better.

The bilingual track and classical languages

From sixième, families choose between two tracks: the German-English bilingual track, with half-group sessions taught by native speakers to work on speaking, or the classic track, with English first and Spanish from cinquième. Latin is compulsory for everyone in cinquième, then students continue Latin, or a Latin-Greek group, until troisième. It is a collège with a strong humanist culture, and a child who enters commits to that path.

Is your child in CM1 or CM2? Tell me about them: I will tell you what their reports need to show, and how to prepare the appointment.

What is at stake in the appointment

Franklin is a Jesuit school, and its educational charter matters: the school asks families to read it before applying. The appointment checks the child as much as the family's commitment to this project: an intellectual, human and spiritual education, a demanding framework, Saturday morning classes from cinquième. A family that comes only for the school's reputation often shows it.
