Guaranteed UNC admission

NCSSM's Residential program comes with a promise few high schools can make. Graduates who meet the conditions are guaranteed admission to any University of North Carolina System institution to which they apply. Together with the UNC tuition grant, it makes NCSSM a path from 11th grade to a public university degree in North Carolina, at very little cost.

The conditions

  • Graduate from the NCSSM Residential program, in Durham or Morganton.
  • Complete all NCSSM graduation requirements and remain in good standing.
  • Hold a weighted GPA at or above the threshold set by the UNC System president and the NCSSM chancellor, currently 3.5.

The guarantee applies to Residential graduates from the Class of 2022 onward. NCSSM Online students are not eligible.

What it does not guarantee

Admission to a UNC System university does not guarantee entry to a particular school, college or program within it. Competitive programs still select. The UNC School of the Arts admits students only into specific programs, so admission there depends on acceptance into the chosen program. Students should still submit their strongest possible application to each institution, both to reach the programs they want and to compete for merit scholarships.

The UNC tuition grant

Separately, eligible Residential graduates who enrol full time at a UNC System university in the academic year after graduation can apply for a grant covering standard in-state tuition, though not room, board or fees. Recipients must stay continuously enrolled, make satisfactory progress and, from the Class of 2024, file a FAFSA each year. The grant is administered by the North Carolina State Education Assistance Authority, and graduates are responsible for applying.

How families should read it

The guarantee is a safety net, not a ceiling. NCSSM graduates have been accepted at a wide range of colleges in recent years, inside and outside North Carolina. For a student aiming at a selective program at a UNC flagship or at a private university elsewhere, the guarantee secures a strong in-state option while they aim higher.
