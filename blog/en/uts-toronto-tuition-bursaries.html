University of Toronto Schools is an independent school with tuition of $40,400 in 2026-27, plus a one-time enrolment fee of $9,500 for new students. It also runs one of the largest bursary programs among Canadian independent schools: last year more than $3.6 million went to 25 percent of students, covering anywhere from 5 to 100 percent of fees. Figures for 2027-28 will be published in January 2027.

I go through these numbers with every family I accompany to UTS. Here they are.

Tuition includes the student activity fee, course fees and the outdoor experiential education trips. Textbooks, optional course-related international travel and specialist sports equipment are extra. In the first year, a full-fee family therefore pays about $49,900 before those extras. UTS is a day school, so there are no boarding costs.

Bursaries: separate from admission

The bursary process is entirely separate from admission. Bursary applications do not affect the admission decision, and UTS admits qualified students regardless of financial circumstances. Families who need help should therefore apply without hesitation. The application fee itself can be waived on the payment page of the application portal.

Bursary applications open on December 1, when the admission portal closes, and are made through Apple Financial Services by January 11, 2027. Apple reviews each family's finances in confidence and recommends an amount; the UTS Bursary Committee decides. Decisions are emailed with the offer of admission on February 26, 2027.

Unsure whether your family would qualify for support? Tell me about your situation before January 11.

How need is assessed

Families provide full financial disclosure, including their Canada Revenue Agency Notice of Assessment, and documents from both parents where two are involved. The assessment uses three tiers of criteria: income and net worth first; then the number of children, childcare costs and siblings at UTS; and finally spending on vacations, camps, lessons, sports and arts, transport, the age and type of work of the parents, and discretionary spending. Awards range from 5 percent of tuition to full support, including the enrolment fee for new students.

Two conditions matter over time. Bursaries must be reapplied for every year, and a returning student receives at least the same support as the previous year if the family's situation is similar. Incomplete documentation can lead to support being withdrawn.

No merit scholarships

UTS offers no entrance scholarships. All support is need-based and reserved for students who have been offered admission. A strong test result does not reduce fees; it opens the door, and the bursary process takes over from there.
