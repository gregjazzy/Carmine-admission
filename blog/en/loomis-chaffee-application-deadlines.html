Loomis Chaffee application deadlines

Loomis Chaffee has a single calendar for every applicant, whether they live in Connecticut or Singapore, apply for 9th grade or a postgraduate year. Five dates frame it, between mid-January and mid-April.

The five dates

DateWhat
15 January 2027Application deadline for fall 2027
31 January 2027Financial aid application (Clarity) deadline
1 February 2027Last day for official test scores to arrive, if submitted
10 March 2027Decisions for all applications received by 15 January, with financial aid awards
10 April 2027Admitted students enroll or decline

Loomis does not admit students mid-year. Applications received after 15 January are considered on a rolling basis after 10 March, only if places remain, and with very little financial aid left.

The autumn before

  • Inquiry form: the first step, which opens access to tours and interviews.
  • Interview: on campus until mid-January, online or in a visited city; after 1 December, only once the Candidate Profile and fee are in.
  • Testing: an SSAT or ISEE in the autumn, if the family decides to submit scores.
  • Recommendations: requested from the English and math teachers and the counsellor well before the holidays.
  • Test Optional Form: filed on the portal whatever the decision on testing; without it, the file is not reviewed.
  • Fee: $75 in North America, $160 elsewhere, paid by card, or a waiver requested from the office.

Ready before 15 January

I plan the Loomis application with families from the spring: testing, essays over the summer, the interview in the autumn, and a complete file before the winter break.

Applying for fall 2027 or 2028? Let's put the dates in order.

Between 10 March and 10 April

Families have a month to decide. Admission and financial aid decisions arrive together, so a family can weigh the real cost before committing. LC Scholars are also told on 10 March. For families comparing several schools, it is the moment to visit again and ask the last questions.

For current families

Financial aid is renewed each year, with an earlier deadline of 1 December for current students, and decisions issued with re-enrollment contracts in late March.

How I keep families on schedule

I have students at Loomis Chaffee, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools. The aim is simple: by the winter break, every piece of the Loomis file is either submitted or requested, so that 15 January passes without stress.
