The BCA admissions timeline

BCA admission runs from September of 8th grade to April, almost entirely through two websites: PowerSchool Enrollment for the application, then the studentrecs.bergen.org portal for everything afterwards. No letters are mailed, and no status is given by phone. A family that knows the dates in advance avoids most of the avoidable mistakes.

Submit early, not on 8 November

Teachers and counsellors receive the recommendation and transcript requests only after the application is submitted. BCA encourages submitting as early as possible, so that schools have time to respond before the January due date. An application sent in late October gives teachers the autumn; one sent on the last evening leaves them the holidays.

The email that decides everything

Every message from BCA goes to one personal email address, checked daily from September to June. School and business addresses are refused, because firewalls block outside messages, and each child in a family needs their own. Adding admissions@bergen.org and the PowerSchool sender to the address book avoids messages being filtered.
