Harvard-Westlake: tuition

St. Saviour's Chapel, Harvard-Westlake, Los Angeles. Photo Keithready, Wikimedia Commons, public domain

US schools November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

In 2026-2027, Harvard-Westlake costs $54,900 a year, with a one-time new student fee of $2,500. Other expenses, books, transport, meals and class activities, typically average $2,500 to $5,000.

Financial aid

About 21% of students receive aid, based solely on demonstrated need, and the average tuition responsibility of aided families is $16,000. Key point: a student who does not apply for aid at admission is not eligible later, except for unforeseen reversals. The aid application goes in with the admission application, by January 15, and the application fee is then waived.
