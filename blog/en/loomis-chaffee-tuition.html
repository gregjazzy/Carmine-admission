Loomis Chaffee publishes its fees in detail, which makes it possible to build an honest budget. Tuition is the large figure, but several smaller ones follow, and an international family pays a few more.

What each fee covers

  • Technology: networks, wireless access, the school's information systems, Microsoft 365 and Adobe Creative Cloud.
  • Health centre: nursing staff day and night, basic assessments, first aid; diagnostic tests such as flu or strep are extra. It is not insurance: boarders must show health cover or join the school's accident and sickness plan.
  • International: the Office of International Student Support, and safekeeping of passports and I-20s.
  • Refund insurance: optional, but strongly recommended for new students.

Beyond the fees

Books, art supplies and other academic materials usually cost $1,200 to $1,600 a year, depending on courses. Field trips and some athletic supplies are extra, as are optional laundry service, music lessons and tutoring. Students bring their own computer; the school provides Microsoft 365. For a boarder, a realistic budget therefore lands above $80,000 a year before travel.

A third of families pay less

For 2026-27, Loomis awarded $14.2 million in need-based aid to 33 % of its students, with an average grant of 80 % of tuition. Aid is currently extremely limited, and must be requested on the application itself and through Clarity by 31 January. LC Scholars also receive a laptop and a stipend for books, whatever their family's means.
