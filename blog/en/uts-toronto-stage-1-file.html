The UTS Stage 1 file

Stage 1 of the UTS application is everything the school sees before deciding who goes on to interviews. It is completed in the UTS Application Portal between mid-September and December 1, and the school is unusually explicit about its limits: the components listed on its admissions site, and only those, are considered. Anything else a family might add is not read.

I help families put this file together every year. Here is each piece and what it does.

Report cards: three years, not one

Three years of report cards show a trajectory, not a snapshot. A child whose marks have risen steadily reads differently from one whose marks have fallen, even at the same level today. Teacher comments matter as much as grades: they are the only place in a Grade 7 file where a teacher's view of the child appears, since references are required only from Grade 9 upward.

The activities list

The Interests and Activities List is the only part of Stage 1 where the child's life outside class appears. A long list is not the aim. UTS states that it values applicants who show their abilities in different ways, and its circumstances letter explicitly mentions children who do not take part in paid activities. A few activities described precisely, with the child's actual role and commitment, say more than many named in passing.

The individual circumstances letter

This optional letter is where a family can explain anything the committee should know: an attendance record affected by illness, the absence of paid or unpaid extracurricular activities, responsibilities the child carries outside school, family circumstances that have affected their education or opportunities. It is not a place for general praise. Used well, it gives context to a grade or a gap that would otherwise be misread. Families whose child has an Individual Education Plan can submit it too; UTS welcomes these applicants and invites families to contact admissions to discuss support.

Where the weight lies

With no interview at this stage, the December test carries much of Stage 1. The file around it confirms or qualifies what the test shows. That is why preparation for UTS starts with the skills the test measures, and why the rest of the file should be complete, accurate and in on time rather than embellished.
