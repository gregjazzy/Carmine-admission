Bangkok Patana welcomes applications from students of all nationalities who can access and benefit from its programmes. Its admissions policy then sets out, in seven points, how an application is judged. Reading them closely tells a family more than any brochure.

The seven criteria, and what lies behind them

The school's criterionWhat it means in practice
Ability to access the curriculum with the support available in the year groupThe child must be able to follow lessons, in English, at that level
Reports, references, confidential recommendations, writing samples, assessmentsThe written file carries real weight
A positive attitude to learningEffort and engagement, as described by the current school
The school's ability to meet social, emotional, behavioural and learning needsNeeds must be disclosed, and matched to what the school can provide
Places in the year group and classMany year groups are full
Balance in gender, nationality and languageThe make-up of the class is a factor
Alignment of values between parents and schoolParents commit to the school's Guiding Statements

Diversity as a rule

To preserve its international diversity, Patana caps any single nationality at 20% of the student body. When a year group or support programme approaches capacity, the school prioritises diversity, aiming for an environment that reflects a broad spectrum of cultures, experiences and languages. For a family from a nationality already well represented, this can make a strong file all the more necessary.

The criteria a family can work on

Access to the curriculum, the writing sample and the assessment can all be prepared. I work on English and mathematics so that these parts of the file are clearly strong.

Living in Bangkok

A student is considered for enrolment only if living in Bangkok with a parent or legal guardian; in exceptional circumstances, the school may allow residence with an appointed guardian. Non-Thai students also need a valid non-immigrant visa before starting.

The values clause

The last criterion is easy to overlook. Patana asks parents to agree to commit to and abide by its Guiding Statements, and every parent signs a safeguarding code of conduct before the child starts. Families should read both early, as the school recommends, so that the commitment is informed.
