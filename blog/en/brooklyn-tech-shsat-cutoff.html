The Brooklyn Tech cutoff

Of the eight test-based specialized high schools, Brooklyn Tech had the second-lowest cutoff in 2026: 506. Families sometimes read that as a safe option. It is better read as what it is: the score of the last student who received an offer, once everyone above had been placed according to their own lists.

Where 506 sits

In the 2026 ranking of cutoffs, Brooklyn Tech came just below the High School of American Studies at Lehman College (507) and above Brooklyn Latin (495). At the top, Stuyvesant required 561; Bronx Science, 525. A gap of 55 points separates the first and seventh schools, which shows how differently the same test sorts students across the eight.

What the number reflects

A cutoff is the product of the score order and everyone's preferences. Students with higher scores take seats at the schools they ranked above Brooklyn Tech; the ones who ranked Brooklyn Tech higher, or could not reach their first choices, fill its seats down to the last place. A lower cutoff reflects the number of seats and the order of preferences, not a weaker education.

Planning around it

  • Set a target above 506, not at it: the line can rise if preferences shift.
  • Keep Brooklyn Tech on the list even if aiming higher: listing it costs nothing at the schools ranked above.
  • Remember the back-up: without a specialized offer, the general high school match becomes final.

Practice scores and real scores

From fall 2026, the SHSAT is computer-adaptive, and practice material from earlier years no longer mirrors the real test exactly. The official practice tests on the NYC SHSAT Portal, in the new format, are the only fair basis for comparing a child's level with 506. Older paper tests remain useful for content, much less for predicting a score.

What comes next

Reaching Brooklyn Tech opens a second competition: grades in 9th and 10th grade build the Power Index that decides each student's major. A student who scraped in and a student who arrived comfortably start that race on equal terms, but the habits that produced the SHSAT score often show again in the first two years.
