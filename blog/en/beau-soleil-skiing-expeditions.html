Beau Soleil: skiing and expeditions

In Villars-sur-Ollon, the mountains are part of the school.

Winter

In the winter term, skiing or snowboarding replace PE, with qualified instructors, up to two and a half days a week, on slopes five minutes from campus. A child who has never skied can start. The best join the teams, up to European competition; others qualify as ski instructors.

Expeditions

From Grade 6 to 11, at least two expeditions a year: hiking in the first term, hiking, biking or water-based in the third. In Grade 12, they are optional.

What to take from it

Time on the snow means less time in class: students must keep up with fewer hours.

Keeping up with both

I support your child in maths and science so they keep their level while enjoying the mountains.
