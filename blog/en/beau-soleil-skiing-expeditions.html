Swiss boarding schools

Skiing and expeditions

Beau Soleil sits in the heart of Villars-sur-Ollon, and the mountains are part of its curriculum rather than a backdrop. Outdoor education is described by the school as a cornerstone of its programme, and winter means skiing for everyone.

Winter on the slopes
Skiing or snowboardingAn integral part of the winter sports programme
FrequencyUsually at least two to three times a week in the winter term
LevelComplete beginners welcome
CompetitionSki is among the school's sports teams; a ski race team is an athlete development option at extra cost

A year of expeditions

  • Term 1, the Alps: an overnight expedition for all students, sleeping in alpine huts, with activities from hiking to rock climbing, via ferrata, glacier crossings or ice-capped summits, matched to experience. No student repeats the same route.
  • Term 2, winter skills: ice climbing, Nordic skiing, snowshoeing and building snow shelters; for the more advanced, ski touring, split boarding and avalanche safety.
  • Term 3: hiking, biking or water-based expeditions.

Students in Grades 6 to 11 take part in at least two expeditions a year; for Grade 12 they are optional. Each year, some expeditions go further, to Mount Kilimanjaro in Tanzania or the jungles of South America.

Alongside the expeditions, every student in Grades 6 to 11 goes on an end-of-year grade trip, covered by the annual trip fee of CHF 8,490, and optional trips during exeat weekends and half-term combine adventure, culture and service.

The classroom still counts

The mountains take time, and the IB programme does not wait. I support Beau Soleil students in mathematics and the sciences so that both fit into the week.

What it develops

The school's reasoning is that challenge outside the comfort zone builds confidence, teamwork and leadership, and that preparing for a demanding expedition teaches students to set ambitious goals and follow them through. Seven whole-school challenges a year, contested by boarding house, add to the same culture.

Is your child more at home on skis or with a book? Let's talk about the balance.

Practical points

Specialist clothing for skiing and expeditions is part of the uniform package for new students. Expeditions and other activities fall within the annual fees of CHF 26,460, while equipment rental or purchase, private coaching and athlete development programmes are charged separately.
