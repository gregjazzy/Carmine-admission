Families arriving from another country often ask which class their child will join at Bangkok Patana. The answer does not depend on the child's current grade or on a test: it depends on the date of birth. Placement follows the age norms of the British system, and places outside them are offered only in exceptional circumstances.

Why a child may land in an unexpected year

Systems with a January cut-off, or where children start school at a different age, can place a child a year ahead of or behind where the British calendar puts them. A child born in October, for instance, may find themselves among older or younger classmates than at home. Patana's curriculum is differentiated to cater for the normal range of ability within a year group, but the first admission criterion is that the student can access that year's curriculum in a meaningful way.

Two years to discuss first

The school asks families to contact the admissions office before applying for Year 11 or Year 13. These are the second years of two-year programmes, IGCSE and the IB Diploma, and joining midway raises questions of subjects and examinations that a date of birth alone does not answer.

Not sure which year group your child will join? Send me their date of birth and current class.

Option choices at entry

A student joining in Year 10 or Year 12 enters at the moment of option choices, for IGCSE or the IB, and the application includes the corresponding forms. Choosing subjects one can follow matters as much as the year group itself.
