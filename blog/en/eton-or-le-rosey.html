Eton or Le Rosey: how to choose

Eton College Chapel. Photo David P Howard, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

UK boarding schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Families torn between Eton and Le Rosey are looking for the same thing: one of the most renowned boarding schools in the world. But the two schools neither recruit the same child nor recruit the same way.

Two ways of recruiting

Eton is a boys' boarding school, ages 13 to 18, that selects very early: registration before 10, Pre-Test and interviews at 11, a place confirmed by an exam at 13. Le Rosey is co-educational, takes students from age 8, and decides mostly through a day on campus, with tests and an interview, then a committee that rules within weeks.

Two applications, one strategy

I prepare students for Eton as for Le Rosey. I tell you which suits your child, and prepare both applications, which have neither the same timeline nor the same tests.

Two schools, two worlds

Eton educates its boys in the English system, within a mainly British community. Le Rosey is built on diversity: more than half its students have neither French nor English as their mother tongue, and the school applies nationality quotas. For a child aiming at English universities, Eton is a direct path; for a child with an international life, Le Rosey offers a continuity few schools match.
