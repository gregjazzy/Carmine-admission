Roxbury Latin: a boys' school

Boston from the Charles River; Roxbury Latin is in West Roxbury, within the city. Photo King of Hearts, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Roxbury Latin says it plainly: it cares most about what kind of person a boy is.

What the school values

A life marked by honesty, simplicity, respect and concern for others. Trust runs so deep that backpacks are left unattended and lockers open. The founder believed a man's life should be directed toward public service: the school seeks to instil a desire for a life of noble purpose.

Competitive and compassionate

Boys learn to applaud others' successes, to recover from disappointment and find other ways to contribute. The school wants students both tough and responsive to others' needs.
