Tuition at Montgomery Bell Academy is $40,627 for 2026-2027. For a day school, the figure is more inclusive than it looks at first glance, and the list of what it covers is worth reading before comparing MBA with other Nashville schools.

What the $40,627 includes

  • Lunch every school day
  • Lab fees
  • Fitness packs
  • Activity fees
  • Student publications
  • Admission to on-campus arts and athletic events, except playoff games

What it does not

Books, trips, and travel for athletics and extracurricular activities are extra. For a boy who plays a travelling sport or joins one of the school's trips abroad, those lines can add up, and families should ask early what a typical year costs for the activities their son is likely to choose. The morning shuttle buses that serve Davidson and Williamson counties are a separate question to raise with the school.

Where aid comes in

MBA grants more than $3.4 million a year in need-based aid, and the average family receiving aid pays a little over $16,000. The details, who qualifies and when to apply, are covered in the article on financial aid. The essential point here is that the aid application shares its deadline with the admission application: January 1.

Why the price does not decide

I have students at Montgomery Bell, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year on admissions to the most selective schools in the United States, England and Switzerland. At MBA, what separates applicants is not the family's ability to pay but the boy: his ISEE, how he spends his shadow day, how he talks in the interview, and what his teacher writes. That is where I work, so that he arrives at each step ready.
