Foundation Year is the first year of The ISF Academy's primary school, the equivalent of the last year of kindergarten in the local system. It is the only entry point with no language requirement, which makes it the natural door for families who want bilingual immersion but whose child does not yet speak both languages. It also has the earliest calendar of the year.

I help families prepare this entry. Here is how it works for 2027-28.

Age and deadlines

Applicants for Foundation Year 2027-28 are born between September 1, 2021 and December 31, 2022, with a minimum age of four years and eight months on August 31, 2027. Children born between September and December can apply both for Foundation Year and for Grade 1; that means two application fees and two assessments, and the school places the child in whichever grade it judges more appropriate, with no preference for either.

Relationship applicants, meaning children of alumni, Capital Note nominees, siblings and ISF Pre-School children, must apply by August 31, 2026; all others by September 15.

The two stages

StageFormatDates
1Group assessment of about 45 minutes in groups of ten, half the activities in English and half in Putonghua, led by a group leader in the child's stated native languageBetween September 21 and October 2, 2026
2Parent-child meeting with a senior member of the school; the child is interviewed mainly in their native language, and parents can choose Cantonese, English or PutonghuaOctober to November 2026

All applicants are invited to Stage 1; selected applicants are invited back to Stage 2. Results are notified by December 11, 2026.

What the school is looking at

The criteria for all applicants include satisfactory socio-emotional and cognitive skills, with the school naming willingness to communicate, curiosity to learn and teamwork. In a group of ten four-year-olds working half in a language they may not know, those are the qualities that show: a child who joins in, listens, tries, and copes calmly with not understanding everything.

The parent-child meeting looks at the family too. Parents commit to the school's philosophy and values and to working with the school, and the meeting is where that commitment is discussed.
