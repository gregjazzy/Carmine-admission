Foundation Year is The ISF Academy's first class, equivalent to the final year of kindergarten in Hong Kong.

Who can apply

For 2027 entry, children born between September 1, 2021 and December 31, 2022, at least 4 years 8 months by August 31, 2027. No language requirement at this level: the school looks at curiosity, willingness to communicate, teamwork.

The dates

Application by September 15, 2026, or August 31 for alumni children, siblings, ISF Pre-School students and Capital Note nominees.

Supporting without moulding

At this age, there is no cramming. I help you approach the assessment calmly.
