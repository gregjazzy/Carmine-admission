How to get into CDNIS

Canadian International School of Hong Kong, CDNIS, is a not-for-profit IB World School teaching children from Nursery, at age two, to Grade 12, with a bilingual English and Mandarin programme in the early grades and an IB Diploma combined with the Ontario Secondary School Diploma at the end. Its two main entry points are Nursery and Grade 6; other grades depend on available places.

The application

Families apply online, which takes about an hour. They upload the birth certificate, passport, Hong Kong visa or identity card where relevant, a photograph, school reports for the previous and current years translated into English, and from Grade 10 an official transcript. The child's current teacher sends a Student Reference Form directly. The application fee is HK$2,350, with HK$1,650 more if the child is selected for assessment; for Nursery, a single HK$1,800 fee covers both. Children must live in Hong Kong with a parent or guardian who is a full-time resident.

Screening in two stages

Stage 1 combines the assessment or interview, school results and the reference form, weighted according to age. Each applicant is then ranked Excellent, Good, Marginal, admitted only in extenuating circumstances, or Not suitable.

Stage 2 applies only to applicants ranked Excellent or Good. Priority then goes to debenture holders or potential holders, siblings of current students, and Canadian citizens. The Director of Admissions and the principals of the Lower and Upper Schools decide.

The order matters: a debenture does not compensate for a weak assessment. It counts only once a child has passed the first stage.

After the decision

Accepted students confirm their place with an HK$80,000 reservation deposit, later credited to fees. Those who pass but find no place enter the wait pool for that year, with no guarantee; the pool does not carry over, and families must reapply for a later year.
