Lycée International: the admissions calendar

Schools near Paris November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

At the Lycée International, there is not one calendar but fourteen: each section runs its own tests, and every admission starts there. Families expecting a single date, as for a local lycée, often find out too late that their section's round has closed.

The general pattern

The most sought-after sections follow a similar rhythm: first contact in the autumn, application in December and January, tests from January to March or April, decisions in spring. Admission is then granted by the education authority, on the head's proposal. Mid-year entries are rare, and some sections exclude them.

Not missing the round

At the Lycée International, an application filed too late waits a year. I tell you when your section opens, and prepare the child for its test accordingly.

Two examples for September 2027

American Section. Online applications from 1 December 2026 to 8 January 2027 for sixième to terminale, and until 22 January 2027 for nursery to CM2. Secondary written exam on 27 January 2027 on site, 23 January online; appointments sent by 15 January. Primary tests between 22 February and 2 April 2027. Possible interviews announced by 15 March. No mid-year entries in 2026-2027.

British Section. Enquiry from October to December, information meetings in November and December, application from December to January, assessments from January to March, decisions in April.

For other sections, contact details are on the Lycée International website: the section is who you contact first.
