Choate: essays, parent statement and recommendations

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Choate reads each file as a whole, and with no required score, the written pieces weigh all the more. They are built over several months.

What the family writes

Part 2 of the application, on Gateway to Prep Schools, covers the student essays and the parent statement. The essays say who the child is beyond their grades; the statement presents them as their parents see them.

What the school receives from others

The principal or counselor sends a recommendation and transcripts for the last two years, with current grades. The current English and maths teachers each send a recommendation. A third teacher recommendation, current or previous, and a personal recommendation from an adult who knows the child well are optional. Current-year grades must arrive by February 1.

A written file that convinces

I work on the essays with your child without ever writing them, review the parent statement with you, and help you choose who will recommend them.

What makes the difference

Essays that ring true

An over-polished essay, visibly touched up by an adult, is spotted. A good essay starts from a moment or a question and says something about the child that nothing else in the file says.

Has your child ever written about themselves in English? Tell me about them.

Optional recommendations

They only help if they add something: a music teacher, a coach, a mentor who has seen the child outside class.

Teachers at a school abroad

A teacher who has never written for an American boarding school often writes too briefly. Ask early, and tell them what is expected.
