NLCS: bursaries

North London Collegiate School, the former Canons mansion, Edgware. Photo Nigel Cox, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

Book a conversation

Since 1850, NLCS has wanted a pupil's potential, not her background, to determine her opportunity. Its bursaries are available for entry at 11+ and 16+.

How they work

They can cover up to 100% of fees, and include lunches, half the coach fare, uniform, materials, exam fees and compulsory trips. As a guide, a family with income below £40,000 and limited assets is likely to be considered for 100%; above £150,000, assistance is unlikely. Assessment is done by an independent agency, and has no influence on admission. It must be indicated at registration. Currently, 78 pupils receive support.
