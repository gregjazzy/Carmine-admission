UK boarding schools

Millfield open days and visits

The entrance to Millfield, Street, Somerset. Photo Colin Smith, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

UK boarding schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Millfield is easier to understand on foot than on paper. A campus of more than 400 acres, nineteen boarding houses, an Olympic-size pool, an equestrian centre, a theatre and a concert hall do not fit into a brochure, and the school offers several ways to see them before applying.

Open mornings

Millfield holds open days three times a year, in February or March, in May and in October, with a smaller open morning in late November. Upcoming dates include a mini open morning on 28 November 2026 and open mornings on 27 February and 1 May 2027.

The morning begins with a talk by the Headmaster, followed by pupil displays and a tour of the campus, then refreshments and a chance to meet staff. Families can tailor the morning, look in on lessons and meet pupils. In the afternoon, they are welcome to stay and watch sport and activities at their own pace, which says a great deal about the school.

Best for: families early in their search, including those with children younger than Year 6, and anyone who wants to see the whole school in action.

Private campus tours

Individual tours run in term time and in the holidays, on weekdays and Saturdays. A pupil and a member of the admissions team usually lead the visit, which lasts around two hours and covers academic buildings, a boarding house and the sports and arts facilities. Tours are designed for families considering entry within the next couple of years, typically with a child in Year 6 or above.

Best for: a family that has already shortlisted Millfield, or that wants to follow up an open morning with specific questions.

From the visit to the interview

After the visit comes the interview. I prepare children to speak about their interests, activities and experiences, and about why Millfield, with the ease the school looks for.

A virtual meeting

Families who cannot come in person can ask the admissions team for a virtual appointment. Millfield also holds receptions abroad with the Headmaster and admissions staff.

Planning a visit? Tell me about your child first, so the visit answers the right questions.

What to look for

  • The pupils. How the pupil guide talks about the school, and whether your child can picture themselves in that conversation.
  • The houses. Rooms are usually shared by two from Year 9 to the Lower Sixth, single in the Upper Sixth; houses are allocated by the school to mix backgrounds.
  • The balance. Millfield is known for sport, with thirty sports and two hundred sports staff, but classes average around twelve pupils and the school selects on academic merit. Ask about both.
  • The weekend. For a boarder, ask what a Saturday evening and a Sunday look like.

After the visit

The next step is registration, which triggers the request for a reference and then the interview and reasoning test. I have worked for seven years with around fifteen families a year aiming at selective schools; for Millfield, I suggest visiting before Year 7 so that the interview, which can come about eighteen months before entry, is not rushed.

For Year 9 entry, places are confirmed early. Get in touch before your first visit.
