The ASIJ application file

An ASIJ application is a file of documents, read without a general entrance examination. That makes each piece count, and it makes completeness essential: the school does not decide until every required item, including any specialist report, is on the portal.

I help families assemble this file. Here is what it contains.

Reports

From Grade 2, families upload the report cards and progress reports for every term of the previous two school years, plus any reports from the current year. Copies of the reports parents received are usually preferred to official transcripts. For Grade 1, any reports or teacher comments from earlier years and the Kindergarten year are enough; children applying at three, four or five may have none, and can still apply. Homeschooled children's families contact the office to agree how to present the program.

Recommendations

Applying toWho writes
Early Learning Center and KindergartenOne teacher
Grades 1 to 5The child's two most recent teachers
Grade 6, from Grade 5The current teacher, the previous teacher and a counsellor or principal (two required)
Grades 7 to 12A math teacher, an English teacher and a counsellor or principal

Families enter each recommender's email in the portal, and the system sends a confidential form. Recommendations in other languages are accepted, though English is much preferred; in some cases the school asks for a certified translation at the family's expense. Personal letters of recommendation are not considered.

The essay and the test

From Grade 6, the student writes an essay, the one place where the file hears the child directly. From Grade 6, a standardized test result from the last eighteen months is required, and for Grades 1 to 5 it is submitted if the child has one. The school accepts a wide range, from MAP, CogAT and the ISA to the SSAT, IGCSEs or the SAT, but not language proficiency tests.

Specialist reports

The application asks about any support a child has received: speech and language therapy, occupational therapy, learning support, gifted programs, English as an additional language, counselling. All related reports, including educational-psychological assessments and individual education plans, must be submitted before a decision, with an exit report if support has ended. ASIJ can revoke an offer if relevant reports were withheld.
