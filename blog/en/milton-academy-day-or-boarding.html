Day or boarding at Milton

Many families near Boston hesitate between day and boarding at Milton Academy. The decision has to be made at the application stage, and it is meant to last: once admitted, a student cannot change between boarding and day status except in special circumstances.

Who can choose

  • Day: only students living in the Greater Boston area, and only for entry into Grades 9 or 10.
  • Boarding: students from anywhere, for entry into Grades 9, 10 or 11.

A family outside Greater Boston therefore has no choice to make: Milton means boarding. A family applying for Grade 11 also has only one option.

What boarding adds

Boarders stay in the same house for all four years, with older housemates guiding newcomers and faculty living alongside them as mentors. Houses gather for family-style dinners twice a week and compete in inter-house events. Milton's Host Family Program lets boarders spend a weekend night or a meal with a vetted local family. For a teenager ready for independence, it is a real education in living with others.

What day life keeps

Day students are encouraged to take part fully in the life of the school, from afternoon sports and rehearsals to evening lectures and weekend events, and they bring their knowledge of the city to friends from elsewhere. They also keep family life every evening, which matters to some fourteen year olds more than they admit.

Questions that decide

  • How long is the daily journey, and what would it cost the student in sleep and activities?
  • Does the student want the independence of boarding, or are the parents the ones who want it?
  • Will the student make the most of evenings and weekends on campus, or come home every day at five?
  • Is the $10,000 difference meaningful for the family, given financial aid?
