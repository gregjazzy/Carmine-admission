NMH and test-optional admission

Northfield Mount Hermon does not require an admission test this year. A family may send an SSAT, ISEE, ACT, PSAT or SAT score, or none at all, and the school states its rule plainly: a score that strengthens the application is considered, a score that does not is not taken into account. For an English-language test, the rule is different, and the two are often confused.

I help families make this decision every year, score report in hand. It is less obvious than "send it if it is good".

What "optional" means at NMH

NMH's rule leaves no room for doubt: only a helpful score counts. In practice, that makes sending a score low-risk. It does not make it free, though. Testing takes preparation time in the autumn, and NMH grants no fee waivers for these tests precisely because they are optional.

The useful question is not whether the score is good in absolute terms, but whether it adds information the rest of the file does not already give. A strong student at a well-known school with glowing recommendations gains little from a score that confirms what everyone already says. A strong student at a school NMH sees rarely, or with grades that are hard to read, gains a common yardstick.

A score worth sending

When a score adds to the file, it should be a strong one. I prepare candidates for the SSAT, the ISEE and the English tests, and help the family decide, with the result in front of them, whether it belongs in the application.

When a score helps

SituationSend the score?
High score that matches strong grades and recommendationsUsually yes, though it adds little
High score, school or grading system unfamiliar to NMHYes: it gives a common reference
High score, uneven grades for a reason now pastYes: it shows the current level
Score clearly below what grades and teachers suggestNo: the file is stronger without it

Applicants to the upper grades can choose among more tests: the ACT, PSAT and SAT sit alongside the SSAT and ISEE. For a student already preparing the SAT for college, a good result there may be the simplest score to send.

The English test is not optional

For applicants whose first language is not English, a TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo score is required, sent directly by the testing organization. NMH may waive it for a student who has spent the last two academic years in a school taught in English. The recommended minimums are high: 100 on the TOEFL iBT under the old scale, or 5.5 on the new one, 7 overall on the IELTS Academic, 125 on Duolingo.

A strong score on the SSAT does not replace this test, and a family should not let the optional status of one blur the requirement of the other. For many international applicants the English test is the real testing work of the year.

When to test

A first sitting in early autumn leaves time to decide calmly and, if needed, to sit again before the January 15 deadline. A family that tests for the first time in December has to decide in a hurry, often with a score that reflects nerves more than ability, and that is the situation in which "optional" becomes a reason not to send anything at all.
