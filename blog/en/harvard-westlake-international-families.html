International families and Harvard-Westlake

Harvard-Westlake receives enquiries from families all over the world, but its answer to international applicants is precise. It is a day school, and it considers international students only under three conditions.

The three conditions

  1. The family is relocating to Los Angeles. The student must live with parents or guardians full-time while enrolled. Harvard-Westlake offers no boarding and no homestay arrangements.
  2. The student intends to stay through 12th grade and complete secondary education in the school's academic programme.
  3. The student is completely fluent in English, in reading, speaking and writing. There is no English as a Second Language programme.

A family that does not meet all three should look elsewhere, however strong the student.

Timing a move

The main entries are 7th grade, with about 215 places, and 9th grade, with about 90; 10th grade offers only five to ten. Eighth grade opens only when a place becomes free, and 11th and 12th grades are considered only in special circumstances, of which a family relocating to Los Angeles is one. A relocation planned for a 7th or 9th grade start gives the best odds; a mid-high-school move depends on space.

Applying from abroad

  • Interview: can be held on Zoom, on a weekday during Los Angeles school hours, so families in other time zones should expect an early morning or late evening slot.
  • ISEE: required of every applicant, one sitting between 1 August and 15 January; the at-home online version makes it possible from abroad.
  • Recommendations and transcript: from the current school, including the English teacher. In a non-English-speaking school, this letter carries real weight in showing the student's level.
  • Deadlines: form by 13 November to guarantee an interview; everything by 15 January; decisions on 12 March.

The English bar

Without ESL support, a student must be able to follow every class, write analytical work and take part in discussion from the first day. For a child educated in another language, the questionnaire and the interview, both in English, are where that shows. Spoken fluency usually comes before writing, and writing is what the classroom will demand.

Have a second plan

Harvard-Westlake receives more applications from qualified students than it has places, and advises every family to apply to other schools. For a relocating family, that advice is essential, because a school place is part of the move.
