SSAT or ISEE for Newark Academy

Livingston, New Jersey, home of Newark Academy. Photo Scott Brody, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Unlike many schools that went test-optional, Newark Academy still requires a standardised test from every applicant. It is equally clear that the score is one piece of a larger picture, and it publishes a useful reference point: admitted students average around the 70th percentile.

The rules
Required forAll applicants to grades 6 to 11
Tests acceptedSSAT or ISEE; only one is needed
PreferenceNone between the two
RegistrationIn advance, at ssat.org or iseetest.org; Newark Academy cannot offer fee waivers
DeadlineWith the supplemental materials, 11 January 2027
Admitted studentsAverage around the 70th percentile

International applicants take the same tests; Newark Academy does not ask for the TOEFL, since fluent English is expected.

What the score is used for

Newark Academy describes testing as important but only one piece of the puzzle. With the transcript and the teacher recommendations, it helps the committee judge academic readiness and fit. The school also looks closely at who students are beyond the classroom and how they will contribute. A score near the 70th percentile is therefore a reference, not a cutoff. Supplementary materials, or an extra letter from a coach, teacher or mentor sent to the admission office, can add context to a file; they do not replace the test.

SSAT or ISEE?

Since Newark Academy has no preference, the choice should follow the student. The two tests differ in format, section order and pacing. A practice run of each, a few months before registration, usually shows which one suits a given child. Families applying to several schools should also check what the others accept.

Timing

A test in the autumn leaves room for a second sitting before the 11 January deadline if the first result is disappointing. Both tests require registration in advance, and test dates fill.
