A decline from the Davidson Academy is not final. The school says so: it is not unusual, though never guaranteed, to get in on the second try.

What changes

A student already assessed fills in a condensed form: the application branches to it when a previous assessment is indicated. SAT or ACT scores must be from the past year: often the test must be retaken, at the new grade's threshold.

Using the year

I work on the weak points of the assessment, maths, science or writing, and prepare the new test.
