How to get into Choate Rosemary Hall

The Andrew Mellon Library, Choate Rosemary Hall, Wallingford. Photo Daderot, Wikimedia Commons, public domain

Choate Rosemary Hall, in Wallingford, Connecticut, is one of the most sought-after boarding schools in the United States. It has students from more than 60 countries, and about 18% of its students are not US citizens. Its process resembles that of other leading New England boarding schools, with two particularities: an online-only interview, and optional testing.

The file, in three parts

Part 1: the candidate profile on Gateway to Prep Schools. Part 2: the student's essays and the parent statement. Part 3: the principal or counselor recommendation with transcripts for the last two years, and recommendations from current English and maths teachers. A third teacher recommendation and a personal recommendation are optional.

An application worthy of Choate

Interview, essays, choice of recommenders, testing or not: I prepare every part of your child's file to the level Choate expects.

Interview and testing

The interview is "strongly encouraged", held online only, between September and February, and can take place only once. Testing, SSAT or ISEE for grade 9, is optional: Choate publishes no average score or cutoff, and reads each file as a whole.

The dates

Files complete by January 15 receive full consideration; after that, a March 10 answer is not guaranteed. Current-year grades must arrive by February 1. Decisions, admission and financial aid, come on March 10.

What I do for your family

For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year towards the most selective schools, and I have students at Choate. For Choate, I start with an assessment of written English and maths, which also tells us whether a score is worth sending. I then prepare your child for the interview, work on the essays with them, and help you choose their recommenders.
