How to get into The ISF Academy

The ISF Academy in Pok Fu Lam is a not-for-profit independent school built around bilingual immersion in Putonghua and English, from Foundation Year to the IB Diploma. Admission runs on a precise annual calendar, with staged assessments in which every applicant is invited to the first stage and only some go on. From Grade 1, a child must show grade-level proficiency in both Chinese, spoken in Putonghua, and English.

I prepare children and families for ISF every year. Here is how admission works for 2027-28.

Who can apply

At least one parent or legal guardian must live in Hong Kong. The child must be a Hong Kong permanent resident, or have a parent employed and resident in Hong Kong with a valid work permit and hold the corresponding dependent visa. Parents also agree to the school's philosophy and values, its community code and its rules. Meeting the criteria does not guarantee a place; the school decides at its sole discretion.

Assessments by grade

EntryStages
Foundation YearGroup activities, half in English and half in Putonghua; then a parent-child meeting
Grade 1Group activities in both languages; then a parent-child meeting
Grades 2 to 5Written papers in Chinese, Chinese mathematics, English, English mathematics, non-verbal reasoning and a computerized test; oral interviews in both languages; a parent meeting
Grades 6 to 11Written papers in Chinese, English, English mathematics, non-verbal reasoning and a computerized test; orals in both languages; a parent meeting

Applicants must be in Hong Kong for the first stage on the given dates; the school offers at most one alternative date. Families living more than six hours' direct flight away can ask, when they submit the application, for the stages to be grouped over two or three days.

Priority

No group is guaranteed admission, but Capital Note nominees receive an early assessment for Foundation Year, an extra assessment opportunity and admissions priority. Children of alumni, siblings of current students and children from The ISF Pre-School also get an additional assessment opportunity and favourable consideration if they pass. Other applicants are not reassessed in the same year.

Calendar and cost

Applications for 2027-28 opened on August 1, 2026. Deadlines run from August 31 for Foundation Year relationship applicants to January 25, 2027 for Grades 2 to 5. Tuition for 2026-27 is HK$251,130 in primary, HK$291,770 in Grades 6 to 10 and HK$317,190 in Grades 11 and 12, plus an Annual Capital Levy of HK$40,000 unless the child is a Capital Note nominee. Financial aid covers 20 to 100 percent of fees, and admissions assessment is need-blind.
