How to get into The ISF Academy

The ISF Academy, Hong Kong. Photo Ceeseven, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

International schools in Asia November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

The ISF Academy is a not-for-profit independent school in Hong Kong, Chinese-English bilingual, from Foundation Year to Grade 12, preparing for the IB programmes. Over 2,000 students, one teacher for seven students, and a 38.8 IB Diploma average for the Class of 2026.

The steps

1. Joining Friends of ISF, required to apply and to sign up for briefings and tours. 2. The online application, HKD 1,000 non-refundable, by the grade's deadline. 3. Admissions assessments and interviews. 4. The offer, and a deposit to secure the place.

The conditions

At least one parent resides in Hong Kong; the child is a permanent resident or holds a dependent visa. Parents agree with the school's Eight Virtues. From Grade 1, the child has grade-level Putonghua and English.

What the school says, and I respect

ISF deals with parents only and works with no agents. I do not apply on your behalf: you remain the school's sole contact. My role is to prepare your child.

A child who is ready

I assess your child, then work on English, maths and reasoning for the assessments.
