For many families, Nursery is the most natural moment to join Aditya Birla World Academy: it is one of only three main entry points, and children who start here can continue through the Cambridge programmes to the IB Diploma or A Levels.

Who is eligible

Eligibility is set by date of birth. For 2027-28, children born between 1 April 2023 and 31 March 2024 could apply for Nursery. Each following year, the window moves forward by twelve months. Admission remains subject to seat availability and to the school's final decision.

What Nursery looks like

ApproachIn practice
MontessoriStructured, sensory-based learning with tactile materials, daily tasks such as pouring or tidying up
Reggio EmiliaCreative, project-driven exploration, artistic expression and collaborative projects
RoutineCircle time, familiar routines and cosy corners for security
Early learningLiteracy and numeracy introduced through games, stories and role play

Teachers act as facilitators and observers, respecting each child's pace, and music, movement, storytelling and art help children express their emotions. Kindness, taking turns and making friends are practised every day.

A calm start

At Nursery level there is nothing to drill. I help parents understand the process and prepare for the family meeting, so that the child's first school step is a calm one.

The process

The six stages are the same as for other grades: an online enquiry, the application and documents when invited, an assessment and interaction suited to the child's age, a meeting with shortlisted families, the decision and fee payment. At this age, the school's description of the meeting matters most: it is designed to understand the child's environment, values and aspirations.

Is your child born in the eligible window? Let's talk about the next cycle.

What parents can do

A young child cannot be prepared for an interaction the way an older student prepares for a test. What helps is ordinary: a child used to playing with others, listening to stories and following simple routines. For parents, the meeting is a chance to explain how they see their child's education, and why ABWA's values fit their family.

Timing

The admission process usually begins at the end of May or early June for the following academic year. Families should keep an eye on the enquiry form in the spring.

How I help

I have students at ABWA, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools. For Nursery, my role is advice to parents rather than preparation of the child.
