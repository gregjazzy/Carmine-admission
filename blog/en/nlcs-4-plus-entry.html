Book a conversation

At 4, NLCS sets no exam: it observes children playing and learning.

How it works

All registered girls attend a first round of about an hour, in groups of 12 to 16, with pre-school activities: colouring, cutting and sticking. A smaller group returns for a second round: a group activity, then puzzles and games with a member of staff. Offers come in late January.

What the school looks for

Curiosity, the confidence to try something new, and the ability to listen and follow simple instructions, in a group or alone with an adult.

Supporting without moulding

At 4, you do not prepare a child as for an exam. I help you understand what the school observes, and approach the day calmly.
