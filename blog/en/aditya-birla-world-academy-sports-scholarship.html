Aditya Birla World Academy's scholarship programme is aimed at one kind of excellence: sport. Its Sports Scholarship recognises exceptional student-athletes who can balance a demanding training schedule with a rigorous academic programme, and it is open at the two main secondary entry points, Grade 8 and Grade 11.

The scholarship at a glance
Entry gradesGrade 8 and Grade 11
ValuePart or full tuition fees
DurationThree years from Grade 8 (Grades 8 to 10); two years from Grade 11 (Grades 11 and 12)
Level requiredExcellence in an individual or team sport at state, national or international level
2027-28Applications closed

What the committee looks for

Beyond sporting results, ABWA looks for leadership qualities, a healthy competitive spirit, consistent effort and progress in the chosen sport, and the ability to balance academics with athletic commitments. A student who wins but struggles to keep up in class is not the profile the scholarship describes.

Training and studying

For student-athletes, I keep mathematics and the sciences on track around training and competitions, so the academic side never weakens the application.

How to apply

  1. With the admission application, complete and upload the ABWA Scholarship Application Form.
  2. Add a statement of purpose setting out goals and aspirations in the chosen sport.
  3. Upload supporting sports documents showing the level reached.
  4. If shortlisted, meet the Chairperson and the Scholarship Committee to share mindset, aspirations and vision.

The decision of the Chairperson and the Scholarship Committee is final.

Is your child competing at state level or above? Let's talk about the application.

The statement of purpose

The statement of purpose is where an athlete explains more than results: what drives them, what they want to achieve, how they plan to combine sport and school. It should be the student's own words, specific about goals and honest about the effort involved.

The admission comes first

The scholarship is applied for alongside admission, so the usual process still applies: application, assessment, family meeting. A student must be admitted to receive it, and the school describes its selection as structured and rigorous, so that the most deserving candidates receive the award.
