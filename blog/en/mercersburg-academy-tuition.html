In 2026-2027, Mercersburg Academy tuition is $81,600 boarding and $53,340 day. It covers academics, activities, meals and lodging for boarders, and health, technology and laundry fees.

What comes on top

Textbooks, private music lessons, athletic attire, certain health services, and fees for senior year and study abroad. The school says the true cost per student is closer to $95,000, the rest coming from giving.

Aid

Nearly $12 million in aid last year: half of families receive merit- or need-based aid.
