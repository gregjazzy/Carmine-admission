Institut auf dem Rosenberg: where its students go after school

Institut auf dem Rosenberg, St. Gallen. Photo Rene Nueesch, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

Boarding schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

From 2024 to 2026, Rosenberg students received offers from some of the world's best universities, across three continents. What makes it interesting is its range: a school of this size sending students to the Ivy League as well as to ETH Zurich or the LSE says something about the flexibility of its pathways.

United States, United Kingdom, Europe

In the United States, students received offers from Columbia and Cornell, the University of Chicago, Williams, Babson, Berkeley, UCLA and Carnegie Mellon. In the United Kingdom, from the LSE, Imperial College, St Andrews, Durham, UCL, King's College and Edinburgh; in 2026, 82% of its UK applicants received an offer from a UK top-10 university, and all from a Russell Group university. In Europe, from ETH Zurich, the Technical University of Munich, Politecnico di Milano, Bocconi and the University of St. Gallen.

What the list says, and what it does not

These are offers, not enrolments, and the list does not say how many students stand behind each name. Above all, the list shows what a tailored path allows: a student who chose A-levels naturally aims for London, another who chose APs aims for the United States, a third for ETH. What then decides, in each country, is the coherence between the chosen path, the profile and the requirements of the target university.
