Applying from out of town or abroad

Sidwell Friends receives applications from families outside the Washington area and abroad every year, often families relocating for work. It welcomes them, but its rules are clear: it is a day school with no boarding or homestay, it offers no English as a Second Language program, and it does not hold places for families who arrive after the admissions season.

I work with families relocating to Washington. Here is what applies to them.

The rules

QuestionAnswer
Where must the student live?In the DC area, with a parent or legal guardian

Applying from a distance

The school advises out-of-area families to begin as early in the season as possible. Families outside the DC, Maryland and Virginia region who cannot travel are told, once they have applied, how the in-person steps will be handled by other means. The deadlines are the same: the Family Information Form by December 1, recommendations, report cards and the parent meeting by December 15, and the remaining pieces by December 31. For a family abroad, report cards from another system and teachers unfamiliar with American recommendations take extra time.

Moving to Washington and considering Sidwell Friends? Tell me about your child and your timing.

Guardianship

When a student's legal guardian is not a parent, the school sets conditions: notarized guardianship papers, a meeting between the guardian and the head of school or a designee, a signed acceptance of responsibility, and written confirmation from the parents that the guardian will be present at the Washington residence whenever the student is using it. These rules close the door to arrangements where a child would live essentially alone.
