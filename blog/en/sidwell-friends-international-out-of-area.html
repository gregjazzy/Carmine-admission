Sidwell Friends welcomes families from afar, with clear rules.

What changes

In-person steps are handled by other means for families outside the DC region. The school advises applying as early as possible: it holds no places for families arriving after the admission season.

English and the visa

No ESL programme: students must be fully fluent in English reading, speaking and writing, and a non-native speaker submits TOEFL scores. The school issues the I-20, for the F-1 visa, once the student is admitted and enrolled. No boarding: the student lives in the area with a parent or legal guardian.
