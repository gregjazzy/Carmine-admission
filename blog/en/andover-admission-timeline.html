The Andover admission timeline

Andover has one deadline for fall 2027: February 1, 2027, for both the application and financial aid. Aid decisions are communicated on March 10. Between now and then sit an interview, a required test, a graded paper to choose, an essay, a video and four recommendations. The only sensible way to read this calendar is backwards, from February 1.

February 1: what must be in

On that date Andover expects the test score, sent directly by the testing agency, the graded writing sample, the essay, the short video in the Andover portal, the parent statement, the grade reports and the four recommendations, plus the Clarity aid application if the family is applying. As the date approaches, the portal sends reminders about missing items. A reminder in late January is not the moment to discover that a teacher abroad has not started writing.

January: the last interviews

Interviews run through the month of January. New slots are added on a rolling basis, and appointments can be cancelled or moved in the portal, which makes it tempting to leave the interview late. That is a mistake for two reasons: the end of the season is crowded, and a child interviewed in late January is preparing the interview, the video and the last essay drafts in the same weeks.

The calendar as a plan of work

I turn Andover's calendar into a plan for the child: what is prepared when, in what order, so that every piece reaches its best level by February 1.

December and earlier: the test

A score is required. A family that wants room for a second sitting needs the first one in the autumn, and must register in time: late registration charges for the SSAT and ISEE are substantial, and they are not covered by the fee waiver available to families applying for aid. Working back again, test preparation belongs to the spring and summer before.

For a child at a school abroad that does not teach in English, the TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo is a second test with its own calendar. The level most admitted international students reach, 100, 7.5 or 135, is rarely reached in a few weeks.

The autumn: visits, events and the Candidate Profile

Everything on Andover's side opens with the Candidate Profile, part one of the Gateway application. Twenty-four hours after it is submitted, the family receives its Andover portal credentials, which give access to the interview scheduler and to on-campus events. The application fee can be paid later.

This year's virtual tours run on November 21, December 9 and 12, and January 9 and 20. Discover Andover mornings on campus, from 9 to 11, give a feel for the classroom; families are asked to register for one only. Andover also travels: regional "Conversation with Andover" evenings, and receptions with the Ten Schools Admission Organization, which this autumn included Zurich. For some students from under-represented backgrounds living in the United States, LEAP offers a two-day visit paid by the school, with nominations closing in early November.

The autumn: asking the recommenders

Four recommendations are required: the principal or counselor, the current English teacher, the current math teacher and a personal recommender. The English and math teachers must be current ones, so they may have known the child for only a few weeks when asked. The request should go out early in the autumn, with time for them to observe the child and write properly.

Spring and summer: the assessment

Before any of this, someone has to say honestly where the child stands: in English, in mathematics at the level of the Upper Level SSAT, in writing. That first assessment decides which test to sit, how long to prepare, and which school paper is likely to become the graded writing sample.

How I build the year with a family

I have students at Andover, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools in the United States, England and Switzerland. With each family I start from the assessment and work forward: test preparation in spring and summer, recommenders asked early, essay and video drafted in the autumn, interview practised before it is booked. When the family applies to several schools, one calendar holds them all.
