SHSAT accommodations for English learners

The SHSAT is the only factor in admission to Stuyvesant, so the conditions under which a student takes it matter enormously. New York City provides accommodations for two groups: English language learners and students with disabilities. Each comes with clear rules and some firm deadlines.

Who qualifies

  • Students with disabilities who have an IEP or a 504 Plan that includes testing accommodations.
  • Current English language learners.
  • Former English language learners who achieved proficiency on the New York State English as a Second Language Achievement Test, the NYSESLAT, in the past two years.

Accommodations requested only for the SHSAT are not allowed: the student must have a documented history of needing and using them.

For English language learners

AccommodationDetail
Extended time360 minutes instead of 180, counted from the start of the test, with two 15-minute breaks after the first 180 minutes
Translated footnotesOn screen, for ELA passages, in languages including Chinese, French, Spanish, Arabic, Bengali, Korean, Russian and others
Bilingual glossariesCommercial glossaries of about 20,000 words, provided on paper by the testing site; students may not bring their own

The questions and answers themselves are never translated, orally or in writing.

For students with IEPs or 504 Plans

Students receive the accommodations in their plan unless one is unnecessary for the SHSAT, such as speech-to-text, since there are no essays, or not permitted because it would interfere with what the test measures. The test is taken on computer; paper versions exist only for the very small number of students whose plans require them. Students bring their own aids, such as masks, markers or magnifiers; the sites do not supply them. 504 Plans must be reviewed and approved each year, no later than the last day of school in June of the previous year.

Does your child have an IEP, a 504 Plan or ELL status? Let's check what applies.

The deadlines

  • During registration, the student's school enters or confirms accommodations in MySchools.
  • Students at charter or private schools with similar plans must send documentation at least three weeks before the registration deadline.
  • Emergency accommodations are possible for conditions arising within thirty days of the test, with the principal's approval.
  • Approved accommodations appear on the test ticket; on the day, they cannot be changed without written parental consent.

Everyone stays 180 minutes

All students must stay in the testing room for the full 180 minutes of standard time. Students with extended time may leave after that only if they have finished.
