When to apply to Beau Soleil

Beau Soleil, Route du Village, Villars-sur-Ollon. Photo En08sijp, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Beau Soleil has no single deadline for most grades, but the calendar still matters. The school receives a large number of applications for a limited number of places, and availability shrinks as the year goes on. The right time to apply depends mainly on the grade a child will enter.

The key timings

SituationWhen to apply
Any grade, following SeptemberFrom September; ideally between September and December
Entry into Grade 10 or 11Before the end of November
Entry into Grade 12Not possible; Grade 11 or earlier
Full year groupsWaiting lists may apply for some ages and nationalities

For a visit, the school recommends a weekday in term time, when daily life can be seen; families who cannot travel can book a Virtual Discovery Meeting or meet the admissions team in their region.

Why the senior grades come earlier

Grades 11 and 12 form a two-year programme, the IB Diploma or the US High School Diploma, with subject choices made at the start. A student joining in Grade 11 is expected to stay for both years, and the school does not admit new students into the final year. That is why it asks families aiming at Grade 10 or 11 to apply before the end of November.

Minimum stays

A student in Grades 6 to 10 commits to at least one full academic year; a student entering Grade 11 is expected to stay for two years to complete the senior programme. Families planning a shorter stay, for a term abroad or a gap between schools, should look at Beau Soleil's summer and winter programmes instead.

After an offer

Once a student is accepted, fees for Terms 1 and 2 are due by 15 April for the coming year, together with the one-time charges for new students; later enrolments pay on receipt of the bill. Where a visa is needed, the school provides the documents after acceptance, and the Swiss embassy or consulate sets the rest of the timetable, so it is wise to start early.
