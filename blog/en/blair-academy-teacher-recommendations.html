Teacher recommendations for Blair

At Blair Academy, two teachers speak for every applicant: the current English teacher and the current mathematics teacher. Their forms are a required part of the complete application, and the school gives clear guidance on when to ask for them.

The rules
RequiredCurrent English teacher; current mathematics teacher
HowOnline through the SAO or Gateway, or printed forms emailed to the application office or mailed to Blairstown
When to askBlair recommends teachers complete the online forms after 1 November
DeadlineReceived by 15 January
OptionalA personal recommendation from one or two people with insights not covered elsewhere

Why after 1 November?

By November, a current teacher has had two months with the student: enough to write about how they work, think and engage in class this year, not only last year. Asking earlier risks a thinner letter; asking much later collides with the winter break and the 15 January deadline.

Each letter in its place

I help families plan the recommendations so that the English and math teachers, and any personal referee, each bring something the rest of the file does not.

What teachers can show

Blair looks for strong academic ability, a passion for learning, a strong work ethic and intellectual curiosity, and for kind, collaborative students who take part fully in school life. Teachers who see the student every day are the best witnesses to those qualities, far better than a list of awards.

Not sure who should write the personal letter? Let's talk it through.

The personal recommendation

A personal recommendation is not required, but can help complete the picture. Blair asks families to limit it to one or two people who can offer insights not covered elsewhere: a coach, a music teacher, a mentor in a community project. A letter that repeats what the teachers say adds little.

Other optional pieces

Beyond recommendations, Blair's application includes two optional forms: a special-interests and talents form, where a student can describe a serious commitment in the arts, athletics or elsewhere, and a parent statement. Neither is required, but each gives the committee another angle on the student. Together with the teachers' forms, they should tell a consistent story rather than repeat one another.

Practical points

  • Check that teachers' email addresses are correct when sending the online requests.
  • Give each teacher a short note on the student's interests and reasons for Blair.
  • Confirm in early January that both forms have arrived.
