The SHSAT math section

For a school whose identity is science, it is fitting that half of the SHSAT score comes from mathematics. The section is not advanced in content: it covers middle-school mathematics. Its difficulty lies in the way questions combine ideas, and in the pace.

Six domains

DomainTypical work
Ratios and proportional relationshipsRates, percentages, scale, unit conversion
Numbers and expressionsFractions, exponents, order of operations, number properties
EquationsLinear equations and systems, set up from words
InequalitiesSolving and interpreting ranges of values
Statistics and probabilityMean, median, data displays, simple probability
GeometryAngles, area, volume, the coordinate plane

Questions appear as multiple choice and in other formats, on screen. The raw math result is converted into a standardised score, then added to the English one; a strong math score therefore lifts the composite as much as a strong English score, no more and no less.

No skipping

From fall 2026, the math section is computer-adaptive, and every question must be answered before the next one appears. A student cannot leave a hard question for later. That changes strategy: the right reflex is to estimate, eliminate, commit and move on, rather than freeze. Time spent stuck on one item is time taken from the rest.

Where the points are

Strong students rarely lose points on unfamiliar content; they lose them on translation and care. Turning a paragraph into an equation, reading which quantity is asked for, keeping track of units: these are where errors gather. A student who has studied algebra seriously in 7th and 8th grade sets up these problems faster and makes fewer slips.

How does your child handle multi-step word problems? Let's test it.

A year of preparation

  • Spring of 7th grade: a diagnostic on the official practice test, and a list of weak domains.
  • Summer: content work, domain by domain, with emphasis on algebra and proportional reasoning.
  • Autumn of 8th grade: mixed sets under time on computer, and full practice tests in the new format.
