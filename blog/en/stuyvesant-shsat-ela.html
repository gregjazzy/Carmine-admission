The SHSAT ELA section

Students aiming at Stuyvesant often see themselves as math students, and treat the English section as something to get through. That is a costly view. The composite score adds the two sections, and for many strong math students the ELA section is where the margin over the cutoff is won or lost.

What the section contains

The ELA section has 50 questions of two kinds:

  • Revising and Editing: questions on a written text that ask the student to correct errors, improve sentences and organise ideas, testing grammar, usage, punctuation and the logic of a paragraph.
  • Reading Comprehension: sets of questions on reading passages, testing understanding of ideas, structure, inference and evidence.

There is no essay or extended written response. Questions are in multiple-choice and other formats.

How navigation works

From fall 2026, the test is computer-adaptive. For passage-based sets in the ELA section, students can move back within the set and change their answers; once they complete and submit the set, they cannot return to it. Stand-alone questions must be answered before moving on. A student who habitually rereads the whole passage at the end now needs to finish each set properly before submitting it.

Reading and editing, on screen

I prepare SHSAT students for the ELA section: precise grammar for revising and editing, efficient reading of passages, and the discipline of closing each set well.

Support for English learners

Current English language learners, and former ones who reached proficiency in the past two years, receive double time, 360 minutes, with two breaks. Footnotes in ELA passages can be translated on screen into a number of languages, and bilingual glossaries are provided on paper. The questions themselves are not translated.

A preparation rhythm

Reading improves slowly and steadily, so it should start early: daily reading from the spring of 7th grade, then focused work on grammar rules over the summer, then timed sets on screen in the autumn. Revising and Editing usually improves fastest, which makes it the best place to gain points quickly.
