Many families moving to Singapore have one of its selective secondary schools in mind. The usual way in, beyond the national exam at the end of primary school, is Direct School Admission to secondary schools, DSA-Sec, which lets Primary 6 pupils apply on the strength of a talent. For international children, the AEIS route they take, and when, decides whether this door is open.

Why timing matters

DSA-Sec is an exercise for Primary 6 pupils, with school choices made in May and June; in 2026, from 6 May to 2 June. A child who enters a Singapore primary school through the AEIS Primary, for example in Primary 4 or 5, reaches Primary 6 inside the system. A child who arrives at secondary age enters through AEIS Secondary, and accepting that offer closes later DSA-Sec exercises. For a family with a younger child and flexibility on dates, arriving at primary age keeps more options open.

What DSA asks of a child

Each school sets its own talent areas, selection process and schedule, published on its website by late April. A child admitted through DSA-Sec commits to the school for the length of the programme: no school choices at Secondary 1 posting, and no transfer after the Primary 6 results. The ministry asks families to weigh this together with the child, since the route does not suit everyone.
