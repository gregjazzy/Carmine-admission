Gordonstoun says it in its own process: not every school is right for every child. That is rare, and useful, because the school is unusual.

What makes it unusual

A 200-acre woodland campus in the north of Scotland, near the sea and hills. A character education that makes the location part of the project. A day that mixes lessons, sport, projects, services and outdoor activities. The school puts forward one idea: all young people need opportunities to prove themselves to themselves.

The right choice, then the right preparation

I help you judge whether Gordonstoun suits your child, then prepare them for the interview.

Which child

A child who loves the outdoors, accepts physical effort, enjoys group life and needs concrete challenges to grow will find at Gordonstoun what few schools offer. A child who needs the city, or a purely academic setting, may be better elsewhere.
