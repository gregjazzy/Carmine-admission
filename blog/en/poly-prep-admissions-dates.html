Poly Prep follows the shared calendar of ISAAGNY, the association of New York independent schools, so its key dates line up with those of many other schools a family may be applying to. Within that calendar, the dates differ by grade: two deadlines for the preliminary application, one for supporting materials, and four different decision days.

I set this calendar with every family applying to New York schools. Here it is for 2027-28.

The dates inside the dates

Several smaller rules sit inside these deadlines. Teacher recommendations must be requested after November 1, and for Grades 1 to 8 the school will not accept forms submitted earlier. For Grade 1, the current school sends the transcript after December 1. For Nursery applicants with a summer birthday, the school suggests booking the playgroup after November 1. For tests, the school recommends sitting the SSAT or ISEE by December 31 so that scores arrive on time.

Reading the decision dates

Families receive financial aid decisions together with admission decisions. Because reply deadlines differ, a family with children applying to two different divisions, or applying to several ISAAGNY schools at once, can face two windows a few weeks apart. Knowing them in advance avoids having to answer one school before hearing from another.
