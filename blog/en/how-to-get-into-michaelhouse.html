How to get into Michaelhouse

Michaelhouse is a boys' boarding school in South Africa, on a 680-hectare campus. Most boys join in Grade 8, called E Block, for five years. The school describes itself as "a club for life".

What decides entry

Admission to Grade 8 rests on four elements: previous academic performance, a personal interview with the Rector or a Deputy Rector, the most recent school report, and a confidential report from the current Headmaster.

The steps

Registration, possible from birth, by the end of Grade 6 at the latest, with a ZAR 1,200 fee. The visit and interview: during Grade 6, the registered boy and his parents visit the school and meet the Rector. Offers: in April of the year before entry. For 2027 entry, applications received after 31 January 2026 went on the waiting list.
