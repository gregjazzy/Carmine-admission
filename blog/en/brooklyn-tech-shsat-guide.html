Getting into Brooklyn Tech means passing one test: the SHSAT, which changed format in fall 2026. I know the school through my students at Brooklyn Tech. For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year with applications to leading schools in New York.

What the city says

The test is on computer, in 180 minutes: 50 English and 50 math questions, in the order the student chooses. It is adaptive, each answer steering the next. There is no going back, except within a group of questions on one passage. The two scores are converted then added. Brooklyn Tech is one of the test sites, for students attending school in Districts 13 to 16, 19, 23 and 32.
