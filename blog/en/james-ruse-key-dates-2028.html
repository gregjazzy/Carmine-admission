Entry to Year 7 at James Ruse in 2028 runs on a calendar set by the NSW Department of Education for all selective high schools. It begins with applications in November of Year 5 and ends, for families on a reserve list, at the end of Term 1 of Year 7. Most of it is fixed; a few dates are not yet published.

I build this calendar with each family I accompany. Here it is.

Three dates that deserve attention

February 15. Applications close, and so does the window for requesting test adjustments for a child with disability or medical needs. A family that needs adjustments should prepare the supporting documents well before that day.

May 13 and 14. All students from the same primary school sit on the same day, so both days must stay free until the ticket arrives. The test is held only in NSW; families overseas or interstate must plan the trip.

June 25. This is the last day to change the order of school choices, after the test but before outcomes. It is the moment to reconsider whether James Ruse should be first, second or third, knowing that an offer comes only from the highest choice the result reaches.

Years 8 to 11 entry in 2028

Later entry follows a separate calendar. The Department's timetable for 2028 is: applications open in June 2027 and close in July, schools announce their assessment requirements in July and August, assessments take place in August and September, outcomes are released in September and October, and successful students start in late January 2028. James Ruse uses its own Edutest exam and three-stage process; for its 2027 intake, registration ran from June 17 to July 24, with the exam on August 15. Year 8 places from 2028 are filled from the Department's reserve list.
