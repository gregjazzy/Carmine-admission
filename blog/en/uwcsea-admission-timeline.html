The UWCSEA admissions calendar

UWCSEA runs one admissions cycle a year, for entry in August. There is no rolling admission and no advantage in being first in line, but there is a real advantage in being complete on time. The cycle for August 2027 entry looks like this.

The cycle, month by month

WhenWhat happens
1 September 2026The Family Dashboard opens for August 2027 applications
October to DecemberVirtual Immersion Programme sessions for first-round High School applicants
2 November 2026First-round deadline, day and boarding
January 2027First-round offers
8 January 2027Second-round deadline for boarders
January to MarchVirtual Immersion Programme sessions for the second round
1 February 2027Second-round deadline for day students
March 2027Second-round offers; by mid-March, families are told if no place is likely
May to July 2027Late offers, rare, only if places free up

Deadlines fall at 11.59 pm Singapore time. An application counts only once every document has been uploaded and the fee paid in full.

Who should not expect a first-round offer

  • Applicants needing learning support. The school confirms its capacity only in February, after reviewing current students.
  • Younger applicants needing English support. EAL places in the Primary and Middle School are usually decided in the second round; High School EAL applicants are considered in both.
  • Singapore citizens. The Ministry of Education waiver follows the January to December school year, so offers usually come in the second round.

For these families, applying for the first round is still wise: the application rolls over automatically and is fully ready when capacity is known.

After an offer

An offer must usually be accepted within about two weeks, by paying the acceptance fees: the one-off enrolment fee and the first term's fees. UWCSEA bills a term in advance rather than taking a deposit, so new families are typically billed for a second term before their child starts.

Planning an August entry? Tell me your child's grade and situation.

January entry

A limited number of places open for January, in Term 2, when students leave between August and December. January entry is for day students from K1 to Grade 10 only; boarders and Grade 11 applicants are not eligible. Applications stay open until July or early August, and offers come in mid to late October. Subject and language options can be limited for January joiners.

Corporate nominations

Families applying through a company's UWCSEA Nominee Programme follow different deadlines: 1 April for K1 to Grade 8, and 31 January for Grades 9 to 11. Boarding applicants cannot use this route.

Aim for November, whatever the case

Over seven years, I have accompanied around fifteen families a year towards very selective schools. For UWCSEA, I advise aiming at the November deadline in every case, with reports, statement and documents ready in September and October.
