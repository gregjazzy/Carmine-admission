The Direct School Admission exercise, run by the Ministry of Education, allows a Primary 6 student to secure a secondary school place on the strength of a particular talent, academic or not, rather than on the PSLE alone. At Raffles Institution, it is one of three routes into Year 1, and the only one decided before the PSLE results.

For admission in 2028, Raffles Institution will publish its next DSA-Sec details by early May 2027.

What Raffles looks for

The school assesses applicants against its selection criteria, which may include their potential; students without prior experience in a talent area may still apply. Meeting the criteria does not guarantee a place, and selection trends can change from year to year with the profile of applicants. The school does not disclose details of its process or reasons for non-selection.

The commitment

  • A student admitted through DSA-Sec cannot submit school choices in the S1 Posting.
  • He cannot transfer to another school after the PSLE results.
  • He is expected to stay for the full six-year Integrated Programme.
  • He takes part in the activities linked to his talent area from Year 1 to Year 4.

Choosing wisely

The Ministry encourages families to decide with their child, considering his strengths, interests and the school's programmes. DSA suits a boy who is genuinely committed to a talent area and confident that Raffles is the school he wants; it closes the other doors once accepted.
