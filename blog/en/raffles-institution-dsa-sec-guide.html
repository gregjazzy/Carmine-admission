Raffles: DSA-Sec

Raffles Institution campus, Singapore. Photo Lim Toh Han, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Direct School Admission, DSA-Sec, is a Ministry of Education initiative: it allows entry to secondary school on talents and achievements, academic or not, beyond the PSLE. For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year towards the most selective schools, and I have students at Raffles Institution.

What the school says

Applicants without prior experience may apply: the school assesses against its criteria, potential included. A student admitted through DSA commits to the six-year Integrated Programme, and takes part from Year 1 to Year 4 in activities linked to the talent they were selected for. They can no longer submit choices in the PSLE posting. The 2026 exercise has closed; the next will be published in early May 2027.
