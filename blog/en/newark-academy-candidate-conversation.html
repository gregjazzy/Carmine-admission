The Candidate Conversation

Livingston, New Jersey, home of Newark Academy. Photo Scott Brody, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

US independent schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Newark Academy deliberately avoids the word "interview". Its admission process includes Candidate and Family Conversations: casual exchanges meant to get to know the applicant, and to let the family decide whether the school suits them. The tone is relaxed; the purpose is serious.

The format
With whomAn admission officer or a Newark Academy faculty member
WhereOn campus, or on Zoom for families outside northern New Jersey
Who takes partThe candidate, and the family in its own conversation
ContentThe student's interests, and their questions about the school
DeadlineCompleted with the supplemental materials, by 11 January 2027

What it is for

Newark Academy says it does not just read applications; it builds relationships. The conversation is where that happens. The school looks for students who are curious, motivated, passionate and collaborative, and who believe in its mission; a conversation shows those qualities more directly than any form. It also lets the family test whether the school's values match their own.

At ease, not rehearsed

I help students talk naturally about what they love and ask real questions, through practice conversations rather than memorised answers.

Book a conversation

Preparing well

  • Know your own interests in detail: a project, a book, a team, a question that keeps coming back.
  • Know the school: its IB programme, its Immersion trips, the activities that attract you.
  • Bring questions: a conversation runs both ways, and good questions show genuine interest.
  • Be yourself: the school asks applicants to bring their authentic self to the process.

Has your child been invited to a conversation? Let's practise together.

The family's side

Parents have their own conversation. Newark Academy describes admission as a partnership, and wants to understand the family as well as the child: what they hope for, how they see the school's role, what questions they have. It is also the parents' chance to judge fit. Families who want a second view can contact current Newark Academy parents through the school's website before or after their conversation.

After the conversation

Notes from the conversation join the rest of the file. An admission officer reviews everything first; the Admission Committee then discusses the application in the context of the grade applied for, aiming to build a class with varied interests and backgrounds. Decisions are released on 19 February.

Scheduling

Because the conversation counts among the January materials, families should book it in the autumn rather than leave it to December, when calendars fill with holidays and other schools' interviews.
