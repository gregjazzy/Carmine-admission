At 11+, the exam is not the whole file.

The reference

Each consortium school requests, before the exam, a reference from the pupil's current head: detailed commentary on her academic performance, with wider information on attitude and character.

Access arrangements

Godolphin welcomes applicants with individual learning needs. Extra time and other arrangements are requested on the exam entry form, with written evidence: usually a doctor or educational psychologist, and a letter from the current school on the pupil's normal way of working.
