Applying to Loomis Chaffee from abroad

Loomis Chaffee has welcomed international students since its founding, and today about 14 % of its students come from more than 40 countries. There are no quotas for any country. The process is the same as for American applicants, with three differences: proof of English, the order in which things happen, and a few costs.

Who counts as international

The test is residence. A student whose permanent residence is outside the United States is considered international in the admission process, because they must provide evidence of English proficiency. Citizenship is an optional, self-reported field and is not a factor in admission.

English proficiency
Who must testStudents whose first language is not English and who have had fewer than two years in an English-speaking school
Accepted testsTOEFL or IELTS (Academic); Duolingo if neither is available locally
Typical scoresTOEFL 5 or higher on the new scale (100 or higher on the old), IELTS 7 or higher
TOEFL code8137

Loomis does not offer English as a Second Language courses: an admitted student follows the full curriculum in English from the first day.

English first, then everything else

I work with international students on the English level Loomis expects, then on the essays and the interview, often online from abroad.

The order of steps

For an international family, proficiency testing comes first, because no interview can be booked without it. Interviews can then take place online, or in a city the admission team visits abroad. SSAT and ISEE scores are not required; the application deadline of 15 January and the financial aid deadline of 31 January are the same as for everyone.

Costs specific to international students

  • An application fee of $160, rather than $75 for families in the United States, Canada or Mexico.
  • An international student fee of $1,140 a year, covering the Office of International Student Support and safekeeping of passports and I-20s.
  • An I-20 is issued to admitted international students.
