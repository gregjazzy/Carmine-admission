Winchester College Sixth Form entry at 16+, for girls and boys

At 16+, Winchester is a different school from the one boys join at 13. The Sixth Form welcomes girls as well as boys, as day pupils or boarders, with two girls' houses, St Cross and St Catherine. A girl or a boy coming from another school joins a year group shaped by Div, Winchester's non-examined course of ideas across disciplines, and by teaching that deliberately goes beyond the A level syllabus.

What the file contains

References from the current school, school reports, a personal statement, a CV and the General Ability Test. Shortlisting rests on all of them together. The personal statement deserves the most care: it is the only piece in the applicant's own voice before the interviews, and it should say concretely what they have read, built or pursued in the subjects they want to study, and why Winchester's way of teaching suits them.

The Winchester Sixth Form

We prepare 15- and 16-year-olds for Winchester's Sixth Form assessments: the GAT, the extended writing, the humanities or STEM paper, and the two interviews.

November: two interviews and two papers

Shortlisted candidates come to Winchester for two interviews in person: one with a member of the pastoral care team, one with a member of the academic staff. During the same visit, they write an extended piece of writing and sit a humanities paper, a STEM paper or both, depending on their three intended A levels.

The academic interview is where a strong candidate distinguishes himself or herself: not by reciting the syllabus, but by showing interest beyond it, a book read, a problem pursued, an argument followed to its end. The pastoral interview asks whether the candidate will thrive in a House and contribute to it.

The GCSE grades that confirm the offer

Offers are conditional. To take up the place, a candidate needs grade 7 or above in all GCSE subjects, grade 8 or above in the subjects proposed for A level, and grade 9 in extension subjects such as Further Mathematics. Candidates from other systems submit an equivalent qualification. The place is confirmed in August, once results arrive.

Those thresholds should guide the choice of A levels as much as the interview does. A candidate whose likely grade in a subject is 7 rather than 8 should think twice before proposing it.

Where Winchester Sixth Formers go

In 2026, Winchester's Sixth Form pupils received 36 offers from Oxford and Cambridge and 42 from American universities. The Futures programme provides individual university and careers advice, and alumni panels connect pupils with Old Wykehamists working around the world.

Financial support at 16+

Founder's Scholarships, worth 25 percent and awarded on merit alone, are open to Sixth Form entrants, day or boarding. Means-tested bursaries can reach 100 percent, and the Wykeham Award is reserved for entrants from UK state secondary schools. Music and Sport Awards exist too. All are requested on the registration form.

How I prepare Sixth Form candidates

I work with candidates on the subjects they intend to take, at the level of the humanities and STEM papers, and on the extended writing. Then mock interviews, academic and pastoral, built on their real interests, until they can discuss their subjects with the ease of someone who has gone beyond the course. I also read the personal statement with them, so that it sounds like them and says something precise.
