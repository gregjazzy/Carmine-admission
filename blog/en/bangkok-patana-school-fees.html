Bangkok Patana is a not-for-profit school with no shareholders: its foundation board reinvests fees in staff, equipment and facilities. For 2026/27, the published fees are as follows. All figures are in Thai baht and, as the school notes, subject to change.

Tuition is paid termly. In the third term of Years 11 and 13, no tuition is charged, only external examination fees. Lunch is included for primary students, and the compulsory residential visits from Year 3 to Year 10 and in Year 12 are included in tuition.

One-off payments for a new student

  • Application fee: THB 5,500, non-refundable, with no guarantee of a place or an assessment.
  • Entrance fee: THB 250,000 for the first child, THB 200,000 for each subsequent child, non-refundable. It can be staggered for entry into Foundation Stage or Year 1, with THB 40,000 paid first.
  • Deposit: THB 50,000, returned on withdrawal with proper notice, less any outstanding charges.
  • Initial fee payment: THB 50,000 on acceptance of an offer, non-refundable, offset against the first tuition invoice.

The EAL fee is avoidable

A child whose academic English is ready does not need the EAL programme. I work on that English before entry, so it supports the application and the budget.

Annual extras

A capital assessment fee of THB 30,000 a year applies to every student. From Year 1 to Year 9, students who need to develop their academic English join the EAL programme at THB 105,000 a year; when the school considers a child needs it, it is not optional, and it is charged for no more than six terms. School buses cost from THB 47,000 to THB 120,800 a year depending on zone and whether the trip is one way or return.

Budgeting for Patana? Let's talk about your child's English; it can change the bill.

Large families and leaving

Tuition discounts apply from the third child: 5%, then 10% for the fourth and 15% from the fifth. Leaving requires a full term's notice, half a term in the first term; late notice costs THB 50,000.
