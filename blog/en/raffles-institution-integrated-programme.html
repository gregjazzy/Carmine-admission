Raffles Institution's Integrated Programme carries students through six years of secondary and pre-university education as one continuous course. Choosing it is choosing a school, a pace and a community for most of a child's adolescence.

What the programme offers

In Years 1 to 4, the school's Curriculum Innovation framework combines academic work with initiatives such as the Future Learning Programme, Raffles Playground, GapX and Outdoor Adventure Learning, designed to prepare students for a world of complexity and change. Talent development programmes run alongside. In Years 5 and 6, character education and enrichment programmes accompany the academic subjects.

The commitment

The Ministry of Education's note to families is plain: students admitted to an Integrated Programme are expected to complete it, transfers are strongly discouraged, and transfers to another IP school are not allowed except in exceptional circumstances. Students who enter through DSA also commit to the activities of their talent area. The Ministry's advice is to choose a school according to the child's aptitude and strengths, not its name.

Seeing it for yourself

Raffles publishes a brochure for each stage, both under the school motto Auspicium Melioris Aevi, and holds open houses, including a Year 1 to 4 open house and a virtual session after the PSLE results in 2026. For a family weighing a six-year commitment, these are worth more than any ranking: they show the pace, the activities and the students the child would spend those years with.

Who it suits

A six-year programme suits a student who is curious beyond the syllabus, comfortable with a demanding pace and ready to commit to one community for the long term. It suits less well a family that may move abroad, or a child who would benefit from a change of environment at 16.
