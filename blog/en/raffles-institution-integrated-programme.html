Raffles: the Integrated Programme

Raffles Institution campus, Singapore. Photo Lim Toh Han, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Singapore schools November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Raffles Institution offers the Integrated Programme, a six-year course: boys from Year 1 to Year 4, then boys and girls in Years 5 and 6.

A commitment

The Ministry of Education is clear: students commit for six years. Transfers are strongly discouraged, and to another IP school, not allowed except in exceptional circumstances. Students already in an Integrated Programme are not considered for Year 3 entry.

What to take from it

Choosing Raffles means choosing for six years: by the child's aptitudes and strengths, not just reputation.
