Ransom Everglades charges one tuition for every grade from 6 to 12: $57,300 in 2026-27, with lunch included every day. The figure is simple; a few rules around it are worth knowing before a family accepts a place.

I go through these figures with Miami families weighing the school. Here they are.

One consequence of a single rate is that the cost does not rise when a student moves from the Middle School to the Upper School, unlike at schools that charge more for the older grades. Any change from year to year comes only from the rate the school sets each year.

Instalments, siblings and seven years

Families paying full tuition can pay in one sum, in two instalments or in ten, with an added fee for the instalment plans. Families receiving aid can spread their share over ten payments with no fee. There is no discount for siblings; support comes only through need-based grants. At the 2026-27 rate, seven years from Grade 6 to Grade 12 represent about $401,000 before any increases.

Why aid is requested at entry

Ransom Everglades awards over $9.5 million a year in aid and tuition remission, supporting 18 percent of students with an average grant of $42,850, and applicants' finances are not considered in the admission decision. The school specifically advises against a family paying full tuition for a year or two from savings or borrowing, then asking for aid: a full-pay application signals the family can cover costs for the foreseeable future, and the funds for families who ask later are reserved for unforeseeable reversals. A family that might need help should apply from the start, by January 10.
