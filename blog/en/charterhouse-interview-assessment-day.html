Charterhouse: interview and assessment day

Charterhouse, Godalming, from the air. Photo Tim Sheerman-Chase, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0

UK boarding schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Candidates selected after the Pre-Test are invited to Charterhouse, in the spring of Year 6, for an interview and activities day. The school designed it to get to know each candidate: group activities, interviews and a written assessment, a piece of creative writing.

What the day looks for

Charterhouse speaks of the "complete child". It wants to uncover each child's talents, how they work with others, think aloud and write freely. Offers come by the end of the term, usually before the end of March.

Preparing for Charterhouse's day

I prepare your child for the interview, the group activities and the creative writing, so they show themselves as they are, at their best.

What makes the difference

The group

In a group activity, the child who listens, builds on ideas and helps others stands out as much as the one who finds the answer.

Is your child at ease in a group they do not know? Tell me about them.

Creative writing

Free writing, timed, in English: for a child who rarely writes fiction, it is an exercise to practise.

The interview

Talking about oneself, one's reading, what one loves: simple questions that need more than a one-sentence answer.

What I do for your family

I know the school through my students at Charterhouse. For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year with applications to leading schools in England. I prepare your child for all three parts of the day, in short, regular sessions. For the test before it, see the ISEB Pre-Test.

Is your child applying to a selective school?

Tell me about their situation: current class and school, the school you are aiming for, the entry year, what worries you. I reply personally within 24 hours.
