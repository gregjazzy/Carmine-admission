Phillips Exeter: entry after grade 9

One of the academic buildings on the Exeter campus, New Hampshire. Photo Transmarinus, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Grade 9 is Exeter's main entry point. But the school also admits students in grade 10 and grade 11, one-year seniors for the final year alone, and students who have already finished high school, for a postgraduate year. Each door has its own tests and logic.

Tests, by grade

  • Grades 10 and 11: one of Upper Level SSAT or ISEE, PSAT, SAT, PreACT or ACT.
  • One-year senior and postgraduate: PSAT, SAT, PreACT or ACT. The SSAT and ISEE are no longer accepted.

In every case, the test must be from no earlier than one year before January 15. The rest of the file does not change: interview, two essays, parent statement, recommendations and transcripts.

The right door, at the right time

Grade 10, grade 11 or a postgraduate year: I tell you which entry makes sense for your child, prepare them for the matching test, then the rest of the file.

Which student

Grade 10 suits a student who was not ready at 14, or whose family is moving to the United States. Grade 11 and the senior year alone often suit a student from abroad who wants an American record before university. The postgraduate year serves a student who has finished high school and wants to strengthen their record, or their English, before applying to American universities. In these three cases, an SAT- or ACT-type test replaces the SSAT: the preparation is not the same.
