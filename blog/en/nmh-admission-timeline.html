The NMH admission timeline

Northfield Mount Hermon's calendar has two dates everyone knows, January 15 for the application and March 10 for decisions, and several that families discover too late: the interview that has to be booked by phone, the InitialView appointment two weeks ahead for some international applicants, the aid file that waits for a complete application, and a first payment on July 1.

I build this calendar with every family I accompany to NMH. Here it is for entry in September 2027.

Spring and summer: the inquiry and the first visit

Everything starts with NMH's inquiry form. It has to be completed before a visit or an interview can be scheduled, and it is how the admission office begins to follow a family. Campus tours run four days a week at fixed times, with two morning slots on Wednesdays, and last about an hour. A visit in spring or early summer, when the campus is in session or close to it, gives a child something real to talk about at the interview.

The application platforms open over the summer. Gateway to Prep Schools reopens around July 1 for the following year, and the Standard Application Online runs alongside it. Families choose one; both lead to the same file.

Autumn: interview, tests and teachers

The interview is required and booked by phone or email with the admission office, on campus or by video. Autumn slots are the comfortable ones; December interviews leave no margin if something has to be moved. Any optional SSAT, ISEE, PSAT, SAT or ACT score, and the required English test for non-native speakers, should be taken early enough to decide what to send.

The English and math teachers who write recommendations are the child's current ones, so they can only be asked once the school year has started. September, with a short note on why NMH, is the right moment. From October to January, NMH also runs free virtual workshops on the application process, useful for families new to American boarding schools.

Early January: the steps that cannot slip

Initial paymentBy July 1

The aid file deserves particular care. NMH reviews it only once the admission application is complete, and it needs documents from every parent or guardian. A family that submits the aid file on January 15 but is still missing a recommendation is late on both.

After March 10

Decisions and aid arrive almost together, so a family knows its net cost straight away. Updates to a file, a new report or an additional recommendation go through the SchoolAdmin parent portal. The first payment is due by July 1; a family that pays the full year by then avoids the mandatory tuition refund insurance, billed otherwise at 1.85 percent of what it owes.
