La Tour: three languages for everyone, and bilingual sections by exam

Paris schools November 2026 4 min read

At La Tour, languages are not optional: every student learns three, and keeps them until the end of their schooling. The school offers eight modern and two classical languages, grouped into three strands, and in 2023 received the Euroscol label recognising its European outlook. For a family wanting a genuinely multilingual child, it is a strong reason to choose the school, provided the path is built from sixième.

The path, year by year

In sixième, every student takes six hours of English, and can start a second language as an option, among German, Italian, Russian, literary Arabic or Chinese, or Spanish and Italian at an advanced level. In cinquième, they choose a European modern language: German, Spanish, Italian or Russian. In quatrième, they add a classical language, Latin or Greek, an oriental language, Arabic, Chinese or Japanese, or a beginner Mediterranean language. These three languages are kept until the bac, and at the end of seconde the student chooses the order in which to present them.

Bilingual and advanced sections

For the most confident students, La Tour offers bilingual sections in English, literary Arabic, Chinese, Spanish and Italian, by exam: a programme of literature, history, geography and art history in the language. Several exam sessions are held in spring, early July and late August. Advanced sections in the same languages offer further study for very good students. Classes are taught by native speakers, in small level groups.
