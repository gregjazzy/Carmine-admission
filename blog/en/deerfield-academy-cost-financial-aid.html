Deerfield: cost and financial aid

The Main School Building, Deerfield Academy, Massachusetts. Photo John Phelan, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

US boarding schools November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Deerfield meets 100% of the demonstrated need of every admitted family, with grants that need no repayment. In 2026-2027, 38% of families receive aid, 35% of aided families pay nothing, and the average boarding grant reaches $68,235. The school spends over $15 million a year on it.

What the price covers

Tuition covers room and board, textbooks, a MacBook Air and the health center. With the move to full boarding in fall 2027, the school announces a single, truly comprehensive rate, with no additional fees. The application fee is $60, $125 from outside the United States.

A place cannot be bought

At Deerfield, aid is based on need alone, never merit. What earns admission is the file: I prepare your child for every part of it.

Who can be aided

For American W-2 families, incomes up to $150,000 pay nothing, and above that, no more than 10% of total income per student. All other families, international included, can receive up to 100% aid; 14% of aided students are international. Deerfield offers no merit scholarships, athletic or academic.
