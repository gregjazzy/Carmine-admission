Aiglon College: fees 2026-2027

Villars-sur-Ollon, home of Aiglon. Photo Bledard92, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Aiglon College publishes its fees every January. For 2026-2027, boarding costs CHF 168,000 a year for the final two years, the International Baccalaureate years, CHF 156,800 for Years 10 and 11, CHF 151,200 for Year 9, and CHF 119,800 for Years 7 and 8. The youngest can board from age 9 for CHF 100,800; day places exist at every age, from CHF 49,700 to CHF 99,400.

On joining come a non-refundable CHF 3,000 registration fee, a CHF 15,000 deposit to reserve the place, a CHF 4,000 capital investment contribution, CHF 2,000 for alumni programmes, and CHF 4,100 to 5,400 for clothing. The school offers 5 to 15% off from the second child enrolled at the same time.

A place cannot be bought

At Aiglon, the interview, the assessments and the child's fit with the school's values decide admissions, every month. I prepare your child for that encounter and build with you an application that shows what the school is looking for. My aim: that they get in because they belong there.

What the price does not say

Aiglon is a not-for-profit school, owned by an association through its founder's bequest: its surpluses are reinvested in the school. That is not a detail. A family choosing Aiglon is not paying for a brand; it is paying for a project built on effort in the mountains, expeditions and house life, and the school checks for it at admission. Families who see Aiglon as just another prestigious boarding school are often the ones choosing the wrong school.
