At Bangkok Patana, an incomplete file is not considered. Everything must be gathered before applying.

The documents

The recommendation: sent directly by the child's current teacher, with a writing sample. Documents: reports from the last two years, birth certificate, passports, photos. Forms: application agreement, letter of guarantee, doctor's letter and year-group forms, including option choices in Years 10 and 12. Any document not in English must be professionally translated.
