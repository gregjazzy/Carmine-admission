At Bangkok Patana, an incomplete file simply waits. The school is unable to process an application until all required documentation has been received, and a file that is not complete is not considered for placement, nor placed in the waiting pool. Assembling everything before submitting is the first real step.

The checklist

PartWhat it containsWho provides it
School recommendationA confidential recommendation with a writing sample from the childThe current teacher, sent directly to the school
DocumentsReports for the last two years, birth certificate or adoption papers, passports of child and parents, photos; Thai ID for dual nationals with one Thai parentThe family, scanned and uploaded
FormsApplication agreement, application fee, Letter of Guarantee, doctor's letter, year group forms (none for Year 2), Year 10 or Year 12 optionsThe family, printed, completed and scanned

Any document not in English must be professionally translated before the process is started.

The pieces that weigh most

Among Patana's admission criteria are previous reports, references, confidential recommendations and writing samples. Two of these are written by others or by the child alone. The teacher's recommendation is confidential and goes straight from the teacher to the admissions office, so the family should simply make sure the teacher sends it promptly. The writing sample shows, in a few paragraphs, how the child handles written English, which is often the clearest indicator the school has before any assessment.

Disclosing needs

Applicants with any potential or confirmed learning support needs should disclose them fully when submitting the application; further assessments may be required. The school judges its ability to meet each student's needs, and full information allows it to do so fairly.

Before the first day

Once a place is offered, the family signs and returns the offer letter and pays a non-refundable initial THB 50,000 to hold the place. Copies of valid Thai non-immigrant visas must be provided before the child starts, and parents sign the safeguarding code of conduct in the admissions office.
