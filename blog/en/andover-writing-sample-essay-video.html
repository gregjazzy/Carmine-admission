Andover: the graded writing sample, essay and video

US boarding schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Andover asks for three pieces few schools combine: a writing sample already graded by a teacher, submitted on Gateway, an essay, and a short video response, submitted in Andover's portal. Add the parent statement.

What each reveals

The graded writing sample shows the student's real classwork, with their teacher's comments. It cannot be manufactured: it is chosen. The essay says who the child is beyond their grades. The video shows them speaking, for a few minutes, without a safety net. Together with the test, these pieces show how they think, how they write and how they express themselves.

Three consistent pieces

I help you choose the graded sample, work on the essay with your child without ever writing it, and prepare them for the video.

The pitfalls

The wrong sample

The best paper is not always the one with the best grade: it is the one that shows thinking. And for a student at a non-English school, the language of that paper becomes a question.

Not sure which paper to send? Tell me about your child: we will choose it together.

The over-polished essay

Between the graded sample and the essay, the committee sees two texts by the same student. An essay at a completely different level stands out.

The recited video

A memorised answer shows on screen. Practise the format, not the script.
