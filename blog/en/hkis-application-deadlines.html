HKIS accepts applications from May to November each year. Two dates matter for 2027-2028 entry, and both concern complete files, not merely started applications.

The calendar

WhenWhat
From MayApplications open on the OpenApply portal; virtual tours, campus tours and Open House
1 October 2026Deadline for the Primary School, Reception One to Grade 5
1 November 2026Deadline for the Secondary School, Grades 6 to 12; deadline for debenture applications for the following year
After reviewSupporting documents requested; selected applicants invited to the pre-entry assessment
From March 2027Admissions decisions, then on a rolling basis as places open

The deadlines may be reviewed depending on the flow of applications. Applications after the deadline may be accepted case by case.

What "complete" means

An application is ready for review only when HKIS has received the HK$2,000 application fee, the completed Family Questionnaire and every required document. Applications with documents still outstanding at the deadline are not considered. The OpenApply checklist shows what is missing, and families should watch it closely in the weeks before the deadline.

Complete, and on time

I help HKIS families meet their division's deadline with a complete file, then prepare the child for an assessment invitation.

Why early matters

  • Invitations depend on priority and volume: particularly for local and regional applicants, being complete early avoids being left out when invitations are limited.
  • Debentures close with the Secondary deadline: a family that wants the priority a debenture brings must apply for it by 1 November.
  • Documents take time: reports and recommendations from the current school, translations and identity documents rarely arrive in a single week.

After the decision

An accepted family secures the place with a reservation deposit equal to the first semester's tuition, capital levy and entry fee. Qualified applicants without a place enter the wait pool, organised by priority, and may receive an offer later in the year as places open.

How I plan it

For seven years, I have worked with around fifteen families a year on applications to very selective schools, many in Hong Kong. For HKIS, I aim for a complete file by early September for the Primary School and early October for the Secondary School, leaving room for the unexpected: a late recommendation, a translation, or a document requested after review.
