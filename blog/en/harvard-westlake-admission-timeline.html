The Harvard-Westlake calendar

Harvard-Westlake's admission year can be summed up in four dates. Three of them are well known to Los Angeles families. The fourth, in November, is the one most often missed, and it decides whether an applicant is guaranteed an interview.

1 August: the ISEE window opens

Every applicant must take the ISEE once, and the sitting that counts must fall between 1 August 2026 and 15 January 2027. The test can be taken on paper, online or at home, using school code 052135. Families who take it in the autumn leave themselves room if something goes wrong.

1 November: recommendations open

The current English teacher, the current math teacher and an administrator complete their recommendation forms between 1 November and the application deadline. The forms are emailed to them once the family enters their addresses in the online application, so entering them early helps teachers plan.

13 November: the date families miss

For 7th and 9th grade applicants, submitting the application form by 13 November at 11:59 pm Pacific time guarantees an interview invitation. After that date, an interview is no longer guaranteed. Interviews take place on weekdays during school hours, in person or on Zoom, and the student questionnaire must be in at least two days before.

Every date, on time

I set the Harvard-Westlake calendar with the family in September, so that the form goes in before 13 November and the questionnaire, interview and ISEE are ready in turn.

15 January: everything else

By 15 January 2027 at 11:59 pm Pacific time, the full application and the financial aid application must be complete: ISEE score, recommendations, transcript release form and questionnaire. Families who need aid must apply now; students who do not apply for aid at admission are not eligible later, except through a small fund for unforeseen reversals. Applicants who start after this date are considered only for the waitlist.

12 March: decisions

Admission and financial aid decisions are released together on 12 March 2027 at 5 pm Pacific time, through the online portal. They are not mailed or emailed. Because admission is never guaranteed, families should have applied to other schools as well.

Between the dates

Optional events, information sessions for parents, campus tours, arts performances and athletics competitions, run through the autumn. Families can also email links to arts or athletics work, with videos of up to five minutes, and one supplemental letter of recommendation.
