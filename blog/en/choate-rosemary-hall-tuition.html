Choate Rosemary Hall tuition and fees 2026-2027

The Andrew Mellon Library, Choate Rosemary Hall, Wallingford. Photo Daderot, Wikimedia Commons, public domain

In 2026-2027, Choate's boarding tuition is $73,530, plus a $1,000 technology fee, for a total of $74,530. Day tuition is $56,610, or $57,610 with the technology fee.

What comes on top

  • A 10% non-refundable deposit, due on signing the enrollment contract.
  • Books and supplies: the school advises budgeting $1,000 a year.
  • Health insurance, required, and a phone with a US data plan.
  • Travel, including during breaks when the school is closed.
  • Music lessons, depending on frequency.

A place cannot be bought

At Choate, price does not separate families: the interview, the essays and the recommendations do. I prepare your child for each.

Financial aid

Choate meets 100% of demonstrated need for aided families. In 2026-2027, the published average family contribution ranges from $570 for incomes below $75,000 to $45,320 above $400,000. The application is due by January 15. The school notes one thing worth knowing: when the overall need of the applicant pool exceeds its budget, financial need may influence admission decisions.
