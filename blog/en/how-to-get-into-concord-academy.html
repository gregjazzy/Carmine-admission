How to get into Concord Academy

Concord Academy describes itself as an independent school for independent-minded students. Set in the town of Concord, near Boston, it is neither a boarding school with day students nor a day school with boarders: 60% of students are day, 40% board, and both share a residential life that keeps day students on campus for dinner and weekend activities.

The application in six parts

Every family starts by creating a Finalsite account and submitting the inquiry form, even if they apply through the Gateway to Prep Schools or the SAO. Through that account they book the interview, register for events, request recommendations and receive the decision. The family portion includes biographical information, a student application and a parent or guardian statement, completed on one platform only. The school then needs current-year grades, the previous year's report, and three recommendations: from a counsellor or school official, the current English teacher and the current mathematics teacher. An official SSAT or ISEE score is required. Supplements in athletics, visual arts or performing arts are optional.

What the committee reads for

Concord Academy's review is holistic. Beyond academic performance, the admissions committee hopes to learn about intellectual curiosity, character, maturity, community involvement, potential for growth, particular talents and passions, the capacity for positive relationships with adults and peers, and individual expression and open-mindedness. A strong transcript without a person behind it rarely fits a school that prizes self-direction.

Cost and aid

For 2026-27, tuition is $84,410 for boarders and $67,610 for day students. The school meets 100% of computed financial need, entirely through grants, and about 23% of students receive aid. Families who may need help should apply by 15 January, with the admission file.
