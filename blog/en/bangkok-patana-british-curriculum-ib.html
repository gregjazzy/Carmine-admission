The British curriculum and the IB at Patana

Bangkok Patana's curriculum is British up to Year 11 and International Baccalaureate in the last two years. For a family choosing an entry point, each stage has its own logic and its own demands.

Years 7 to 9: Key Stage 3

Students join the secondary school from the age of 11. Key Stage 3 has its roots in the English National Curriculum, adapted to the school's position in South East Asia, with international themes across subjects. In the second term of Year 9, students begin choosing their Key Stage 4 courses, with an Options Morning for parents and students and guidance from careers advisers.

Years 10 and 11: the (I)GCSE

CompulsoryChosen
English Language and Literature, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, a Modern Foreign LanguageThree further subjects, including one humanity: History, Geography, Business Studies or Economics

Year 10 students build their programme from 30 courses, and most obtain nine or ten (I)GCSE qualifications. The school expects two to two and a half hours of home learning a night, and some subjects include coursework that counts towards the final grade.

Mathematics and sciences, from IGCSE to IB

I prepare Patana students for (I)GCSE and IB mathematics and sciences, and help those joining in Year 10 or Year 12 reach the level quickly.

Years 12 and 13: Senior Studies

Most students follow the IB Diploma: one course from each of six groups, plus Theory of Knowledge, CAS and the Extended Essay. IB Courses allow fewer subjects for a more tailored programme. The IB Career-related Programme combines two Diploma courses with a Pearson BTEC Level 3 qualification in Art and Design, Business or Sports. The school's stated requirement for Senior Studies is motivation and enthusiasm; IB classes are generally smaller than lower down the school.

Results

In 2026, the school's seventieth year, its 137 IB Diploma students achieved an average of 36 points, with 22% scoring 40 or more and a pass rate of 99.3%; one student reached the maximum of 45. The class received 644 university offers from 194 institutions in 18 countries, including Oxford, Cambridge and Brown.

Joining midway

A student entering in Year 10 or Year 12 arrives at the moment of option choices and submits the option forms with the application. The subjects chosen at (I)GCSE lay the foundation for the IB, so the school stresses picking subjects a student enjoys and can succeed in.
