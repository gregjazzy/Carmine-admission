At Roxbury Latin, voices other than the applicant's matter.

The pieces

A graded essay: work already marked by the current school, showing writing and creativity. The counselor and teachers: a window on classwork, drive, mindset, creativity and how he works with others. A personal recommendation: from someone who knows the boy well and can speak to his character, not necessarily a teacher or someone from school.

The right pieces
