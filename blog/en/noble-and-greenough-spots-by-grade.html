Places at Nobles vary with enrollment, but the orders of magnitude are stable.

On average

Grade 7: about 55 places.

Grade 8: about 4, varying widely.

Grade 9: about 65.

Grade 10: no more than 10.

Grade 11: fewer than 5.

Grade 12: none.

What to take from it

Grades 7 and 9 are the real doors. Beyond that, each place is rare.
