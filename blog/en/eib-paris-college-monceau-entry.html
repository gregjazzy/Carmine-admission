EIB: getting into Collège Monceau, in sixième or mid-collège

Paris schools November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

EIB Collège Monceau has more than 800 students of 57 nationalities, on three sites in the 8th and 17th arrondissements. It is a secular private school under contract with the state, with a 100% pass rate at the brevet. For a family coming from elsewhere, the difficulty is not the level: it is the place.

A sixième already partly full

Sixième (age 11) is the main entry point, but most CM2 pupils from EIB primary schools continue there, after an entrance test at the end of CM2: at EIB Monceau primary, 94% of CM2 pupils move on to the collège. Places for students from other schools are therefore limited, and the school advises applying from October. In cinquième, quatrième and troisième, places open as students leave, and the collège also takes students mid-year.

A place at Collège Monceau

When places are scarce, every file counts. I know EIB through my students, and I prepare applications and children to the level the collège expects, for the bilingual track as for the international section.

How admission is decided

The application is online, in two phases, with a €300 application fee then, if favourable, €1,000 in registration and exam fees. The decision rests with the head, on the file, reports and teachers' comments, and, depending on the profile, on additional tests and an interview. One particularity: the collège holds neither open days nor webinars, and a visit is only offered once the file has been accepted.

Bilingual track or British section

In the bilingual track, about a quarter of lessons are in English; depending on level, teaching in a foreign language can reach 43% of the timetable. The British international section prepares the international option of the brevet and Cambridge IGCSEs, and requires very good English. From sixième, a second language is added: Mandarin, Spanish, German or Italian.
