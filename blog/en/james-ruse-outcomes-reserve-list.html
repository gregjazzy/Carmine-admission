Places at James Ruse are given on merit. Outcomes arrive in August, in the application dashboard.

The performance report

For each section of the test, the report places the student in a band: the top 10%, the next 15%, the next 25%, or the lowest 50%. It gives neither the percentage of correct answers, nor the score, nor the rank: these are never disclosed.

The reserve list

A student not offered a place may be placed on reserve. The Department publishes reserve bands that give a sense of whether and when an offer might come.

Reading the report

The report shows where your child lost points. I use it to target work, for a later entry or another school.
