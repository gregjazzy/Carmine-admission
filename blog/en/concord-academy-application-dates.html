Concord Academy's admission calendar is short to state and easy to misjudge, because three of its deadlines fall within the first fifteen days of January. Families who plan from the end backwards avoid the January rush.

Every date, in order

DateWhat happensWho it concerns
17 August 2026Application opens, interviews bookableEveryone
1 January 2027Vericant interview submitted, to be considered for a CA interviewApplicants living or studying abroad
4 January 2027Supplements received in SlideRoom for guaranteed reviewOptional arts and athletics submissions
15 January 2027All materials due; last SSAT or ISEE date; English test scores; financial aid applicationEveryone
10 March 2027Decisions released; financial aid disbursedEveryone
10 April 2027Admitted students replyAdmitted families

A sensible order of work

Late August and September: create the Finalsite account and inquiry form, book the interview, register for events. October and November: visit, interview, sit a first SSAT or ISEE and, if needed, the English test; ask the three recommenders early. December: retake a test if useful, finish the student and parent forms, send any supplement. January: check that every piece has arrived, and submit the financial aid application.

The autumn, well used

I organise test preparation, interview practice and the written answers across the autumn, so that nothing waits for January.

One platform, one account

CA accepts its own Finalsite application, the Gateway to Prep Schools and the SAO, whose users complete an additional CA form. Whatever the platform, the Finalsite account and inquiry form come first, because that is where the interview is booked, recommendations requested and the decision delivered. Family forms should be completed on one platform only, which saves a good deal of confusion in January.

Events along the way

In 2026, CA held an Open House on 17 October and a reception in Boston on 12 December, with virtual sessions on boarding life, athletics, the arts and financial aid in November. Its admissions officers also travel each autumn, to fairs across the United States and to events in Asia and Africa.

Starting late? Let's see what can still be done before 15 January.

10 March

Decisions arrive through Finalsite on 10 March, the same day financial aid is disbursed. Families then have a month, until 10 April, to decide. For a family with several offers, that month is the time to revisit, ask questions and compare aid packages.
