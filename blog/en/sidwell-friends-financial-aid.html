Sidwell Friends states that family income should never be a barrier to a Sidwell education, and presents its financial aid program as one of the most generous in the Washington area. The program is guided by the school's philosophy and diversity statements and by the National Association of Independent Schools Principles of Good Practice. For a family weighing tuition above $55,000, the aid process deserves as much care as the admission file.

I help families plan both. Here is how aid works.

Applying through Clarity

Sidwell Friends processes financial aid through Clarity. Clarity runs information webinars for families in the autumn, including one on December 8. Families who want aid should start that application in the fall alongside the admission application.

One practical point: the school's admissions process page gives February 1 as the financial aid deadline for new families, while its tuition and aid page gives January 15. A family that submits by January 15 is safe under either reading, and should confirm with the admissions office.

Our experience with Sidwell Friends

We have a track record of admissions to Sidwell Friends, and we put that experience at your family's disposal: an admission file completed by December 31, and an aid application filed safely before the earliest deadline.

Costs beyond tuition

DivisionAdditional expenses that aid families usually see discounted
Lower SchoolEarly Risers and after care, homework club, special after-school programs, the shuttle, school-approved testing or tutoring
Middle SchoolBooks, after care, the laptop program fee, Minimester travel, PE uniforms, the shuttle, school-recommended testing or tutoring
Upper SchoolAthletic equipment, books, trips for athletics, service or academics, the yearbook, the shuttle, school-recommended testing or tutoring

Families receiving aid generally get these costs discounted at the same percentage as their tuition award, which matters because extras at Sidwell Friends run from several hundred to several thousand dollars a year.

Funding from elsewhere

The school points families to a list of additional funding and scholarships on the Association of Independent Schools of Greater Washington website, and, for families living in Washington, to the Opportunity Scholarship Program run by Serving Our Children.
