How to get into Greenwich Academy

Greenwich Academy is an independent girls' school in Greenwich, Connecticut, from Pre-Connecting, its Pre-Kindergarten, to Group XII. Grades are called Groups and written in Roman numerals: Group I is first grade. The school accepts applications for every year except Group XII, through a two-part process with two deadlines, December 15 and January 15.

I work with families applying to Greenwich Academy and similar schools. Here is how admission works for 2027-28.

Two parts, two deadlines

What weighs

The school says plainly that test scores are only one piece of information, balanced against school references, report cards and interviews. For older applicants, that means the essays, the student interview and the teachers' recommendations carry as much as the ISEE or SSAT. For the youngest, the parent interview and the girl's visit or small-group assessment are the heart of the file.

Cost and aid

Tuition for 2026-27 ranges from $54,260 in Pre-Connecting and Connecting to $60,820 in the Upper School, with lunch, snacks, supplies and class trips included. Admission is need-blind, and 23 percent of students receive financial aid, averaging $43,015. Aid requires US citizenship or permanent residency and a Clarity application by December 15, a month before Part II.

Families from abroad

Greenwich Academy welcomes applications from families outside the United States, but it is not an I-20 school and does not issue student visas, so families must arrange their immigration status independently.
