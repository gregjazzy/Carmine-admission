La Tour: joining mid-collège or in lycée

Paris schools November 2026 4 min read

Outside sixième, La Tour takes new students mid-collège and in lycée. But the calendar is different, places are few, and a new student has to join classmates who have already been taking three languages for several years.

The calendar

Applications for cinquième, quatrième, troisième and lycée open in early January for the following September. The procedure is the same as for sixième: online pre-registration, a complete file with a motivation letter to the head of school, an answer within six weeks, an appointment with the child and both parents. Since places mid-collège mostly come from departures, a file submitted as soon as applications open stands a far better chance than one submitted in spring.

A place along the way

When a place opens, every application is compared with the students already there. I know what La Tour expects of a new student, and I prepare applications of excellence that stand that comparison.

Languages, the main obstacle

At La Tour, every student takes three languages, chosen in sixième and kept until the bac. A student arriving in quatrième or seconde must fit into that system: join a language already started, or choose one that opens at their level. The advanced and bilingual sections require a verified level. It is often the language question, more than grades, that decides whether entry is feasible.

Could your child join in quatrième or seconde? Tell me about them: I will tell you whether it is realistic.

Joining along the way

I have students at La Tour, and every year I support around fifteen families towards the leading Paris schools. For entry mid-way, I start with a frank reading of the student's file and languages: what makes entry possible, what will have to be caught up, and the other schools worth keeping in parallel. Then I prepare the letter and the appointment, which weigh as much as in sixième.

In lycée

In lycée, students choose their specialities from seven, including life and earth sciences, economics and social sciences, history-geography-geopolitics, literature-philosophy and English, and must take three languages in première. The pace is steady: written tests on Saturday mornings, grouped exams twice a year, oral tests in French and languages. Results follow: at the bac, more than half the students earn a mention très bien (highest honours). Guidance is provided by advisers, for study in France as abroad.

Applications open in January. Write to me: the file needs to be ready before.
