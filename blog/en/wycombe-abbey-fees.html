Wycombe Abbey is a full-boarding school, and its fees reflect a seven-day life: tuition, a room, meals, laundry and a weekend programme. Fees are charged in advance at the start of each term and reviewed every year.

What the fee includes, and what it does not

Boarding fees cover tuition, lodging, laundry, meals and snacks, personal accident insurance, most games, most educational materials, many curriculum trips, pastoral care and the co-curricular and weekend activities programme. Day fees cover the same apart from lodging and laundry. Billed separately, in arrears: external exams, extra music, dance and drama lessons, specialist sports coaching, some trips and optional insurance.

A place is earned on the papers

Fees never decide who is admitted at Wycombe Abbey; the Pre-Test, the papers and the interview do. I prepare girls for each.

Reductions

  • Siblings: 5 % off for the second child, increasing by 5 % for each subsequent child, on day and boarding fees.
  • Armed Forces and Diplomatic Service: 25 % off boarding fees for families receiving the Continuity of Education Allowance, in addition to the sibling discount.
  • Bursaries: means-tested, for families who could not otherwise afford the fees; applications by 1 September of the year before entry. A family applying for a full bursary can discuss the registration fee with the Director of Admissions.

Scholarships are not a discount

Academic scholarships are worth up to 5 % of the fees, exhibitions £600 a year, and music awards cover instrumental lessons. They are distinctions first; a family should not count on them to change the budget.
