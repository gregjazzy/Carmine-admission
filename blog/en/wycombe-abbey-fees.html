Wycombe Abbey: fees

Entrance to Wycombe Abbey, High Wycombe. Photo David Hillas, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

English boarding schools November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

From September 2026, Wycombe Abbey costs £21,500 a term boarding and £16,400 day, inclusive of VAT. Fees cover tuition, lodging, laundry, meals, most games and trips, and the weekend programme.

What comes on top

  • Registration: £420, non-refundable, at 11+ and 13+; requested later at 16+.
  • Deposit: £3,500; a full term for families living outside the UK or needing a visa.
  • Extras: exams, music, dance, drama, specialist coaching.

A 5% discount applies for a second daughter, and means-tested bursaries must be applied for by 1 September of the year before entry.

A place cannot be bought
