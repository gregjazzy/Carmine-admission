How to Get Into Phillips Exeter: Tests, File and Timeline

The Academy Building, the historic heart of Phillips Exeter. Photo Quintin Soloviev, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0

US boarding schools November 2026 9 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Exeter enrolled 355 new students in 2026, across four grades and a postgraduate year. The school does not post an acceptance rate on its website, and it does not publish average SSAT scores. What it does spell out, in detail, is what goes into the file. That is where an application is decided: in the quality of each part of the file, far more than in the test score.

Which grade to apply for

Exeter admits students into ninth, tenth and eleventh grade, as one-year seniors, and as postgraduates who have already finished high school. Ninth grade is the main entry point. The others are real but narrower: in the class of 2026, 35 of the 312 seniors had come in as one-year seniors or PGs, about 11 percent of the class. To come in as a one-year senior or a PG, a student needs a transcript of mostly A's and B's in demanding courses. PG applicants also need a real record outside the classroom: athletics, the arts, leadership.

Parents regularly ask whether tenth grade is easier to get into. The claim comes back every year, and nothing supports it: Exeter publishes no admission figures by grade.

Testing: required, and it depends on the grade

Exeter is not test-optional. Every applicant submits a score, and the accepted tests change with the entry grade:

  • ninth grade: SSAT Upper Level or ISEE Upper Level;
  • tenth and eleventh grade: SSAT, ISEE, PSAT/NMSQT, SAT, PreACT or ACT;
  • one-year seniors and PGs: PSAT/NMSQT, SAT, PreACT or ACT. The SSAT and ISEE are no longer accepted at that level.

Two rules trip families up. The first is the window: the test must have been taken no earlier than one year before the January 15 deadline, so an SSAT taken "for practice" in seventh grade will not count for a ninth-grade application. The second concerns older applicants. Most guides talk only about the SSAT, but a student applying as a one-year senior or PG cannot use it at all and needs an SAT or ACT score, which is a different kind of prep.

What score is "enough"?

Two numbers circulate everywhere: an acceptance rate around 17 to 18 percent and an average SSAT in the mid-90s percentile for admitted students. Exeter itself publishes neither. And parents have argued for years over whether there is a minimum score, with answers ranging from the 85th percentile to "no floor at all".

The fair reading is that Exeter admits roughly one applicant in six, and that a strong score helps without deciding anything by itself. The test is one of seven parts of the file. A 96th percentile with lukewarm teacher recommendations will not get through. A high-80s score with an outstanding interview and recs sometimes will.

The seven parts of the file

PartWhat Exeter requires
InterviewRequired: on campus, virtual, or a recorded video answering set questions
EssaysTwo essays of 200 to 500 words
Parent statementWritten by the parents
School recommendationFrom the principal or school counselor
Teacher recommendationsCurrent English teacher and current math teacher, both required
Personal recommendationAn adult who knows the child outside school, on character; plus an optional "special interest" rec from a coach or music teacher
Transcripts and testCurrent and previous year, plus the score

Recommendations and transcripts reach Exeter only through Gateway to Prep Schools, submitted by the school. The admissions office does not accept documents sent by the family. Ask both teachers early in the fall, tell them why Exeter, and give them time: a rushed rec reads like one.

The timeline

The cycle repeats every year. For fall 2027 entry, it runs as follows:

StepDate
Candidate Profile on Gateway to Prep Schoolsfrom July
Financial aid application opensSeptember
Recommendations and transcripts open on GatewayOctober
Application and financial aid deadlineJanuary 15
Decisions releasedMarch 10

The application fee is $60. In practice the work starts well before July: test prep, choosing the recommenders, drafting and redrafting the essays. A family that starts over winter break is already behind.

Applying from outside the United States

International applicants follow the same process and pay the same fee. Three points deserve attention. Transcripts must be in English, sent by the school or by a certified translator through Gateway, so two years of reports may need translating: start in November. At a school where English is taught as a foreign language, the "English teacher" recommendation comes from that language teacher and carries the same weight as the math one. And for students who have not been taught in English for the past two years, Exeter "strongly recommends" a TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo score. It is not required; if the score is good, I would send it anyway.

What the committee is really reading

Every class at Exeter is taught around an oval table of twelve students, the Harkness method, and the file mirrors that. Two teachers describe how the student works in class, an adult describes who the child is, the interview shows whether they can carry a conversation, and two essays show how they think on paper. A brilliant student who never speaks up in class will have a hard time. A student who talks, listens and builds on what others say, with strong grades and a solid score, has a real shot. That is what preparation should aim for, from picking essay topics to how your child answers in the interview.
