For most schools, admission is the last test. At IMSA, there is one more after the offer. All admitted students, and waitpool students too, sit a placement test in late May. It does not decide admission; it decides where each student starts, and whether they need a summer bridge before joining.

What EXCEL is

EXCEL is a three-week summer programme at IMSA, required only for students selected on the basis of their placement results. It runs in July, before sophomore move-in. For a student invited, it is three weeks on campus before the school year even begins. Families should keep the July dates free until placement results are known.

Preparing without stress

The placement test comes after admission, so it is not a moment to cram. The useful preparation is consolidation: making sure Algebra 1 and the high school science course taken in 8th or 9th grade are solid, and that the student can work through problems without hesitation. A student who finishes the school year well in those subjects is already preparing.

For waitpool students

Waitpool students take the same placement test, so that if a place opens, as late as convocation at the start of the year, they can join without delay. It is worth taking the test seriously even without an offer in hand.
