Choate or Exeter: how to choose

The Andrew Mellon Library, Choate Rosemary Hall, Wallingford. Photo Daderot, Wikimedia Commons, public domain

Book a conversation

Many families apply to both Choate and Exeter. It is often a good idea: the two schools share the same January 15 deadline and release decisions on the same day, March 10. But their applications are not prepared in quite the same way.

What sets them apart

Testing. Exeter requires it: Upper Level SSAT or ISEE for grade 9. Choate has made it optional.

The interview. Exeter requires it of everyone and lets families choose the format: campus, online or video. Choate holds it online only, once.

The classroom. Exeter teaches around a table of twelve, the Harkness method, which assumes a student at ease thinking aloud. That is worth weighing for the child themselves.

Two applications, one strategy

I prepare students for Choate as for Exeter. I tell you how to run both applications side by side, with a single preparation timeline.

How to run both

An SSAT prepared for Exeter can be sent to Choate if it is good, or kept back if it is not. Recommendations go through the same platform, Gateway. The essays, however, answer different prompts, and each interview is prepared for its own format.
