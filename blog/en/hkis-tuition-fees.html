HKIS tuition and fees

HKIS is a not-for-profit school that sustains its operations through tuition. Fees are reviewed every year and billed by semester. For 2026-27, a family pays tuition according to division, a capital levy that every student pays, and, in the first year, an entry fee.

Tuition and levy, 2026-27

DivisionTuition per semesterTuition per yearCapital levy per year
Reception One to Grade 5HK$115,800HK$231,600HK$24,500
Grades 6 to 8HK$122,250HK$244,500HK$24,500
Grades 9 to 12HK$136,300HK$272,600HK$24,500

The capital levy, paid in two instalments of HK$12,250, funds long-term development: redevelopment projects, acquisitions and major facility improvements. Every student pays it.

One-time and application costs

  • Application fee: HK$2,000, payable with the application, non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Entry fee: HK$15,000, charged once to every new student; not charged to a student returning within two years.

The fee follows the place

At HKIS, the place depends on priority and on the assessment. I prepare children for the assessment that makes them qualified in the first place.

The reservation deposit

New students secure their place with a reservation deposit at acceptance, equal to the first semester's fees: tuition, capital levy and entry fee. Enrolment is final only once the full deposit is received. It is credited to the annual fees and refunded only if the school receives written notice of cancellation within ten calendar days of the payment due date. Parents remain responsible even when an employer pays. A late payment charge of 2% a month applies to outstanding balances.

What comes on top

School bus, compulsory for the Primary School, lunch, uniforms and, from Grade 6, a MacBook under the Bring Your Own MacBook programme. Experiential learning is charged separately: HK$3,000 to HK$12,500 for the Middle School PEAK programme, HK$3,000 to HK$26,000 for High School Interim. Basic supplies from Reception One to Grade 8 are included in tuition.

Debentures, and the Black Kite Program

Debentures, at HK$3 million or HK$5 million, are optional and affect priority in the wait pool, not the annual fees. For local Hong Kong families with significant financial need, the donor-funded Black Kite Program covers full tuition and fees from Grade 7.
