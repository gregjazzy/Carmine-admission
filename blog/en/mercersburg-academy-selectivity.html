Last year, nearly 800 students interviewed at Mercersburg Academy for about 150 places.

What the school looks at

Academic achievement and promise, through transcripts, tests and recommendations. And personal qualities: motivation, character, extracurricular interests and the ability to adapt to the community.

The waitlist

With more applicants than places, some are waitlisted. When a place opens, the committee chooses the student who best fits that opening, not the first in line.
