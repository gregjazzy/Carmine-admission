Concord Academy publishes two tuition figures for 2026-27, one for boarders and one for day students. The difference between them, $16,800, is the cost of living on campus; the education is the same.

The figures

2026-27Tuition
Boarding$84,410
Day$67,610
Books, studio fees and miscellaneousAbout $1,100

The enrolment or registration deposit paid on accepting a place is credited to the tuition balance rather than added to it.

Day or boarding

At many schools, the day option is a lesser version of the boarding experience. CA describes itself differently: neither a boarding school with day students nor a day school with boarders. With 60% day and 40% boarding students, residential life is something the whole school shares; day students often stay for dinner and take part in weekend activities. For families within reach of Concord, the day option offers the full school at a lower price.

The price is not the filter

Admission is decided by the file, the test and the interview. I prepare students for each, so that cost becomes the only question left.

When the price is too high

CA states that financial constraints should not deter any student from applying and meets 100% of computed need, entirely through grants. About 23% of students receive aid, with average grants of $74,907 for boarders and $47,271 for day students. Applications go through Clarity by 15 January, alongside the admission file, and aid is not available later to students not funded at entry.

Weighing boarding against day? Let's talk about what suits your child.

What the figures include

Tuition covers the academic programme, including the arts, which are part of the curriculum, and the weekly one-on-one meetings with an advisor that every student has. For aided families, grants can also help with books, travel, equipment, computers, trips, music lessons and the college application process.

What a year at CA looks like

For that price, a student chooses from more than 200 courses and over 100 clubs and affinity groups, in a school small enough for everyone to know each other. Teachers, coaches and the college counsellor get to know each student well, and students lead through student government, clubs and their own initiatives, from a sustainability campaign to a senior project.
