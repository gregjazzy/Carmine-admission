In 2026-2027, Concord Academy tuition is $84,410 boarding and $67,610 day. Books, studio fees and miscellaneous add about $1,100. The enrollment deposit is credited to tuition.

Aid

The school commits to meeting 100% of computed financial need, in grants. About 23% of students receive it.
