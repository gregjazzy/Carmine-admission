Aiglon College: where its students go after graduation

Villars-sur-Ollon, home of Aiglon. Photo Bledard92, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Boarding schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Aiglon's class of 2026 had 97 students. 60% went to study in the United States, 23% in the United Kingdom, 12% elsewhere in the world, and 5% chose a gap year. That is Aiglon's signature: an English-speaking school, oriented first towards American universities.

Recent destinations

Among the universities its students have gone to in recent years, in the United States: Stanford, Penn, Cornell, Northwestern, Georgetown, Berkeley, UCLA, NYU, Tufts, Boston University and USC. In the United Kingdom: UCL, the LSE, King's College, Edinburgh, St Andrews, Warwick and Central Saint Martins. Elsewhere: McGill, Toronto, Trinity College Dublin, IE University and Amsterdam. More than fifty universities visit the campus each year to meet students.

First Aiglon, then the best universities

I prepare your child for admission to Aiglon, and when the time comes, support them towards the most selective universities: since 2021, my students have been admitted to Columbia, Stanford, Harvard, Penn, EPFL, Bocconi, McGill, Imperial and King's College. My aim: both doors, one after the other.

Guidance that starts early

Guidance starts in Year 10: students and their parents can meet the university advisors, who help them choose IB subjects according to the universities they are aiming for. In Year 12, every student has individual meetings, and in Year 13 they finalise their list, essays and applications. The school runs an intensive SAT course on campus and organises UK admissions tests.

Is your child at Aiglon and aiming for a highly selective university? Let's talk about their plans: an outside view of their application often makes the difference.

What these destinations say

They say Aiglon prepares students well for English-speaking universities, and that its graduates are present everywhere, from major research universities to art schools. They do not say who, in a year group where several students aim for the same universities, gets the place. There, the profile, the coherence of subject choices, the essays and the letters decide, and that is often where an outside view adds the most.

How I support families

I have students at Aiglon, and every year I support around fifteen families with their admissions to the leading Swiss boarding schools and top schools. For a family aiming for Aiglon, I prepare entry: English, the interview, the application. For a student already at Aiglon, I bring an outside view of their university application: IB subject choices, the list of universities, tests, essays.
