The school records are the part of a Ransom Everglades application that families do not write and cannot polish. They come from the current school, through a Request for Student Records that the family sends from the applicant checklist to the school's registrar. What arrives describes the child's last year and a half in plain figures and comments.

I help families make sure this part arrives complete and on time. Here is what it contains.

What the registrar sends

ItemDetail
Current-year gradesThrough at least the first quarter or trimester
Last yearFinal grades
Standardized testsAny scores held by the current school
AttendanceAttendance records
ConductConduct grades or behaviour reports

All of it must be in the checklist by January 29. The school does not review an application until every required item has arrived.

Attendance and conduct count

Ransom Everglades lists attendance records and behaviour reports among the pieces of a complete file, next to grades and tests. For most students, these lines are unremarkable. Where something stands out, such as a period of absence for health or family reasons, it is better explained by the family or the school than left for the committee to interpret. A short, factual note is enough.

Is there something in your child's record you would like to explain? Tell me about it.

Making the request work

The family enters the registrar's email address in the checklist, and the request goes out automatically. Registrars handle many such requests between December and January, so sending it as soon as the application is submitted, and confirming with the school that it has arrived, avoids a file waiting for one missing transcript at the end of January. For a student whose first-term grades come late, the family should ask the school when they will be available.
