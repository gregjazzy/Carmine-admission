The ISEE for Montgomery Bell

Montgomery Bell requires the ISEE, the Independent School Entrance Exam, and describes it as a measure of each applicant's academic readiness. Registering is step four of the process, and the family registers on its own. MBA also offers a sitting on its campus on January 30.

Which level

The ISEE level is set by the grade the boy is applying to. For MBA's Junior School, grades 7 and 8, that is the Middle Level. For the High School, grade 9 and above, it is the Upper Level. The structure is the same; the content and difficulty rise with the level.

What is in it

SectionWhat it tests
Verbal ReasoningVocabulary and sentence completion
Quantitative ReasoningMathematical reasoning, often in unfamiliar formats
Reading ComprehensionPassages and questions on them
Mathematics AchievementCurriculum mathematics
EssayNot scored, but sent to the school

There is no penalty for wrong answers, so a boy should answer every question. The essay is the part most often neglected; because MBA receives it, it deserves a little preparation.

The January 30 question

Sitting the ISEE on MBA's own campus is convenient: the boy knows the place, and the family does not travel. But January 30 comes after the January 1 application deadline and only days before the February 1 deadline for supplemental materials. If the result is disappointing, there is no time for another attempt before decisions on March 6. The ISEE can be taken only once per testing season, fall, winter or spring. Families who want a margin should plan a fall sitting elsewhere and keep the January date as a second chance.

Where good students lose points

The Quantitative Reasoning section asks familiar mathematics in unfamiliar ways, and boys who are strong in class can be thrown by it. Verbal Reasoning depends on a wide vocabulary that school does not necessarily build. And timing: a boy who has never sat a two-and-a-half-hour test needs at least one full practice run to learn how to pace himself.

How the score is read

MBA does not publish an expected score. The ISEE is read beside the transcript, the teacher evaluation, the Character Skills Snapshot, the shadow visit and the interview. A strong score helps; it does not replace a good shadow day or a convincing interview.
