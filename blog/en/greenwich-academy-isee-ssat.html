The ISEE or SSAT from Group V

From Group V, fifth grade, every applicant to Greenwich Academy must take the ISEE or the SSAT. The school hosts the ISEE on its own campus on two Saturdays, November 7 and December 12, 2026, and other local independent schools offer it on weekends during the season. Scores must arrive by January 15, 2027, together with Part II of the application, which for these groups includes student essays.

I prepare older applicants for these pieces every year. Here is how they work.

The ISEE level follows the grade the student is applying to. Middle and Upper Level ISEE papers have four sections, Verbal Reasoning, Quantitative Reasoning, Reading Comprehension and Mathematics Achievement, plus an unscored essay that is sent to the school.

Timing the test

The school asks candidates to test by early January so that results arrive by January 15, and warns that dates fill quickly. The ISEE allows one sitting per season: a girl who takes it in the autumn season, for example on November 7, can sit it again in the winter season, from December, if she wants to improve. Planning a first attempt in November leaves that option open; a first attempt in January does not.

Greenwich Academy recommends the free practice resources from ISEE and Test Innovators. It also states that scores are only one piece of information, weighed with school references, report cards and interviews.

The essays

From Group V, Part II includes a few essay questions for the applicant, found in the Student Information Form on Veracross or under Student Response on SAO. They are where a girl speaks for herself in writing. Together with the ISEE or SSAT essay, they give the school two samples of her writing, one under time pressure and one prepared, and the two should sound like the same person.
