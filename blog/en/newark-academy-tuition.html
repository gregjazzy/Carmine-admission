Newark Academy is a day school, so its price covers tuition and a few required services, not boarding. The school publishes both the main figures and an estimate of costs beyond them, which makes an honest budget possible.

Costs beyond tuition

  • Textbooks and course materials: about $500 to $750 a year.
  • Athletic team gear: about $100 per sport.
  • Transportation: $6,750 to $7,825 a year, for families who use it.
  • Immersion trips: $2,400 to $4,500, a one-time expense.
  • Athletic pre-season training or travel: $500 to $2,000 per trip.

A realistic annual budget

Adding the required items to books gives a first-year figure of roughly $58,500 to $58,700. A family using the school's transportation should add $6,750 to $7,825, bringing the total close to $66,500 in the most expensive case, before any Immersion trip or pre-season travel. In later years, the $100 matriculation fee disappears, but tuition is set each year, so families should plan for it to change.

One family in five pays far less

About 20 % of students receive need-based aid, and Newark Academy meets 100 % of demonstrated need for admitted families. For them, aid also applies proportionally to lunch, technology, transport, books and trips, and comes with a discretionary allowance. There are no merit scholarships; aid is not available to international families.

What proportional aid means

For an aided family, the school applies its support across the required costs rather than to tuition alone. In practice, a family whose need covers a large share of tuition receives help in the same proportion on lunch, technology, transport, books, and the Immersion and Capstone experiences. A discretionary allowance then helps with smaller school-related expenses, so that no student is kept out of a trip or a team for lack of money.
