Le Rosey: where its students go after graduation

The Château du Rosey, Rolle. Photo Eric Bajart, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Boarding schools November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

A family considering Le Rosey is really asking one thing: where will it lead their child? The answer is more interesting than the legend. Every Le Rosey student goes on to university, and every year 10 to 15% of them are admitted to one of the eight Ivy League universities, Stanford, MIT, Oxford or Cambridge. But the map of their destinations says something other than what people imagine.

London first, New York next

Among students who enrolled between 2019 and 2025, the most frequent destinations are not Harvard or Oxford. They are King's College London, with 27 students, the École hôtelière de Lausanne, with 24, UCL and IE University in Madrid, with 22 each, NYU and Babson, with 17. Next come Northeastern, Chicago, Georgetown, USC, Tufts and Imperial College.

The most famous universities are there too, in smaller numbers: 7 students at Oxford, 7 at Penn, 6 at Columbia, 5 at the LSE, 4 at Harvard, 3 at Stanford, 2 at Cambridge, 2 at Yale. These are remarkable figures for a school of about 460 students. But they also show that Le Rosey does not get everyone into the Ivy League: it gets its best applicants in.

First Le Rosey, then the best universities

I prepare your child for Le Rosey's exams and campus day, to the level of a school that turns away excellent applicants every year; and when the time comes, I support them towards the most selective universities, in Europe and the United States. My aim: both doors, one after the other.

What the list says, and what it does not

The list counts enrolments, not admissions: a student admitted to several universities appears only once. It does not say how many students aimed for Harvard and enrolled elsewhere, or what made the difference for those who got in. Le Rosey has a guidance office of four counsellors who support each student individually, and a dedicated programme from classes 3 and 2. It is a real asset. But in a year group where several students aim for the same universities, it is the profile, the essays and the coherence of the application that decide.

Is your child already at Le Rosey and aiming for a highly selective university? Let's talk about their plans: an outside view of their application often makes the difference.

The French bac, an unexpected passport

One figure sums up the school's openness: about two thirds of its French bac graduates go to the United States or the United Kingdom. At Le Rosey, the diploma does not decide the country; the student's plans do, and how their application is presented.

How I support families

Fifteen of my students have been admitted to Le Rosey, and every year I support around fifteen families towards the most selective schools and boarding schools. For a family aiming for Le Rosey, I prepare entry: an assessment in maths, English and French at the level of the school's exams, then the tests, the interview and the campus day. For a student already at Le Rosey, I bring an outside view of their university application: the list of universities, tests, essays and letters. Since 2021, my students have been admitted to Columbia, Stanford, Harvard, Penn, EPFL, Bocconi, McGill, Imperial and King's College.
