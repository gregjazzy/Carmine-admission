Swiss boarding schools

Lyceum Alpinum Zuoz summer camps in the Engadine

For more than thirty years, Lyceum Alpinum has opened its campus in Zuoz, in the Upper Engadine, to children from around the world in summer. For many families it is a language holiday in the mountains. For some, it is also a first encounter with a Swiss boarding school.

Three camps, three ages

The summer camp, residential, takes 10 to 17 year-olds for two or four weeks. In the morning, 29 language lessons over the stay, German, English or French, in classes of 15 at most after an online placement test, or digital workshops in AI, programming and robotics. In the afternoon, each participant chooses a programme: film acting with a former student turned Hollywood actor, football coached by professionals, golf three hours a day in groups of ten at most with professionals from Golf Engadin St. Moritz, or mixed sports. At weekends, rafting on the Inn, a rope park, hikes.

The junior day camp, for 6 to 9 year-olds, has no overnight stay: English or German in the morning, activities in the afternoon, bus pick-up, from 8:30 to 4 pm. The new wilderness camp brings together twenty participants per session, ten girls and ten boys, to learn to bivouac, navigate and plan an expedition; in 2027, from 4 to 16 July and 18 to 30 July.

From camp to boarding school

A summer in Zuoz says a lot about a child's ability to board. I know the leading Swiss boarding schools from the inside, and I prepare applications to the standard of the most demanding.

What is included

The summer camp fee is all-inclusive: lessons, eight additional hours of study, round-the-clock supervision, single or double room, full board, laundry, three to four hours of activities a day, evenings, weekends, and transfer from Zurich airport. No deposit is required: the full invoice follows acceptance of the registration, payable within fifteen days. A four-week stay carries a supplement for the weekend between the two camps, and golf equipment hire is charged separately.

A summer to test boarding

Every year I support around fifteen families towards the most selective boarding schools and schools, in Switzerland and France, and fifteen of my students have been admitted to Le Rosey alone. Many families hesitate about boarding itself more than about the school. Two or four weeks in Zuoz answer that better than a visit: the child lives with separation, group life, other people's languages. I advise treating the camp as a trial, then choosing year-round boarding with full knowledge.
