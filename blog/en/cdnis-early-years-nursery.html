Nursery is one of the two main entry points to CDNIS, the other being Grade 6. It welcomes children from the age of two at the school's Early Years Centre, in a half-day bilingual programme in English and Mandarin. For many Hong Kong families, it is the first school application they will ever make.

At these ages, placement is determined primarily by age.

Applying for Nursery

Nursery applications for 2027/2028 are accepted from 1 to 30 November 2026, with a single HK$1,800 application and assessment fee. The file includes the birth certificate, passport, Hong Kong visa or identity card if applicable, a photo, and any playgroup report or attendance record. Reports are not compulsory at this age, but because of the volume of applications, assessments are scheduled first for children who meet one or more priority criteria: debenture nomination, a sibling at CDNIS, Canadian citizenship, a parent who is an alumnus or staff member, or a child waitlisted after passing the previous year.

The assessment at two

Nursery assessments take place in January and February at the Early Years Centre for families in Hong Kong, or by video for families abroad. They last 45 to 60 minutes: a play-based activity observing cognitive and motor development and interaction with parents, group activities, and a first chance of simple conversation in English or Mandarin. There is nothing to rehearse; a rested, fed child who is used to playing near other children is well prepared.

No automatic step to Early Years 1

A place in Nursery does not guarantee a place in Early Years 1. Every Nursery child must pass an internal assessment to move up, for which a fee is charged. Families should treat Nursery as a first stage, not a promise of the whole school career.

Costs at this age

Nursery tuition for 2026/27 is HK$142,780, paid in eleven monthly instalments, with the place secured by the first instalment. Nursery children pay no capital levy and need no debenture. From Early Years 1, tuition rises to HK$161,000 and the entry fee and capital levy, or a debenture, apply.

Early Years 1 to Preparatory

Applications for these years close much earlier, on 2 October, with assessments in November and December. Late applications are not considered.
