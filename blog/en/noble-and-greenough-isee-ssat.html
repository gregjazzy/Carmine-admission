Nobles requires a test, depending on grade, taken in the fall or winter of the application year.

What to know

Grades 7 and 8: the ISEE; a student repeating 8th grade may take the SSAT. Grades 9 to 11: the SSAT; for grades 10 and 11, the PSAT is accepted instead. Results come directly from the testing agency.

What the school says

Every file is read in full, whatever the score. Nobles receives many applicants with very high scores, but each class includes students with a range of scores.

A score at the right level

I prepare your child for the ISEE or SSAT, so the score does not hold back a strong file.
