For many families, HKIS is above all a route to universities in the United States and around the world. Its High School, on the Tai Tam campus at 1 Red Hill Road, has more than 800 students in Grades 9 to 12, and is built on an American model of course choice, rigorous electives and close counseling.

The academic model

  • Choice: a wide range of standards-based courses in humanities, arts, mathematics, science, physical and health education, technology and world languages, in person and online, so students can build a programme around their interests.
  • Advanced Placement: college-level AP courses, open to any student motivated to take on the challenge, alongside honors courses and independent study.
  • The day: four 80-minute periods, eight blocks rotating over two days, a later start at 8:35 and a common lunch break.
  • Interim: a weeklong off-campus learning experience, built around creativity, collaboration and resilience, at an extra cost of HK$3,000 to HK$26,000.

Counseling for four years

The High School has eight counselors who work in pairs, and each student and family stays with the same pair through all four years. Counselors cover academic, career and personal development, and are members of the National Association for College Admission Counseling, the International Association for College Admission Counseling and the International School Counseling Association. HKIS welcomes hundreds of universities to campus each year.

Joining in the High School

Applications for Grades 9 to 12 close on 1 November for the following August. Placement is by grade completed, and a student entering High School must provide records of all previous high school coursework, because graduation credit cannot be given without a full history. Applicants from Grade 3 upward take a computer-adaptive assessment in math, reading, writing and oral language, and the committee weighs English, academic achievement, personal characteristics, fit with the school's Student Learning Results and success in similar schools.

Support

Some students receive learning support in co-taught classes, and those with more significant needs may take a Learning Strategies class instead of an elective. Office hours with teachers before and after school are open to all.

What it costs

High School tuition for 2026-27 is HK$136,300 per semester, HK$272,600 a year, plus the annual capital levy of HK$24,500, a one-time HK$15,000 entry fee for new students, a MacBook and the Interim programme.
