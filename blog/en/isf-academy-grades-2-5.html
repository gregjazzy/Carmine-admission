From Grade 2, entry to The ISF Academy becomes a written examination as well as a conversation. Applicants sit papers in both languages and in mathematics taught in both, then orals in English and Putonghua, then their parents meet the school. Each stage is a filter: all applicants sit Stage 1A, and only selected ones go on.

I prepare children for these assessments every year. Here is how they work for 2027-28.

Three stages

StageContentDate
1AWritten papers in Chinese, Chinese mathematics, English and English mathematics; non-verbal reasoning; a computerized assessmentSunday, February 14, 2027
1BIndividual and group interviews and activities in English and PutonghuaMarch 10 to 12, 2027
2Parents meet a senior member of the schoolMarch to April 2027

Applications close on January 25, 2027, and families are notified by April 23. Applicants must be in Hong Kong for Stage 1A; the school offers at most one alternative date, and absence without one closes the application without refund.

Mathematics in two languages

One detail deserves attention: mathematics is assessed in both Chinese and English, in separate papers. This mirrors the school, where between Grades 2 and 5 teaching shifts from 70 percent Putonghua to an even split. A child strong in mathematics but taught it only in English can lose marks on the Chinese paper simply because the vocabulary of the problems is unfamiliar. The fix is specific and learnable: mathematical language in Chinese, from operations to word problems.

Has your child learned maths in only one of the two languages? Tell me about them.

Orals and non-verbal reasoning

Stage 1B puts the child in individual and group settings in both languages. The school's general criteria name willingness to communicate, curiosity and teamwork; in a group interview, these show in how a child takes part, listens and answers in the language being used. The non-verbal reasoning paper and computerized assessment in Stage 1A sit apart from language and test reasoning directly, which helps children whose languages are still developing to show their ability.
