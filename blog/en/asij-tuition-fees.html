ASIJ charges tuition in four bands and adds several fees that families need to count from the start: two one-time payments on entry, an annual capital assessment and, for most Chofu families, the bus. The school is also clear that it offers no scholarships or financial aid to new applicants, so the full cost is the cost.

The first year

In the year of entry, the one-time fees add ¥1,825,000 to tuition and the capital assessment. A Kindergarten child joining in August 2027 therefore costs at least ¥5,573,000 in the first year, and a High School student ¥5,901,000, before the bus, meals, trips and devices. Students in Grades 4 and 5 need an iPad, and from Grade 6 a MacBook. Most curricular and co-curricular trips are paid separately, except the Grade 5 trip and some Middle and High School programs.

Paying

Fees are invoiced and paid in yen by bank transfer. Families paying from their own funds can ask the accounting office about instalments, with a carrying charge; companies cannot. Employers who cover education costs are invited to join the Corporate Contribution Program, which the school presents as a potentially more cost-effective route than paying fees and the Japanese income tax on that benefit; families in that program also receive consideration in admissions. For a withdrawal, written notice by the first day of a semester allows a refund of tuition and bus fees for full quarters not attended.
