Newark Academy attracts families relocating to northern New Jersey from other countries. The school treats them like any other applicant, with a few practical rules that follow from being a day school.

What is the same

International applicants submit the same Online Admission Form and the same materials: transcripts, teacher recommendations, and an SSAT or ISEE score, which is required for every applicant to grades 6 to 11. The deadlines are the same too: the form by 1 December, everything else by 11 January, decisions on 19 February.

Timing a relocation

Newark Academy does not admit students mid-year. A family moving in the spring or summer should apply in the autumn before, for September entry. The test can be scheduled ahead and the conversation held by video, so the whole application can be completed before the move, provided the family can show that the child will live with them in New Jersey. The school also lets prospective families message current parents through its website, a useful way to ask about settling in.

English and the curriculum

Because there is no ESL support, a student must be able to follow every class in English from the first day. In 11th and 12th grade, the IB programme expects a native language and a second language, among other subjects; families from a non-English background should look at how the school's language offer fits their child.
