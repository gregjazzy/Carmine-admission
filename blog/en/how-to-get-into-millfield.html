How to get into Millfield

The entrance to Millfield, Street, Somerset. Photo Colin Smith, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

With some 1,270 pupils, Millfield is the largest co-educational boarding school for 13 to 18 year olds in the United Kingdom, on more than 400 acres of Somerset countryside. Its size means many doors in. It also means a process that is simple to describe and harder to get right than it looks.

The steps, for every entry

  1. Registration. The form and fee are a firm expression of interest and the first step towards a place.
  2. Reference. Millfield then writes to the candidate's current school.
  3. Interview and assessment. About 30 minutes with a senior member of staff, on the candidate's academic interests, activities and experiences, plus a computer-based verbal and non-verbal reasoning test of just under an hour. Both can be done remotely.
  4. Offer. Usually about a week after the interview; two to three weeks for scholarship candidates.
  5. Joining. In the term before entry, families receive joining information and the name of their child's house. New pupils arrive on the Sunday before term.

The interview is where it is won

I prepare children for the Millfield interview, for UKiset when it is required, and for the scholarship assessments, at the level the school expects.

"Academically selective", Millfield-style

Millfield selects on academic merit, through tests, interview and references. But it also looks for pupils who will benefit from a broad education and contribute to the life of the school. With thirty sports and two hundred sports staff, art, music, drama, dance and an equestrian centre, the interview is where a candidate's interests become part of the file. A strong reasoning score with nothing to say about life beyond lessons is a weaker application than it seems.

Families abroad

About a fifth of Millfield pupils are international, from more than seventy countries, and another fifth are British families living overseas. Candidates outside the UK may be asked to sit UKiset before the interview, and those who cannot visit take it in place of the on-site test.

A note on timing

For Year 9 entry, Millfield aims to confirm places about eighteen months ahead, which means assessments in Year 7 for most prep school pupils. Scholarship applications for September 2027 entry close on 1 December 2026.
