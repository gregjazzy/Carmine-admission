Ransom Everglades requires three recommendations from every applicant, and it names exactly who should write them. The first comes from the current English teacher, the second from the current mathematics teacher, and the third from someone who sees the student across the school.

I help families organise these requests every year. Here is how they work.

Who writes

RecommenderWhat they can speak to
Current English teacherReading, writing, participation in discussion
Current mathematics teacherReasoning, persistence with problems, working method
Principal, counselor or school administratorThe student in the wider school: conduct, involvement, relationships

Families enter each recommender's email address in the applicant checklist, and the request is sent online. All three must be in by January 29, when the checklist must be complete. The request for school records goes to the same school through the same checklist, so the family can organise both with the school at once.

Current teachers, not favourite ones

The school asks for current teachers, those teaching the student this year. A teacher from last year who knew the child well may seem a better choice, but the requirement is clear, and a current teacher's view of the student now is what the committee wants. For a student who joined a new school in the autumn, the teachers may have known them only a few months; giving them a short note on the application and the student's interests helps them write something specific.

Not sure who should write the third recommendation? Tell me about your child's school.

How they are read

The admission office describes recommendations as one of the main elements of its evaluation, alongside tests, records and the interview, and states that good grades and high scores alone do not guarantee admission. Recommendations are often where the personal qualities the school looks for become visible: how a student treats classmates, how they respond to a setback, whether they ask questions. A recommendation that matches what the student shows in the interview and the Snapshot strengthens the whole file.
