Milton: optional testing, should you send the SSAT?

Boston from the Charles River, a few miles from Milton Academy. Photo King of Hearts, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

US boarding schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

At Milton, testing is optional, and the school says so clearly: an applicant unable to submit scores is not disadvantaged. I know the school through my students at Milton. For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year with applications to leading schools in New England.

What the school says

Applicants may submit an SSAT or ISEE; for grade 11, also a PSAT, SAT or ACT. Testing should be completed during the current school year, before January 15. For non-native English speakers, or students without at least two years in English-medium schooling, the school strongly suggests submitting an English test.

Prepare, then decide

I prepare your child for the SSAT or ISEE, then help you decide, results in hand, whether to send it to Milton.

What the school does not say

"Optional" does not mean "without effect". A good score confirms reports that are hard to compare, those of a school abroad especially; an average score can weaken a strong file. It is all in the choice.

How to decide

Does the score confirm the reports?

That is the only real question. If it confirms or strengthens them, send it; otherwise, keep it.

Has your child never taken the SSAT? Tell me about them: a practice test already says a lot.

The English test

For a non-native speaker, the English test often matters more than the SSAT. It can be prepared.
