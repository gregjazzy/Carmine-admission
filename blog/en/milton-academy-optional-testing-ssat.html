Milton Academy does not require entrance tests. Every candidate is welcome to send SSAT or ISEE results, and students who are unable to submit scores are not disadvantaged. That leaves a real choice for each family, and the choice deserves more thought than a reflex to send or to hold back.

Any testing should be done during the current academic year and before the 15 January deadline.

When sending a score helps

  • The score is clearly strong and confirms what the grades and teachers say.
  • The student comes from a school whose grading Milton may know less well, abroad or in a small school, and the score gives a common reference point.
  • The student's grades are uneven for a reason that has passed, and a recent strong score shows the level today.

When holding back is wiser

  • The score is noticeably below what the grades and recommendations suggest. Without it, the file rests on the stronger evidence.
  • The student had little time to prepare and the result does not reflect their ability.

The English tests are different

For a student whose first language is not English, or who has spent less than two years in an English-medium school, Milton strongly suggests TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo. This is not the same choice as the SSAT: the school needs to know the student can learn, write and live in English from the first day. A family in this situation should plan to send one of these tests and prepare for it seriously.

Preparing without losing the year

The SSAT and ISEE reward familiarity with their formats and timing, as well as solid reading and maths. A few months of regular, targeted work in the autumn is enough for most strong students. What should not happen is test preparation crowding out the fall grades, which Milton reads in every file.

How I advise families

I have spent seven years working with around fifteen families a year aiming at very selective schools. For Milton, I usually recommend a diagnostic test early in the autumn, then a decision: prepare and send, or skip testing and put the time into the interview and profile.
