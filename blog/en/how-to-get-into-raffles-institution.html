How to get into Raffles Institution

Raffles Institution campus, Singapore. Photo Lim Toh Han, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Singapore schools November 2026 6 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Raffles Institution offers a six-year Integrated Programme that takes students from Year 1 to the end of junior college without a national examination in between. Years 1 to 4 are for boys; Years 5 and 6 are co-educational. Admission is on merit, through exercises that are mostly run by Singapore's Ministry of Education, with a few the school runs itself.

A six-year commitment

The Ministry of Education is explicit: students in an Integrated Programme are expected to stay for its full duration, transfers are strongly discouraged, and transfers to other IP schools are not allowed except in exceptional circumstances. Students admitted through DSA must also take part in the activities of their talent area. Choosing Raffles is therefore a decision about six years, not one.

From outside Singapore's schools

International students who wish to enter Singapore's mainstream schools first contact the Ministry of Education and take part in the AEIS, a centralised admission test held around September. Singaporean citizens and permanent residents returning from abroad go through the School Placement Exercise for Returning Singaporeans: SPERS-Sec for Years 1 to 3, SPERS-JC/MI for Year 5.

Cost

Raffles is an independent school with fees set each year; those for 2027 are published by 15 December 2026. Singaporean families can draw on the Independent School Bursary, Edusave scholarships, the UPLIFT Scholarship and the school's own Raffles Scholarship.
