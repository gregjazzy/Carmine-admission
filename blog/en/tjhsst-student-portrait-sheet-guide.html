The Student Portrait Sheet is TJ's second written assessment, taken on the same day as the problem-solving essay. Students show the qualities Fairfax County expects of its graduates.

The five attributes

  • Collaborator
  • Communicator
  • Creative and critical thinker
  • Ethical and global citizen
  • Goal-directed and resilient individual

The school specifies it evaluates these broad skills, not each detailed bullet of the framework.

Preparing the portrait

I prepare your child to show these qualities through precise examples from their life, in clear English.

What makes the difference

Concrete examples rather than claims: a group project, a difficulty overcome, an idea defended. A student who writes "I am resilient" without showing it does not convince.

Can your child tell an experience that shows who they are? Tell me about them.
