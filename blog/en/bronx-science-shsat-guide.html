The SHSAT for Bronx Science

For students applying to Bronx Science in fall 2026, the SHSAT is not quite the test their older siblings took. It is now sat on a computer and adapts to each student. The content and the purpose stay the same: one test, two subjects, one score that decides everything.

The test, from the student's chair

English Language ArtsMathematics
Questions5050
ContentRevising and editing; reading comprehensionRatios, numbers and expressions, equations and inequalities, statistics and probability, geometry
FormatMultiple choice and other question types, computer-adaptive
Time180 minutes in total; the student chooses which section to begin with

What "adaptive" changes

Questions adjust to the student's answers. A student must answer each question before moving on, so there is no skipping ahead and returning at the end. The one exception is in English: within a reading passage set, a student can go back to earlier questions in that set until it is submitted. After that, the set is closed.

In practice, this rewards steady accuracy from the first question, and a clear plan for time across 180 minutes.

How the score is built

Each section produces a raw performance score, converted into a standardised score; the two standardised scores are added to form the composite. Offers are made on that composite. A student cannot compensate a very weak section by being only slightly better in the other: both count equally.

Which section is your child's weaker one? Let's find out with a diagnostic.

On the day

Students use Department of Education computers. Students from districts 7 to 12 who test at a central site do so at Bronx Science itself. Accommodations such as extended time are available to eligible students with IEPs, 504 Plans or English learner status, with documentation in advance.

Official preparation

The NYC SHSAT Portal offers a Student Readiness Tool and practice tests in the new format, together with the official guide for students entering high school in fall 2027. These should be the starting point for every family.
