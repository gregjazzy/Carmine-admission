Blair Academy's testing policy sits between required and optional: every candidate is asked to take the appropriate standardised tests, but submission is not compulsory. The school's own words set the tone: test scores give important information, but "they do not tell the whole story".

Which test at which grade

Applying forAccepted tests
Grade 9SSAT or ISEE, and/or state, county or district tests from earlier grades
Grade 10SSAT or ISEE, and/or state, county or district tests
Grade 11SSAT, PSAT, SAT, ACT or district tests
Grade 12 or postgraduatePSAT, SAT, ACT or district tests

Blair prefers tests taken within one year of the application deadline of 15 January.

Should you send a score?

Because testing is preferred, a good score is a useful addition to a file, giving the committee a common reference alongside grades from very different schools. A score that clearly undersells a student is a different matter; since Blair looks thoroughly at every aspect of the application, a strong transcript, good recommendations and a convincing interview can carry the file. The decision is best made after one diagnostic test, not before.

International applicants

International candidates are asked for the SSAT too. Those whose first language is not English, and who have not studied for two years in an English-speaking school, must show their English level through the TOEFL, the TOEFL Essentials, the IELTS or the Duolingo English Test. Blair's TOEFL code is 9415. The requirement is waived after two years at an international school or a US junior boarding school. Blair has no ESL programme.

Scores and the paper application

Families who choose the printable application instead of the online platforms must still include test results with the rest of the file, mailed to the admission office in Blairstown and postmarked by 15 January. Either way, a score that arrives after the deadline weakens a file that is otherwise complete.

Timing

A first SSAT or ISEE in the autumn of 8th grade leaves room for another attempt before 15 January. International families should also plan the English test early, since an on-campus interview can only be booked once the application and test scores have been received.
