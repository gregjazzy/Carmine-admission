How to get into Aditya Birla World Academy

Aditya Birla World Academy, ABWA, was established in 2008 under the Aditya Birla Education Trust and has become one of Mumbai's leading international schools. It combines Cambridge programmes, from primary to IGCSE, with the IB Diploma and A Levels in the last two years, and places equal weight on character, emotional intelligence and values.

I have students at ABWA. Admission works as follows.

Three entry points

EntryProgramme that follows
NurseryA Montessori and Reggio Emilia approach, then Cambridge Primary
Grade 8The last year of Middle School, then Cambridge IGCSE in Grades 9 and 10
Grade 11IB Diploma Programme or A Levels, for two years
Other gradesOnly when a chance vacancy arises

There are no mid-year admissions: the cycle is completed before the school year begins. The school's bus routes cover much of South and Central Mumbai, from Colaba, Malabar Hill and Breach Candy to Worli, Dadar and Sion.

Six stages

  1. Enquiry: the online enquiry form.
  2. Application and documents: eligible applicants are invited to complete the application.
  3. Assessment and interaction: depending on the grade, an entrance test, a parent group discussion and/or an interaction with the child.
  4. Family meeting: for shortlisted families, to understand the child's environment, values and aspirations.
  5. Decision: communicated after evaluation.
  6. Fee payment: admission is confirmed once fees are paid within the period given.

What the school looks for

ABWA describes the students it hopes to shape: curious, persevering, adaptable, compassionate, and leaders with integrity. Admission depends on seat availability and on meeting the eligibility criteria, and the school reserves the final decision.

When it happens

The admission process usually begins at the end of May or the beginning of June for the following academic year. For 2027-28, it has concluded, as have the sports scholarships for Grades 8 and 11. Families aiming at 2028-29 should prepare over the coming school year.

How I prepare

I have students at ABWA, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools in India and abroad. For ABWA, I start with an assessment in mathematics and English, then prepare the entrance test and the interaction.
