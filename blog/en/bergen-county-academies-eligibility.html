Who can apply to BCA

BCA is a county public school whose tuition is paid by each student's home district, and its eligibility rules follow from that. They are strict, and an application that does not meet them is rejected before any test or essay is read.

The conditions

ConditionRule
ResidencePrimary residence in Bergen County by 1 September 2026
DistrictIf accepted, registered in the school district of the town of residence
Grade8th grade only, for entry into 9th grade
AttemptsOne, ever; no reapplication the following year
TransfersNone into BCA in later grades
ApplicationsOne per student across the county's technical schools district

Families living outside Bergen County cannot apply, even if the student attends a school in the county.

Proving residence

Proof must be a current electric, gas or water bill, from September to November, in the name of a parent or guardian. Driver's licences, property tax bills, mortgage statements, phone, cable and credit card bills are not accepted. A lease is accepted only if it states that the landlord pays the utilities. If the bill is in another name, the family uploads a note explaining why.

Eligible, then competitive

Once eligibility is settled, the work is the test, the essay and the interview. I prepare Bergen County 7th and 8th graders for all three.

Students outside the public system

  • Private, parochial and charter schools: eligible. The family completes a form signed by the local Board of Education confirming registration in the town of residence; a student at a private school outside the county must prove residence before the test.
  • Homeschooled students: considered in the same way, with standardised test scores for each middle school year, recommendations from instructors who are not family members, and the entrance test.

A cap per district

BCA limits the number of students it accepts from each school district, based on the enrollment of the local high school. Eligibility therefore opens the door, but the competition is partly local.
