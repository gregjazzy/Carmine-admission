Franklin school fees: the cost, year group by year group

Franklin is a private school under contract with the French state: teachers are paid by the Ministry of Education, and what families pay is not a "fee" in the sense of an international school. It is a contribution, which funds the buildings, equipment, the school's distinctive character and a few services. It is modest compared with the school's reputation, which is one reason places there are so fiercely contested.

The family contribution, at full rate

In 2025-2026, the full-rate annual contribution was:

  • €2,435 in nursery and from CP to CE2 (ages 6 to 8);
  • €2,815 in CM1 and CM2 (ages 9 and 10);
  • €3,243 in sixième and cinquième (ages 11 and 12);
  • €3,028 in quatrième and troisième (ages 13 and 14);
  • €3,153 in lycée (ages 15 to 17);
  • €3,285 in the prépa.

It includes additional language teaching, insurance, fixed catering costs and compulsory subscriptions. Families who wish can choose a "solidarity" rate, 20% above the full rate, the difference being a donation that funds the schooling of less well-off families.

A place at Franklin

At this price, Franklin is one of the most sought-after schools in Paris, and it says nothing about its criteria. I know them through my students: that is what makes it possible to present an application of excellence, and a child ready for the appointment.

A contribution adjusted to income

Since 2025-2026, the contribution has been adjusted to the family quotient, thanks to the JES-Franklin Foundation: reductions of 40% to 90% for families whose quotient is below €17,500. On top of that, there is an automatic 50% reduction from the third child at the school, 75% for the fourth and 90% beyond. The stated principle is clear: no student should be prevented from attending Franklin for financial reasons.

What comes on top

Lunches are billed separately: in 2025-2026, from €257 a year for one lunch a week to about €1,400 for five lunches in collège and seconde. Add trips and outings, supervised study if chosen, flat-rate supplies in primary, and a €300 deposit on registration, later deducted from the first invoice. All told, a year in collège with lunches comes to around €4,500 at full rate.

The budget is not the obstacle, the place is. Tell me about your child: I will tell you at which level they stand the best chance.

What the price does not tell you

I have students at Franklin, as at the other leading Paris schools, and every year I support around fifteen families with their admissions. At this level of contribution, budget never separates the families who get in from the others: what does is the school reports read by the director or the préfet, the family's fit with the Jesuit project, and the moment the application arrives, since for sixième the window lasts only two weeks. That is what I work on with families, to the same standard as for a Swiss boarding school.

Between leading Catholic state-contracted schools, price almost never decides. The real choice is about the project and the child: see Franklin, Stanislas or Saint-Jean-de-Passy.

Fees change every year. The amounts above are those for 2025-2026; the school sets its contribution each year and publishes its financial regulations for the current year.
