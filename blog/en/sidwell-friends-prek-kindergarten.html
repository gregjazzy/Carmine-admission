The Lower School of Sidwell Friends, on a five-acre campus in Bethesda near the Metro, takes its youngest students into PreKindergarten and Kindergarten. Admission at these ages rests on four pieces: the Family Information Form, a group visit for the child, a confidential form from the current teacher or caregiver, and a meeting with the parents. There is no test.

The steps and their dates

The Family Information Form is due by December 1. By December 15, families confirm the date of the child's group visit and request the confidential recommendation from the current teacher or caregiver; forms received before November 1 are not accepted, because the school wants someone who has known the child for part of the year. For Kindergarten, families also provide the child's current progress report or report card. The parent meeting, in person or by video, is scheduled in the same period.

Preparing a young child

A group visit is not something to rehearse. What helps is a child used to being with others, comfortable for a while without their parents, and told simply what will happen. The parent meeting matters as much: it is a conversation about the program and the fit, not an evaluation of the child, and families who come with their real questions get the most from it.

Sidwell Friends is a Quaker school, and the youngest children already take part in its community life, from weekly Meeting for Worship in each division to a PreK play titled "Peace".
