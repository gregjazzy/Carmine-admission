Blair Academy's calendar is built around one firm date, 15 January, by which the whole application, including the interview, must be complete. Everything else falls before or after it.

The 2027-2028 calendar

DateStep
1 August 2026Applications open for 2027-2028, through SAO or Gateway
3 September 2026On-campus tours and interviews begin
1 November 2026From this date, teachers can best complete the online recommendations
15 January 2027Complete application due: profile, interview, questionnaire, transcript, English and math recommendations
1 February 2027Clarity financial aid application due
10 March 2027Admission decisions by email

After 15 January, Blair continues to accept applications on a rolling basis, but interviews can no longer be scheduled, and aid funds beyond the first round are not reserved.

What to do, and when

  • Before September: submit the online inquiry; consider the Fall Preview in mid-September or a summer tour.
  • September to November: book and attend the interview; take the SSAT or ISEE if testing will be submitted.
  • November: ask the English and math teachers for recommendations; request transcripts including first-quarter grades.
  • December to mid-January: complete the questionnaire and check every piece has arrived.

Why the interview date matters most

Interview times are limited and the schedule fills quickly. A family that waits until December risks finding no on-campus slot before 15 January; international families must also have submitted part of the application and test scores before they can book. Booking in September or October is the safest course.

Applying after 15 January

Blair keeps accepting application materials after the deadline and reviews late files, but it can no longer schedule interviews once 15 January has passed. If the committee needs more information, possibly including an interview, the admission office contacts the family. A late application is therefore possible, but it starts without the element Blair values most.
