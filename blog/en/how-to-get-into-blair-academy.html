How to get into Blair Academy

Blair Academy, Blairstown, New Jersey. Photo Mcollins109, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Book a conversation

Blair Academy, a co-ed boarding school in Blairstown, New Jersey, founded in 1848, has 484 students, 85% of them boarders. Its acceptance rate is 15%.

The file

An application through the Standard Application Online or Gateway to Prep Schools, either one. An interview, required. Recommendations from the English and maths teachers. Transcripts for the current year and the two before. Testing is encouraged but optional.

The calendar

Applications open August 1, 2026, a complete file by January 15, 2027, decisions by email on March 10.
