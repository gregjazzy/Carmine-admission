CDNIS does not run one deadline for everyone. Each section of the school has its own window, and the earliest, for Early Years 1 to Grade 5, falls early in the school year, on 2 October. Families who think of applying "in the autumn" for a young child can find the door already closed.

What "applied" means

An application is complete only when every required document is uploaded and the application fee is paid. The school will not process an incomplete file. For the younger grades, school reports for the previous and current years, translated into English where needed, are part of that file, along with the Student Reference Form sent directly by the current teacher.

Nursery and Early Years 1: priority for assessment

Because of the volume of applications, Nursery and Early Years 1 assessments are scheduled first for applicants who meet one or more criteria: a debenture nomination or potential debenture, a sibling at CDNIS, Canadian citizenship, a parent who is an alumnus or staff member, or a child who passed the assessment the previous year but was waitlisted. Other applicants come after them. Applications after the deadline are not considered.

Planning backwards

  • Early Years 1 to Grade 5: gather reports and translations over the summer, and submit in September.
  • Nursery: prepare documents in October, submit in early November.
  • Grades 6 to 11: submit in the autumn, so that the questionnaire and reference form are ready before the winter holidays.

Main campus tours run on Tuesdays during term, and Early Years Centre tours on Saturdays from September to November: visiting before the deadline is possible only if planned early.

If the deadline has passed

For Grade 6 and above, a late application may still be considered if places remain. For younger grades, the realistic option is the following year's cycle, as applications and wait pool status never carry over.
