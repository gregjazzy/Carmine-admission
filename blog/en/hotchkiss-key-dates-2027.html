Hotchkiss key dates for fall 2027 admission

The main building of The Hotchkiss School, Lakeville, Connecticut. Photo Adrien Delessert, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.5

US boarding schools November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

The Hotchkiss calendar is short and front-loaded. Almost everything happens between September and mid-January of the year before entry, and the only fixed deadline, January 15, covers both the application and financial aid. Students applying for fall 2027 should be working backward from that date by early fall 2026.

The dates that cannot slip

January 15 is the one that matters. After it, Hotchkiss describes the process as much more competitive, on-campus interviews stop for the winter, and financial aid funds are not reserved. Applications may still be considered through the summer if space allows, but a family relying on that is gambling.

The dates best met early

Recommendations by November 1, because teachers write better letters before the end-of-semester rush. Tests in November or December, because a January score delays the file. Interviews in October or November, because Saturday slots fill first and the conversation then feeds the essays.

After March 10

Admitted students can return in the spring for a day-long visit program; Hotchkiss does not allow prospective students to sit in on classes or stay overnight before then, given the number of visitors. The reply is due April 10. Students weighing several offers should use the visit day to ask the questions that will decide between schools.

A realistic plan for a ninth-grade applicant

June to August: complete the inquiry form, register for an October or November SSAT or ISEE, and, for non-native speakers, book the TOEFL, Duolingo or IELTS. September: book the interview, ideally an October Saturday on campus, and submit the Gateway Candidate Profile beforehand. October: interview, then start the essays while the conversation is fresh; send recommendation requests before November 1. November: first test sitting; essay drafts. December: second test sitting if needed; final essays; financial aid documents gathered. Early January: submit everything, well before January 15, so that a missing recommendation can still be chased.

Families applying to several boarding schools should put every deadline in a single calendar, with one testing schedule and one round of interviews in the fall. That is far less stressful than treating each school separately, and it lets the same test scores and recommendations serve all of them.
