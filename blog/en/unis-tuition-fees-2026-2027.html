UNIS tuition and fees 2026-2027: the real cost

UNIS publishes its full fee grid, and it is easy to read: tuition rises with the division, from $46,856 in Pre-K to $52,167 in the final year of Tutorial House for 2026-2027. What families underestimate is the first year, when several one-time charges are added, and the way the money is collected.

What is added on enrolment

When a place is accepted, UNIS asks for $5,050: a $250 admissions fee, a $1,000 tuition pre-payment, a $300 security deposit refunded on graduation or withdrawal, and a one-time New Parent Levy of $3,500 per family. Only the levy and the admissions fee are pure additional cost; the rest is either credited to tuition or returned. On top of that come, every year, a capital surcharge of $1,400 per child and $200 per family for the Parents' Association. From 5th grade, a one-time $250 deposit covers the school device and is refunded when it comes back in good condition.

The first year, in one figure

Take a child entering 9th grade in September 2027 at this year's rates. Tuition of $50,911, the $3,750 of new-family charges, the $1,400 capital surcharge and the $200 Parents' Association: about $56,300 of real cost, before lunches, after-school programs, private music lessons, textbooks and trips, which are billed separately. Add the $1,300 pre-payment and deposit that are advanced and later credited or refunded, and the device deposit. Fees are set each year, so budget for an increase.

A second child does not pay the New Parent Levy again; the capital surcharge, on the other hand, is per child.

How the money is collected

The standard schedule is 70 percent of tuition by August 1 and 30 percent by January 1, after an initial payment in the spring. Families who prefer monthly payments can use the FACTS plan: ten interest-free instalments starting on May 1, before the school year. For a family relocating to New York in the summer, the August payment usually arrives at the same moment as the deposit on an apartment, and it is worth setting up FACTS before the move rather than after.

Financial aid, and what it never does

UNIS gives need-based financial aid and states that a family's aid status never influences the admission decision. Applications run through SSS and the Parents' Financial Statement, using the UNIS school code 4581, with a $60 fee and a deadline around the end of December. Supporting documents are sent after admission, and they differ by situation: US tax returns for American families, a salary statement for UN staff, an official statement of salary and housing or education allowance for mission and consulate staff, and home-country tax forms converted to dollars for everyone else. Aid can also help with costs beyond tuition.

Many UN and diplomatic families have an education allowance from their employer. Check whether it covers the one-time levy and the capital surcharge as well as tuition, before you count on it.

Why the price is not the hurdle

UNIS admits students on their intellectual capabilities and personal qualities; ability to pay buys nothing in the process. That is why I tell families to think about the cost once, then put their energy where it decides something: a strong test score from 5th grade, a convincing teacher form, a child at ease in the meeting on campus.
