Testing for Hotchkiss: SSAT, ISEE, SAT and the English tests

Unlike several of its peers, Hotchkiss has not gone test-optional: standardized testing is required for every applicant. It is also explicit about the role of the score. Testing "is used to support the other academic materials in the application", and every part of the file is considered equally. The score therefore matters most at the margins, where it confirms or questions what the transcript and recommendations say.

Which test for which grade

Applying toAccepted tests
Ninth or tenth gradeSSAT or ISEE
Eleventh gradeSSAT, PSAT, SAT or ISEE
Twelfth grade or postgraduate yearSAT, ACT or PSAT

Score codes: SSAT 4012; ISEE, PSAT, SAT and ACT 070335; TOEFL 8199.

The English requirement

Applicants whose first language is not English must also submit a TOEFL, Duolingo English Test or IELTS result, in addition to the grade-level test. The exemption is narrow: three years or more at a school where English is the main language of instruction. Hotchkiss offers no ESL support, and indicates that a TOEFL of 100 on the internet-based test, or 600 on paper, shows English strong enough for full participation.

That matters for preparation. A bilingual student at a French or Spanish school abroad, fluent in conversation, must still sit both tests, and the SSAT verbal and reading sections will be the harder of the two.

Scores that support the file

We prepare applicants for the SSAT or ISEE and, where needed, the TOEFL, Duolingo or IELTS, so that the scores Hotchkiss reads reflect what the student can really do.

When to test

Hotchkiss strongly recommends testing in November or December of the year before enrollment, and gives the reason: it leaves time for a second sitting if the first scores disappoint. January tests are accepted within the regular cycle, but the file is only reviewed once scores arrive, and registration deadlines fall well before test dates. Planning the first sitting for October or November is the safe course.

SSAT or ISEE?

Hotchkiss accepts both for ninth and tenth grade and states no preference. The practical differences decide. On the ISEE, wrong answers cost nothing; the SSAT treats guessing more strategically. The ISEE can be taken once per testing season; the SSAT offers more frequent paper sittings, at-home testing in the US and Canada, and Prometric centers abroad. Students who take the SSAT also get free access to the Character Skills Snapshot.

The Character Skills Snapshot

Hotchkiss is interested in the Snapshot, an online assessment of character skills meant to complement cognitive tests, and calls it an important opportunity for the committee to know an applicant better. It is not mandatory. For SSAT takers it is free; others can request access for $25, with waivers available. For a school that names character as its first criterion, it is worth taking seriously.

How I prepare students for Hotchkiss testing

I start with a full timed diagnostic on the right test for the grade, then concentrate on the sections where the student loses points, usually verbal reasoning and reading speed for students educated in another language, and quantitative reasoning for others. For non-native speakers, TOEFL or Duolingo preparation runs alongside, so both tests are done by December.
