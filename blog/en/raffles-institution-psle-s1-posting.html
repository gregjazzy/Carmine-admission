The PSLE and S1 Posting

Raffles Institution campus, Singapore. Photo Lim Toh Han, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Singapore schools November 2026 4 min read

For many boys who join Raffles Institution in Year 1, the route is the simplest one: the PSLE, followed by the Ministry of Education's Secondary 1 Posting Exercise. Results decide, through a centrally managed allocation in which Raffles is one of the schools a family can choose.

Who can take this route

  • Boys in Primary 6 at mainstream primary schools in Singapore, who have sat the PSLE and are eligible for secondary school.
  • Singapore citizens, permanent residents or international students.
  • Students who did not accept any Direct School Admission offer.

Boys admitted through DSA-Sec must honour that commitment and cannot take part.

The 2026 dates

DateStep
Tuesday 24 November 2026, 11.30 a.m.PSLE results released; S1 Portal opens for choices
Until Monday 30 November, 4.30 p.m.Six school choices submitted, in order of preference
Friday 18 December 2026 (tentative)Posting results through the S1 Portal and SMS

The S1 Portal stays open around the clock during the choice period. A family that cannot collect the PSLE results in person can nominate another adult with the Ministry's proxy form.

Six choices in a week

The choice window is short: from the morning the results come out to the following Monday afternoon. The Ministry encourages families to discuss secondary school options with their child before the results, so that the six choices reflect the child's strengths and interests rather than a rushed reaction to the score. Placing Raffles first only makes sense for a boy who wants the six-year Integrated Programme and its commitment.

If the posting is not Raffles

A boy not posted to Raffles whose PSLE score meets the school's cut-off for his cohort can apply through the S1 Posting Appeal Exercise, by an online form available only during the appeal period. Places depend on academic merit and vacancies, and the decision is final. Later, a smaller Year 3 intake offers another chance.
