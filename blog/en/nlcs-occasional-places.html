NLCS: occasional places

North London Collegiate School, the former Canons mansion, Edgware. Photo Nigel Cox, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

Book a conversation

NLCS mainly recruits at 4, 7, 11 and 16. But a few places sometimes open in Years 8, 9 and 10.

How it works

Families complete an expression of interest form and are notified if a place opens; they stay on the list while the pupil remains eligible. There are never places in Year 11, and other years do not have them every year. If a place opens, assessment includes written papers and interviews. A Year 7 place that opens goes first to families already assessed and still on the waiting list.
