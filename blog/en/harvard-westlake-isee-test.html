The ISEE for Harvard-Westlake

St. Saviour's Chapel, Harvard-Westlake, Los Angeles. Photo Keithready, Wikimedia Commons, public domain

Unlike many schools that have made testing optional, Harvard-Westlake requires the Independent School Entrance Exam of every applicant. It is equally clear about how it wants the test approached: one sitting, without heavy preparation. Understanding both halves of that position saves families a great deal of time and stress.

The rules

RuleDetail
WhoEvery applicant for entry in fall 2027
WhenA single test taken between 1 August 2026 and 15 January 2027
FormatAny format and any site: paper, online, or ISEE At-Home
RegistrationOn the Educational Records Bureau (ERB) website, listing Harvard-Westlake as a recipient, code 052135; fee waivers exist
Several sittingsScores are never combined; the family tells the admission office which sitting to use, or the most recent is taken

Why one sitting

Harvard-Westlake does not recommend taking the ISEE more than once, nor tutoring or other special preparation. The school wants a reading of the student's academic preparedness and potential, as one element of a holistic review that also includes grades, three recommendations, a questionnaire and an interview. A score inflated by months of drilling tells it less, not more.

Familiar, not drilled

I make sure Harvard-Westlake applicants know the ISEE's format and timing before their single sitting, and put the rest of the effort where the school looks hardest: the questionnaire and the interview.

What sensible preparation looks like

There is a difference between drilling and familiarity. A student who has never seen the ISEE's sections, its timing or its answer format can lose points to surprise. A few practice sections, taken calmly at home, remove that surprise without turning the autumn into test preparation. Beyond that, the best preparation is the student's own schoolwork and reading.

Has your child already taken the ISEE once? Let's look at what to do next.

Choosing the date and format

  • Date. Early enough that a disrupted sitting, illness or a technical problem, still leaves time before 15 January; late enough that the student has settled into the school year.
  • Format. Paper suits some students, a screen suits others. A student who reads faster on paper should not choose the at-home online version for convenience alone.
  • Several schools. Families applying to other Los Angeles schools can send the same sitting to each.

What the score is not

No single factor decides admission at Harvard-Westlake. A strong score with a thin questionnaire and a flat interview is not a strong application; a solid score with a vivid one often is.

My approach

For seven years, I have worked with around fifteen families a year on applications to very selective schools. For Harvard-Westlake, I respect the school's position on the ISEE: familiarity, one sitting, then the real work on the parts of the file only the student can write and say.
