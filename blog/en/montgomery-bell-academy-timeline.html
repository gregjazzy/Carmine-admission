Montgomery Bell's calendar is compact. Almost everything that matters happens between January 1 and February 6, and decisions follow a month later. A family that waits for the new year to start will find four deadlines in five weeks.

The fall: tours and the first steps

From late August, MBA holds a Parent Coffee and Tour every Thursday at 10 a.m. while school is in session. That tour, or an online inquiry, opens the process and the admissions portal. The official application form follows. The shadow visit, step three, is best placed in the fall too: it lets the boy see the school, and it gives the family time to adjust the rest of the plan if the day reveals something.

The ISEE on January 30

MBA offers the ISEE on its own campus at the end of January. It is convenient, and it is late: a boy who sits it there has no second chance before decisions. Families who want room for a retake should plan an earlier sitting elsewhere in the fall and treat the January date as a fallback. The ISEE allows one sitting per testing season, so the timing has to be thought through.

The teacher evaluation, before the holidays

The teacher evaluation is part of the February 1 materials, but teachers write it in December or January, alongside their own end-of-term work. Asking in November, with a polite reminder in January, is the safe route.

March 6 to March 10

Four days separate the decision from the reply. A family considering other Nashville schools should know before March what it will do with each possible answer, and, if it applied for aid, should have read the aid estimate that arrives in the first days of March.

How I plan the year

I have students at Montgomery Bell, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools in the United States, England and Switzerland. For MBA, I set the ISEE date first, then the shadow visit, then the interview practice, so that the January rush is only a matter of submitting what is already ready.
