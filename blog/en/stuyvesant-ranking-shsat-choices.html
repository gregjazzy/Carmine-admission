Ranking your SHSAT choices

The SHSAT score decides who gets in. The ranked list of schools, entered at registration, decides where. Families spend months on the first and minutes on the second, yet a poorly built list can cost a student a seat they had earned.

How the list is used

  1. All students are ordered by composite SHSAT score, from highest to lowest.
  2. The top student is offered their first-choice school.
  3. The next student is offered their first choice if a seat remains; if not, their second; then their third.
  4. This continues down the list until seats run out.

A student who receives an offer to their fourth choice does so because their first three were already full with higher-scoring students. A student with no offer at all scored below the last seat filled at every school they listed.

What follows from it

RuleWhat it means for the list
Order follows score, not choiceRanking Stuyvesant first never lowers a student's chance at a school ranked second; the algorithm only moves down the list if the first choice is full.
Unlisted means impossibleStudents are never considered for schools they did not list. Leaving off a school the student would happily attend is the only way to lose a seat by ranking.
Preference should be realA student offered a school ranked high on paper but not wanted in practice has blocked their own better option lower down.

Building the list in practice

  • Visit or research each school. The eight testing schools differ in size, focus and location: Stuyvesant in Lower Manhattan, Brooklyn Tech in Fort Greene, Bronx Science in the Bronx, Staten Island Tech, and smaller schools such as HSMSE, HSAS and Queens Sciences on college campuses.
  • Consider the commute. A daily journey of an hour or more each way is four years of a teenager's life.
  • Rank honestly. Put first the school the student most wants, regardless of cutoffs; then every other school they would actually attend.
  • Leave off only real refusals. If a student would not attend a school, it should not be on the list.

When it is entered

The ranking is entered in MySchools during SHSAT registration, which for the fall 2026 test opened in early October and closed on 30 October. After the deadline, the order is fixed.
