The Milton file and recommendations

Boston from the Charles River, a few miles from Milton Academy. Photo King of Hearts, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

US boarding schools November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Much of a Milton Academy application is written by people other than the candidate. Three recommendations are required, two more are optional, and the grades come straight from the school. A family's main job is to make sure each of these voices arrives on time and says something specific.

Required pieces

  • School administrator recommendation. A head of school, division head or counsellor, who describes the student's place in the school as a whole.
  • Current English teacher recommendation. The teacher who sees the student read, write and argue this year.
  • Current math teacher recommendation. The teacher who sees how the student reasons and handles difficulty.
  • Official grades. Final grades for the past two years and the current fall term or semester, sent by the school, preferably online with the Transcript Form. Personal copies of reports are not accepted. If older grades are held by a previous school, the family must request them there.
  • The Candidate Biographic Profile. The student's own part of the file, to be submitted well before 15 January.

Optional pieces

PieceWorth adding when
Graded writing sampleThe student has a strong recent piece, marked by a teacher, that shows thinking and voice
Personal recommendationAn adult outside the classroom, a coach, a mentor, a community leader, knows a side of the student the teachers do not see
Special interest recommendationThe student has a serious commitment in music, art, athletics or another field, and someone who can describe it with authority

Optional pieces should add something new. A personal recommendation that repeats the teachers adds length, not information.

A coherent file

I help Milton families decide which optional pieces to send, prepare the student's profile, and make sure recommenders have what they need to write precisely.

Helping teachers write well

Recommenders are busy, and January is their worst month. Asking early, in September or October, gives them time. A short, polite note to each, reminding them of the deadline and of a piece of work or a moment from their class, often produces a more specific letter. What families should not do is suggest what to write.

Not sure which optional pieces to send? Let's go through the file together.

Grades, read in context

Milton receives grades from schools of every kind, in the United States and abroad. Two years of finals plus the current fall show a trajectory, and the teachers' letters explain it. A candidate with an uneven year should make sure someone, a teacher or the administrator, can explain it in context.

Where the candidate speaks

Apart from the interview, the Candidate Biographic Profile is the only place where the student writes in their own voice. It should sound like a thirteen or fifteen year old who thinks, not like an adult who edits.
