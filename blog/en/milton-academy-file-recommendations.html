Milton: the file and recommendations

Boston from the Charles River, a few miles from Milton Academy. Photo King of Hearts, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

US boarding schools November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

With no required test, Milton's written file weighs heavily. It consists of recommendations and official transcripts.

What to provide

Three required recommendations: from a school administrator, the current English teacher and the current maths teacher. An optional special interest recommendation. Transcripts: current fall grades and final grades for the two previous years, sent by the school, preferably online; Milton does not accept personal copies.

A file that convinces

I help you choose and brief the teachers, and review the whole so it tells a consistent story.

What makes the difference

A teacher outside the United States often writes too briefly. Asked early and told what Milton looks for, they can write a real letter. The optional recommendation only helps if it adds something new.
