Bangkok Patana keeps no more than 20% of its students from any single nationality, and places for Thai families are limited. To manage them, the school runs a dedicated process for Thai nationals, with its own calendar, separate from the rolling admissions used for international families.

Who must use it

The Thai process applies to a student who holds only a Thai passport, or whose two parents both hold Thai passports, even if other passports are held as well. A dual national with one Thai parent applies through the non-Thai route. Families unsure which route applies should ask the admissions team before starting.

The 2027/28 timeline

  1. 1 September 2026 to 15 January 2027: online application, with no application fee at this stage.
  2. After the deadline: all applications are reviewed and a shortlist drawn up according to places in each year group.
  3. February: shortlisted applicants are contacted, invited to an assessment and asked for additional documents; the non-refundable THB 5,500 application fee is paid at this point.
  4. By around 1 May 2027: the school aims to give its decision; successful families are invited to school to receive the offer letter.
  5. Enrolment: signing the offer and paying a non-refundable initial THB 50,000 to hold the place.

Ready for February

The assessment comes only a few weeks after shortlisting. I prepare Thai students in English and mathematics in the months before, so that February is not a rush.

Documents

School reports must be in English for the last two years. Documents in other languages than English or Thai need professional translation. The family also provides the Thai ID and house registration, passports, photos and the application agreement, plus entry questionnaires for Nursery and Foundation Stage.

Once a year

Thai applications are considered only once per year. If no place is available, the family is invited to update the application for the following year; existing applicants can ask the Thai admissions team to reactivate their file for editing. A single missed window means waiting a year.

How I help Thai families

I have students at Bangkok Patana, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective international schools in Asia. For Thai applicants, I work on English, the language of every assessment and every lesson, as well as mathematics.
