The Godolphin 11+ timetable

Godolphin's 11+ runs on fixed dates with almost no flexibility: several of them carry the words "we are unable to offer an alternative date". For a family, the calendar is less a guide than a set of deadlines to put in the diary in the summer before Year 6.

Entry in September 2027

Candidates must be born between 1 September 2015 and 31 August 2016, which places them in Year 6 in 2026-27.

The November week that matters

Registration on 6 November is only the first step. After it, Godolphin issues the Examination Entry Form, on which a family requests extra time, the music scholarship and a bursary. That form, and a complete bursary application with every supporting document, must be in by noon a week later. Assembling bursary documents takes about two weeks, so a family needing help should start before registering. Evidence for access arrangements is due to the Consortium schools by 6 November.

From offer to acceptance

Offers arrive on 12 February, the same date for every Consortium school. A family holding several offers has until noon on 3 March to accept, and bursary decisions come at the same time as the offer, so the comparison can be made on full information. On acceptance, a deposit of £3,300 is due.

Dates that move every year

The structure stays the same from year to year: open days in the autumn, registration in early November, the test before December, interviews in January, offers in February. The exact dates for the next cycle appear on the school's website, and families should check them before the summer.
