Poly Prep paused test requirements during the pandemic and brought them back from September 2024. Standardized testing is now part of admission for every grade from Grade 2 to Grade 12. The school explains why: test results add a data point on a child's readiness for its academic program, read together with transcripts, essays and recommendations.

What superscoring changes

Superscoring means the school takes a student's best result in each section across all the sittings submitted. A student who does well in verbal and reading on a first attempt can concentrate on mathematics for a second without risk to the other sections. For that to work, the first sitting has to come early enough to leave time for targeted work and a second date before December 31: in practice, a first attempt in October or early November.

SSAT or ISEE?

With no preference at Poly Prep, the choice depends on the child and on the other schools on the family's list. The two tests differ in structure: the ISEE has two reasoning sections, verbal and quantitative, plus reading and mathematics achievement; the SSAT has verbal, quantitative and reading sections, with a different question style and timing. A practice test of each, taken early, often shows which suits a child better. Since most ISAAGNY schools accept both, a family can usually settle on one test for the whole list.
