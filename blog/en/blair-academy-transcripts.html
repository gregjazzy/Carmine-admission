Of all the pieces of a Blair Academy application, the transcript covers the longest period: three school years. It is also one of the few that the family does not write and cannot polish, which is why the committee reads it closely.

What Blair requires
Years coveredThe current school year and the two previous years
Current yearMust include first-quarter or first-trimester grades
ContentCourses and grades listed
Sent byA school administrator, after the family signs the transcript request form
From abroadEnglish translations required
DeadlineAll forms received by 15 January

What the committee reads

Blair looks for strong academic ability, a work ethic and a commitment to personal growth. A transcript shows all three over time: the level of the courses taken, the grades in them, and their direction. A student whose grades rise through the three years tells a different story from one whose grades slip, even at the same average.

The grades behind the file

The transcript is built in class, not in December. I work with students in mathematics and the sciences during the year so that the grades support the application.

Why the first quarter matters

Because Blair asks for current-year grades from the first quarter or trimester, the autumn of the application year counts directly. A strong start in the year of applying shows the committee the student's present level, not only past results. Families should treat that first quarter as part of the application.

Context matters

Blair reads transcripts from many kinds of schools, in the United States and abroad. Recommendations from the English and mathematics teachers, the interview and any test scores help the committee interpret grades from a system it knows less well.

On paper or online

Families can apply through the SAO or Gateway, with the school administrator handling the transcript request. Blair also accepts a printable application; in that case the official transcript travels with the rest of the file to the admission office in Blairstown, postmarked no later than 15 January. Either way, the transcript must come from the school, not from the family.

Practical points

  • Sign the request form early and give it to the principal or counsellor.
  • For schools abroad, arrange English translations in time.
  • Check that the current-year quarter grades are included.
