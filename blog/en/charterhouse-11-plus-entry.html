Charterhouse at 11+

Most children join Charterhouse at 13, after a prep school that runs to Year 8. But many schools, in the UK and abroad, finish at Year 6. For those children, Charterhouse offers an 11+ pathway: a single admissions process that secures both a place at one of its prep schools for Years 7 and 8, and a place at Charterhouse from Year 9.

The three prep schools

The pathway runs through Edgeborough, Windlesham House or Amesbury. The family indicates its preferred school on the registration form. Visiting both the prep school and Charterhouse before applying is the best way to judge the fit, and the school asks families to arrange visits well in advance.

One process, step by step

  1. Registration by 30 September of Year 6, with a combined fee of £450 including VAT, covering both schools.
  2. The ISEB Common Pre-Test in the autumn term of Year 6: online, age-standardised tests in mathematics, English, verbal and non-verbal reasoning, read with a reference from the current school.
  3. An Interview and Activities Assessment at Charterhouse in the spring term of Year 6: group activities, friendly interviews and a piece of creative writing.
  4. Offers in March of Year 6. Families sign one contract with Charterhouse and pay one deposit.

For a child starting Year 7 in September 2028 and Charterhouse in September 2030, registration closes on 30 September 2027.

An 11+ application, prepared like a 13+

The test and the assessment day are the same as at 13+. I prepare children for both, at the level Charterhouse expects, from the end of Year 5.

Who the pathway suits

It suits families whose child's school ends at Year 6, in the UK or abroad, and who want a seamless transition: two years in a prep school that prepares children for Charterhouse, then entry at 13 without a second round of applications. For a family abroad, it is also a way to join the English system two years before senior school, with the support of a smaller school.

What happens in Years 7 and 8

Once a place is accepted, the prep school runs onboarding activities before September, and during Years 7 and 8 families and children are invited to special events at Charterhouse to prepare for 13+. Children who show exceptional academic potential at the assessment may be invited onto the Academic Scholars Pathway, ahead of the 13+ scholarship assessments in Year 8.

The same bar as at 13+

The 11+ pathway is not a side door. The Pre-Test and the assessment day are the same instruments Charterhouse uses for its main entry, sat at the same moment in Year 6. A child applying at 11+ needs the same preparation in reasoning, English and mathematics, and the same ease in a group and in an interview.
