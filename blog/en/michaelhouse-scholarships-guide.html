Michaelhouse offers several scholarships to outstanding boys entering Grade 8. They last for the boy's whole career, five years, or three for certain music awards, provided he maintains acceptable performance and involvement.

The awards

  • Open Scholarships, major and minor: up to 75%, for independent school candidates.
  • Trust Scholarships, major and minor: up to 75%, for state school candidates who have attended their school for at least two years.
  • Closed Scholarships: up to 30%, for traditional feeder schools.
  • All-Rounder and sport: up to 75%.
  • Music: up to 50%. Art and drama: up to 15%.
