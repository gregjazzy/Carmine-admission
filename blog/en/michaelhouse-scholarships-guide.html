Michaelhouse runs two separate programmes to help boys enrol. Scholarships and exhibitions reward ability and potential, and are awarded without looking at the family's finances. Financial assistance and discretionary awards depend on need. A boy can hold both: assistance can top up a scholarship that does not cover what the family requires.

Percentages apply to board and tuition, R417,000 for 2026. All awards are for entry into E Block, Grade 8, and their value is fixed for the duration of the award.

How long, and on what condition

A scholarship normally runs for the boy's whole time at Michaelhouse, five years, or three years for some music scholarships. It holds as long as he maintains acceptable levels of performance, involvement and conduct.

Competing for an award

I prepare boys for the Michaelhouse academic scholarship exam and for the weekend as a whole, so that the assessors see each boy's real level.

What some awards ask in return

  • Sport scholars take full part in the strength and conditioning programme and play a team sport in both the summer and winter seasons, for all their years at the school.
  • Music scholars take Music as a matric subject and play an active part in the school's performances.
  • Art exhibitioners take Art as a matric subject; drama exhibitioners take full part in the performance programme.

Families should weigh these commitments before applying. A strong young musician who does not want Music among his final subjects should think twice about the music scholarship.

Financial assistance

Funds are limited, but Michaelhouse helps families for whom the fees are beyond reach. The first request is made on the boy's application form, by the 31 January closing date of Grade 7; the Finance Department then guides the family, and full financial disclosure to the Financial Assistance Committee is required. Assistance must be re-applied for every year, although some discretionary awards are renewed automatically. Unlike scholarships, discretionary awards can sometimes be made to boys entering Grades 9 or 10, if places and funds allow.

The Rector's Discretionary Award

Outside the formal scholarship programme, the Rector can grant an award for excellence. It also requires full financial disclosure, in the format set by the Financial Assistance Committee.
