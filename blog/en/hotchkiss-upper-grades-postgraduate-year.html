Entering Hotchkiss in 11th or 12th grade, or for a postgraduate year

The main building of The Hotchkiss School, Lakeville, Connecticut. Photo Adrien Delessert, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.5

Hotchkiss admits students into ninth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth grade, and offers a postgraduate year for students who have finished high school elsewhere. The process is the same for everyone: inquiry, interview, testing, Gateway application, the same January 15 deadline and March 10 decisions. What changes with age is the testing, and what the committee needs to see.

Testing by grade

Applying toRequired test
Tenth gradeSSAT or ISEE
Eleventh gradeSSAT, PSAT, SAT or ISEE
Twelfth grade or postgraduate yearSAT, ACT or PSAT

Non-native English speakers add a TOEFL, Duolingo or IELTS score unless they have spent three years or more at an English-medium school.

What an older applicant must show

For a student entering in eleventh or twelfth grade, the transcript is long and the recommendations come from teachers who know high school work well. The committee reads more evidence of academic level, so the bar is the same as for ninth graders, A-range grades, strong recommendations and writing, but tested against harder material. The essays need to explain the move: why Hotchkiss, and why now, rather than finishing where the student is.

A strong upper-grade applicant usually has a clear reason. A student whose current school cannot offer the courses they need; a family relocation; a student who has outgrown a small program. The essay and the interview should make that reason concrete.

Late entry, well argued

We prepare older applicants for the SAT, ACT or PSAT their grade requires, and help them explain, in essays and interview, why a move to Hotchkiss makes sense now.

The postgraduate year

Hotchkiss's postgraduate applicants have finished high school and come for one more year, tested with the SAT, ACT or PSAT. The year must be justified by what it adds: a stronger academic record before college, more time to mature, or the chance to compete in a sport at a higher level while continuing serious study. Athletes can complete the Athletic Interest Form and contact coaches directly.

Considering a move for junior or senior year? Tell me the reason; it shapes the whole application.

Timing for college

A student entering in eleventh grade begins college applications within a year; one entering in twelfth grade or for a postgraduate year applies to college within months of arriving. The Hotchkiss counselors then have little time to know the student, and the student's own clarity about goals matters more. Families should weigh that before choosing a late move.

Reading an upper-grade transcript

By eleventh grade, a transcript shows not only grades but choices: which mathematics level, which sciences, whether the student took the hardest courses their school offered. For Hotchkiss, that matters because a new junior or senior must fit into courses already in progress. An applicant should make sure the transcript, or a short note from the counselor, makes the level of each course clear, especially if the school's course names are not self-explanatory or the student comes from another system.

How I prepare upper-grade and PG applicants

I prepare the SAT, ACT or PSAT at the level a selective boarding school expects, work on the essays that explain the move, and run interviews where the student talks about their academic interests at high school level. For postgraduate candidates, I also help think through how the year will strengthen the college application that follows.
