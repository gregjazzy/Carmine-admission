Eton: the registration and admissions timeline

Eton's timeline spans four years, and its first date is the hardest: a family that discovers Eton when their son is 11 has already missed the main entry.

The dates, for entry in September 2029

Year 5 (age 9-10): registration by 31 August 2026; financial declaration from all families by 30 September 2026.

Year 6 (age 10-11): school reference by 31 October 2026; ISEB Pre-Tests by 30 November; Stage 1 results in mid-December; Stage 2, interviews and tests at Eton, from January to March 2027; offers in the spring.

Year 7 (age 11-12): house choice with House Masters; acceptance fee due by end of March; further assessment for waiting-list boys.

Year 8 (age 12-13): King's Scholarship, Common Entrance or Eton Entrance exam, which confirms the place.

The right timetable for your son

At Eton, every stage is prepared months ahead. I set out your son's timetable with you, from registration to the final exam, and prepare him for each step.

What the timeline means

A missed date cannot be recovered: Eton applies its deadlines strictly. After the registration date, only the Year 8 scholarships and Sixth Form entry remain. Preparation follows the same rhythm: the Pre-Test is prepared from the spring of Year 5, the interview during the autumn of Year 6.
