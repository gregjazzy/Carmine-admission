Lyceum Alpinum Zuoz entrance test: what you need to know

At Zuoz, admission is not a competitive exam, and many families conclude that the test is a formality. It is not: it decides admission as much as the programme the child will enter, and so the universities that will open to them. I have helped fifteen students into Le Rosey and every year I support families towards the leading Swiss boarding schools.

How admission works

The school takes new students from grade 7 to grade 11, exceptionally grade 12. It judges on past school results, its own assessments and teachers' recommendations. Applications are reviewed as they arrive; shortlisted candidates are invited to an interview and an admissions test, in Zuoz or online. The CHF 1,600 application fee is paid before the test.

Preparing for Zuoz's test

I prepare each applicant for Zuoz's test and interview, with work timed to the programme they are aiming for and the date of their tests. Let's start with a first conversation about your child.

What the school does not say

Zuoz publishes neither the content of the test, nor its length, nor the level expected by grade and programme. Yet the bar is not the same for entering the IGCSE, the IB or the Matura, which is taught in German. That is precisely what decides, and it is what I know.

Why strong applicants choose the wrong door

Aiming for the Matura without German

The Matura opens all Swiss universities without entrance exams, but requires real German: B1 is required to enter it. Without it, the only route is the German Intensive Year, an extra year of boarding.

IB or Matura for your child? Tell me about their plans: I will tell you which programme to aim for.

Spoken English that is not enough for the IB

The IB Diploma is taught in English. A child at ease orally but fragile in writing finds out at the test, then pays for it for two years.

Joining too late

A student arriving in grade 11 goes straight into the two decisive IB years. Arriving in IGCSE gives two years to settle.

When to start

Admission is rolling, but a boarding place is only guaranteed with a file submitted six to eight months before the late-August start. A trial day in boarding is possible before deciding. The test is therefore prepared from the previous winter.

What I do for your family

Every year I see families choose the programme on image rather than on the child's real level. My job is to avoid that mistake. It starts with an assessment in English and maths, and German if the Matura is considered, which shows which programme is realistic; I then prepare your child for the test and the interview, and follow the application with you.
