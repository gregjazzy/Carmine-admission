At Bangkok Patana, assessments follow the file review, subject to available places. I follow students at Bangkok Patana and at the leading schools in Asia, and every year I support around fifteen families with their admissions.

What the school says

English: any non-native or multilingual student is assessed by an EAL specialist, on site, before a place is confirmed. The youngest: in Nursery and Foundation Stage, an in-person readiness assessment; a preliminary online meeting is possible from overseas. Additional needs: to be disclosed with the application, sometimes with further assessments.

I prepare your child for the English assessment, spoken and written.

What the school does not say

No minimum level is published. But the student's ability to access the curriculum with available support is the first criterion.
