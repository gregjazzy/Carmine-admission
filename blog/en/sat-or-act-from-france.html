SAT or ACT from France: which to choose, and how the scores convert

SAT · ACT October 2026 6 min read

American universities ask for an SAT or an ACT score, at the applicant's choice. Most students at French lycées never ask the question and take the SAT, because it is the one people talk about. It is often the right choice, but not always, and the question deserves an hour of thought before months of preparation go into it.

SAT or ACT: universities have no preference

Universities say so plainly. Stanford has no preferred exam, Yale considers both equally, Dartmouth states no preference, Harvard asks for "the SAT or ACT". The choice therefore rests on a single criterion: which of the two will give this student the best score.

Two tests that demand a different pace

Both exams have changed recently. The SAT has become digital and adaptive: 98 questions in 2 hours 14 minutes, in two sections, Reading and Writing then Math, each in two modules, the second adjusting to the result of the first. A graphing calculator, Desmos, is built into the app for the whole maths section.

The ACT was shortened in 2025, and the new version applies to all international sittings since September 2025. The composite score, out of 36, now counts only three tests: English (50 questions in 35 minutes), Math (45 in 50 minutes) and Reading (36 in 40 minutes), a total of 131 questions in 125 minutes. The Science test has become optional; it gives a separate score.

The average time per question is our calculation, from the official figures. The gap is the difference that matters most for a student from a French lycée: they read English more slowly than an American, and the ACT, which leaves about 30% less time per question, penalises them more. Conversely, a fast bilingual student comfortable with direct questions may gain from it.

Taking either test from France

Outside the United States, both are taken on computer at an approved centre. The SAT has six sittings in 2026-2027: 3 October, 7 November and 5 December 2026, 6 March, 1 May and 5 June 2027. The international ACT offers seven, from 5 September 2026 to 9-10 July 2027, most over two days. The list of SAT centres in France is on the College Board website, and on 3 October 2026, several Paris centres were already full for November and December. For the ACT, the centre is chosen at registration.

2026-2027 fees and dates. SAT outside the United States: $68 registration plus a $43 international fee, $111 in total (fees valid until June 2027). International ACT: $188.50, or $198.50 with Science. Dates and prices published by the College Board and ACT, checked in October 2026.

How to decide

The safest method: one full practice test of each, in real conditions, a few days apart. The SAT offers them in the Bluebook app, the ACT on its website. Compare the two scores using the concordance table, and look above all at where the student ran out of time. Once the choice is made, stick to it: preparing both in parallel scatters the work.
