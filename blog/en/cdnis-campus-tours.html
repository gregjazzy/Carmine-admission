A visit is the best way to understand a school, and at CDNIS it also needs planning. The two parts of the school receive visitors on different days and in different seasons, and the deadlines for most grades fall before the end of the visiting season.

Where and when

VisitWhenFor
Main campus tour, Nam Long ShanTuesdays during the school year, September to MayFamilies considering the main campus grades
Early Years Centre tourSaturdays, September to NovemberFamilies considering the youngest years
Upper School Admissions and Curriculum WebinarFriday 11 December 2026, 10 amFamilies considering the Upper School
Recorded webinars and virtual toursAny timeFamilies who cannot visit, including those abroad

Guided tours walk families through teaching and learning spaces, give a sense of daily life and allow questions to the admissions team. Availability can vary through the year.

Timing a visit against the deadlines

  • Early Years 1 to Grade 5: applications close on 2 October. A family that wants to visit first should book a September tour, or apply and visit afterwards.
  • Nursery: applications run from 1 to 30 November, within the Early Years Centre's Saturday tour season.
  • Grades 6 to 11: applications close on 31 December, after both the Tuesday tours of the autumn term and the December webinar.

From the visit to the assessment

After the visit comes the application and the assessment. I help families keep the calendar and prepare the child for the assessment and interview.

What to look for

  • The bilingual programme in the early grades: how English and Mandarin share the day, and how children new to one of the languages are supported.
  • The Upper School pathway: how the IB Middle Years Programme and Diploma connect with the Ontario diploma, and what an open-access Diploma means for every student.
  • Daily logistics: the school bus, available on the main campus only, and lunch arrangements.

From abroad

Many families apply before moving to Hong Kong. Recorded webinars, covering Nursery to Early Years 1, Early Years 2 to Grade 5 and the Upper School, as well as student, athletics, arts and Chinese tours, give a clear picture before arrival. Assessments for overseas candidates are held by video or online interview, so a visit is not required to apply.
