Godolphin and Latymer fees

Godolphin and Latymer School, Hammersmith. Photo Des Blenkinsopp, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

London schools November 2026 4 min read

Godolphin and Latymer is a day school, so its fees cover tuition only, with no boarding. They are charged per term, in advance, by direct debit on the first day of each term, and reviewed every January, which is why the autumn term costs less than the two that follow.

The figures for 2026-27

ItemAmount, VAT included
Registration, non-returnable£210
Deposit, on acceptance of a place£3,300
Autumn term 2026£11,698

Summer term 2027£12,341
School year 2026-27£36,380

The deposit is credited against the pupil's account at the end of her time at the school.

What the fee covers

  • Textbooks and stationery.
  • Public examination fees.
  • Day visits out of school for whole year groups, Years 7 to 9.
  • Personal accident insurance.

Lunches and extra subjects are charged separately; a full term's notice in writing is needed to stop them. No reduction is made for absence, but the school offers a fees insurance scheme against loss of fees through injury or illness. Medical insurance is available at extra cost.

The test decides, not the price

At Godolphin, fees never separate candidates: bursary applicants sit the same test as everyone else. I prepare girls for the test and the interview that do decide.

Increases

The governors reserve the right to increase fees and give parents at least a term's written notice. Because the review happens each January, a family planning seven years at Godolphin should expect the figure to change from one calendar year to the next. Late payment incurs a penalty, added to the following term's bill.

Seven years, in round figures

At the 2026-27 rate, seven years from Year 7 to Upper Sixth come to about £255,000 in tuition alone, before any annual increase, plus lunches and extras. There is no school bus: the school is under ten minutes' walk from Hammersmith Broadway, so most girls travel by Tube or bus, a modest but daily cost.

If the fees are out of reach

Godolphin offers means-tested bursaries of up to 110 % of the fees, so that girls can take up a place whatever their family's finances, and may waive the registration fee in cases of particular need. Music scholarships at 11+ and 16+, and an art scholarship at 16+, are worth up to 30 % of the fees.
