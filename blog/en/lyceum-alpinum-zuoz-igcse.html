Lyceum Alpinum Zuoz: joining at 12 or 14, Pre-IGCSE and IGCSE

People often think of Zuoz for the IB or the Matura, at the end of schooling. But the international track starts much earlier, and the best entry is often before the IB: at 12, in Pre-IGCSE, or at 14, in IGCSE.

The pathway

On the international side, schooling has three stages: Pre-IGCSE in grades 7 and 8, IGCSE in grades 9 and 10, then the IB Diploma in grades 11 and 12, all in English. Zuoz has been accredited since October 2020 to teach the Cambridge IGCSE and run its exams, a qualification recognised worldwide, equivalent to the British GCSE with an international focus. The IB has been offered there for twenty-five years.

What students choose

In IGCSE, students build their programme across six subject groups: first and second languages, social sciences, sciences, mathematics and the arts. In sciences, they take biology, chemistry and physics but are examined in only two, of their choice. In mathematics, three levels exist, from core to additional mathematics. In the arts, they choose between design and technology, visual art and music. These choices matter: they prepare those of the IB, and so the university application.

Joining at the right time

To aim for the IB at Zuoz, joining in IGCSE rather than in the final years changes everything. I tell families the right moment for their child, and I prepare applications to the standard of the leading boarding schools.

Why join before the IB

Zuoz admits new students up to grade 11, exceptionally grade 12. But a student who arrives in IGCSE has two years to settle into academic English, boarding and the school's methods before the two decisive IB years. One who arrives directly in the IB has to do everything at once.

My view

Every year I support around fifteen families towards the most selective boarding schools and schools, in Switzerland and France, and fifteen of my students have been admitted to Le Rosey alone. For Zuoz, the age of entry often matters more than the programme: a well-run IGCSE prepares a solid IB, and a solid IB opens British and American universities. That is the timeline I advise families on, always comparing with the other boarding schools offering the same track.
