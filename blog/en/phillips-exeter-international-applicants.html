Phillips Exeter: applying from abroad

The Academy Building, the historic heart of Phillips Exeter. Photo Quintin Soloviev, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0

Exeter says it plainly: the process is the same for international and domestic applicants, with the same $60 application fee. That is good news for equal treatment, and a challenge for a child who did not grow up in the American system.

Before admission

English. For a student whose primary language is not English and who has not been in an English-speaking school for the last two years, the school strongly recommends the TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo English Test, without requiring it.

The interview. It can be held online or by recorded video: distance does not prevent applying.

The test. The SSAT or ISEE, as for everyone, sent directly by the testing company.

Preparing for Exeter from afar

I prepare students all over the world, online, for the SSAT, the essays and Exeter's interview. The gap with an American applicant can be closed, provided you start early.

After admission

A student with neither an American passport nor a valid study visa receives Form I-20 from Exeter, needed to apply for the F-1 visa; the process starts in early May. The school's health insurance is mandatory for international students residing overseas, $2,595 for 2026-2027. A dedicated orientation welcomes them in late August, before term starts.
