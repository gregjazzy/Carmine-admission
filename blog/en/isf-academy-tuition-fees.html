The ISF Academy is a not-for-profit independent school that receives no government subsidy for its daily running. Its fees fund, above all, a high teacher-to-student ratio. For a family, the annual cost has three parts: tuition, the capital contribution, and extras that depend on the child's grade and choices.

I go through this budget with each family I accompany to ISF. Here it is for 2026-27.

Tuition

SectionGradesAnnual tuition
PrimaryFoundation Year to Grade 5HK$251,130
SecondaryGrades 6 to 10HK$291,770
Secondary (IB Diploma)Grades 11 and 12HK$317,190

Tuition is paid in ten equal instalments by bank autopay. For new students, the first instalment is part of the Acceptance Deposit, which secures the place, is credited towards tuition and is not refunded if the student later withdraws. Withdrawal requires 90 days' written notice, July and August excluded, so a withdrawal for a new school year must reach the school before April 1.

The capital contribution

Every new student pays an Annual Capital Levy of HK$40,000, non-refundable and subject to annual adjustment, unless they are the nominee of a Capital Note holder. Adding tuition and levy, a primary student costs about HK$291,000 a year, a Grade 6 to 10 student about HK$332,000 and an IB student about HK$357,000, before extras.

Extras

The application fee is HK$1,000, non-refundable. On top of tuition come the Experiential Learning Programs and overseas excursions, co-curricular activities, optional courses with their own fees, and, in Grades 11 and 12, textbooks and IB Diploma registration and subject fees.

Financial aid

The Financial Aid Program grants fee remission from 20 to 100 percent. Admissions assessment is need-blind, so applying for aid does not affect the decision. A request can be made at any point during the process, even after an acceptance letter, but only families who submit the complete application before the financial aid deadline learn the outcome with their acceptance package. The school also runs scholarships, separate from need-based aid, for academic and special talents from Grade 6.
