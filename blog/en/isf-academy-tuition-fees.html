The ISF Academy is a not-for-profit independent school, with no government subsidy for daily operations. In 2026-27, tuition is HKD 251,130 a year from Foundation Year to Grade 5, HKD 291,770 from Grade 6 to Grade 10 and HKD 317,190 in Grades 11 and 12, in ten instalments.

What comes on top

  • An acceptance deposit to secure the place, credited to fees, non-refundable on withdrawal.
  • The application fee, HKD 1,000.
  • Experiential learning programmes, trips, activities, extra; in Grades 11 and 12, textbooks and IB fees.
  • A Capital Contribution Scheme, specific to the school.

Aid

The Financial Aid Program reduces fees by 20 to 100%.
