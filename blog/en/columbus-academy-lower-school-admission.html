From first to fifth grade, Columbus Academy's admission process adds a school record to the early childhood steps. Families still have a parent conversation and the child still visits campus, but the current teacher now writes a recommendation, and from second grade the school asks for transcripts. The child's visit combines a screening in a play-based setting with time in a classroom.

What the school sees

The teacher recommendation and the transcripts describe the child at school; the visit lets Columbus Academy see them directly, in an activity and in a classroom. For a child coming from another system or country, the transcripts may need a short explanation of the grading, and the recommendation should come from a teacher who knows the child well this year. Families should warn that teacher early: the request goes out from the portal from December 1, in the busiest weeks of the school year.

Cost and aid

Lower School tuition for 2026-27 is $33,200 in first and second grade, $33,600 in third and fourth, and $34,200 in fifth, with textbooks, supply kits and some activities billed separately. Need-based tuition assistance is available through Clarity with the same February 1 deadline, and its decision arrives with the admission decision on March 1.
