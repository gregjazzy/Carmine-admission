EIB: nursery and primary fees, school by school

Paris schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

The article on EIB fees focuses on collège and lycée. In primary, the gap between the network's schools is far wider, and it comes down to one thing: the school's status.

2026-2027 fees

  • Lamartine (16th) and Monceau (8th): €8,820 a year, from nursery to CM2.
  • Grenelle (7th): €16,575 in nursery, €17,910 in elementary.
  • La Jonchère (La Celle-Saint-Cloud): €14,100 in nursery and elementary.
  • Immersion classes at Monceau: €13,455, with a €1,750 deposit.

Trocadéro and Wagram, like Grenelle, are half-English schools; their fee grids are provided by the school.

Where the gap comes from

Lamartine and Monceau are under contract with the state: their fees combine a contract-related part and a compulsory "EIB pack", which funds reinforced English in particular; at Monceau, €3,124 and €5,696. In the half-English schools, the price reflects full bilingual teaching, with English-speaking teachers for half of the lessons.

The right school, at the right price

Half in English or a reinforced French curriculum: the price difference only makes sense if it serves your child. I tell you which one truly suits them.

What comes on top

For a new pupil, a €300 application fee, then a registration fee: €1,000 at Lamartine and Monceau, €1,800 at Grenelle, Trocadéro and Wagram, plus a €300 excellence fund at Grenelle. Add uniform, insurance, lunches and options. A 25% reduction applies to the third child's tuition. Payment can be made in one, three or nine instalments, with a €75 fee for monthly payment.
