Harvard-Westlake: what the school looks for

St. Saviour's Chapel, Harvard-Westlake, Los Angeles. Photo Keithready, Wikimedia Commons, public domain

US schools November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Harvard-Westlake says it in one sentence: it seeks kind, curious and engaged students, eager to contribute to the community. No single factor decides, but a strong academic record is expected.

What to know

Students come from 247 different schools, public, religious, independent and international: no school of origin opens the door automatically. Siblings are only admitted if they show the qualities likely to make them succeed. And since the school receives more qualified applicants than places, it advises every family to apply elsewhere too.

Showing who they are

I prepare your child to show, in the short answers and the interview, what Harvard-Westlake looks for: curiosity, engagement, kindness.
