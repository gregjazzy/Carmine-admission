Mercersburg Academy awards scholarships for merit as well as for need. There is no separate scholarship application: all domestic applicants who meet the criteria are considered automatically. But one condition catches families out.

The condition: test scores

For Grades 9 and 10, Mercersburg does not require standardised tests for admission. For merit scholarships, it does: SSAT or ISEE scores are required. A family that skips testing because it is optional rules its child out of merit consideration. Candidates for merit scholarships are also not eligible for an application fee waiver.

The general profile: applications complete by 15 January, typically A averages, strong test performance, broad extracurricular involvement and exemplary character. A Clarity financial aid application is required only for the two Lenfest scholarships. Recipients are told by 10 March and reply by 10 April.

Access scholarships

Mercersburg describes its access scholarships, including the Arce Scholarship for Grade 9 applicants and the Hale Scholarship, as among its most prestigious and competitive awards. All domestic boarding applicants who are admitted and qualify for full need-based aid are considered automatically. Recipients generally have A or high-B averages and test scores at the 85th percentile or above, with SSAT, ISEE or HSPT required. They receive full tuition, room and board each year, plus $5,000 for expenses ranging from a laptop and textbooks to music lessons, travel home and college application fees.

International applicants

Merit and access scholarships are described as open to domestic applicants. International families can still apply for need-based aid, which covers the full demonstrated need of admitted students.
