The 11+ Assessment Day

Entrance to Wycombe Abbey, High Wycombe. Photo David Hillas, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

English boarding schools November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

At 11+, Wycombe Abbey does not filter candidates on the online test before meeting them: every registered girl who has sat the ISEB Pre-Test comes to the school for the Assessment Day. The day is designed to give her a taste of life at Wycombe Abbey while the school assesses her academic ability and her suitability for boarding.

The day, part by part

ElementDetail
English paperOne hour, written by the school
Mathematics paperOne hour, written by the school
InterviewIndividual, with a member of staff
Group activitiesIn small groups
ReferenceRequested from the current school

For 2027 entry, the Assessment Day was on 19 November 2026. All candidates must attend in person at Wycombe Abbey, whatever their country.

"No preparation is necessary"

The school states that no preparation is needed for any element, and sends sample questions in the autumn term. That is honest advice about the format: there is nothing to memorise. It does not mean the level is modest. The papers are there to tell apart many able candidates, and an hour of English or maths at that level rewards a girl who reads widely, writes clearly and reasons calmly through unfamiliar problems.

Depth before November

I work with girls in Year 5 and 6 on the reading, writing and mathematical reasoning that the Assessment Day papers reveal, rather than on tricks.

After the day

  • December: firm offers and waiting-list offers, made on the papers, the interview and the group activity.
  • Late January: scholarship papers for a small number of girls holding firm offers; for 2027, on 28 January, with music auditions on 27 January.
  • Early March: acceptance and deposit, on a date the school confirms.

Group activities and interview

A boarding school wants to see how a girl behaves among others as well as on paper. In a small group, the qualities that show are listening, contributing without taking over, and staying engaged with a task. In the interview, a girl who talks honestly about what she enjoys, and thinks aloud when a question surprises her, gives the school what it needs.

How I prepare

I have pupils at Wycombe Abbey, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools in England, the United States and Switzerland. For the 11+, I start in Year 5 with the Pre-Test and move on to longer English and maths papers, so that the November hour feels familiar.
