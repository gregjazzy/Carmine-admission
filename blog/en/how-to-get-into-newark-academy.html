How to get into Newark Academy

Livingston, New Jersey, home of Newark Academy. Photo Scott Brody, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

US independent schools November 2026 6 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Newark Academy is a day school on a 68-acre campus in Livingston, New Jersey, and the first school in the state, still the only private one, to offer the International Baccalaureate Diploma. Its admission team describes the process as a partnership: understanding the child, and letting the family decide whether the school is right for them.

I have students at Newark Academy. The process runs as follows.

The pieces

PieceWhat it shows
Online Admission FormThe student's story, through questions about interests in and beyond class
Candidate and Family ConversationsInformal conversations with an admission officer or a faculty member
Transcripts and SSAT or ISEEAcademic context; scores never define a student

How files are read

An admission officer reviews each file first, considering every piece. The Admission Committee then discusses it in the context of the grade applied for, with the aim of building a class with diverse interests and backgrounds. Newark Academy looks for students, and families, who share its mission: curious, motivated, passionate and collaborative inside and outside the classroom.

A complete picture

I prepare students for the SSAT or ISEE, the written questions of the form and the Candidate Conversation, so that every piece shows who they are.

Key dates for 2027 entry

For entry in September 2027, the Online Admission Form opened on 1 September 2026 and is due on 1 December. The prior year's transcript follows by 15 December. Conversations, recommendations, test scores, current grades and the financial aid application are due by 11 January 2027. Decisions are released on 19 February, and families reply by 5 March.

Is your child in 5th or 8th grade? Tell me about them; the form is due on 1 December.

Grades 6 to 11

Newark Academy accepts applications for grades 6 to 11. Grades 6 and 9 are the main entry points; places in other grades are fewer. There is no mid-year admission.

Cost and aid

Tuition for 2026-27 is $55,525, plus $2,350 in required fees. All aid is need-based, there are no merit scholarships, and the school meets 100 % of demonstrated need for every admitted family. The Newark Scholars Program offers full scholarships to up to six students a year from the city of Newark.
