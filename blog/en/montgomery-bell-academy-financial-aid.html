Financial aid at Montgomery Bell

Montgomery Bell publishes its financial aid in unusual detail, and the numbers deserve a careful reading, because they contradict a common assumption. Aid at MBA is not reserved for low-income families. The average household income of families receiving need-based aid is $207,000.

The figures

IndicatorValue
Aid granted each yearOver $3.4 million

Average tuition paid after aid$16,316
Eligible applicants who received aid100%

Read together, these say that a family with two professional incomes and more than one child in private school should not assume it is out of range. Every family's situation is analysed individually, and aid is based on need alone; there are no merit awards.

Aid and admission are separate

Aid awards are determined independently of admission decisions. For the family, the practical consequence is simple: applying for aid does not cost the boy his place. The condition MBA sets is that the boy be a strong fit for the school; if he is, the school encourages the family to apply and works with it to make the education possible.

First, a strong candidate

Aid follows admission. I prepare boys to be the strong candidates MBA describes, at the level of the most selective schools: the ISEE, the shadow day, the interview.

The calendar

  • Fall: applications for 2027-28 open on Clarity, which can import the family's 2025 tax forms directly from the IRS. Clarity runs information webinars, including one on December 8.
  • January 1: completed Clarity application due, the same day as the admission application.
  • March 1 to 5: aid estimates sent to newly admitted students, just before decisions reach all families on March 6.
  • Mid-April: final aid awards.

Aid is not guaranteed for families who apply after January 1. With the holidays in the last two weeks of December, the real deadline is earlier than it looks.

What happens between estimate and award

Admitted families receive an estimate in early March, then reply to the offer of admission by March 10, before the final award arrives in mid-April. A family that needs aid to enrol should read the estimate carefully and ask questions before replying, because the reply comes first.
