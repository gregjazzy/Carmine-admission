Montgomery Bell: financial aid

The Montgomery Bell Academy campus, Nashville. Photo Wikimachine, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Montgomery Bell says it plainly: every qualified student deserves access to its education, regardless of family finances. The school invites families of a boy who is a strong fit to apply for aid, and works with them to make it possible.

What to remember

Tuition is $40,627 a year in 2026-2027. Aid is requested during the admission process, through the Director of Admissions and Financial Aid. What counts first is that the boy is a strong candidate: that is the condition the school itself sets.

A strong candidate first

Aid follows the strength of the file. I prepare your son for the ISEE, the shadow visit and the interview.
