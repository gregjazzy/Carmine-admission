The HKIS wait pool and priorities

Many families hear "waitlist" and imagine a queue. HKIS is explicit that it runs a wait pool instead. The difference matters: in a pool, there is no fixed place in line, and a family's position can change after it has applied.

Pool, not list

When no place is available in a grade, qualified applicants, those who meet the admissions requirements, enter the pool for that grade. When a place opens, the school selects from the pool according to its Admissions Priority System. Because priority comes before date, a later application from a family with higher priority can move ahead of an earlier one. HKIS commits to keeping families informed about space availability so they can plan.

The three groups

PriorityWho
A, debenture holdersApplicants holding a valid corporate or family HKIS debenture
B, community-connectedSiblings of current students; returning HKIS students; applicants abroad with at least two consecutive years in accredited American-curriculum schools; applicants abroad in international schools outside Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area
C, local and regionalApplicants currently in schools in Hong Kong or the Greater China area; invitations to assess depend on application volume and the school's resources

Qualified first

Priority only matters for qualified applicants. I prepare children for the HKIS assessment so that, whatever their group, they meet the requirements clearly.

What debentures change

  • Family Debenture, HK$3 million: supports several children of the same immediate family, and is the only one transferable, to the purchaser's children or grandchildren.
  • Standard Corporate Debenture, HK$3 million: a company supports one child's application.
  • Comprehensive Corporate Debenture, HK$5 million: a company supports several children of one family.

Debentures are non-interest-bearing, retain their value and cannot be sold. They are optional and do not guarantee admission; their effect is to place qualified applicants at the top of the pool. Applications for the next school year must be made by 1 November.

What families in group C should know

For families already in Hong Kong schools, the first hurdle is being invited to assess at all, which depends on volume and resources. A complete file submitted early, before the 1 October or 1 November deadline, and strong reports in English matter even more for this group.

Planning around the pool

A place in the pool is a real possibility, not a promise. Families should secure a good alternative school, keep HKIS informed of any change, and follow the OpenApply checklist. Decisions start in March and continue on a rolling basis as places open.
