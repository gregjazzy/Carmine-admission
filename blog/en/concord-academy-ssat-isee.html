Concord Academy reads applications holistically, but it does not make testing optional: every applicant must submit an official score report from the SSAT or the ISEE. For families coming from schools where test preparation is not part of the culture, the timing and the choice of test deserve early thought.

Choosing between the SSAT and the ISEE

CA accepts both without preference. The two tests cover similar ground, verbal reasoning, reading, mathematics and a writing sample, but differ in format and style. Students often do better on one than the other, so a practice test of each, taken in realistic conditions, is the most reliable way to choose.

Planning the dates

With a window running from June to mid-January, the sensible plan is a first attempt in the autumn, leaving room for a second sitting before the deadline. A score from a test taken after 15 January cannot be considered at all, so the last sitting should not be left to chance.

Not sure which test to choose? Let's run a diagnostic.

What the score does

The score sits alongside current grades, the previous year's report, three recommendations, the interview and the family's forms. CA's committee looks for academic performance, but also curiosity, character, maturity and potential for growth. A good score opens the file; it does not close the decision. In a holistic review, it is one element among several.

Two tests for students abroad

A student applying from outside the United States whose first language is not English has two scores to send by 15 January: the SSAT or ISEE, and the English proficiency test. Both require registration, a test date and time for results to reach the school, so families abroad should book both early in the autumn, and the Vericant interview alongside them.
