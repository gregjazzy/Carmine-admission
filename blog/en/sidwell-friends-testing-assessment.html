At Sidwell Friends, what is assessed depends on age. I know the school through my students at Sidwell Friends. For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year with applications to leading schools in Washington, DC.

What the school says

The youngest come for a group visit. For other grades, students register for the Sidwell Friends School Assessment. Standardised in-school test results are requested; for upper grades, they are required, while the SSAT or ISEE remain optional.

Preparing the assessment

I prepare your child for the school's assessment and, if useful, the SSAT or ISEE.

What the school does not say

Optional does not mean useless: a strong SSAT or ISEE can support a file, an average one does not help. Decide with full knowledge.
