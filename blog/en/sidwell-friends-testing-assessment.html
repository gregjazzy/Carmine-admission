Testing and the Sidwell assessment

Sidwell Friends combines three kinds of testing in its application, and they do not carry the same weight. Its own assessment, required for middle and upper school applicants, is run by the school on its campus. Standardized tests that the child already takes at their current school are required from third grade. External admissions tests such as the SSAT or ISEE are optional.

The in-house assessment

The Sidwell Friends School Assessment lasts 90 minutes and has three sections: mathematics, reading and writing. The school runs its own sessions on campus so that every applicant is assessed under the same conditions. Registration is a scheduling step, done by December 15, and registering early gives the family first choice of dates; students with documented testing accommodations should say so at registration.

Ninety minutes for three sections leaves roughly half an hour each. Writing, in particular, has to be organised quickly: a clear point, developed with examples, in a short time.

School testing and optional tests

In-school testing means the standardized assessments the child already takes through their current school; it costs the family nothing and gives the committee a longer view. If a family is unsure whether its school's tests count, the admissions team can confirm. The SSAT or ISEE is optional because it measures something narrower, and the school states that skipping it puts no one at a disadvantage. It can help a child whose school record does not show their level well. If the family chooses it, it should plan early, since testing windows can be tighter than the December 31 deadline suggests.
