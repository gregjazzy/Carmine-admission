The required interview

Book a conversation

At Blair Academy, the interview is part of a complete application, not an extra. The school calls a personal interview the most helpful element for its Admission Committee, and asks every applicant to visit Blair, see the school and meet its people.

The formats

FormatDetail
On campusStudent-led walking tour, then an interview with an admission officer; about two hours
DatesFrom 3 September to 15 January; limited times, best booked early
VirtualFor families unable to come to campus; times in Eastern Time
Mainland ChinaVericant interview, which fulfils the requirement; a follow-up call in January is possible

Booking

An interview can only be scheduled after the online inquiry form is complete; the family then emails or calls the admission office. International applicants at school abroad must first submit part one of the application, the student profile, with payment. For an on-campus visit, international candidates send the application and test scores, TOEFL and SSAT, before booking. All visits must take place before 15 January.

What the interview shows

Blair looks for kind students of high character with a real passion for learning, a strong work ethic and curiosity, who will take part fully in school life and respect different viewpoints. An admission officer can see in a conversation what a transcript cannot: how a student talks about a book, a project or a team, how they listen, and whether they genuinely want this school.

Has your child booked a Blair visit? Let's prepare the conversation.

Making the most of the visit

  • Use the tour: students lead it, and their answers say more about daily life than any brochure.
  • Prepare a few questions about the courses, activities or boarding life that interest the student.
  • Arrive with examples of what the student does and enjoys, not rehearsed lines.

Visiting before the season

Families who want to see Blair before the interview season can join the Fall Preview, held on a Saturday in mid-September, or request a student-led campus tour in the summer, between June and mid-August. These visits do not replace the interview, but they help a student arrive at it already familiar with the place.
