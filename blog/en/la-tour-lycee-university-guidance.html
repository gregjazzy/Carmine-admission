La Tour lycée: specialities, guidance and university

Paris schools November 2026 5 min read

In lycée, La Tour has a motto: "S'élever ensemble", rising together. Behind it is a demanding lycée that prepares students for the bac and higher education with a well-honed method, and one distinctive feature: two guidance services, one for study in France, the other for English-speaking universities.

Specialities and pace

In première, students choose three specialities from seven: maths, physics-chemistry, life and earth sciences, economics and social sciences, history-geography and geopolitics, humanities, literature and philosophy, and English literature. They must keep three languages. For practice, supervised tests on Saturday mornings, mock exams twice a year, and French and language orals during the year.

The best doors, after La Tour

I support lycée students towards the most selective programmes: specialities chosen at the right time, a solid Parcoursup application, carefully built applications abroad. Since 2021, my students have been admitted to Columbia, Stanford, Imperial, EPFL, Bocconi, McGill and King's College.

Guidance over three years

Guidance starts in seconde, with self-knowledge, discovering careers and choosing specialities. In première, compulsory sessions and careers breakfasts with alumni. In terminale, presentations of study routes, Parcoursup support, help with cover letters for highly selective programmes and interview practice. Every première and terminale student has a yearly personality interview before a panel of two parents, and guidance evenings are run with Saint-Jean-de-Passy, Gerson, Blomet and Notre-Dame-des-Oiseaux.

Is your child in lycée at La Tour? Let's talk about their plans: their application is built from seconde.

Towards English-speaking universities

For more than twenty years, the Anglophone Section has supported applications to English-language programmes in Europe and abroad: cover letters, CVs, recommendation letters, translation of documents, predicted grades. For a student aiming for London, Canada or the United States, it is valuable support, best complemented by a sharp strategy on the university list and essays.

How I support families

I have students at La Tour, as at the other leading Paris schools, and every year I support around fifteen families with their admissions. For a La Tour student, I work on what decides entry to the most selective programmes: the choice of specialities, the level in maths, the Parcoursup application, applications abroad, essays and interviews.
