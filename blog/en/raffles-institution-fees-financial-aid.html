Raffles fees and financial aid

Raffles Institution states that it wants every student to experience the full Rafflesian education whatever their family's finances. As an independent school, it sets its own fees; for Singaporean citizens, several national and school schemes reduce the cost considerably.

Fees and payment

Fees for the 2027 academic year will be published by 15 December 2026. They can be paid annually in advance or monthly, by GIRO, cash or PayNow; the school provides a GIRO form for automatic payment.

How the bursary works

The Independent School Bursary is a Ministry of Education scheme. Families can apply at any point in the school year; the subsidy takes effect from the month the application is approved and is not backdated. A family that becomes eligible during the year should therefore apply without waiting, through the Ministry's online portal for financial assistance.

The Raffles Scholarship

The school's own scholarship is designed so that students from all backgrounds can take part fully in school activities alongside their peers. It comes on top of the bursary: eligible families are invited to apply once their ISB application is confirmed. The UPLIFT Scholarship needs no separate application; eligible students are notified by email.

Beyond fees

Families on lower incomes can also turn to IMDA's Digital Access at Home scheme for subsidised broadband and devices. For enrichment activities and purchases, Raffles invites families to contact the school directly.
