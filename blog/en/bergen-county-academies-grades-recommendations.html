Grades, records and recommendations

The entrance test is a single morning. The school record behind it covers four years. When BCA's committee reviews a file in January, it reads report cards from 5th to 8th grade, standardised test scores and three teacher recommendations alongside the test and essay.

What the counsellor sends

After the application is submitted, BCA sends a transcript request to the middle school counsellor or administrator named in it. The school returns the cumulative record: grade reports from 5th to 8th grade, attendance, discipline and health records, special education records and evaluations where relevant, and state and standardised test scores, including the NJSLA. Students at private schools have their standardised scores sent by their counsellor. The counsellor also confirms any testing accommodations.

The three recommendations

SubjectWho can write it
EnglishA teacher from 7th or 8th grade
ScienceA teacher from 7th or 8th grade
MathThe current 8th grade math teacher, or a high school math teacher

All three are mandatory, and no others are needed, except that AVPA applicants may send one more letter from an art, music or theatre instructor.

Grades or course level?

BCA states that while grades are certainly important, it also looks at the level of the courses. A student who takes some courses at a high school should have both the middle school and the high school send transcripts. BCA also notes that students who took algebra and geometry in middle school typically do better on the entrance test.

Where files go wrong

  • A misspelt email: requests go to the addresses entered; a ".com" for a ".org" delays the recommendation. Addresses can be corrected until 1 December.
  • The wrong math teacher: only the current 8th grade teacher qualifies, even if a 7th grade teacher knew the student better.
  • A late application: teachers can only respond once the application is submitted, and files must be complete by mid-January.

There is no need to call to check whether a file is complete: if something is missing, BCA contacts the school directly.
