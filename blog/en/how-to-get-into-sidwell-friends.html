How to get into Sidwell Friends

Sidwell Friends is a Quaker day school founded in 1883, from PreKindergarten to twelfth grade, with its Lower School in Bethesda and its Middle and Upper Schools in northwest Washington. It admits most new students at seven entry points, in small numbers, and asks families to complete every step of the application by December 31.

I work with families applying to Sidwell Friends and similar schools. Here is how admission works for 2027-28.

Three December deadlines

Everything starts with a Ravenna account and the Family Information Form, due December 1, with a $75 fee that can be waived for hardship. By December 15, families register for the group visit or the Sidwell Friends School Assessment, have teachers submit their confidential recommendations, provide last year's report cards and hold the parent meeting. By December 31 come the current first-term report card, and from Grade 3 the school's standardized testing; from Grade 6, the applicant video as well.

The school asks families to send only what is requested, so that every applicant goes through the same process.

Assessment and testing

For Grades 6, 7 and 9, applicants sit a 90-minute in-house assessment on campus in mathematics, reading and writing, under the same conditions for all. Standardized testing from the current school is required; an SSAT or ISEE score is optional, and a family that does not send one is not at a disadvantage.

Cost and aid

Tuition in 2026-27 runs from $55,250 in the early Lower School grades to $62,330 in the Upper School. Sidwell Friends describes its financial aid as one of the most generous in the DC area and states that family income should never be a barrier.
