The SHSAT year is short and fixed. Registration happens in October, the test in November, offers in March. For a student aiming at Stuyvesant, the real calendar starts much earlier, because the test rewards months of preparation, not weeks.

The official dates, fall 2026 test

WhenWhat
Early October to 30 October 2026Registration in MySchools, with the ranked list of schools
After the deadlineTest tickets available, showing date and location
Saturday 14 or Sunday 15 November 2026Eighth graders from 6-12, K-12, charter, private, parochial or independent schools, and homeschooled students, at a central site
Wednesday 18 November 2026Eighth graders from public middle schools ending in 8th grade, at their own school during the school day
Saturday 21 November 2026All ninth graders, at a central site; an alternate date for those who cannot test on Saturday
March 2027Offers and scores, with Discovery information where relevant
Spring 2027Test views, by sign-up through the SHSAT Family Survey

Central sites are assigned by school district; Stuyvesant itself hosts students from districts 1 to 4.

Working backwards from November

  • Spring of 7th grade: a diagnostic on both sections, to see the distance to the target.
  • Summer: the heaviest preparation, especially in math content not yet taught at school and in reading speed.
  • September and October: full timed practice on the computer-adaptive format, using the city's Student Readiness Tool and practice tests on the NYC SHSAT Portal, and building the ranked list.
  • November: rest before the test; the work is done.

A calendar that peaks in November

Accommodations have earlier deadlines

Students with IEPs or 504 Plans, and English language learners, have accommodations entered or confirmed by their school during registration. For private or parochial school students with similar plans, documentation must reach the Department of Education at least three weeks before the registration deadline. Emergency accommodations are possible for injuries or conditions arising within thirty days of the test.

A second chance in 9th grade

A student who does not get a seat in 8th grade can register again in 9th grade, the first time in that grade, and test on the 9th grade date for entry in 10th grade. It is a second chance, not a plan A.
