Aditya Birla World Academy does not only assess children; it also meets their families. After the assessment stage, shortlisted families are invited to a meeting, the fourth of the school's six admission stages, before the decision is made.

Where the meeting sits

StageWhat happens
1Online enquiry
2Application and documents, on invitation
3Assessment and interaction: entrance test, parent group discussion and/or child interaction, depending on the grade
4Family meeting, for shortlisted families
5Decision
6Fee payment and confirmation

What it is for

ABWA states the purpose plainly: to understand the child's environment, values and aspirations. For a school that places emotional intelligence and values at the centre of its education, and that describes the parent-student-teacher partnership as essential to wellbeing, knowing the family is part of knowing the child.

Clear about your child, clear about the school

I help parents think through what they want to say about their child and why ABWA fits, so the meeting is a genuine exchange.

Book a conversation

Preparing honestly

  • Know the school's values: curiosity, perseverance, adaptability, compassion and integrity are the qualities it hopes to develop.
  • Describe the child as they are: interests, strengths, what they find hard, how they react to change.
  • Explain the family's choice: why this school, this curriculum, at this moment.
  • Ask real questions: about pastoral care, the curriculum or the transition.

Invited to a family meeting? Let's prepare it together.

The parent group discussion

For some grades, the assessment stage itself already includes a parent group discussion. It is another sign of the importance ABWA gives to families: parents are seen as partners in the child's education, not just as applicants on the child's behalf.

Values in daily school life

The meeting makes more sense once a family sees how values show up at ABWA every day: a school-wide social-emotional learning programme, regular classroom circles led by homeroom teachers, lessons built around real-life dilemmas, and student initiatives such as the Anti-Bullying Squad and the Mental Health Champions. A family that shares these priorities will find it easy to say so; one that does not may want to reconsider.

After the meeting

The school informs families of its decision after evaluation. Admission is confirmed only once the fee payment is completed within the period given.
