Book a conversation

Andover asks for four recommendations, all sent through Gateway: the principal or counselor, the current English and maths teachers, and a personal recommendation. A special interest recommendation, from a coach or art teacher for instance, is optional.

The rules to know

The English and maths recommendations must come from current teachers, not former ones. A former teacher who knows the student well can write the personal recommendation. And a teacher who does not speak English may write in their own language; an official translation helps but is not required.

Recommendations that carry weight

I help you choose who will recommend your child, brief those teachers on what Andover expects, and keep to the deadlines.

What makes the difference

A teacher at a school outside the United States has often never written for an American boarding school. Without guidance, they write three polite lines. Ask early, tell them what the school looks for, and give them time. The personal recommendation should show the child outside class: a chance to hear from someone who has seen them commit.
