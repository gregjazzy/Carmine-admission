Newark Academy relies on the student's current teachers to understand how they work in class.

What is asked

Teacher recommendations, on how the student collaborates, thinks and contributes as a classmate and community member. In addition, a coach, teacher or mentor can email a letter directly to the school, and the student can attach other materials to the form. Recommendations are due by January 11, 2027, with the conversations, testing and current grades.
