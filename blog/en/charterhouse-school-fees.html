Charterhouse: school fees 2026-2027

Charterhouse, Godalming, from the air. Photo Tim Sheerman-Chase, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0

UK boarding schools November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

In 2026-2027, Charterhouse costs £21,015 a term for boarding and £16,369 for day pupils, including VAT. Over three terms, that is about £63,000 and £49,100 a year. Registration costs £450, non-refundable, and an offer is accepted with a deposit.

Support

The school offers means-tested bursaries to widen access, and scholarships in several fields: academic, art, music, drama and sport.
