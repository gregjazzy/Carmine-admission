Boarding schools worldwide

Michaelhouse open days and weekends

Book a conversation

Michaelhouse asks families to make a large decision: five years of full boarding for a boy of 13, often far from home. It compensates with an unusually rich set of ways to meet the school before committing. Each has its own audience, and its own use.

Parent information evenings

For: parents at the beginning of their search, registered or not.
When and where: from May of Grade 6 into the following months, in cities across South Africa, from Cape Town and Bloemfontein to Durban, Johannesburg and Pretoria, in Nairobi, and online.
What happens: parents meet the Rector, staff, Old Boys and current parents, alongside other families going through the same questions.
What to take from it: a first sense of the school's tone, and the right questions for the next step.

Day@Michaelhouse

For: families who have not yet registered, though everyone is welcome.
What happens: presentations in the theatre, a guided group tour for parents, and activities for the children.
What to take from it: the campus itself, 680 hectares in the Midlands, its ten Houses around six red-brick quadrangles, and whether the family can picture its son there.

From the first visit to the offer

I help families use these events to decide, then prepare the boy for the moment that weighs most: his interview with the Rector, and, if he aims for one, the scholarship weekend.

The tour and interview

For: registered boys and their parents, during Grade 6; for the 2027 intake, these began in late July.
What happens: a tour of the school and the personal interview with the Rector or a Deputy Rector.
What to take from it: this is the only event in the list that is part of the admission file. It deserves preparation.

Mums@Michaelhouse

For: mothers of registered Grade 8 applicants.
What happens: an overnight stay at the school, meeting current Michaelhouse mothers and living something of the routines of a boarding day.
What to take from it: for a mother uneasy about full boarding, the answers of other mothers who made the same choice.

Unsure which events are worth the trip? Let's plan them together.

Weekend@Michaelhouse

For: all boys registered for the Grade 8 intake, in September of Grade 6.
What happens: boys explore the campus, try the facilities, share meals in the dining rooms and spend a night in a boarding House.
What to take from it: the boy's own reaction. How he talks about the weekend afterwards tells a family more than any brochure.

The scholarship weekend is another matter

The scholarship weekend in February of Grade 7 is not a discovery event: it is by invitation and it is assessed. Boys stay overnight, as on Weekend@Michaelhouse, but they are there to show their level.
