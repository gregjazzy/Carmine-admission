Concord Academy states that financial constraints should not deter any prospective student from applying. Its aid programme backs that up with three commitments: it meets 100% of a student's computed financial need, it gives aid only as grants with no loans to repay, and it publishes enough figures for a family to form a realistic idea before applying.

The programme in figures, 2026-27

  • Budget: $6.7 million, about 22% of tuition revenue.
  • Students aided: 100 new and returning students, about 23% of the school.
  • Range of grants: from $7,400 to full tuition.
  • Average grant: $74,907 for boarding students, $47,271 for day students.

Income is only one factor: family size, siblings' tuition, assets and home equity are also considered. The table shows that families with high incomes can still receive substantial grants. The school also publishes a detailed financial aid FAQ, and its Director of Financial Aid answers families' questions directly.

Admission first

Aid follows admission. I prepare students for the SSAT or ISEE, the interview and the written file, the parts that decide whether a grant becomes relevant.

How to apply

  1. Start the financial aid application on the Clarity website and create a login.
  2. All parents, including spouses if remarried, complete the Clarity application.
  3. Submit by 15 January; later applications cannot be guaranteed consideration.

Fee waivers for the aid application apply automatically to families who meet certain income guidelines.

Unsure whether to apply for aid? Let's talk it through.

The rule to remember

All aid is disbursed on 10 March, the day decisions are released, and the school has no budget for returning students who were not funded at entry. A family that might need help at any point during the child's years at CA should therefore apply on entry, even if the need seems distant.

More than tuition

Aid also helps with books, travel, athletic equipment, computers, school trips, music lessons, tutoring and the college application process, so an aided student can take part fully in school life.
