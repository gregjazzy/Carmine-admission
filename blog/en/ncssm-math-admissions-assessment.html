NCSSM: the math admissions assessment

The NCSSM entrance in Durham, North Carolina. Photo Warren LeMay, Wikimedia Commons, CC0

Book a conversation

NCSSM no longer considers the SAT or ACT. But for the residential programme, it sets its own math assessment. For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year towards the most selective schools, and I have students at NCSSM.

What the school says

The assessment has 30 questions, in 40 minutes, without a calculator. It covers pre-algebra, algebra and geometry, material usually taught in 7th and 8th grade, Math 1 and algebra. Applicants register through their application account.

Preparing for the math assessment

I prepare your child for NCSSM's assessment: the basics reviewed thoroughly, calculation without a calculator, and time management over 40 minutes.

What the school does not say

No expected score is published. But the test covers older material: a student strong in high school maths can lose points on basics they think they have mastered.

Why good students fail

No calculator

Many high school students have lost the habit of calculating by hand, quickly and accurately.

Time

One question every 80 seconds: you need to know when to skip and come back.

Geometry
