Grade 1 entry and age at ISF

Grade 1 at The ISF Academy is the first entry point with a language requirement: applicants must show grade-appropriate proficiency in both Chinese, spoken in Putonghua, and English. It is also where the school's age rules matter most, since children born late in the year can be considered for two grades.

I help families decide and prepare for this entry. Here is how it works for 2027-28.

The age window

Grade 1 applicants for 2027-28 are born between September 1, 2020 and December 31, 2021, and must be at least five years and eight months old on August 31, 2027. The age range in each grade is typically sixteen months. A child born between September 1 and December 31 can apply for the lower grade, the higher one, or both.

For an autumn-born child, applying for both can make sense, but each assessment must be prepared: the Grade 1 one requires both languages, the Foundation Year one does not.

The assessment

Stage 1 is a group assessment of about an hour, in groups of twelve, with half the activities in English and half in Putonghua, held between November 19 and 24, 2026. Selected applicants return in January 2027 for a parent-child meeting with a senior member of the school; parents can choose Cantonese, English or Putonghua, and the child is interviewed in English and Putonghua. Results are notified by February 26, 2027. Applications close on November 5, 2026.

Language at five

In Grade 1, about 70 percent of teaching is in Putonghua. A child who arrives with strong English and little Chinese will be assessed, and then taught, mostly in the language they know less. For such children, the Foundation Year route, with no language requirement, can be the more realistic entry; for children already comfortable in both languages, Grade 1 is a natural step.
