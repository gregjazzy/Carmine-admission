Choate: day or boarding

The Andrew Mellon Library, Choate Rosemary Hall, Wallingford. Photo Daderot, Wikimedia Commons, public domain

US boarding schools November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

For a family living in Connecticut, Choate offers two doors: boarding and day. The choice is made when the application is submitted, and it is made once.

What the school says

Only Connecticut families may apply as day students. You cannot apply as both: you must choose, and any later change is requested in writing. Both pools are competitive, and the school recommends the day option for students within a reasonable commute, while considering situations where commuting would be especially difficult. Many day students come from New Haven County, others from Hartford and Middlesex counties.

The right choice, the right file

Day or boarding, the preparation is the same: interview, essays, recommendations. I prepare your child for each.

What it changes

Price first: $57,610 a year for day against $74,530 for boarding in 2026-2027, technology fee included. Life next: the day student goes home, the boarder lives the school day and night. But day entry is not an easier door: the school says so clearly.

I know the school through my students at Choate. For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year with applications to leading schools in New England. I help you decide between the two, then prepare your child for the interview and the file. For the procedure, see how to get into Choate.
