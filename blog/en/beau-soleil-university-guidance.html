At Beau Soleil, university preparation does not wait for the last year. The school's University Counselling Team begins working with students as early as Grade 9, learning their interests, strengths and ambitions, so that course choices and activities in the following years point somewhere.

How the guidance works

StageSupport
From Grade 9One-to-one sessions with a team of three counsellors
ThroughoutChecking that academic programmes and co-curricular activities fit the student's goals
Grades 11 and 12Help with applications and personal statements; trips to universities in Switzerland and abroad; visiting university representatives on campus

The class of 2026

Beau Soleil's 2026 graduates applied to 141 universities and received 265 offers. Destinations named by the school include King's College London, Imperial College, University of the Arts London, Manchester, Edinburgh, Warwick and St Andrews in the United Kingdom; Vanderbilt, Boston University, Northeastern, Miami, Loyola Marymount and George Washington in the United States; and Sydney, Utrecht, EHL Hospitality Business School and the American University of Paris elsewhere.

A wide spread

Most graduates head for the United Kingdom and the United States, while others choose Switzerland, the rest of Europe or other parts of the world. The range reflects both the international mix of students and the two senior pathways: the IB Diploma, recognised by universities worldwide, and the US High School Diploma, oriented towards business, hospitality and technology.

What families can do

The counselling team does the detailed work. Families help most by encouraging the student to explore subjects early, to take part seriously in activities they enjoy, and to visit universities when the chance comes. The IB results, 35.0 points on average in 2026, are the academic foundation on which applications rest. Weekly updates from the boarding house and the Principal, and the Parent Portal, let families follow progress from a distance.

How I help

I have students at Beau Soleil, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools and universities. For Beau Soleil students, my role is academic: strengthening the subjects that decide an IB score and a university offer.
