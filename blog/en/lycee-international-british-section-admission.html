British Section of the Lycée International: admission, assessments and cost

Schools near Paris November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

The British Section does not recruit a single profile. It distinguishes three cases, and a family's first task is to know which one their child is in. I follow students at the Lycée International, and every year I support around fifteen families towards the most sought-after bilingual schools in the Paris region.

Three profiles, three doors

Bilingual: native, age-appropriate English and French. Anglophone: native English but limited French, leading to the Français Spécial class. Francophone: native French and advanced English, with two dedicated entries, Sixième Fast-Track and Seconde Accueil. In primary, pupils are full-time, at the Lycée International or a partner school in Le Pecq, or come two half-days a week; from collège, all are full-time.

A place in the British Section

The British Section's assessments cannot be prepared with past papers: they measure language and aptitude. I know what they look at, and I prepare children to show their real level.

The assessments

The section publishes no past papers: it wants to measure natural aptitude and language, not "coached" knowledge. In nursery and primary, a one-to-one interview, plus in primary a written reading and creative task. In collège and lycée, written English comprehension and composition exams, then an oral for selected candidates. For Français Spécial, the assessment focuses on mother-tongue English and the aptitude to learn a second language quickly. Sixième Fast-Track follows the collège format.

The calendar

Enquiry from October to December, with information meetings in November and December; application from December to January; assessments from January to March; decisions in April, when the admissions committee recommends candidates to the head of the French school. A waiting list exists for qualified candidates.

Bilingual, anglophone or francophone? Tell me about your child: I will tell you which door to aim for.

What it costs

Application fees: €160 in nursery, €200 in primary, €250 in secondary, €150 for Sixième Fast-Track and Seconde Accueil. Tuition when the family pays: €4,051 from the second year of nursery to CM2, €4,427 in collège, €5,746 in lycée; when an employer pays, €7,642, €7,885 and €9,272. On admission, a registration fee of €1,500, or €1,750 in lycée.

What I do for your family

I measure the child's English at the level their entry route requires, then prepare them for the written assessments and the oral, without cramming, since the section does not reward it. To compare with the other large English-speaking section, see the American Section.
