NMH tuition and fees in 2026-27

Memorial Chapel, Northfield Mount Hermon, Massachusetts. Photo Daderot, Wikimedia Commons, CC0

The figure that circulates for Northfield Mount Hermon is $79,382, the boarding rate for 2026-27. It covers tuition, room and board. It is not the bill. Mandatory fees, health insurance and, for most families, tuition refund insurance come on top, and the line items differ for boarding, day and international students.

I go through this budget with every family I work with on NMH. Here it is, line by line.

The three rates

2026-27Tuition (and room and board)Mandatory feesTotal
Boarding student$79,382$1,625$81,007
Day student$52,181$3,025$55,206
International student$79,382$2,675$82,057

The boarding fees cover activities, laundry, technology and health services. Day students pay higher fees because their package includes meals. The international package adds health insurance and an international student fee that covers the maintenance of the Form I-20 and SEVIS records.

What is not included

Textbooks and required supplies average about $500 a year. Every student must bring a laptop. Weekend activities, athletic equipment and extracurricular costs vary with the child. Health insurance is mandatory for everyone: students on an F-1 visa must join the school plan, which is already in their fees, while all other students must show proof of primary coverage.

Then there is tuition refund insurance. It is mandatory unless the full year's tuition and fees are paid by July 1, and it is billed at 1.85 percent of the family's tuition responsibility, meaning tuition minus any grant. On full boarding tuition, that is about $1,469.

The real annual bill

For a full-pay boarding family that pays in installments, the year comes to about $83,000: $81,007 in tuition and fees, about $500 in books, about $1,469 in refund insurance, before the laptop, travel and the student's own health coverage. For an international family in the same situation, it is about $84,000 before flights. Paying the full year by July 1 removes the refund insurance; whether that is worth it depends on how the family weighs liquidity against roughly $1,500.

Weighing NMH against other boarding schools? Tell me about your list: the real costs rarely line up with the published ones.

Need-based aid changes the picture

More than 40 percent of NMH families receive need-based aid, and the school has distributed $14.5 million. It commits to meeting 100 percent of the demonstrated need of every admitted student, domestic or international. The average award in 2026-27 is $57,472 for boarding students and $35,802 for day students. For an aided boarder at that average, the refund insurance falls to about $405, since it is calculated on tuition minus the grant.

Aid is requested through Clarity by January 15, alongside the admission application, and the award is known within twenty-four hours of the March 10 decision. A family that hesitates should apply: the answer comes with the admission decision, not weeks later.

When to pay

Initial payments are due by July 1, with payment plan options set out in the enrollment contract. That date matters for families abroad who transfer money in another currency: exchange rates and transfer delays need to be planned in June, not on the last day.
