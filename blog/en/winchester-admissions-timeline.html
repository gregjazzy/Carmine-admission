Winchester College admissions timeline: Year 5 to Year 9

Winchester College, the Chapel and Chamber Court from above. Photo Tim Sheerman-Chase, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0

UK boarding schools November 2026 6 min read

A boy joining Winchester in September 2029 will have started the process in the autumn of 2025 or 2026, when he was nine or ten. Most of what decides his place happens in six months of Year 6. The rest of the calendar matters too, but for different reasons: setting, scholarships and settling in.

Year 5: look, and start reading

Open events for 13+ families run through the year, from 9.30am to noon, with additional Tuesday and Thursday morning visits in term and virtual events for those who cannot travel. Families meet a Housemaster and other staff. Families considering the 11+ route tick a box to visit The Pilgrims' School the same day.

This is also the year to build the habit Winchester values above all others for its assessments: wide reading for pleasure, and conversation about ideas at home.

Year 6: the year that decides

When (2029 entry)Step
By 1 October 2026Registration, £480, with reports, CV, referee and identity documents; tick the bursary box if needed
16 to 20 November 2026General Ability Testing, online
11 to 15 January 2027Interview and writing assessment in Winchester, for shortlisted boys
March 2027Offers, with bursary decisions
By 1 June 2027Acceptance and £2,000 acceptance fee

A boy who begins to prepare in November is late: by then the file is in and the GAT is days away. The useful months are the summer before Year 6 and the early autumn.

Year by year, prepared

We plan each family's Winchester calendar from Year 5 and prepare the boy for every stage: reading and writing, the GAT, the interview, then Election if he aims for College.

Missed the date?

Registration after 1 October of Year 6, or applications for boys in Year 7 or 8, go through the Registrar's Route, processed as places become available. It is not a closed door, but it depends entirely on the places that remain.

Is your son about to start Year 6? Write to me now; October is close.

Year 7: a House, and a first taste

Boys who have accepted a place are placed in a boarding house and invited to taster lessons and activities. They meet their Housemaster, their matron and the boys they will live with. Families with a previous connection to the College could request a House at registration; for everyone else, placement happens now.

Year 8: Entrance, Election, awards, induction

  • Winchester Entrance: a set of academic assessments used to plan teaching and set classes; an updated school reference is requested beforehand. It no longer decides admission.
  • Election: for boys of exceptional academic promise, the scholarship examination taken instead of Entrance. For 2027: application by 21 January, papers and interviews 26 to 28 April, results 11 May.
  • Music and Sport Awards: assessed during Year 8.
  • Parent contract and deposit: £4,000 for UK residents, £20,700 for families abroad.
  • Visa: families who ticked the box are contacted after Easter.
  • Choices and induction: electives and music lessons chosen; an induction day in person.

Year 9

In September, boys arrive in their House, in classes set using their Entrance results, with five years ahead of them: GCSEs, A levels and Div throughout.

How I accompany families through the calendar

Reading and conversation in Year 5; the GAT format, timed handwritten writing and mock interviews in Year 6; Election preparation in Year 8 for boys who aim for College or a Founder's Scholarship. At each step, the boy knows what is coming and the family knows the dates.
