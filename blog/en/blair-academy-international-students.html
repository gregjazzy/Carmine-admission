About 17 % of Blair Academy's students are international. The school welcomes them through the same process as American applicants, with a few additions and a set order of steps that families abroad should know before they start.

Visa or residence: the definition

An applicant is international if they need an F-1 student visa and a Form I-20 from Blair to study in the United States, or if, whatever their citizenship or visa status, their family's main residence is outside the United States. The application fee is $140 rather than $75.

Four steps in a fixed order

StepWhat to do
1Complete the online inquiry
2Submit part one of the application, the student profile, with payment
3Send test scores: SSAT and, if required, an English test
4Book the interview: on campus after the application and scores arrive, or by Vericant for applicants at school in mainland China

All visits and interviews must take place before 15 January. Transcripts must include English translations.

Proving English

Applicants whose first language is not English must show their level through the TOEFL or TOEFL Essentials (Blair's code is 9415), the IELTS or the Duolingo English Test. The test is waived for students who have spent two years at an international school or a junior boarding school in the United States. Blair has no ESL programme: every student must be fluent.

Applicants in mainland China

Because of the volume of requests, applicants at school in mainland China interview through Vericant rather than Zoom. The Vericant interview fulfils Blair's requirement, though the admission team may follow up in January. Blair's application fee does not cover Vericant's own charges.

Aid is open to them

Blair offers financial aid to international students, several of whom currently receive support, and meets the demonstrated need of any admitted family. A separate guide exists for families who do not file US taxes.
