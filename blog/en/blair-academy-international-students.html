For Blair Academy, a student is international if they need an F-1 visa or their family's main residence is outside the US. International students make up 17% of the student body, from 27 countries.

What changes

Fees: $140 instead of $75. Interview: submit part one of the application and pay first, then contact the school; students in mainland China use Vericant before January 15. English: TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo if English is not the first language, unless two years at an international school or US junior boarding school. Blair has no ESL programme: the student must be fluent. Aid: open to international students.
