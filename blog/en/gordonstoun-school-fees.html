Gordonstoun: fees and support

Gordonstoun does not publish its fees openly: the school sends its full termly fee breakdown to families who request it. What it says publicly, however, is that financial circumstances should never be a barrier to attending.

Support

Bursaries are means-tested, funded by the school and its supporters, and need not be repaid. The school prioritises applications from pupils who have earned scholarships. Scholarships themselves are honorary: they carry no automatic fee reduction, but give access to benefits such as free one-to-one music lessons for music scholars, personalised mentoring and leadership events.

A place is won on the file

What opens Gordonstoun's door, and often that of bursaries, is the interview and, where relevant, the scholarship assessments. I prepare your child for each.
