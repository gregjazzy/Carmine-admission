Applying to Deerfield from abroad

Deerfield does not publish a share of international students on its admission pages, but one figure says a good deal: 14% of the students it currently funds with financial aid are international. The school meets the full demonstrated need of admitted international students, and its process can be completed entirely from abroad. A few rules change along the way.

Before the application

The Candidate Profile costs $125 for students living outside the United States, against $60 for those in the US or Canada. A need-based fee waiver can be requested once the profile is completed, until January 31. The profile opens the Deerfield Admission Portal, which has its own checklist; families abroad, who cannot drop by the office, should check it carefully.

The interview without travel

The Zoom interview lasts about an hour: a one-on-one conversation with the student, then time for the parents with an admission officer. For a family abroad, two things deserve care. The time difference: choose a slot when the child is awake and at their best, not one squeezed in before school. And the language: both child and parents should be ready to talk naturally in English. Slots are scarcer in December and January, which matters more when only certain hours work across time zones.

English: the three-year rule and the home rule

A student adds the IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT or Duolingo to the SSAT or ISEE if English has not been their main language of instruction for at least three years, or if English is not the main language spoken at home. Many children at international schools meet the first condition and still fall under the second. If no test centre is available in the country, the admission office asks families to get in touch.

The graded essay from a non-English school

Deerfield's graded essay must come from a current course and carry the teacher's comments. For a child taught in another language, the choice is between a substantial paper in that language and a shorter one from an English class. Neither is wrong; what matters is that the reader can see the child thinking and responding to feedback. The same three current teachers must write recommendations, and teachers unfamiliar with American letters benefit from a clear explanation of what is expected.

Financial aid from abroad

International families are eligible for need-based aid up to 100%, and if admitted, their full demonstrated need is met. The Clarity application costs $65. Non-Canadian international families upload a current bank statement, salary verification or non-US tax filing by January 15. As for every family, applying for aid may affect admission when the budget is stretched, so the file itself has to be strong.

What I do for families abroad

Among my students are several at Deerfield, and a large share of the fifteen or so families I work with each year live outside the United States. With them, I start by checking whether the English rule applies and measuring the gap, then prepare the SSAT or ISEE and the English test together. We choose the graded essay from the child's real schoolwork, work on the short answer and personal essay in English, and rehearse the Zoom interview at the hour it will actually take place.
