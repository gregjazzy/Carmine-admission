Deerfield: applying from abroad

The Main School Building, Deerfield Academy, Massachusetts. Photo John Phelan, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

US boarding schools November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Deerfield welcomes students from all over the world, and its process allows it without travel: the interview can be held online. A few rules change for families living outside the United States.

What changes

Application fee: $125. English test: required, on top of the SSAT or ISEE, if English has not been the language of instruction for at least three years or is not the language spoken at home: IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT or Duolingo, taken in the same date window. If tests are not offered in the country, the school asks families to contact it. Financial aid: international families are eligible, up to 100% of demonstrated need.

Preparing for Deerfield from afar

I prepare students all over the world, online, for the SSAT, the English test, the interview and Deerfield's three essays.

What weighs

Two tests instead of one, an interview in English, three essays including a graded paper which, for a student schooled in another language, raises a real choice. All of this takes earlier preparation than families expect.
