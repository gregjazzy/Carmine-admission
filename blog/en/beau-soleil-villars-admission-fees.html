Beau Soleil: admission and fees

Beau Soleil on the Route du Village, Villars-sur-Ollon. Photo En08sijp, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Boarding schools November 2026 6 min read

Collège Alpin Beau Soleil was founded in 1910 and has been in Villars-sur-Ollon, in the Vaud Alps, for most of its history. Part of the Nord Anglia Education group since 2011, it now welcomes about 300 boarders aged 11 to 18 from around the world, in six boarding houses, with classes of ten to twelve students.

I have students at Beau Soleil. Admission and costs work as follows.

Admission in four steps

StepWhat it involves
1. ApplicationForm (PDF or online), school reports from the last two years, a handwritten motivation letter by the child; the school requests a reference from the current school
2. FeeCHF 3,000, non-refundable, paid by secure link
3. Getting to know youCampus tour, Virtual Discovery Meeting, or a meeting with the admissions team in your region; written placement tests in maths and English
4. DecisionThe Admissions Committee decides within a week; offers are made in writing

What the school looks for

Beau Soleil looks for academic readiness alongside kindness, curiosity and an open mind, and for students willing to take part in school life. Expectations vary by entry grade, and each application is reviewed individually.

The tests and the letter

I prepare children for Beau Soleil's maths and English placement tests, and help them find what to say in a letter that must be their own.

Adding the annual items, a year costs about CHF 171,990 in Grades 6 to 10 and CHF 183,390 in Grade 11, before personal expenses. Beau Soleil offers no financial aid or scholarships.

Places and timing

Places are limited and the school receives many applications; it recommends applying between September and December for the following year, and before the end of November for Grades 10 and 11. New students are not admitted into Grade 12.
