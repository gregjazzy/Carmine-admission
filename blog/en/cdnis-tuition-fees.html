CDNIS is a not-for-profit school whose running costs are met from fees, set each spring by the Board of Governors for the following year. For 2026/27, the bill has four parts for a student from Early Years 1 upward: tuition, a one-time entry fee, an annual capital levy unless a debenture covers it, and a reservation deposit on acceptance.

From Grade 4, tuition includes a HK$1,500 technology fee and the IB Diploma examination fee of HK$6,000; in Grades 11 and 12 it also includes HK$2,000 for IB Diploma textbooks. Tuition from Early Years 1 is invoiced in two instalments.

On top of tuition, from Early Years 1

  • Entry fee: HK$12,500, once, non-refundable; waived for students who leave and return within two years.
  • Annual capital levy: HK$43,000, non-refundable, unless the student is covered by a debenture. Nursery students do not pay it.
  • Reservation deposit: HK$80,000 to confirm a place, credited to the first term's tuition and capital levy.

A new Grade 11 student without a debenture therefore pays about HK$317,000 in the first year, before extras.

The fee follows the place

At CDNIS, the place is decided by the assessment and the school record before any priority applies. I prepare children for that assessment.

Other costs

Uniforms and devices are compulsory and bought through vendors: an iPad in Grades 1 to 3, a laptop from Grade 4. Field trips, the Grade 8 milestone celebration and Experience Week programmes, which cost from HK$1,500 to HK$60,000 depending on the trip, are optional but strongly encouraged. The school bus and lunches are optional too.

Paying, and who pays

Application and assessment fees, HK$2,350 and HK$1,650, are paid by card with the online application. The deposit and entry fee are paid by bank transfer. Parents remain responsible for all fees even when an employer pays them. A student joining mid-year pays the full levy and entry fee and prorated tuition.

Help with fees

The scholarships CDNIS presents are merit awards: the CDNIS Scholarship, by invitation, for new Upper School students, and the Jockey Club Scholarship, worth 50% of tuition in Grades 11 and 12 for three returning students each year.
