Occasional places in Years 8 to 10

Most girls join Godolphin and Latymer at 11+ or 16+, and the school is typically full to capacity in every year group. Very occasionally a girl leaves, often because her family moves abroad, and a place opens in Year 8, 9 or 10. For a family arriving in London, or one that missed the 11+, that is the only door in between.

The profile expected

Godolphin looks for girls who will flourish academically and keep pace with classmates who have been there since Year 7. In practice, that means:

  • Strong skills in mathematics and English.
  • Three separate sciences currently studied: biology, chemistry and physics.
  • At least two modern languages, or one modern language plus Latin.
  • The potential to take part in, and contribute to, a broad extra-curricular life.

Catching up to the year group

For girls arriving from other systems, I close the gaps in mathematics and help plan the sciences and languages Godolphin expects, so that a vacancy can be taken when it comes.

The curriculum question

The subject conditions matter more than they look. At Godolphin, every girl studies French, German, Mandarin and Latin in Year 7, chooses two languages in Year 8, and takes the three sciences separately through to GCSE. A girl from a school that teaches combined science, or only one modern language, would join a class that has covered different ground. Families coming from abroad, in particular from bilingual or international systems, should check this before applying.

Keeping 16+ in view

Because mid-school vacancies are rare and unpredictable, a family should treat them as a chance, not a plan. The Sixth Form entry at 16+, with a regular intake from many schools, remains the reliable second route.

How I help

I have pupils at Godolphin, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools, many of them moving between countries. For an occasional place, I assess where a girl stands against the Godolphin year group and work on the gaps.
