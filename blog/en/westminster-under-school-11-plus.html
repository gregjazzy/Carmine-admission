Westminster Under School at 11+: a through place to Westminster

College, the King's Scholars' house at Westminster School, from the garden. Photo stevecadman, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

London schools November 2026 6 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

At 11+, about 40 girls and boys join Westminster Under School in Year 7 for their last two years of prep education, alongside pupils moving up from within the school. It is a unique entry point: a place at 11+ secures entry to both schools. Pupils go on to Westminster School in Year 9 with no further entrance testing. The Under School has expanded its central London site with Chapter House, a new building for 11- to 13-year-olds, and the two schools are ten minutes' walk apart.

Who it is for

Westminster advises families whose children are at primary schools ending in Year 6 to apply at 11+, and those at prep schools running to Year 8 to apply at 13+. A child cannot apply for both at the same time. The Under School is a day school only: overseas applications are accepted only if at least one parent lives in London at all times and the child can travel to school daily.

The January papers

The mathematics paper assumes the full Key Stage 2 programme, or its equivalent, and children able to reach the top level in their Year 6 tests; the school is particularly interested in how a child applies what they know to problem solving. The English paper tests reading and writing in forms that vary from year to year: comprehension, questions on a text, descriptive writing, a short review, continuing a passage in the author's voice. The school looks for wide, ambitious reading, an awareness of how great writers use language, a wide vocabulary, and fluent, accurate writing.

The day is meant to be informal and friendly. A teacher is on hand to support and prompt children who need it, and most come out having enjoyed the challenge.

"Avoid outside tuition"

The Under School goes further than most schools in saying what it wants: it recommends that parents avoid outside tuition, which it believes can detract from freshness, spontaneity and eagerness to learn, the very qualities it is looking for. Take that seriously. A child drilled on past papers arrives tired and predictable; a child who reads widely, likes puzzles and is used to talking about ideas arrives as the candidate the school describes. Any help a family seeks should build those habits, not replace them with technique.

The interview

In February, the children selected after the written papers meet two senior staff, one from the Under School and one from Westminster School, who encourage them to talk about themselves to discover their genuine interests and enthusiasms. A child who can talk with real pleasure about a book, a hobby or a question they have been wondering about is exactly who that conversation is designed to find.

Money

Under School fees for 2026-27 are £11,671 a term from Year 2 upward. Entry involves a £300 registration fee, a £3,000 deposit kept until the child leaves and a further £5,000 set against the first spring term. Bursaries are available at 11+ for families who meet Westminster's residence criteria, and Music Scholarships worth up to 10 percent with free instrumental tuition for children of at least Grade 5.

How I work with 11+ families

Consistent with what the school asks, I keep preparation light and real: a reading plan chosen with the child, mathematics as problem solving rather than procedure, short writing exercises in different forms, and a few conversations with an unfamiliar adult so the interview feels natural. The ISEB format is introduced once, so nothing in November is a surprise.
