Joining the Godolphin Sixth Form

Godolphin and Latymer School, Hammersmith. Photo Des Blenkinsopp, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

London schools November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Each year, girls from a range of schools join Godolphin and Latymer's Sixth Form, bringing, in the school's own view, new perspectives to a community that has largely been together since Year 7. The admissions process is selective and designed to establish academic potential and the capacity to thrive.

Two pathways

New Sixth Formers choose between A levels, three or four subjects studied in depth, and the IB Diploma, a broader international qualification. Godolphin was named Sunday Times IB School of the Year in 2023. Subject choices are part of registration, so the decision between pathways comes early.

The academic bar

Entrants are expected to achieve grades 8 or 9 at GCSE in the subjects they intend to take at A level or IB Higher Level. For subjects they have not studied at GCSE, the school looks for strength and interest throughout the process. Girls aiming to study mathematics at a higher level should be highly numerate and analytical, and relish complex problem solving; that is what the separate maths assessment looks for.

Timing against GCSEs

Offers arrive on 1 December of Year 11, months before GCSE results. They are made on the exam, the interview and the school record; the expectation of grades 8 or 9 then has to be met in the summer. A girl applying should treat the autumn exam and her GCSE preparation as one piece of work.

After arrival

New joiners are helped to settle quickly by the Sixth Form team. A dedicated higher education team, with Oxbridge coordinators, a US and international universities advisor and a medical applications coordinator, supports applications to the UK, Europe, the US and Canada.
