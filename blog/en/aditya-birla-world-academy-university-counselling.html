At Aditya Birla World Academy, preparing for university is described as integral to secondary school rather than a task for the final year. Counselling starts in Grade 8, which is also one of the school's three entry points, so a student joining then has five years of guidance ahead.

Fit over prestige

ABWA's counselling states a clear principle: university counselling is not about rankings or prestige, but about finding the institution where each student will thrive and feel a sense of belonging. Its mantra is that one size does not fit all. In practice, that means plans built around each student's interests, strengths and ambitions rather than a fixed list. A student drawn to design in Europe and another aiming at engineering in India will not follow the same plan, and the counselling is built to accept that from the start.

The grades behind the applications

Counsellors plan the applications; results make them possible. I support ABWA students in mathematics and the sciences through IGCSE, the IB and A Levels, and for the SAT.

The role of the curriculum choice

Counselling begins in earnest with curriculum choices: which IGCSE subjects, then whether to take the IB Diploma or A Levels. Those decisions shape which university courses remain open, particularly in the sciences and engineering, where specific subjects are often required. A Level students may also add subjects beyond the school's standard combinations.

Is your child choosing between the IB and A Levels? Let's think about where each leads.

Beyond applications

ABWA complements counselling with internship opportunities in professional settings, a career exploration programme and community projects. These experiences give students something concrete to write about in applications and help them test their interests before choosing a course.

How I help

I have students at ABWA, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools and universities. For ABWA students, my role is academic: the subjects and tests on which university offers depend.
