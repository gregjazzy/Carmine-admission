Choosing a BCA academy

Most high school applications ask which school. BCA asks which academy, and the answer shapes four years. A student's academy determines their course sequence, and once admitted, a student cannot change academy or transfer between them. BCA advises choosing according to interests rather than a future career.

The seven academies

AcademyFocus
AAST, Advancement of Science and TechnologyHands-on science, real research questions, sustainability, materials, energy
ABF, Business and FinanceCorporate finance, markets, economics, marketing, entrepreneurship; IB option
ACAHA, Culinary Arts and Hospitality AdministrationCulinary and pastry arts with hospitality management; IB option
AEDT, Engineering and Design TechnologyDesign, computer science, manufacturing, electronics
AMST, Medical Science TechnologyAnatomy, pharmacology, developmental biology, bioethics, research
ATCS, Technology and Computer ScienceProgramming, algorithms, architecture, software engineering, AI, security
AVPA, Visual and Performing ArtsThree concentrations: music, theatre, visual arts

Students can take electives, projects and clubs across academy boundaries, so the choice does not confine them to one field.

Seats per academy

Depending on the number and strength of applications, each academy runs one to three classes, of 20 to 22 seats each. The number of seats in a given academy can therefore vary from one year to the next, between about 20 and 66.

The second choice

The application allows a second-choice programme, and BCA advises taking it seriously: a student may be offered their second choice rather than their first, depending on seats. A student can also apply jointly to BCA and Bergen County Technical High School in Teterboro, which counts as one application; in that case the essay must discuss both programme choices for each school.

Unsure which academy fits your child? Let's talk about their interests.

How to choose well

  • Look at the evidence: what the student already does in their own time, not what sounds prestigious.
  • Read the course sequence: four years of medical science or finance should appeal on their own terms.
  • Think of the essay and interview: both ask why this academy, and a real answer is the strongest.
  • Check the arts requirements: AVPA adds an audition or a portfolio.
