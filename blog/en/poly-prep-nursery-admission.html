Entering Poly Prep in Nursery

Most families who join Poly Prep do so in Nursery, the program for three-year-olds in the Lower School in Park Slope. The school enrolls about 30 children a year at this age, and as a main entry year, every family that applies on time is interviewed. Pre-K and Kindergarten take only 5 to 10 new children each, so for a family set on Poly Prep, Nursery is the door to plan for.

I help families with this first application. Here is how it works for 2027-28.

The speech video and the playgroup

The school asks for a short video of the child speaking naturally, a minute or less, and says plainly that it is not looking for a polished or memorised answer. The point is to hear a three-year-old as she or he really talks. The playgroup works the same way: a small group, familiar activities, teachers who watch how a child plays, listens and joins in. For children with summer birthdays, the school suggests booking the playgroup after November 1 so that the visit reflects their development.

The parents' part of the file

At three, the parents provide most of what the school reads: the ISAAGNY parent statement, the interview and the choice between half-day and full-day programs. Choosing "flexible" may mean being placed in the half-day program automatically. A teacher recommendation is required if the child attends a school or early childhood program; if not, the family contacts the office to have it waived. Current families applying for a sibling follow an earlier timeline.
