Grade 9 is the main entry point to Poly Prep's Upper School, with 35 to 40 new students a year joining classmates who have often been at the school since the Lower or Middle School. Grades 10 to 12 take students only when places fall free. For a student in eighth grade elsewhere, Grade 9 is the realistic way in.

I prepare Grade 9 applicants every year. Here is how this entry works.

The interview visit

For Grade 9, the interview is individual and takes place during the school day, with the student and at least one parent. The visit includes thirty minutes of writing and a tour led by a current student. The Grade 9 open house does not include a formal tour, so for most families this visit is their first real look at the Dyker Heights campus in action.

Thirty minutes is long enough for the school to see how a student organises an argument, not only how well they spell. A student who has practised planning a short piece, stating a position and supporting it with one or two concrete examples, writes with far more control than one who starts typing at once.

The written file

The ISAAGNY common essay and the interests and activities form are shared with other New York schools, so one well-prepared version serves several applications. The math and English recommendations come from current teachers, requested after November 1. Since the school superscores the SSAT or ISEE, a second sitting before December 31 can only help a student's best section scores.

How I prepare Grade 9 applicants

For seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming for the most selective schools around the world. For Grade 9 at Poly Prep, I prepare students for the SSAT or ISEE, the one-on-one interview and the timed writing, and work with them on the ISAAGNY essay.
