Grade 8 is the only main entry point between Nursery and the final two years at Aditya Birla World Academy. Students joining at this stage have one year of Middle School to settle in before the two-year Cambridge IGCSE programme begins in Grade 9.

Where Grade 8 sits

GradesProgramme
6 to 8Middle School: subject-specific proficiency and cognitive skills, with a spiral curriculum
9 and 10Cambridge IGCSE, with flexible subject choices
11 and 12IB Diploma Programme or A Levels

The process

Families start with the online enquiry form, then complete the application and submit documents when invited. The assessment stage depends on the grade and may include an entrance test, a parent group discussion and/or an interaction with the child. Shortlisted families are then invited to a meeting about the child's environment, values and aspirations, before the decision and fee payment.

Preparing academically

The Middle School builds subject knowledge and independent, analytical thinking, and the IGCSE that follows encourages inquiry and application of knowledge. A child joining in Grade 8 benefits from solid mathematics, confident English reading and writing, and the habit of explaining their reasoning, since that is what the following years will ask for.

Wellbeing from day one

ABWA places emotional intelligence at the centre of school life, with a social-emotional learning programme, regular classroom circles led by homeroom teachers, and student initiatives such as the Anti-Bullying Squad and Mental Health Champions. For a student arriving in Grade 8, among classmates who may have known each other for years, these structures help with settling in.

Sport and university

Athletes entering Grade 8 can apply for the Sports Scholarship, which covers part or all of tuition for Grades 8 to 10. University counselling also begins in Grade 8, helping students explore interests well before they choose between the IB and A Levels.

How I help
