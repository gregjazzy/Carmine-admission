ASIJ has no single deadline. Applications for August 2027 open in mid-November 2026, for local and overseas families and for every grade, and decisions roll out from late February or early March through the summer. That flexibility is useful for families whose move to Tokyo is not yet fixed. It also means that places are filled progressively, so a file completed in January is read before one completed in May.

What slows a file down

The steps run in order: account on Veracross, online form, required items uploaded to the portal, initial review, decision. A file waits at the third step until everything is in. The usual causes are a teacher who has not returned a recommendation, a missing standardized test result, or a specialist report the school needs before it can decide, such as a learning-support or speech-therapy report. The school may also ask for an academic or English screening, or for a test if the child has none from the last eighteen months.

Families arriving during the year

The school year runs from August to June. Families new to Japan who arrive during the year should ask about places in their child's grade before applying. Families already living in Tokyo cannot transfer mid-year; they apply in the normal cycle for the following August.
