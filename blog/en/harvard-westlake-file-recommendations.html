The file and recommendations

Admission at Harvard-Westlake is holistic. Beyond the ISEE and the interview, the file is made of documents that tell the school how the applicant works and lives at school today. Most are written by others; one is written by the student. Each has a precise role.

The required pieces

PieceWho provides itWhen
English teacher recommendationThe current English teacherBetween 1 November and 15 January
Math teacher recommendationThe current math teacherBetween 1 November and 15 January
Administrator recommendationA current administratorBetween 1 November and 15 January
Transcript releaseThe family signs, the registrar or an administrator sends the recordThrough the online application
Student questionnaireThe applicantAt least two days before the interview

Recommendation forms are emailed automatically once the family enters each recommender's address in the online application. Harvard-Westlake accepts the Los Angeles Independent Schools common forms for the administrator, English teacher and math teacher, which saves teachers from writing the same letter twice for several schools.

What families may add

  • One supplemental letter of recommendation, from someone who knows a side of the applicant the teachers do not see.
  • Media showing arts or athletics, sent by email to the admission office: a link, video clip, photo or PDF, with the applicant's name and details such as the instrument or jersey number. Videos should not exceed five minutes.

These additions should bring something new. A supplemental letter that repeats the teachers adds nothing; a two-minute clip of a young cellist playing well can add a great deal.

A coherent file

Helping recommenders

Teachers in Los Angeles independent schools often write many letters in the same weeks. Entering their email addresses early, thanking them in person, and reminding them politely of the deadline are the most useful things a family can do. What a family should never do is suggest wording.

Not sure whether to send a supplemental letter or a video? Let's look at the file together.

The student's own voice

The questionnaire is the only written part of the file in the applicant's voice. Its short answers about interests are read before the interview, and the interviewer may come back to them. Harvard-Westlake looks for kind, curious and engaged students; the questionnaire is where a child can show, in a few lines, what they are curious about.
