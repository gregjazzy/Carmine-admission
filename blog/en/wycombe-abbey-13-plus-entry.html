Wycombe Abbey at 13+

At 13+, Wycombe Abbey runs entry and scholarship as a single examination. Every girl invited to the Assessment Day sits the same papers, and those papers are written at a standard designed to identify scholars. A girl therefore competes for a place and for an award at the same time.

The papers

  • Compulsory: English, mathematics, humanities, and a practical science assessment.
  • Optional: French, German, Spanish or Mandarin Chinese, Latin and Greek.
  • Written by: the school's own heads of department, at scholarship standard.
  • Specimens: available on request once a girl has been invited to the Assessment Day.

Each girl also has an individual interview with a senior member of staff.

Entry and scholarship together

Because the 13+ papers are set to scholarship level, I prepare girls for both at once: the Pre-Test first, then the depth the January papers require.

Two filters, not one

Unlike the 11+, where every candidate attends the Assessment Day, the 13+ has a shortlist. The Pre-Test and the school reference come first; only selected girls are invited to High Wycombe. A strong Pre-Test in the autumn is therefore the gateway to everything else, and the reference matters earlier than families expect.

The optional papers

Optional papers in languages, Latin and Greek allow a girl with real strength in those subjects to show it. A girl who has studied Latin seriously at prep school, for example, gains a chance to stand out that a girl without it does not need to worry about.
