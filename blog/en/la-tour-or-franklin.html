La Tour or Franklin: two Catholic schools in the 16th, two temperaments

Paris schools November 2026 5 min read

Many families in the 16th arrondissement apply to both La Tour and Franklin. Both are Catholic, state-contracted, demanding, a few streets apart. Yet they are not alike, and do not suit the same child.

Two projects

Franklin, founded by the Jesuits in 1894, offers a classical, highly structured project: Latin for all in cinquième, German-English bilingual track from sixième, Saturday-morning classes from cinquième, human and spiritual formation in the timetable, and an economics prépa that sends students to HEC, ESSEC and ESCP every year. La Tour, founded in 1901 by the Sisters of Sainte-Clotilde, has made languages its identity: ten languages offered, three taken by every student until the bac, an anglophone section created in 1989 for families returning from abroad, and bilingual sections in Spanish, Italian, Arabic or Chinese.

Two calendars, two procedures

Franklin opens sixième applications from 15 to 30 September only, with an online file then an appointment with the director or préfet. La Tour also opens sixième in mid-September, but without closing after two weeks; it asks for a letter to the head of school and meets the child with both parents. Other years open in mid-September at Franklin, in January at La Tour. A family aiming for both must be ready from the start of CM2.

Torn between the two? Tell me about your child: I will tell you which one fits.

For which child

I have students at both La Tour and Franklin, and every year I support around fifteen families towards the leading Paris schools. Franklin suits the child who likes a clear framework and tradition, and looks naturally towards the prépas; it requires real commitment to the Jesuit project. La Tour suits the child curious about languages and the world, who will find there a language pathway unmatched in Paris within an open Catholic setting. The right choice depends on the real child, and on what the family imagines after the bac.

The cost

The basic contribution is close in both schools: around €3,000 a year at Franklin at full rate depending on the year group, about €2,200 to €2,500 at La Tour, plus flat-rate fees. The difference comes from languages: at La Tour, the anglophone section adds nearly €2,000 a year. Details are in La Tour school fees and Franklin school fees.
