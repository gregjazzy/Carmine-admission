Entering GSIS in KG1 or Year 1

For many families, German Swiss International School begins at three. Kindergarten One in the English International Stream, or DKG1 in the German International Stream, is the first entry point, and the one where the waitlist weighs most. Applications are accepted year-round, even from birth, and that is not a figure of speech: the date the application is received is one of the criteria that decide who is invited.

I help families plan this entry, often years in advance. Here is how it works.

The assessment at three and five

In KG1 and Year 1, children are assessed individually but within a group of children. Written English and mathematics papers start only from Year 2. Year 1 in the EIS is also taught at the Pok Fu Lam campus. In the EIS, a general condition is understanding English; in the GIS Kindergarten, children do not need to speak German, since the school runs special German language programmes at that level.

For a three-year-old, the main preparation is ordinary: plenty of conversation and reading in the language of the stream, time in groups with other children, and a calm explanation of what the visit will be.

Why the date matters so much

After a successful assessment, places go by priority: German, Swiss or Austrian passports and Infrastructure Debentures first, then siblings, staff, alumni and vacant debenture holders, and finally everyone else by date of application. For a family in that last group, applying at birth rather than at two can be the difference between an invitation and a deferral. A deferral keeps the original date, but moves the child to the next available year group.
