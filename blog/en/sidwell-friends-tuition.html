Sidwell Friends publishes four tuition rates, one per section of the school, and a list of additional fees and optional services. Lunch is included in tuition. Families who receive financial aid generally see the additional costs discounted at the same rate as their tuition.

What a year really costs

Added up, the figures look different from the headline rate. An Upper School student costs about $63,250 to $63,525 a year once textbooks, the technology fee and the yearbook are included, before any travel. A Middle School student costs $62,330 with books and laptop, or $65,930 with the full-year extended day. A Lower School child in PreK to Grade 2 who uses the round-trip shuttle and the Early Risers program costs $60,745. Families comparing Sidwell Friends with other Washington schools should compare these totals, not the tuition lines.

Three ways to pay

Tuition can be paid in full by June 15, in two payments of 70 percent by June 15 and 30 percent by November 15, or through an interest-free ten-month plan running from May to February. For a family receiving aid, the same schedules apply to the reduced amount.
