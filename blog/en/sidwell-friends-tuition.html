Sidwell Friends: tuition

Sidwell Friends, Wisconsin Avenue, Washington, DC. Photo Tony Webster, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0

In 2026-2027, Sidwell Friends tuition, lunch included, is $55,250 for PreK to Grade 2, $58,050 for Grades 3 and 4, $61,830 in middle school and $62,330 in upper school.

What comes on top

  • The shuttle between campuses, optional: $1,750 one way, $3,500 round trip.
  • Books and laptop: $500 in middle school, $500 to $700 in books and a $420 technology fee in upper school.
  • After care and programmes, optional.

Payment in full by June 15, in two parts, or over ten months interest-free. No school bus: both campuses are near the Metro.
