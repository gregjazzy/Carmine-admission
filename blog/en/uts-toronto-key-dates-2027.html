UTS key dates for 2027 entry

The UTS admission cycle is short and fixed. Everything happens between mid-September and the end of February, with a single test date and a single interview day. Apart from a make-up test for a child who is ill, a missed step is not repeated that year, and the steps are close together.

I build this calendar with every family I accompany to UTS. Here it is for entry in September 2027, with what each date requires.

Our experience with UTS

We have a track record of admissions to University of Toronto Schools, and we put that experience at your family's disposal: a calendar held step by step, and a child ready for December 5 and January 23.

Autumn: the file must be complete before the test

Only applicants whose application is completed and paid by December 1 can write the test four days later. The portal is deactivated at 11:59 p.m. that evening and nothing can be changed afterwards. For Grade 9, 10 and 11 applicants, that also means the two teacher references must be in by then, so teachers need to be asked in October. Applying before November 1 saves $50; a fee waiver can be requested on the payment page.

The October open house is the main chance to see the school before applying, and UTS encourages every applicant to attend.

Applying this year? Tell me where your child stands: the window before December 5 is short.

December and January: two weeks that decide

The test on December 5 leads straight to the Stage 2 invitations on January 8. Between them come the holidays, and the bursary deadline of January 11. A family hoping for support should prepare its Apple Financial Services file in December, before knowing the test result: waiting for the invitation leaves three days.

Invited applicants then have about two weeks before the interviews on January 23, with the virtual information session on January 16 in between. For a child who has never done timed scenario interviews or a group task with strangers, two weeks is enough to become comfortable only if practice starts as soon as the invitation arrives, or better, before.

February 26

Admission decisions are announced on February 26, 2027, and bursary decisions are emailed the same day to students who receive an offer. Tuition and enrolment fee figures for 2027-28 are published in January 2027, so families know the cost before the decision.
