CDNIS is an IB World School from Nursery to Grade 12, with the Primary Years Programme, the Middle Years Programme and the Diploma Programme. Its Canadian identity adds a second layer in the Upper School: the Ontario Secondary School Diploma, the OSSD, integrated into the MYP and the DP so that students graduate with both qualifications.

How the pathway is built

GradesProgramme
Nursery to Grade 5IB Primary Years Programme, bilingual English and Mandarin in the early grades
Grades 6 to 8IB Middle Years Programme; English, Mandarin and French offered
Grades 9 and 10IB Middle Years Programme, with credits toward the OSSD
Grades 11 and 12IB Diploma Programme, with credits toward the OSSD; Spanish added as a language option

An open-access Diploma

At CDNIS, every student in Grades 11 and 12 is fully enrolled in the IB Diploma Programme, with differentiated support and personal goals. Families should read this carefully: there is no lighter track to fall back on. A student who joins the Upper School is joining a programme that ends with the full Diploma.

Why two diplomas

The IB Diploma's external examinations and the OSSD's course-based record give universities two kinds of evidence. The combination is designed to strengthen applications across systems, including Canada, the United Kingdom, the United States and Asia. The strong Chinese programme also allows the IB Bilingual Diploma through Chinese A.

Ready for a demanding Upper School

I prepare Upper School applicants to CDNIS for the assessment, with particular care for the written English that both the IB and the OSSD will demand.

What it means for admission

  • Grade 6 is a main entry point, at the start of the MYP years, and the moment many families aim for.
  • Grades 9 and 10 applicants join the OSSD track mid-way; from Grade 10, an official transcript is required.
  • Grade 11 is the start of the Diploma; applicants need a level that will hold up in a two-year programme.
  • Grade 12 applications are usually considered only for students already in an IB Diploma Programme elsewhere, because the Diploma runs over two years.

Language of instruction

In the High School, English is the main language of instruction. Students may continue Mandarin or French, and Spanish is offered in Grades 11 and 12. For an applicant whose first language is not English, the short written task and interview in the admissions assessment are where readiness shows.
