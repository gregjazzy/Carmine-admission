Michaelhouse runs one main admissions cycle a year, for boys entering E Block, Grade 8, in January. The cycle opens about twenty months before entry and closes nine months before it. The calendar below is for the Grade 8 intake of 2028, that is, for boys in Grade 6 in 2026. Individual dates can still move; the order rarely does.

The date that cannot slip

The 31 January deadline of Grade 7 closes three things at once: the registration itself, the scholarship application and the request for financial assistance. A family that registers late goes on the waiting list, and a scholarship that was not requested by that date cannot be competed for that year. Registration can be made at any time from birth; Michaelhouse encourages it by the end of Grade 6, which also leaves time for the interview.

Is your son in Grade 5 or 6 now? Let's set his calendar.

What each stage asks of the boy

  • Information events and open days ask nothing of him. They are for the parents, and for the boy to see the place.
  • The interview asks him to speak about himself, with ease, to the head of the school.
  • Weekend@Michaelhouse asks him to spend a night away from home among boys he does not know, which tells the family a good deal too.
  • The scholarship weekend asks for his best level, in the field he chose.

How I use this calendar

For seven years, around fifteen families a year have trusted me with applications to highly selective schools. For Michaelhouse, I start from these dates and work backwards: what the boy needs to be ready for the interview in Grade 6, and, if a scholarship is in sight, for February of Grade 7.
