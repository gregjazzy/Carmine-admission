Beau Soleil: the MYP

From 11 to 16, Grades 6 to 10, Beau Soleil students follow the IB Middle Years Programme.

What it offers

Teaching across eight subject groups, linking lessons to real life and preparing for the IB Diploma. Beau Soleil is an IB World School, authorised for this programme and the Diploma.

Languages

In the French-speaking canton of Vaud, the school strongly encourages French from Grade 6 to 10, with classes from beginner to native level. Classes of 10 to 12 students, 16 at most.

Arriving at level

I prepare your child for the entrance tests and help them adjust to the MYP, in maths and science.
