The Middle Years Programme

Most Beau Soleil students spend their first years in the International Baccalaureate Middle Years Programme. From Grade 6 to Grade 10, roughly from 11 to 16, the MYP is the academic frame in which they learn, before choosing between the IB Diploma and the US High School Diploma.

The programme in brief
Grades6 to 10, ages 11 to 16
StructureEight subject groups, with links between subjects and real-world experience
Class sizeTypically 10 to 12, at most 16
FrenchStrongly encouraged, from beginner to native level
Other languagesGerman, Italian, Spanish included; others at extra cost
SupportA Learning Support Team open to every student

What the MYP aims for

The MYP asks students to connect what they learn with the world around them, to explore global issues, and to develop the attitudes and skills that matter beyond exams. At Beau Soleil, students in these years are expected to discover their strengths, learn by doing and take up new opportunities, so that by Grade 10 they can shape their own path into the Diploma years.

Learning beyond the classroom

Boarding is part of the programme's logic. Living on campus in an international community builds independence and resilience. Grades 6 and 7 follow a Junior programme with outdoor education on Saturdays; all students from Grade 6 to Grade 11 go on at least two expeditions a year and ski regularly in winter.

Coming from a national curriculum? Let's look at the transition to the MYP.

Arriving from another system

For a child arriving from a French, British, American or other national system, the main adjustments are usually English, if it is not their first language, and the MYP's emphasis on projects and inquiry. Small classes and the Learning Support Team help, and the school asks families to share any existing assessments during admission.

Toward the Diploma

By the end of Grade 10, the MYP has prepared students for the choice between the IB Diploma, in English, French or bilingually, and the US High School Diploma. The academic team works with each family on that decision.
