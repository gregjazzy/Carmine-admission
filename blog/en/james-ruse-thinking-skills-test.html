Thinking skills count for a quarter of the selective test. I know the school through my students at James Ruse. For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year with applications to leading schools in Sydney selective schools.

What the Department says

40 multiple-choice questions in 40 minutes, testing critical thinking and problem-solving. No prior knowledge is required.

What makes the difference

One minute per question: spotting a flaw in an argument, drawing a valid conclusion, solving a logic puzzle. These habits build over time, by reasoning aloud and justifying, not by drilling series.

Learning to reason

I have your child reason aloud on logic problems and arguments, so they quickly see what holds and what does not.
