Hotchkiss for international students: English, testing and aid

Book a conversation

Hotchkiss recruits internationally, and it shows: the school even maintains an admission reception in Tokyo, in the Ark Hills Club in Akasaka. International students apply through the same process as American students, with the same January 15 deadline. Three things differ in practice: English, testing logistics and the financial aid paperwork.

English: fluency, with no safety net

Hotchkiss offers no ESL support, and every student must be fluent in English. Applicants whose first language is not English submit a TOEFL, Duolingo English Test or IELTS result on top of the SSAT or ISEE, unless they have spent three years or more at a school where English is the main language of instruction. On the TOEFL, the school indicates that 100 on the internet-based test, or 600 on paper, shows English strong enough to take a full part.

That is a demanding level for a fourteen-year-old schooled in another language, and it matters beyond the test: classes, essays and dorm life all happen in English from the first day. Families should treat the English score as a signal of readiness, not a box to tick.

Testing from abroad

For ninth and tenth grade, the SSAT or ISEE; for older applicants, the SAT, ACT or PSAT. Outside the United States and Canada, the SSAT is offered on paper at international sites and at Prometric centers, and the ISEE online or on paper depending on location. Hotchkiss recommends sitting tests in November or December so a second attempt remains possible; from abroad, that means registering in early autumn, when seats are still available.

The interview, virtual or on campus

Interviews can be held virtually, which makes the process manageable from abroad. Hotchkiss still strongly encourages a campus visit, because it is the best way to judge fit. A family that can combine a visit to several New England schools in October or November gives its child the clearest basis for a decision in March.

Applying from Asia, Europe or the Middle East? Tell me where; the calendar starts in the summer.

Financial aid is open to international families

Hotchkiss's need-based aid is open to international families, through the same Clarity platform, with documents from the family's own country: tax records where they exist, employer statements and three months of recent bank statements. Hotchkiss cannot waive the aid application fee for international applicants. Gathering documents abroad takes time; starting in the fall is essential to meet January 15.

Costs particular to international students

International students pay the same tuition, $80,800 with fees for boarders in 2026-27. Health insurance is $2,895 for international students, against $4,495 for domestic students. Travel is the main extra, and families should plan for school breaks.
