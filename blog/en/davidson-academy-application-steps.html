The Davidson application, step by step

The University of Nevada, Reno campus, home of the Davidson Academy. Photo NevadaMarCom, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

The Davidson Academy application is online and fairly short, but it is only complete when several people have done their part: the student, each custodial parent, three recommenders and, through the test report, a testing organization or psychologist. And because assessments are scheduled month by month from September to January, a few weeks lost in assembling the file can push the assessment later.

I help families put this application together. Here is the order that works.

The steps

Recommendation formsMath teacher, English teacher, one teacher or other adultSent by email on submission

The application is not complete until every parent agreement and all three recommendation forms have arrived. Late test reports are not accepted.

Losing no time

The forms go out automatically the moment the application is submitted, from an unmonitored address that spam filters often catch. Families should add the Academy's domain to their safe senders and ask recommenders and the other parent to do the same, then check that each form has been completed. The primary contact receives an email at each stage: receipt, each recommendation, completeness, and the final decision to invite to assess, decline, or ask for more information.

Timing matters because assessment cut-offs are monthly and places each month are limited. Submitting by a month's deadline does not guarantee that month's assessment, but submitting late guarantees a later one, and the final deadline is January 20 at 4 p.m. Pacific.

Choosing recommenders

The math and English teachers should be the ones who see the student's best work and can speak to how far ahead they are. The third can be another teacher or someone who knows the student well. Since the Academy weighs maturity and commitment as well as ability, a recommender who can describe how the student handles challenge and feedback adds something the scores cannot.
