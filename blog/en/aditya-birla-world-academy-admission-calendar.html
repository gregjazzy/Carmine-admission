Aditya Birla World Academy works to a fixed annual cycle rather than rolling admission. Knowing when it opens, and what happens in between, is the first step for any family aiming at the school.

The year, in outline

WhenWhat
End of May or early JuneAdmission process begins for the following academic year: Nursery, Grade 8, Grade 11
During the cycleApplication, assessment and interaction, family meeting, decision, fee payment
Same periodSports Scholarship applications for Grades 8 and 11, with the admission application
February or MarchChance vacancies in other grades, if any, become known
Before the school yearAll admissions completed; no mid-year entry

For 2027-28, the admission cycle and the sports scholarships have closed.

Working back from June

Because the cycle opens so early, a family aiming at Grade 8 or Grade 11 in a given year should prepare during the year before. A student in Grade 7 or Grade 10 needs to be ready, academically and in their thinking about the school, by late spring. For Nursery, the key fact is the child's date of birth, which decides the eligible year. The months before the cycle are also the time to visit, read about the curriculum and pastoral care, and decide whether ABWA's emphasis on values and emotional intelligence suits the family.

Ready before June

I prepare students over the year before the cycle opens, so that the assessment comes as a familiar step.

After the decision

Admission is confirmed only when the fee payment is completed within the period the school gives. Families should therefore have funds ready to act promptly after a decision.

For families arriving later

A family whose move to Mumbai falls outside the cycle has one route: chance vacancies. Submitting the online enquiry form ensures they are told if a seat becomes available, usually in February or March for the following year.

Contact

The admissions office answers questions by email and phone throughout the year. Admission remains subject to seat availability and eligibility, and the school reserves the final decision.

How I keep families on track

I have students at ABWA, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools. For ABWA, I build the preparation backwards from June, so that nothing has to be rushed.
