How to get into Wycombe Abbey

Entrance to Wycombe Abbey, High Wycombe. Photo David Hillas, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

English boarding schools November 2026 6 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Wycombe Abbey is a full-boarding school for girls in High Wycombe, 35 miles west of London, on a 170-acre site. It looks for potential rather than prior attainment: bright, motivated, engaged girls with an enthusiasm for boarding who will contribute to every area of school life. A limited number of day places exist for families living within 15 miles.

I have pupils at Wycombe Abbey. The admissions routes work as follows.

Registration costs £420, non-refundable, at 11+ and 13+. At 16+ there is no fee at registration; it is asked of shortlisted candidates later. About 15 places for 13+ are also awarded each year through the 11+ process, as deferred entry for girls who stay at their prep school until Year 8.

What the school looks at

Every route combines an objective measure, the online test, with a reference from the current school and a day at Wycombe Abbey where girls are interviewed and sit papers written by the school's own heads of department. At 11+, offers rest on the entrance papers, the interview and the group activity. At 13+ and 16+, the Headmistress, the Deputy Head (Academic) and the Director of Admissions first review the online test and the school report to decide who is invited.

All assessments in person

The online test can be sat at the girl's current school, at a recognised UK centre or at a British Council office abroad. Everything else takes place at Wycombe Abbey: there is no remote Assessment Day, so international families should plan for a journey.

Money

From September 2026, boarding fees are £21,500 a term. Means-tested bursaries are available, with applications due by 1 September of the year before entry. Scholarships and exhibitions bring prestige but limited financial value.
