Academic scholarships at Wycombe Abbey

At Wycombe Abbey, an academic scholarship is a mark of distinction rather than a source of funding. The school says so openly: its awards carry considerable prestige, inside and outside the school, but are of limited financial value. Families who need help with fees should look at bursaries instead.

An award normally lasts for the girl's whole time at the school, subject to regular review and satisfactory progress.

At 11+: by invitation

No application is needed. After the November Assessment Day, a small selection of girls who hold firm offers are invited to sit the Wycombe Abbey scholarship papers at the school in late January or early February of Year 6; for 2027 entry, on 28 January. The papers cover English, mathematics, science and humanities, with a language aptitude test. No sample papers are available.

Beyond the syllabus

Scholarship papers reward depth: reading beyond the curriculum, mathematics with real problems, and the habit of writing an argument. I prepare girls who have that potential.

At 13+: through the entrance papers

At 13+, there is no separate scholarship exam. Every girl invited to the January Assessment Day sits the school's own papers in English, mathematics, humanities and practical science, written by heads of department at a standard designed to identify scholars, with optional languages, Latin and Greek. Scholarships are awarded at the same time as offers, by early February.

What a scholar looks like

The 11+ scholarship includes science and humanities, subjects not tested on the Assessment Day, and a language aptitude test that rewards a quick ear for patterns rather than prior study. A girl who reads widely, follows her curiosity across subjects and enjoys hard problems is better placed than one who has only drilled the entrance syllabus.

The 11+ sequence, in order

For a girl at 11+, the year runs as follows: the Pre-Test in the autumn, the Assessment Day in November, a firm offer in December, an invitation to the scholarship papers, the papers in late January, then acceptance and deposit in early March. The scholarship papers therefore always come after the offer of a place, never instead of it. Music awards follow their own route, with auditions the day before the scholarship papers.
