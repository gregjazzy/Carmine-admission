Andover: the entrance test, what you need to know

Samuel Phillips Hall, Phillips Academy Andover. Photo Daderot, Wikimedia Commons, CC0

US boarding schools November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Andover is one of the leading boarding schools that still require testing. Every applicant sends a score, and students at non-English-medium schools abroad add an English test. I follow students at Andover and at the leading schools in New England, and every year I support around fifteen families with their admissions.

What the school says

The applicant sends one of these tests: Upper Level SSAT or ISEE, PSAT, SAT or ACT, directly from the testing body. International students at schools where English is not the language of instruction also take the TOEFL iBT, IELTS or Duolingo English Test. The school publishes a guideline: most admitted international students score at least 100 on the TOEFL, 7.5 on the IELTS or 135 on the Duolingo.

Preparing for Andover's test

I prepare your child for the SSAT, ISEE or SAT, and the English test, to the level Andover expects, with a timetable set to their sittings.

What the school does not say

Andover publishes no minimum SSAT score. The test is one piece among others, read with the graded writing sample, the essay and the recommendations. But a weak score casts doubt on the rest, and for a student at a school abroad, it is often the only piece directly comparable with other applicants.

Why good students fail

The English benchmark

7.5 on the IELTS is a level many adults do not reach. A very strong student at a non-English school can stumble there, while the rest of their file is solid.

Has your child ever taken a TOEFL or IELTS? Tell me about them: I measure the gap before the sitting.

SSAT vocabulary

The verbal sections assume a very wide English vocabulary, which ordinary schooling does not provide.

Choosing the test

Andover allows a choice of tests. For a given student, one can be clearly more favourable than another: you need to know before registering.

When to start

For a February 1 file, preparation starts the previous spring, with a first sitting in the autumn.

What I do for your family

I have your child sit a practice test of each type to choose the right one, then prepare them for it and for the English test. For the full file, see how to get into Andover.
