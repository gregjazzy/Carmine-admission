Andover or Exeter: how to choose

Samuel Phillips Hall, Phillips Academy Andover. Photo Daderot, Wikimedia Commons, CC0

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Andover and Exeter bear the same name, that of the Phillips family that founded them, and many families apply to both. Both require a test and an interview. But their files differ on points that need their own preparation.

What sets them apart

The deadline: January 15 for Exeter, February 1 for Andover. The pieces: Exeter asks for two essays; Andover for one essay, a writing sample already graded by a teacher and a short video. The test: SSAT or ISEE for grade 9 at Exeter; at Andover, SSAT, ISEE, PSAT, SAT or ACT. The interview: three possible formats at both. Aid: Andover admits need-blind, Exeter too, and both meet demonstrated need.

Two applications, one strategy

I prepare students for Andover as for Exeter, on a single timeline: one test, two interviews, and written pieces specific to each school.

How to run both

The same SSAT serves both. Recommendations go through Gateway for both. The work specific to each: Exeter's essays, and Andover's graded sample and video. Since Exeter closes two weeks earlier, that is where you start.
