US boarding schools

Andover or Exeter

Andover and Exeter are related by birth. Samuel Phillips Jr. founded Phillips Academy in Andover in 1778; his uncle John Phillips founded Phillips Exeter Academy three years later. The two schools are independent of each other, and their rivalry is old enough that they do not agree on when it began: Andover dates it to 1861, Exeter to its first match in 1878. Many families apply to both. The applications look alike from a distance and differ in the details that matter.

What they share

  • A required test. Neither school is test-optional.
  • An interview in three formats. Both offer campus, live online and recorded options, one interview per applicant.
  • Need-blind admission. Both decide without regard to the family's finances, meet full demonstrated need, and use grants rather than loans.
  • Current English and math teachers as required recommenders, alongside the counselor and a personal recommendation.
  • Size and pace. Around 1,100 to 1,200 students, trimesters, five courses a term, small classes.

Andover and Exeter, prepared together

I prepare children for both Phillips academies at the level they expect: one test preparation, two sets of written pieces, interviews in the right format, and one calendar for two deadlines.

Two deadlines, two weeks apart

Exeter closes its file on January 15, and its interview must also be completed by then. Andover closes on February 1 and interviews through January. A family applying to both has to finish Exeter first, then use the last two weeks for Andover's specific pieces. In practice the Exeter deadline drives the whole autumn.

The test: free choice against fixed rules

Andover accepts any of five tests, the Upper Level SSAT or ISEE, the PSAT, the SAT or the ACT, whatever the grade. Exeter sets the test by grade: the Upper Level SSAT or ISEE for ninth grade, a different list for older applicants, and only the PSAT, SAT, PreACT or ACT for twelfth grade and the postgraduate year. For a ninth-grade applicant to both, one SSAT or ISEE serves the two files. Exeter's readers look closely at the SSAT writing sample, which is a reason to prepare it, not just the scored sections.

The writing: three pieces against two

Exeter asks for two essays of 200 to 500 words and a parent statement. Andover asks for one essay, a graded writing sample chosen from schoolwork, a short video response and a parent statement. The Exeter essays can be prepared first; the Andover graded paper has to be chosen from what the child has already done, and the video adds an exercise Exeter does not have. Reusing an Exeter essay for Andover is tempting and rarely works, because the prompts and the reader's other evidence differ.

The classroom and the scale

Exeter teaches every class around a Harkness table of at most twelve students, and offers no AP courses. Andover's classes average about twelve to thirteen students, with a ratio of five students per teacher, and it offers three official AP courses, though its students sit many more AP exams on their own. Exeter grades on a scale from 11 down to 1; Andover from 0 to 6. Neither ranks its students.

The money, in two figures

A boarding year costs $79,800 at Andover and $71,797 at Exeter in 2026-2027. The application fee is $60 at Exeter for everyone, and $125 at Andover for international applicants. The aid policies differ in their details: Exeter offers free tuition to US families earning under $150,000 who meet its conditions; Andover sets no income ceiling for a full scholarship and excludes the family home from assets up to $650,000.

How I prepare a double application

I have students at Andover and at Exeter, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools in the United States, England and Switzerland. For a family applying to both, I set one calendar driven by Exeter's January 15, one test prepared for both, Exeter's essays first, then Andover's graded paper, essay and video, and interviews in formats chosen for the child.
