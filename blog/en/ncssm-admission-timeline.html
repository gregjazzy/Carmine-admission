The NCSSM admissions calendar

The NCSSM entrance in Durham, North Carolina. Photo Warren LeMay, Wikimedia Commons, CC0

NCSSM's admission cycle fits inside a student's 10th grade year, between October and the end of February. Most of its deadlines are firm, and one of them, the application deadline on 5 January, is explicitly without exceptions. For the Class of 2029, the year runs as follows.

October and November: discover and apply

  • 24 October 2026: high school open house on the Durham campus.
  • 7 November 2026: high school open house on the Morganton campus.
  • 15 October 2026, 4 pm: the NCSSM application opens on CFNC.org. One application covers the Residential campuses, NCSSM Online and Summer Ventures, with separate essay sets.

Autumn is also when the student should ask their evaluators, a 9th or 10th grade STEM teacher, a 9th or 10th grade English teacher and the current counselor, and start the residency process.

Winter: the hard deadlines

DateWhat is due
5 January 2027, 11:59 pmEvery section of the CFNC application complete. No exceptions.
30 January 2027Residency Determination Service application

Status notifications follow later, on a date announced by the school.

Transcripts, three ways

Between 15 and 28 February, the student submits grades in one of three ways: through CFNC's Transcript Manager, with a report card added for year-long courses; by having the counselor send an official copy by post or email; or by self-reporting an unofficial transcript in Blackbaud. Any offer made on self-reported grades depends on NCSSM later receiving an official transcript.

The traps

  • The holidays. The 5 January deadline falls just after the winter break, when counselors and teachers are away. Essays left for the holidays become a rush.
  • RDS. Without an RDS number, the CFNC application cannot be completed, so residency should be started well before January.
  • The math assessment. Registration is through Blackbaud; several sessions run through the day, and the test needs weeks of calculator-free practice beforehand.
