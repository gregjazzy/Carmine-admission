The Newark Academy admission timeline

Newark Academy splits its application into two deadlines: a form first, in December, then everything that depends on other people, schools and test centres, in January. Knowing which piece falls when is most of the work of keeping an application calm.

Six dates

DateStep
1 September 2026Online Admission Form opens
1 December 2026Online Admission Form due
15 December 2026Prior year transcript due, with a release form
11 January 2027Supplemental materials: conversations, recommendations, testing, current year grades, financial aid application
19 February 2027Decisions released
5 March 2027New student and family reply date

Newark Academy does not accept mid-year applications.

Why the January date matters most

The 1 December form is entirely in the family's hands. The 11 January deadline is not: it depends on teachers writing recommendations, a school releasing grades, a testing agency sending scores and an admission officer holding a conversation. Each of those can slip by a week. Families who treat 11 January as the real deadline, and aim to have every request made by mid-December, avoid the last-minute scramble.

Spreading the work

  • September and October: visit campus, start the form, register for the SSAT or ISEE.
  • November: finish and submit the form; sit the test; schedule the Candidate Conversation.
  • December: request the prior-year transcript; ask teachers for recommendations before the holidays.
  • Early January: confirm scores, current grades and recommendations have arrived; submit the Clarity application if needed.

Two deadlines, one plan

I plan the Newark Academy application with families from September: the form by Thanksgiving, the test in the autumn, the conversation prepared in advance.

The financial aid calendar

New families complete the Clarity application by 11 January 2027, the same day as the supplemental materials; returning families have an earlier date, 2 November 2026. Aid decisions are communicated in the spring.

After 19 February

Families have two weeks, until 5 March, to reply. For those weighing several schools in New Jersey and New York, it helps to have visited each one and compared costs, including aid, before decisions arrive.
