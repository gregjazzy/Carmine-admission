Applying to Milton from abroad

Milton Academy, just south of Boston, enrols students from 29 countries, among them France, China, Singapore, South Africa, Chile and Hungary. For a family abroad, the process is the same as for an American family, with a few points that deserve particular attention.

From abroad, in practice

StepFor an international family
StatusBoarding, for Grade 9, 10 or 11; day places are only for Greater Boston
ApplicationGateway to Prep Schools or the SSAT Standard Application; fee $125 instead of $65
GradesOfficial grades for two years and the current fall, sent by the school
RecommendationsA school administrator, the current English teacher and the current math teacher
InterviewTwo parts, on Zoom with parents, between September and mid-January
TestsSSAT or ISEE optional; TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo strongly advised if needed
Deadline15 January; aid by 31 January; decisions on 10 March

English

For students whose first language is not English, or who have spent less than two years in a school where English is the main language of instruction, Milton strongly suggests sending results from TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo. The interview, in English, and the "English teacher" recommendation will also show the student's level. A teacher who teaches English as a foreign language may not be the right person to describe how the student reads and writes in a full English curriculum; families should check who will write that letter.

Financial aid outside the United States

Need-based aid is open to students who are not US citizens or permanent residents. On top of the online aid application, international families submit tax documents for the two previous years, with English translations where needed, a letter from the employer specifying the terms of employment, a bank certificate stating all account balances, and bank statements for November and December. All of it is due by 31 January.

The interview across time zones

For a family abroad, the virtual interview is usually the practical choice. Booking early in the autumn gives a choice of times that suit both Boston and home. A candidate who has practised speaking to an adult on screen, in English, about their reading and interests arrives far more at ease.

Life on arrival

Boarders stay in the same house for four years, with faculty living alongside and older students guiding newcomers. Twice a week, houses gather for family-style dinners, and local families volunteer through the Host Family Program to welcome boarders for a weekend night or a meal.
