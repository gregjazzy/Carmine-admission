IMSA runs two parallel calendars: one for Illinois residents and an earlier one for the small number of tuition-paying students from other states. Both start on the same day and converge in late May, when admitted and waitpool students take the placement test.

All materials, including scores and evaluations, must be received by the deadline for the application to be considered.

From May to move-in

  • Responses: admitted and waitpool students must accept or decline to keep their position.
  • 27 to 29 May: placement testing, one date, for all admitted and waitpool students.
  • 31 May: required registration forms in PowerSchool.
  • 11 or 12 June: orientation, required, with at least one parent.
  • 1 July: fee invoices, and tuition invoices for out-of-state students.
  • 11 to 31 July: EXCEL, a three-week summer programme for students selected from placement results.
  • 1 August: first or full payments due.
  • Sophomore move-in and convocation: convocation is the last day a student can be called from the waitpool.

I plan the IMSA year with families from September: test in the autumn, essays before the holidays, evaluations in January, and preparation for the placement test in May.

The dates families underestimate

The SAT or ACT must be taken early enough for official scores to arrive before the deadline. The January window for teacher and counselor forms is short, especially for out-of-state applicants. And the June orientation is compulsory, so family travel plans should be set around it. For out-of-state families, the gap between the 8 March decision and the 22 March response is short; a family weighing other schools should have its comparison ready before decisions arrive.

One calendar for the family

For seven years, I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at very selective schools and programmes. For IMSA, I start in September with a test plan, and keep every later date on one calendar for the family.
