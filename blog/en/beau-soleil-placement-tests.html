At Beau Soleil, every applicant sits written evaluations in maths and English, after meeting the admissions team. For seven years, I have supported around fifteen families a year towards the most selective schools, and I have students at Beau Soleil.

What the school says

These placement tests apply to all applicants; a student already at another Nord Anglia school follows a simplified process. The Admissions Committee then reviews the file and gives its decision within a week, in writing.

Preparing both tests

I prepare your child in maths and English to the level of the target grade, so they show what they really know.

What the school does not say

No minimum level is published. Expectations vary by entry grade, and an older student needs stronger English to follow the programme.
