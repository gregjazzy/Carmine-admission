Placement tests come at a precise moment in Beau Soleil's admission process: after the family has applied, paid the fee and met the school. Every applicant sits them, in maths and in English, and the results then go to the Admissions Committee with the rest of the file.

Where the tests sit

A student currently enrolled in another Nord Anglia school follows a simplified process.

What the tests are for

The school looks for academic readiness, and expectations vary by entry grade. The maths and English tests help it judge whether a child can follow the programme of the grade applied for, the Middle Years Programme in Grades 6 to 10 or the IB Diploma and US High School Diploma in Grades 11 and 12. In English especially, older students need a stronger level to manage their courses.

Maths and English, at the right level

I assess children against the level of their entry grade, then prepare them for both placement tests, especially when they come from another language or curriculum.

Preparing calmly

  • Maths: consolidate the content of the current school year, with attention to problem solving rather than only procedures.
  • English: regular reading and writing in English; for a child schooled in another language, this is where most of the work lies.
  • Format: practise written answers under time, since the tests are written evaluations.

Is your child's English strong enough for their entry grade? Let's check together.

Read with the rest of the file

The tests are not read alone. The committee also has two years of school reports, the current school's reference, the handwritten letter and the impression from the meeting. A child whose tests are uneven but whose reports and letter show steady work and real motivation still presents a coherent file.

How I help

I have students at Beau Soleil, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools in Switzerland, England and the United States. Before the tests, I work on maths and English at the level of the grade the child will enter.
