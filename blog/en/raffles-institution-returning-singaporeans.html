A Singaporean family coming home after years abroad often has Raffles Institution in mind for a son. The route back, however, does not start at the school. Raffles directs returning students to the Ministry of Education's placement exercises, which apply in the same way to every school in Singapore.

Who counts as returning

The Ministry uses the term for Singapore citizen and permanent resident children who are returning to Singapore after living and studying overseas, and who are not currently in local schools. They can apply to join a primary school, a secondary school, a junior college or Millennia Institute.

Which exercise for which year

Entry at RafflesExercise
Years 1 to 3School Placement Exercise for Returning Singaporeans, Secondary (SPERS-Sec)
Year 5School Placement Exercise for Returning Singaporeans, JC/MI (SPERS-JC/MI)

These exercises apply to all government, government-aided and independent schools, so Raffles Institution follows the same rules as any other.

Preparing the return

  • Apply for the right level: the Ministry encourages families to apply for the level appropriate to the child's age cohort.
  • Strengthen English: it is the main medium of instruction, and the Ministry advises refreshing it before returning.
  • Use the Ministry's planning tools: its returning Singaporeans pages explain admissions, fees and support schemes by level.
  • Consider the timing: a return before Primary 6 opens the PSLE route; a return before Secondary 2, the Year 3 intake.

Once back in the system

A boy placed in a mainstream school through SPERS then has the same options as his classmates: the S1 Posting on PSLE results if he is in Primary 6, the Year 3 intake from Secondary 2, or Year 5 through DSA-JC or the Joint Admissions Exercise. Raffles' own pages list these routes for Singapore citizens and permanent residents alike.

Support for families abroad

The Ministry also points families to the Singapore Leaders Network's Overseas Transition Support programme, a fully sponsored scheme for those leaving, living abroad or returning.
