Nursery to Kindergarten at ASIJ

ASIJ's youngest children learn at the Early Learning Center in Roppongi, in central Tokyo: Nursery, Pre-Kindergarten and Kindergarten. Kindergarten is also offered at the Chofu campus, where the rest of the school continues to Grade 12. Families of Kindergarten applicants indicate their preferred campus when they apply.

The screening session

For local families, the main step is a screening session held in winter. For a three- or four-year-old, it is a short time with teachers, in a setting designed for small children, where the school observes how the child plays, communicates and responds. English matters even at this age: the program is taught in English, and ASIJ serves children with an established need for an English-medium education. A child who already hears and uses English every day will show much more of themselves.

Roppongi or Chofu for Kindergarten

The choice of campus for Kindergarten is also a choice about the years that follow. Roppongi is central and dedicated entirely to the youngest children. Chofu is the K-12 campus in western Tokyo, served by the school's bus routes, where a child who starts Kindergarten continues without changing site. Tuition for Nursery and Pre-K is ¥3,143,000 in 2026-27, and ¥3,498,000 from Kindergarten, with one-time fees on entry.
