Eton: conditional place, waiting list and the final exam

Eton College Chapel. Photo David P Howard, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

UK boarding schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

In the spring of Year 6, Eton answers families. Three answers are possible: a conditional place, a waiting-list place, or no offer. The first two still open two years of work.

The conditional place

It is confirmed at 12 or 13, in Year 8, by passing one of three exams: the King's Scholarship, Common Entrance or the Eton Entrance examination. In the meantime, in Year 7, the family meets several House Masters to choose the boy's boarding house, and pays the acceptance fee by the end of March to accept the offer.

Confirming the place

A conditional place is confirmed by an exam at 13. I prepare your son for Common Entrance or the Eton exam, and support him if you are aiming for the King's Scholarship.

The waiting list

Boys who accept a waiting-list place come back to Eton in the Summer term of Year 7 for a further assessment. Each year, Eton accepts a number of them; the number varies. One thing to know: a boy promoted from the waiting list can no longer receive a bursary, as the funds will already have been allocated.

Is your son on the waiting list? Write to me: the further assessment is prepared from now.

What I do for your family

I follow students at Eton and at the leading English boarding schools, and every year I support around fifteen families with their admissions. For a boy with a conditional place, I prepare the final exam with a margin: a place is rarely lost, but it can be. For a boy on the waiting list, I go back over what Stage 2 revealed and prepare him for the Year 7 assessment.
