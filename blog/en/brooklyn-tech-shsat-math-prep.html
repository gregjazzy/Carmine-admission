For a school focused on engineering and science, SHSAT math is natural ground. It counts for half the score.

What it covers

50 questions: ratios and proportional relationships, numbers and expressions, equations and inequalities, statistics and probability. Some ask for several answers, others for a typed answer. The content is end of middle school.

Where points are won

Around Brooklyn Tech's cutoff, the gap is made on medium questions: no careless errors, speed on simple calculations, and the ability to set up a multi-step problem.
