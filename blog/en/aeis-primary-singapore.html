To enter a Singapore government primary school, Primary 2 to Primary 5, the AEIS has two stages. I have students who entered Singapore government schools through the AEIS, and for seven years I have supported around fifteen families a year with admissions.

English first

Before even applying, the child must have reached a minimum score in a Cambridge English Qualifications test, depending on age. The statement of results is attached to the application.

Maths second

The test covers maths from the Singapore curriculum of the previous level: to enter Primary 3, the Primary 2 syllabus. For 2026, tests on September 3, results from December 17 to 21.

Preparing both stages

I prepare your child for the Cambridge English test, then Singapore-curriculum maths.
