For an international child aged eight to twelve, the AEIS Primary is the way into a Singapore government primary school. It has an unusual shape: English is checked before the application, through a Cambridge English result, and the test day itself is about mathematics alone. Both halves need preparing, and in that order.

I prepare children for both. Here is how AEIS Primary works.

The steps

StepDetail
Cambridge EnglishA2 Key for Schools (100, 120 or 130 for Primary 2, 3 or 4) or B1 Preliminary for Schools (140 for Primary 5), sat after the date the ministry sets
ApplicationCandidates Portal: birth certificate (with an English translation if needed), passport bio-data page valid at least six months, Cambridge statement of results; fee $340 plus GST, non-refundable
TestMathematics on the syllabus of the level below the one applied for; in 2026, on 3 September in Singapore
ResultsIn 2026, from 17 to 21 December, then reporting to the school from 18 to 30 December

Mathematics on the level below

A child applying for Primary 3 is tested on Primary 2 mathematics, and one applying for Primary 5 on Primary 4. The content follows the primary mathematics syllabus published by the ministry. A child who is good at mathematics in another system can still meet topics in a different order, or problem formats they have never seen. Working through the previous level's syllabus systematically is the core of preparation.

A lower level is possible

At primary level, the outcome can be the level applied for, a lower one, or no place. A nine-year-old who sits the Primary 3 test can be offered Primary 3, 2 or 1. Placement depends on the result and on vacancies, near the declared home where possible; the school posting is final. Families who would rather wait for an April start can also consider the S-AEIS, which covers Primary 2 to 4.
