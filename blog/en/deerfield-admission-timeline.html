The Deerfield admission timeline

The Main School Building, Deerfield Academy, Massachusetts. Photo John Phelan, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

Deerfield admits for September only, and publishes its own calendar in three phases. Read closely, it is more demanding than the single January 15 date suggests: the school expects the test, the interview and most of the writing to be done well before the deadline, and it warns that interview space runs short in December and January.

Phase one: July to September

The Gateway application opens on July 1. This is the moment for the Candidate Profile, which unlocks the Deerfield Admission Portal, and for visits. In summer, Deerfield offers campus tours and information sessions by appointment, Monday to Thursday, from late June to late August, and tour-and-interview appointments for families applying for the coming fall. A family that visits in summer can often settle the interview at the same time, before term begins.

Phase two: October and November

  • The essays of Part II: short answer, personal essay, graded essay.
  • The test booked, ideally for November or December; registration deadlines fall well before test dates.
  • The interview and tour scheduled, early.
  • Recommendation links sent to the three teachers and the counselor through Gateway, with the request for previous grades.
  • The extra items in the Deerfield portal checklist, which Gateway's own list does not show.

Three phases, one plan

I turn Deerfield's calendar into a working plan for the child, so that the test, the essays and the interview are each prepared in the right phase and none is rushed in January.

Phase three: December and January

Testing should be finished. A January sitting is still accepted, but the file is reviewed only once the score arrives, which leaves little margin. The request for current-year grades goes out, and any missing pieces are completed. On January 15 the complete application and the Clarity financial aid application are both due.

The interview squeeze

Interview space becomes limited in December and January, and booking early is the rule. Because the interview is required, a family that leaves it late can find itself choosing between an awkward slot and a delayed file. The interview is also better after some preparation, so the right moment is usually October or November: late enough to have practised, early enough to have choice.

After January 15

Deerfield makes every effort to consider applications completed after the priority date, testing included, but cannot guarantee a March 10 answer. Late candidates who do not hear on March 10 compete for whatever places remain after April 10, when admitted families return their contracts, and the committee meets periodically through the spring to decide on them. A late file is possible, then, but it waits on other families' choices.

March 10 and April 10

Admission and financial aid decisions arrive together on March 10. Families then have until April 10 to return the enrollment contract and, if aided, to accept the aid offer. A family weighing several schools should prepare the comparison before March, not in the four weeks that follow.

Planning the year with me

I have students at Deerfield, and for seven years I have guided, each year, around fifteen families through admissions to leading schools in the United States, England and Switzerland. With a Deerfield family I plan backwards from the November test date and the interview slot, because those two set everything else: the spring and summer for test preparation, September for the essays and recommenders, October for the interview.
