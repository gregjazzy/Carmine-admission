Swiss boarding schools

The handwritten motivation letter

In an age of online forms, Beau Soleil still asks for something written by hand: a letter of motivation, completed by the prospective student. It goes with the application form and two years of school reports, and it is one of the few pieces in the file where the committee hears the child's own voice.

What the school asks

PieceWho provides it
Application formThe family, on paper or online
School reports, last two yearsThe family sends copies
Handwritten motivation letterThe prospective student
Reference formThe current school, at Beau Soleil's request, with the family's permission

What the letter can show

Beau Soleil looks for kindness, curiosity, an open mind and a willingness to take part in school life, alongside academic readiness. Reports and tests show readiness. A letter can show the rest: why this child wants to board in the Alps, what they hope to try, how they imagine living with students from other countries. Written by hand, it also shows care and effort in a way a typed text does not.

The child's words, made clear

I never write the letter. I help children find what they genuinely want to say about Beau Soleil, then organise it so the committee can read it easily.

Book a conversation

What to put in it

  • A reason that is the child's own: skiing, expeditions, languages, theatre, a programme like the bilingual IB.
  • Something about who they are: an interest, a habit, a moment that says more than a list of qualities.
  • Openness to boarding life: sharing a room, living with other nationalities, learning French in Vaud.
  • Honesty about what is new: a first boarding experience, a language still being learned.

Is your child unsure what to write? Let's talk it through with them.

How parents can help

Parents can help their child think: by asking questions, recalling what attracted them on a visit, and reading a draft for clarity. They should not dictate it. A letter that sounds like an adult loses exactly what makes it useful, and an experienced admissions team will notice.

How I help
