How to get into NCSSM

The NCSSM entrance in Durham, North Carolina. Photo Warren LeMay, Wikimedia Commons, CC0

Book a conversation

The North Carolina School of Science and Mathematics, NCSSM, is a public STEM high school, with two residential campuses, in Durham and Morganton, and an online programme. Students spend their last two years of high school there. The school costs nothing: no application fee, tuition, meals, room and board or textbooks.

The conditions

Only legal residents of North Carolina may apply, and they do so in their second year of high school. The single application, for the residential, online and summer programmes, opens on October 15, 2026 and closes on January 5, 2027 for the Class of 2029.

The pieces

The online form on CFNC, essays, three evaluations, from a STEM teacher, an English teacher and the school counselor, transcripts between February 15 and 28, and for the residential programme, the NCSSM math assessment. The SAT and ACT are neither required nor considered.

A prepared application

Math assessment, essays, choice of evaluators: I prepare every piece of your child's file to the level NCSSM expects.
