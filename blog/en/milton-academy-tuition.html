Milton Academy has chosen a simple pricing model for its Upper School: one annual tuition, which includes on-campus student experiences and tuition insurance, and no mandatory fees. The current figures are $79,900 a year for boarders and $69,900 for day students.

What each fee includes

Teaching and on-campus activitiesIncludedIncluded

MealsLunch; day students are also welcome at weekday dinners and weekend activities with boardersAll meals
RoomNoneIncluded

The gap between the two, $10,000 a year, is the price of a room, meals and health services at a full boarding school near Boston.

What is billed separately

  • Books and supplies, charged to the student's incidental account according to the courses chosen.
  • A laptop, which every student is expected to bring.
  • Optional programmes, such as the daily bus service from Greater Boston, music lessons, cultural exchanges and pre-season athletic trips.

The price follows the place

At Milton, the place is won through the grades, three teachers' letters, the profile and the interview. I prepare each of these with the family.

Spreading the cost

Milton offers payment plans administered by FACTS Management, which spread the year's costs over monthly instalments. Families who enrol without financial aid are expected to continue paying full tuition; they can apply for aid later only if their circumstances change significantly.

Aid changes the figure

Milton strives to meet 100% of the demonstrated need of every student it enrols, and all its aid is need-based; there are no merit scholarships. About 37% of students receive aid, with an average grant of 77% of tuition. Aid applications are due by 31 January, alongside the application process. A family that thinks it might qualify should apply: the first reading of every application ignores ability to pay.

My view

For seven years, I have worked with around fifteen families a year on applications to very selective schools. At Milton, what makes a file succeed is the reading of the student, not the fee; for families who need help, the aid deadline matters as much as the admission one. I help families plan both dates together, so that neither the file nor the aid request is rushed in January.
