Deerfield, Choate or Andover: how to choose

The Main School Building, Deerfield Academy, Massachusetts. Photo John Phelan, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

US boarding schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Families aiming at the leading New England boarding schools rarely apply to just one. Deerfield, Choate and Andover often come up together. Their files all go through Gateway, but each has its own requirements.

What sets them apart

Testing: required at Deerfield and Andover, optional at Choate. The interview: required at Deerfield, on campus or online; online only at Choate; on campus, virtual or recorded at Andover. Written pieces: three essays including a graded essay at Deerfield; essays and parent statement at Choate; essay, graded writing sample and short video at Andover. Dates: January 15 for Deerfield and Choate, February 1 for Andover.

Several applications, one strategy

I prepare students for Deerfield, Choate and Andover. I build a single timeline to run the applications side by side, with the pieces specific to each school.

How to run all three

One SSAT serves all three: required at two, sent to Choate if it is good. One graded paper can serve both Deerfield and Andover. The essays, however, answer different prompts, and each interview is prepared for its own format.
