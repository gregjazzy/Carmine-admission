Deerfield, Choate or Andover

The Main School Building, Deerfield Academy, Massachusetts. Photo John Phelan, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

Deerfield, Choate and Andover often sit on the same family's list. All three use Gateway to Prep Schools, all three are among the most demanding boarding schools in New England, and Deerfield and Choate both answer on March 10. That is roughly where the resemblance ends. Each school has built its application around a different idea of what it needs to see, and a family applying to all three is preparing three files, not one file sent three times.

Where the work overlaps

The test. One SSAT or ISEE, prepared once, serves all three for a ninth-grade applicant: required at Deerfield and Andover, sent to Choate only if it helps. Deerfield reads the writing sample, so that part deserves preparation too.

The graded paper. Deerfield and Andover both ask for writing already graded by a teacher. The same paper can often serve both, but Deerfield wants teacher comments and visible revision, so the choice should be made with Deerfield's criteria in mind.

The recommenders. The current English and math teachers write for all three through Gateway. Deerfield adds a third current teacher; Andover adds a personal recommender.

Three schools, one coherent preparation

I prepare children for Deerfield, Choate and Andover together: one test, the graded paper chosen for the most demanding reader, three sets of essays, and three interviews in their own formats.

Where it does not

The essays. The prompts differ, the readers differ, and the other evidence in each file differs. An essay written for one school and pasted into another reads as exactly that.

The interviews. Deerfield's is one-on-one with an admission officer, two hours on campus with a tour or one hour on Zoom with time for parents. Choate's is online only, forty-five minutes, once. Andover's can even be recorded. A child may face three different exercises, and each needs its own practice.

The video. Only Andover asks for one.

Applying to two or three of these schools? Tell me about your child, and I will tell you how to organise it.

The calendar

Two of the three deadlines fall on January 15, which means Deerfield and Choate drive the autumn; Andover's February 1 leaves two weeks for its specific pieces, the video above all. Interviews are the bottleneck: Deerfield's slots grow scarce in December and January, Choate's run from September to February, Andover's through January. Booking all three in October or November, after some practice, avoids a January pile-up.
