Alongside direct admission by score, the city runs Discovery programs each summer at the eight testing specialized high schools, Stuyvesant included.

What to know

These summer programmes last three to five weeks and are for certain disadvantaged students whose SHSAT score falls within a given range below the cutoff; from fall 2026, a student who scored 495 or above is not eligible. Participation is by invitation, in the March offer letter, and only for schools listed at registration. Students must attend a high-poverty public school, or live in a high-poverty area if at a non-public school, and meet an additional condition such as modest family income. The programme is completed through attendance and passing coursework.
