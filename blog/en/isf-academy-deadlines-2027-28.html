The ISF Academy runs a separate calendar for each entry level. Applications for 2027-28 opened on August 1, 2026, all online through the Friends of ISF website, which families join before they can apply. From there, each grade has its own deadline, its own assessment dates and its own notification date, spread from late summer 2026 to spring 2027.

I build this calendar with every family I accompany to ISF. Here it is.

Relationship applicants for Foundation Year are children of alumni, nominees of Capital Note holders, siblings of current students and children at The ISF Pre-School. Families in the middle of buying a Capital Note for a Foundation Year child should submit by August 31 to benefit from that priority.

Dates you cannot move

The first stage must be attended in Hong Kong on the scheduled dates. A family that cannot attend must tell the admissions office at least three business days ahead; the school will offer at most one alternative date within the same period, and if no date works, the application is closed without refund. A child who is ill on the morning should not attend: with a doctor's note, a new date is given, whereas a child who sits the assessment unwell gets no second chance.

After the offer

Successful families confirm enrolment online by the date in the offer letter and pay the Acceptance Deposit and capital contribution by the invoice due date. A waitlist exists for a select group of applicants; it stays open until the first week after the Lunar New Year break, when the last new students are enrolled, and closes in early February. Families on it should send the school the latest school report as soon as it is available.
