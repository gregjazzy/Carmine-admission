At The ISF Academy, each grade has its own deadline. For 2027 entry, applications opened on August 1, 2026.

The dates

August 31, 2026: Foundation Year, relationship applicants (alumni, siblings, ISF Pre-School, Capital Note).

September 15, 2026: Foundation Year, all others.

November 5, 2026: Grade 1.

November 22, 2026: Grades 6 to 11.

January 25, 2027: Grades 2 to 5.

Applications are made only online, through the Friends of ISF website.
