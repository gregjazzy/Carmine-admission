AEIS and school fees

The AEIS route into Singapore's government schools costs far less than an international school, but it is not free for international students. There are three layers: the AEIS application fee, the Cambridge English test for primary applicants, and, once a place is accepted, monthly school fees set by nationality.

I go through these costs with families planning a move. Here they are.

The costs

ItemAmountNote
AEIS Primary application$340 plus GSTNon-refundable; card, debit card or PayNow
AEIS Secondary application$630 plus GSTNon-refundable
Cambridge English testSet by the exam centrePrimary applicants only, before applying
Monthly fee, mainstream secondary school, 2026 and 2027Up to $1,111.80 (ASEAN) or $2,211.80 (other nationalities)GST included for international students

Monthly fees cover school fees and miscellaneous fees and depend on nationality and the type of school; the ministry's online fee checker gives the exact amount for a given school. Independent and specialised schools set their own fees.

The cost of a failed attempt

The application fee is the smallest cost of an unsuccessful attempt. A child who does not pass the AEIS in September cannot start in January; the next chances are the S-AEIS for an April or May start, which covers fewer levels, or the following AEIS. In the meantime, most families pay for an international or private school. Seen that way, preparation for the right level is the cheapest part of the route. It also spares the child a second round of testing in a new country.

Nationality matters

Fees for students from ASEAN countries, Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Thailand, Timor-Leste and Vietnam, are about half those for other international students. Permanent residents pay less again. A family whose status may change during the child's schooling should check how fees are updated after a change of citizenship.
