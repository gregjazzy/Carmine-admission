Beau Soleil teaches in English and French. The English level expected depends on the entry grade: an older student needs stronger English to follow the programme.

Languages

English, French, German, Italian and Spanish are included in fees. Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Russian, Arabic and Turkish can be added subject to availability, at extra cost. French is strongly encouraged up to Grade 10.

Support

A Learning Support Team helps all students, including those with diagnosed learning differences. Existing assessments should be shared at application.

English at the right level

I work on your child's written English for the entrance tests and for following lessons afterwards.
