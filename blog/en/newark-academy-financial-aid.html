Newark Academy's commitment on aid is unusually clear: it meets 100 % of demonstrated financial need for every family it admits. All aid is based on need; the school does not award merit scholarships. About one student in five receives help.

Aid beyond tuition

Newark Academy watches the total cost of attendance. For families on aid, it provides proportional aid on every required item, including lunch and technology fees, transportation, books, and the Immersion and Capstone experiences. Each aided student also receives a discretionary allowance for other school-related costs.

Admission first

Aid follows admission. I prepare students for the test, the form and the conversation, so that the family's need never becomes the question.

How need is measured

Need is the difference between the cost of attendance and what the family can pay, as calculated by Clarity from income, assets, expenses, debt and other factors. The Financial Aid Committee reviews completed applications and communicates decisions in the spring. The online form typically takes under 30 minutes, and Clarity runs family webinars in the autumn.

Rules families should know

  • Both biological parents are expected to contribute and to provide financial information, unless one is deceased; a waiver exists where one parent has no role in the child's life.
  • Stepparents' income and assets are taken into account, along with their obligations to their own children.
  • A non-working parent with a child over five is assigned imputed wages of $30,000 a year, unless documented circumstances justify otherwise.
  • Every year: awards last one year, and families reapply annually.

Who is not eligible

Families must pay US taxes to receive aid; Newark Academy does not offer financial aid to international students.

The Newark Scholars Program

Students who live or attend school in the city of Newark and complete the admission and aid processes are considered for the Newark Scholars Program, which covers tuition, fees, trips and transportation for up to six students a year.
