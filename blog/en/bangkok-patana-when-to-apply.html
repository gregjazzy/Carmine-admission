For international families, Bangkok Patana has no single deadline: it accepts applications throughout the year. That flexibility suits families whose move to Bangkok depends on a job offer. But rolling admission does not mean any moment is as good as another.

Where things stand

School yearStatusWhat it means
2026/27Still accepting applicationsMany year groups full; files placed in a waiting pool; check availability first
2027/28Open since 1 September 2026Early applications encouraged; if no place yet, the file is processed and placed in a pool

Thai nationals follow a different calendar: one window, from 1 September 2026 to 15 January 2027, for 2027/28.

Why early matters

An application enters consideration only once complete, and in the waiting pool the date of completion is one of the factors, even if it comes last. Gathering two years of reports, a teacher recommendation and professional translations takes time; a family that starts the file as soon as a move becomes likely is better placed than one that starts after arriving.

A file ready when you decide

I prepare children's English and the writing sample ahead of a move, so the application can go in as soon as the family is ready.

The school year

In 2026/27, the first term runs from 19 August to 18 December 2026, the second from 11 January to 2 April 2027, and the third from 19 April to 25 June 2027. Tuition for the first term is invoiced in May, so a family aiming at an August start should expect to commit before the summer.

Planning a move to Bangkok? Let's set the timing together.

Before the first day

After an offer, the family signs the offer letter and pays a non-refundable initial THB 50,000 to hold the place, then the entrance fee by its due date. Non-Thai students must hold a valid non-immigrant visa, with copies provided before the child starts. Families with questions about visas should contact the school before applying.

Visits

The admissions office arranges on-site tours, Zoom tours or video calls with the admissions team, which helps families abroad decide before travelling. The office is open from 7.30am to 4pm during term time, and the school welcomes enquiries throughout the year.
