In the IMSA file, parents write too: a statement of 200 to 300 words.

What the school asks

Help the school know the child from your perspective: what makes them unique, their interest in STEM. IMSA asks parents to be open, honest and authentic, and says there is no right or wrong answer.

What works

Specific facts about your child rather than a list of qualities; and, for a residential school, what shows they can live away from home at 15.

A well-judged statement

I review your statement with you, so it adds to the file rather than repeating it.
