The SHSAT calendar for Brooklyn Tech

Most SHSAT calendars stop at the March offer. For a Brooklyn Tech family, the year continues: an offer to accept, a summer of emails, and a first day in a nine-storey building with ten periods. Here is the whole sequence for students entering in September 2027.

Autumn 2026: the test

DateWhat
6 to 30 OctoberSHSAT registration and ranking in MySchools; High School Application in the same season
14 or 15 NovemberTest at a central site for 8th graders from 6-12, K-12, charter, private, parochial or independent schools, or home-schooled
18 NovemberTest at their own school for 8th graders at public middle schools ending in 8th grade
21 NovemberTest for 9th graders applying to 10th grade

Students from districts 13, 14, 15, 16, 19, 23 and 32 who test at a central site do so at Brooklyn Tech.

Spring 2027: the offer

Offers arrive in March, by score and ranked list. A specialized offer must be accepted in MySchools by the deadline, or it is forfeited. Scores follow the offers, and in spring families can view the test through Test View.

Before October, and after March

I prepare students for the SHSAT through the year before the test, and help admitted students arrive ready for 9th grade mathematics.

Summer 2027: becoming a Tech student

  • July to September: welcome letters from the parent coordinator, sent by email in several instalments.
  • The Blue Card: a form for incoming parents to update email lists.
  • Around mid-July: students appear on the school register; students from private schools or home schooling receive their NYC student number and Department of Education email.
  • September: the first day, with the schedule available the night before.

The first weeks

The school day runs ten periods of 45 minutes, from 8:05 a.m. to 3:34 p.m., on a ten-day cycle. There are no homerooms; students belong to "prefects" that meet once or twice a term. Students stay in the building all day. Families who know this in advance help their child settle faster.

How I plan it

I have students at Brooklyn Tech, and for seven years I have worked with around fifteen families a year aiming at the most selective schools. For the SHSAT, I plan backwards from November; for admitted students, forward to the first grades that will feed the Power Index.
