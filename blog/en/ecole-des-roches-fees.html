École des Roches: fees 2026-2027

École des Roches, Verneuil-sur-Avre. Photo Djampa, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Boarding schools November 2026 4 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

The École des Roches is a full boarding school, and its fees cover the essentials: boarding and accommodation, all meals, teaching, standard extracurricular activities, on-campus healthcare and school insurance. For 2026-2027, a year costs €35,640 to €45,540 for the French baccalaureate in the final two years, €41,580 to €51,480 for the pre-lycée foundation year, and €47,520 to €66,528 for the IB Diploma. Fees are reviewed every year and confirmed in the enrolment contract.

What the price does not say

That is considerably less than the leading Swiss boarding schools, some of which exceed CHF 160,000 a year, for a school just over an hour from Paris, in partnership with the École Jeannine Manuel. But a family that chooses Les Roches for its price is asking the wrong question. What matters is the programme and environment that suit their child: a boarding school of 240 students, in the countryside, with classes of six to twelve.
