Secondary entry to The ISF Academy, from Grade 6 to Grade 11, follows the same staged logic as primary, on an earlier calendar. Applications close on November 22, 2026, the written stage is on Sunday, December 6, and families hear by March 25, 2027. Applicants enter an IB school: the Middle Years Programme from Grade 6 and the Diploma Programme in Grades 11 and 12.

I prepare secondary applicants for ISF every year. Here is how the entry works.

The stages

StageContentDate
1AWritten papers in Chinese, English and English mathematics; non-verbal reasoning; a computerized assessmentSunday, December 6, 2026
1BIndividual and group interviews and activities in English and PutonghuaBetween January 11 and 15, 2027
2Parents meet a senior member of the schoolFebruary to March 2027

Unlike primary, mathematics is assessed in English only. Chinese, however, remains a full written paper: grade-level Chinese, with Putonghua for the orals, is required from Grade 1 to Grade 11.

The language balance in secondary

In Grades 6 to 8, about 30 percent of teaching is in Putonghua and 70 percent in English; in Grades 9 and 10, 20 and 80 percent; in Grades 11 and 12 it depends on the subjects chosen. For a student strong in English, the secondary programme is more accessible than the primary one, but the entry still tests Chinese in writing and in conversation. For a student from a Chinese-medium system, the reverse applies: English must be strong enough to learn most subjects in it.

Scholarships from Grade 6

From Grade 6, ISF offers scholarships on two pathways, academic and special talent in art, drama, music or sport, open to students who show academic excellence, exceptional talent and commitment to the school community. They are separate from need-based financial aid, which covers 20 to 100 percent of fees.
