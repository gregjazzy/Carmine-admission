The bilingual IB and US diploma

At 16, Beau Soleil students choose between two diplomas, or combine them. The International Baccalaureate Diploma is the demanding, broad route favoured by leading universities; the US High School Diploma is a more careers-focused programme. Both run over Grades 11 and 12.

Two diplomas compared

CoreTheory of Knowledge, Extended Essay, Creativity Activity ServiceTheory of Knowledge and Creativity Activity Service

The 2026 results

Beau Soleil's IB students averaged 35.0 points in 2026, more than four points above the world average, with a top score of 45 out of 45, a 98 % pass rate and a third of students earning the Bilingual Diploma. Every HSD student passed. The school was also listed among the top IB schools in Switzerland and Europe in the 2025 IB-schools.com league tables.

The bilingual option

Because the school sits in French-speaking Vaud, it can teach IB subjects in both English and French. A student may follow the Diploma in one language or combine the two for a Bilingual Diploma. For a French-speaking family, this keeps French at an academic level while preparing for English-speaking universities; for others, it turns years of French study into a recognised qualification.

Joining for the senior years

Families aiming at Grade 11 should apply before the end of November of the year before. A student joining in Grade 11 commits to the two-year programme, and the school does not admit new students into Grade 12. Fees for Grades 11 and 12 are CHF 148,440 a year for boarding and tuition, reflecting exam preparation, university counselling and tailored support.
