Lyceum Alpinum Zuoz: the admission interview, how to prepare

Book a conversation

At Zuoz, the test says whether the child will keep up with the programme. The interview says whether they will thrive in boarding, and that is often the more delicate question. I follow students at the leading Swiss boarding schools, and every year I prepare children for these meetings.

How it works

Applicants shortlisted on their file are invited to an interview and a test, in Zuoz or online. The school then decides, taking into account school results, its assessments and teachers' recommendations. A personal tour and a trial day in boarding are possible before committing.

Preparing for Zuoz's interview

A child who talks about themselves accurately, a family that knows why it chooses Zuoz: I prepare both, for Zuoz as for the other boarding schools in view.

What the school looks for

Zuoz is a sporty, well-ordered boarding school at 1,750 metres, where afternoon sport is compulsory, evening study shapes the week and on certain weekends everyone stays on campus. The school sums up its values in what it calls the Spirit of Zuoz: tolerance, respect, fair play, commitment. The interview looks for a child who will recognise themselves in it. The school publishes neither its questions nor what tips the balance.

Topics to prepare

Boarding life. Have they lived away from home before? Does sharing a room with a classmate from another culture worry them?

Sport and the mountains. At Zuoz they are not optional. A child who loves them says so naturally.

The programme in view. IB, Matura, IGCSE: a child who knows why they are aiming for one rather than another shows they have a plan.

Is your child made for boarding? Tell me about them: I will tell you what I think.

The costly mistakes

A child who did not choose

A teenager sent to boarding school without wanting it shows it. Better to have talked about it frankly beforehand, and to do the trial day.

Learned answers

A prepared speech is spotted, online even more than in person. The interview looks for a personality.

Not knowing the school

Arriving knowing nothing of its houses, its sport or its programmes gives the image of a family that chose a name.

What I do for your family

I prepare the child for the interview through mock interviews, in English or German depending on the programme, and help the family clarify what it expects from Zuoz. The test is prepared at the same time. For the whole picture, see Zuoz admission: IB or Matura.
