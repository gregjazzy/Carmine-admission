Westminster School fees 2026-27: day, boarding and the admission costs

College, the King's Scholars' house at Westminster School, from the garden. Photo stevecadman, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

London schools November 2026 6 min read

Westminster's fees cover more than many families expect: full tuition, teaching materials, lunches, and most clubs, societies and enrichment activities, with accommodation and all meals added for boarders. For 2026-27, the school charges three levels at its senior school, all including VAT.

The fees, per term and per year

Day pupil, entering at 16+£17,482£52,446
Boarder, 13+ or 16+£23,094£69,282

King's Scholar, 16+£17,482£52,446

At Westminster Under School, fees are £11,088 a term in Reception and Year 1 and £11,671 from Year 2 to Year 8. The Governing Body usually reviews fees by the end of the summer term and publishes them in early July.

The King's Scholar effect

The table shows something families rarely notice: King's Scholars must board in College, yet they pay the day fee. The difference with an ordinary boarding place is £19,914 a year at current rates, close to £100,000 over five years from 13+. The scholarship is not means-tested, and a bursary can top it up to the full fee where there is need.

Deposits are returned after the pupil has left, once the account is settled; a deposit is not returned if a pupil withdraws before joining. At the Under School, entry involves a £300 registration fee, a £3,000 deposit kept until the child leaves and a further £5,000 set against the spring term invoice of the first year.

Earn the place first

At Westminster, the place is decided by the Pre-Tests, Westminster's own papers and the interview, or by The Challenge for King's Scholars. We prepare candidates for all of them.

A first year, two cases

A day pupil joining at 13+ in September: £360 at registration in Year 5 or 6, £2,400 on accepting in Year 6, £4,000 deposit by September of Year 8, then £49,368 of fees in the first year. An international boarder joining the Sixth Form: £360 at registration, £2,400 and a £5,000 deposit on accepting, £1,200 for visa sponsorship, then £69,282 in fees for the year, plus the family's own travel and guardianship arrangements.

What reduces the bill

King's Scholarships (the boarding fee reduced to the day fee), Music Awards and Scholarships (10 percent of the day fee, with free tuition on two instruments), and means-tested bursaries of up to 100 percent of day fees for families meeting strict UK residence conditions. Boarding bursaries are not given, except combined with a King's Scholarship. Families applying for a bursary may also have the registration and entrance fees waived.

Where to put the effort

None of these sums buys a place: selection is academic at every entry point. The question for families is therefore which door, 11+, 13+ or 16+, and how to prepare the child for what that door tests.
