Applying to Concord Academy from abroad

Concord Academy's international students make up 14% of the school. For a family abroad, the residential life is part of the appeal: a safe, attractive town near Boston, friends from around the world, and house faculty who know every student personally. The application itself follows the same calendar as for American families, with two additions.

Who counts as applying from abroad

The specific instructions apply when a family's primary residence is outside the United States, or when the student attends school outside the United States, whatever the passport.

Addition one: the Vericant interview

All such applicants must schedule their interview through Vericant. The admissions office then contacts families directly if, after reviewing the Vericant interview, it invites the student to interview with a member of the Concord Academy team. For the others, the Vericant interview serves as the interview. Two conditions matter: the inquiry form must be completed before Vericant sends its report, and the Vericant must be completed and submitted by 1 January to be considered for a CA interview. After that date, it simply fulfils the requirement.

Vericant and the CA interview

I prepare students abroad for the Vericant interview and, if invited, for the conversation with CA, in English and with ideas of their own.

Addition two: an English test

Students for whom English is not a first language submit an official TOEFL iBT or ITS, IELTS or Duolingo score by 15 January; CA's TOEFL code is 2255. This comes in addition to the required SSAT or ISEE, which must be taken between June 2026 and 15 January 2027. A waiver can be requested by email if English is the student's primary language or if they have attended an English-speaking school in the United States for the past three years.

Meeting CA without travelling

Admissions officers travel each autumn. In 2026, their international programme included events in Shanghai, Hong Kong, Singapore and Bangkok, and fairs in Johannesburg, Cape Town, Lagos and Accra. Virtual information sessions on boarding life, arts, athletics and financial aid are recorded and available online.

The suggested order

Create the Finalsite account and inquiry form first, book Vericant early in the autumn, sit the SSAT or ISEE and the English test before Christmas, and keep January for the written file. Decisions arrive on 10 March.
