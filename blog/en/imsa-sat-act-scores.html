IMSA asks 13- and 14-year-olds to sit the SAT or ACT, the US college entrance tests. I follow students at IMSA and at the leading schools in US science high schools, and every year I support around fifteen families with their admissions.

What the school says

An official score is required, sent directly by the testing company and received by the deadline. No minimum score: the test provides a common baseline across students from different schools. For the SAT, the standard version, maths and reading and writing, without essay; the PSAT is not accepted. For the ACT, the version without writing; the science section is not required. Accepted test dates run from August to early December.

Preparing the SAT in 8th grade

The SAT is written for 17-year-olds. I prepare your child for the maths and, above all, the reading and writing.

Why good students lose points

Reading

A student strong in maths often stumbles on the reading and writing section, built for high schoolers.
