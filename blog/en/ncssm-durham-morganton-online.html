NCSSM offers three ways in for a North Carolina 10th grader, and one application covers all of them. Two are residential campuses, where juniors and seniors live and study for two years at no cost. The third, NCSSM Online, lets students stay at their own high school while taking NCSSM courses.

Durham

NCSSM opened in Durham in 1980 with 150 juniors. Today, 680 students live in its residence halls, eat in its dining hall and train in its physical education centre, in a mid-size city close to Duke University. In 2023, Durham students counted more Regeneron Science Talent Search scholars than any other single school.

Morganton

The newer campus, in western North Carolina, serves about 300 juniors and seniors in a smaller community, in a region of technology, agriculture, manufacturing and field research, with outdoor recreation close by. It celebrated its third commencement in 2026.

Online

Begun in 2008, NCSSM Online is a two-year, sequenced honors program for students who remain enrolled at their local schools. Courses range widely, from molecular genetics to cryptography and data science, and students are encouraged to join clubs and research. Once a semester, they attend a Saturday session in Durham or Morganton.

Applying to more than one

Because one application covers Durham, Morganton, Online and Summer Ventures, a student can apply to several. A student set on living at NCSSM can list both campuses; a student who wants NCSSM's courses but is not ready to leave home can apply Online as well. The choice should reflect what the student would actually accept.
