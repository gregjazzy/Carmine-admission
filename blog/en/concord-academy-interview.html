The interview is one of the six parts of a Concord Academy application, and the one where the committee meets the student rather than reads about them. For a school that values individual expression and relationships with adults, it carries weight.

How the visit unfolds

  1. Campus tour. Families tour the campus before the interview, usually with student guides.
  2. The conversation. The applicant meets one-on-one with a member of the admissions interview team.
  3. The parents' turn. Parents or guardians then have time to speak with the interviewer.

Families should plan to be on campus for one and a half to two hours. Applicants who cannot travel can interview over Zoom. Booking is done in the applicant's Finalsite checklist, under "Concord Academy Interview", as soon as the process opens on 17 August.

What the interviewer is listening for

CA's admissions committee looks for intellectual curiosity, character, maturity, particular talents and passions, the capacity for positive relationships with adults and peers, and open-mindedness. An interview is where several of these show at once. A student who can explain why a book, a problem or a project mattered to them, and who asks real questions about the school, gives the interviewer something to write.

Is your child shy in interviews? Let's work on it together.

Preparing well

  • Know what draws you to CA: the 60-40 day and boarding balance, the weekly advisor meeting, the arts in the curriculum, a particular course.
  • Have two or three subjects you can talk about in depth, with examples.
  • Prepare questions you actually want answered.
  • Practise speaking with an adult you do not know well, not just reciting answers.

From abroad

Applicants who live or study outside the United States interview through Vericant. If the report reaches CA by 1 January, the school may invite the student to a second interview with its own team; otherwise, Vericant fulfils the requirement.
