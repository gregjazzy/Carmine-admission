Poly Prep belongs to ISAAGNY, the association of independent schools of greater New York, founded in 1965. Member schools share a calendar and several forms, so a family applying to three or four of them writes much of its file once. At Poly Prep, these shared forms, along with a few of the school's own, make up the written part of the application, due January 8, 2027.

The November 1 rule

Recommendations come from the child's current teachers, those teaching in 2026-27. The school asks families to send the request after November 1 so that the teacher knows the child, and for Grades 1 to 8 it states that it will not accept forms submitted before that date. Between November 1 and January 8, teachers have about nine weeks, including the holidays, and many other letters to write. A short, early conversation with each teacher is the simplest way to avoid a missing form.

The essays and the extras

The common essay goes to every ISAAGNY school on the list, so it should speak about the student rather than about one school. The Changing Schools essay, for applicants to non-entry grades, asks the question an admissions committee will have in mind anyway: why move now. Families may add videos or photos of a serious interest, from athletics and debate to visual art, music, theatre or dance, and an optional general recommendation from another adult who knows the child well.
