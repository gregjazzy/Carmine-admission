Grade placement from a calendar-year school

ASIJ's year runs from August to June. Many schools in the Southern Hemisphere, in Australia and New Zealand for example, run from January or February to November or December. A child moving from one to the other arrives halfway through a school year, and families often do not know whether to apply for the grade their child is in or the one above.

ASIJ answers this question with a clear rule. I help families apply it.

The rule

Entry at ASIJGrade to apply forExample, child in Year 5 in Sydney in 2027
AugustThe grade the child is currently attendingGrade 5 in August 2027, halfway through their Year 5
JanuaryThe grade the child has just completedGrade 5 in January 2028, after finishing Year 5 in December

In both cases, the child is placed so that their schooling continues without skipping half a year: in August they start the grade they are already in, extended; in January they join its second half.

What this means for a family

The rule protects children from a jump that would leave gaps. A child who arrives in August will repeat some months of content, especially in mathematics, where the order of topics differs between systems; this is usually easy to absorb. A child who arrives in January joins classmates who have already done the first half of the year at ASIJ, so knowing what that half covers helps. ASIJ also reads report cards for the two previous years plus the current one, which in a calendar-year system means gathering reports from several school years with different dates.

Coming from a calendar-year school? Tell me your child's grade and your dates.

The youngest

For the Early Learning Center and Kindergarten, ASIJ applies age cut-offs on September 1 of the year of entry: three for Nursery, five for Kindergarten. Families whose moving date falls during the year should also ask about places in the grade before applying, since mid-year entry depends on availability.
