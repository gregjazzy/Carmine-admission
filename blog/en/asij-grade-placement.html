ASIJ's year runs from August to June. For students from schools on a calendar year, as in Australia or New Zealand, the school has a clear rule.

The rule

The student enters the grade that extends their current school year: for August entry, they begin the grade they are attending; for January entry, they join the grade they have just completed.

What to take from it

The curriculum changes midway: check for gaps, especially in maths, before arriving.

Closing gaps

I compare what your child has covered with their future grade's curriculum, and close gaps before arrival.
