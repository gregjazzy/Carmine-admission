How to get into Milton Academy

Milton Academy sits just south of Boston and draws its Upper School students from more than 23 states and 29 countries. Its admission process is short to describe and has one date at its centre: 15 January, by which the application, the interview and every supporting document must be in. Decisions follow on 10 March.

The choice matters beyond the application: once admitted, a student cannot change between boarding and day status except in special circumstances.

What goes into the file

  • An online application, through Gateway to Prep Schools or the SSAT Standard Application, with a fee of $65, or $125 for international applicants; fee waivers exist.
  • The Candidate Biographic Profile, to be submitted well before the deadline.
  • Three required recommendations: a school administrator, the current English teacher and the current math teacher. A personal recommendation, a special interest recommendation and a graded writing sample are optional.
  • Official grades: the current fall term or semester and the final grades of the past two years.
  • A two-part interview, on campus or on Zoom, completed by 15 January.
  • Test scores only if the family chooses; students who cannot submit them are not disadvantaged.

How the file is read: two phases

In the first phase, admission officers read each application need-blind, without considering the family's ability to pay. In the final phase, the committee becomes need-aware and weighs its decisions against the financial aid available. Families who need aid should therefore apply for it, and on time: aid goes first to families who meet the 31 January deadline.

What Milton is looking for

Milton is a community where being smart and interested in school is valued, where life is collaborative rather than competitive, and where students bring a range of talents from every background. Read through the file, that means three teachers who describe a curious, engaged student, a profile written in the candidate's own voice, and an interview in which the candidate is plainly glad to talk about ideas.

A common mistake

Interview appointments fill quickly, and they are needed before 15 January. Families who wait until December to request information, activate the portal and book a slot often find only the last days left, at the busiest time of the application.
