Music and art scholarships at Godolphin

Godolphin and Latymer School, Hammersmith. Photo Des Blenkinsopp, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0

Many London schools reward their strongest entrants with academic scholarships. Godolphin and Latymer does not: every girl it admits has passed a demanding test, and it keeps its scholarships for exceptional talent in music and art.

The two awards

MusicArt
Entry11+ and 16+16+ only
ValueUp to 30 % of the fees, plus free lessons on one or two instrumentsUp to 30 % of the fees
ConditionSuccess in the academic entrance assessments
Top-upA means-tested bursary can increase the award

At 11+, the music scholarship is open to a talented musician who qualifies for entry into Year 7; a further music scholarship may be available in the Sixth Form.

The 11+ music timetable

  • September before entry: the Examination Entry Form, on which a family requests details of the music scholarship.
  • 13 November 2026, noon: music scholarship applications due, with the exam form.
  • 25 and 26 January 2027: auditions, with no alternative dates.
  • 12 February 2027: offers.

The 16+ route for artists and musicians

At 16+, the art scholarship is open to a talented artist who qualifies for entry into the Sixth Form. The sequence is short: registration and subject choices by noon on 13 October 2026, art and music scholarship applications by noon on 2 November, the entrance exam on the afternoon of 3 November, interviews on 14 November and offers on 1 December. A girl who wants to study art in the Sixth Form and apply for the award should keep her own artwork and her GCSE preparation moving together from the start of Year 11.

Talent, then the test

A scholarship only counts if the girl clears the academic bar. I prepare musicians and artists for the entrance test, so their talent is not wasted on a missed place.

Music at Godolphin

More than 400 individual instrumental and vocal lessons take place at the school every week. A music scholar joins that life at its centre, with lessons paid for on one or two instruments.

Scholarship or bursary?

At Godolphin, the two answer different questions. A scholarship recognises musical or artistic talent; a bursary responds to financial need, and can reach 110 % of the fees. Both require success in the academic assessments, and a scholar can apply for both.
