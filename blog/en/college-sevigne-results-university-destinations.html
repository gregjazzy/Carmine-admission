Collège Sévigné: bac results and where its students go

Collège Sévigné, rue Pierre-Nicole, Paris 5th. Photo LPLT, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Paris schools November 2026 5 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Sévigné is a small school, and its results have the advantage of being easy to read. At the 2026 bac, its 122 candidates obtained 99% honours: 59 with highest honours, nearly half the class, five of them with the jury's congratulations, 48 with high honours and 14 with honours. Only one student passed without honours.

Where its graduates go

The class of 2025 shows a very open spread. More than a third went to university, first in law, health studies and economics-management, and five to PSL University. A fifth chose the classes préparatoires, mostly scientific, then economics and humanities. Nearly one student in five went abroad, first to Canada and the United Kingdom. The others joined business and engineering schools, Sciences Po and the IEPs, or art and architecture schools.
