Boarding at 11 or 12

Beau Soleil welcomes boarders from Grade 6, around the age of 11. For many families, sending a child that young to a full boarding school in the Alps is the hardest part of the decision. The school has built a separate rhythm for its youngest students to make that step manageable.

The Junior programme, Grades 6 and 7

What it means
HomeworkMostly completed during the school day
EveningsScreen-free activities and an earlier bedtime
SaturdaysOutdoor Education instead of academic lessons: team building, climbing, outdoor cooking, water sports, biking
WeekendsTime together as a Junior group, with the boarding team

The wider frame

  • Phones: access is restricted for students in Grades 6 to 10, with clear arrangements to stay in touch with family.
  • Money: students in Grades 6 to 8 use a school debit card that parents fund in advance.
  • Rooms: most students share a twin room, usually with a student of a similar age and a different nationality.
  • Going home: as a full boarding school, students stay on campus at weekends, with exeats and holidays every three to four weeks.
  • Village: leaving campus to visit Villars is for Grade 8 and above, at set times.

Ready academically, ready to board

For 11 and 12 year olds, I prepare the maths and English placement tests and help the child think about what boarding will mean, before the letter is written.

Is the child ready?

Beau Soleil's own answer is that there is no single right age: every child is different, and parents know best. Many of its students have already been to residential summer camps, which gave them a first experience of time away from home. A child who enjoyed a camp, adapts easily to new groups and is curious about other countries may well be readier than their parents think.

Wondering whether your 11-year-old is ready? Let's talk about them.

Settling in

New students arrive before returning ones, take part in information sessions and icebreakers, and are helped by older students in their boarding house. Boarding staff are available around the clock, along with tutors, nurses and counsellors. Families receive weekly updates from the boarding house and the Principal, and can follow progress through the Parent Portal.
