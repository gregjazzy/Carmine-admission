Joining Milton in Grade 10 or 11

Many students join Milton Academy in Grade 9, but the school also takes new students later. Boarding applicants can join in Grade 10 (Class III) or Grade 11 (Class II); day applicants from Greater Boston can join in Grade 10. The last point of entry is Grade 11: Milton does not admit students for their final, Grade 12 year only.

What stays the same

An older applicant follows the same process as a Grade 9 candidate: the online application through Gateway to Prep Schools or the SSAT Standard Application, the Candidate Biographic Profile, recommendations from a school administrator and the current English and math teachers, official grades for the past two years and the current fall, and a two-part interview completed by 15 January. Decisions arrive on 10 March.

What changes at 15 or 16

  • Tests. Testing remains optional, but Grade 11 applicants may send the PSAT, SAT or ACT as well as the SSAT or ISEE. A strong PSAT or SAT can be a useful signal for a student coming from a less familiar school.
  • The record. Two years of final grades now cover the first years of high school, where course choices and levels are visible. A demanding course load, well handled, says a great deal.
  • The interview. Admission officers can expect a more developed conversation: interests that have taken shape, a reason for wanting to change school now, and some idea of what the student wants from the last years of high school.

A later entry, well argued

Arriving in a formed class

At Milton, boarders stay in the same house for all four years, and new arrivals in Grade 10 or 11 join houses where friendships and traditions are established. The houses are built to welcome them, with older students guiding younger ones and faculty living alongside, but the newcomer has to step forward too. Day students joining in Grade 10 face the same challenge in the wider school.

Why families look later

  • A move to the Boston area, or abroad, in the middle of high school.
  • A student who has outgrown their current school and needs more challenge.
  • A family that was not ready for boarding at 14 and is ready at 15 or 16.

Each is a legitimate reason. The application should say so plainly, in the profile and in the interview.

Status, again

A Grade 11 applicant can only board. A Grade 10 applicant from Greater Boston must choose between day and boarding at the time of application, and cannot change after admission except in special circumstances.
