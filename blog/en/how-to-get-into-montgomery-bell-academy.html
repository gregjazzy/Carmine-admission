How to get into Montgomery Bell Academy

Montgomery Bell Academy is the only all-boys school in Middle Tennessee, and it sums up its admission philosophy in one line: a boy's work and character should define him, not his last name, his ZIP code or his previous school. The process is designed accordingly. There is a test, but two of the five steps are about watching and hearing the boy himself: a day spent at the school, and a required interview.

I have students at Montgomery Bell. Here is how the process works for entry in fall 2027.

The five steps

  1. Inquiry. An online inquiry opens an account in the admissions portal; families can also start with a Thursday-morning campus tour.
  2. The official application form.
  3. A shadow visit, during which the boy follows a student through a school day.
  4. The ISEE, for which the family registers separately.
  5. Supplemental materials and the student interview: a transcript request form, a teacher evaluation form, the Character Skills Snapshot, and an interview that every applicant must have.

What MBA is really reading

MBA describes its ideal graduate in three words, gentleman, scholar and athlete, and spells each one out: integrity, courage and kindness; curiosity, creativity and understanding; resilience, consistency and teamwork. Those nine qualities are the lens for the whole file. The ISEE measures academic readiness. The teacher evaluation and the Character Skills Snapshot describe how the boy works and behaves. The shadow visit and the interview let the school see him in person.

A strong score with a quiet, withdrawn shadow day and a recited interview is a weaker file than families think. A good score with a boy who joins in, asks questions and talks honestly about something he found hard is the profile MBA describes on every page.

Entry points

MBA has a Junior School for grades 7 and 8 and a High School above. The ISEE level follows the grade applied to: Middle Level for grades 7 and 8, Upper Level for grade 9 and above.
