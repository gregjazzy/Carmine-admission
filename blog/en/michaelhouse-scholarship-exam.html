The Michaelhouse scholarship weekend

Michaelhouse scholarships are decided over a single weekend in mid-February of Grade 7, at the school, with the boys staying overnight. For the 2027 intake, it took place on 21 and 22 February 2026. Nobody simply turns up: every boy there has been invited, and the route to the invitation depends on the discipline.

Five routes to the weekend

Academic

Boys sit the academic scholarship exam if they reached an aggregate of 85% or more at the end of Grade 6. That threshold is a condition of entry to the exam, not a guarantee: the exam then ranks candidates who are all strong. The main academic awards are the Open scholarships, for boys at independent schools, and the Trust scholarships, for boys at state schools who have attended their current school for at least two years.

Sport

Sport has a preliminary stage. Invited boys attend an initial assessment at Michaelhouse, on one of two dates, in November of Grade 6 or January of Grade 7. Invitation rests on the highest level at which the boy has represented a team sport, district or provincial, and on his commitment and sportsmanship across several sports. From these assessments, a shortlist is invited to the weekend.

Music

Candidates should have reached Grade 4 in voice or an instrument, with UNISA, ABRSM or Trinity. The award looks at instrumental competence and commitment to musical growth.

Art and drama

Candidates submit a letter of motivation from a teacher, with recent creative work for art, or a record of the productions they have taken part in for drama.

All-round

There is no separate application. A boy qualifies for consideration by being invited to two or more of the other scholarship assessments.

The dates around it

  • 31 January of Grade 7: the closing date for registration and for scholarship applications. A boy who is not registered cannot be assessed.
  • Mid-February: the weekend itself.
  • Early April: offers of scholarships and financial assistance, ahead of the offers of place.

Preparing for it

For the academic exam, the real preparation happens over Grade 6 and the start of Grade 7: a solid aggregate first, since it opens the door, then work beyond the curriculum in the core subjects, and practice answering under time pressure. For the other disciplines, the work is the boy's own practice, but the weekend also tests something else: whether he can show his level calmly, away from home, among boys who are as good as he is.
