How to get into The Hotchkiss School

The main building of The Hotchkiss School, Lakeville, Connecticut. Photo Adrien Delessert, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.5

US boarding schools November 2026 7 min read

Book a conversation

Hotchkiss, founded in 1891 in Lakeville in the northwest corner of Connecticut, admits boarding and day students through a process that looks familiar to anyone who knows New England boarding schools: an inquiry, an interview, a standardized test and a Gateway application. What distinguishes it is less the paperwork than the reading. Every file is read and discussed by several members of the admission committee, each part weighs equally, and the school names the qualities it is looking for: extraordinary character, independence and good citizenship.

The four steps

  1. Admission Inquiry Form. Required before scheduling an interview.
  2. Interview. Recommended, not required; offered from mid-September to mid-January, on campus or virtually, never during Thanksgiving or winter break. Saturday slots go first.
  3. Standardized test. SSAT or ISEE for ninth and tenth grade; other tests for older applicants; an English proficiency test for non-native speakers.
  4. Application. Through Gateway to Prep Schools: the Candidate Profile, ideally before the interview, then the Candidate Project with the essays and activities. The fee is $75, and waivers are available.

The recommendations

Hotchkiss asks for four: a principal or counselor recommendation, an English teacher recommendation, a math teacher recommendation (from last year's or this year's teachers), and a personal recommendation from an adult who knows the student well. Add the official transcript, sent by the school, with current and past grades. Requests go out through Gateway, and Hotchkiss asks families to start them by November 1. Teachers who receive a request in late December, in the middle of grading, write shorter letters.

What the admitted students look like

Hotchkiss describes its admitted class plainly: most new students have grades in the A range, excellent teacher recommendations, strong writing skills, and involvement in their school or community. Standardized testing is used to support the rest of the academic file. In other words, a strong SSAT cannot rescue a thin file, and a thin SSAT will be read alongside everything else.

The phrase that matters is "independence". Hotchkiss is a boarding school in rural Connecticut; the committee is asking whether a fourteen-year-old will manage their own time, seek help when needed and contribute to a dorm. The essays, the recommendations and the interview are where that shows.

Late applications

Students who complete their application after January 15 face a much more competitive process, and on-campus interviews are not scheduled during the winter months. Depending on space, Hotchkiss may consider applications through the summer. Financial aid funds are not reserved for late applicants.

Money

For 2026-27, the boarding total is $80,800 and the day total $68,700. Hotchkiss meets 100 percent of demonstrated need with grants, not loans, and offers no merit scholarships. A third of students receive aid.
