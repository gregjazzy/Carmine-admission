Research and student life at Bronx Science

Admission to Bronx Science depends on one test, but the reasons to want it lie elsewhere: a research tradition few schools can match, an extracurricular life run by students, and classmates from across New York City. Here is what four years there involve, beyond the SHSAT.

Research, by the numbers
9th gradeEvery student takes research
10th gradeAbout 34.9 % continue with research
11th and 12th gradeAbout 27 % do original research
Regeneron Science Talent SearchTwo finalists in 2024, among the top 40 nationally; five scholars in 2025
FacilityThe Manne Institute, a research centre opened in 2023

The school has had more Science Talent Search finalists and semifinalists than any other high school in history, and its nine Nobel laureates credit their foundation in research there.

A demanding curriculum

Bronx Science offers a long list of Advanced Placement courses and post-AP courses, from multivariable calculus and linear algebra to advanced genetics and artificial intelligence for game programming. Grades are not weighted, and students are expected to go beyond the minimum requirements. The class of 2026-27 averaged 1427 on the SAT.

Beyond the classroom

  • More than a hundred clubs, led by students.
  • Speech and debate, ranked first in the country, with more than 50 tournaments a year.
  • Two FIRST robotics teams, one of them for girls and gender minorities.
  • Academic teams: Model UN, Science Olympiad, Math Team, Mock Trial.
  • 43 athletic teams.
  • Summer Institutes in programming, robotics, entrepreneurship, media, research and AI.

The commute

The average student spends about two hours a day travelling, mostly by public transport; many leave home before dawn and return in the evening after activities. The school presents this as part of the experience, a lesson in independence and time management, under its slogan "We're Worth the Trip!" Families should weigh it honestly, especially from Brooklyn or Staten Island.
