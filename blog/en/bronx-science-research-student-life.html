Bronx Science: student life

The Bronx High School of Science, New York. Photo Bxsstudent, Wikimedia Commons, public domain

US selective public schools November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Bronx Science has 2,964 students from across the city, mostly from Queens, the Bronx and Manhattan. The average student commutes two hours a day; the school's slogan owns it: "We're Worth the Trip!"

What students do

Research from 9th grade, Advanced Placement courses and beyond, up to differential equations and multivariable calculus, sequences in biology, physics and engineering, and research. Grades are not weighted: the school expects students to choose the most demanding courses themselves.

Alumni

Nine Nobel Prize winners, eleven Pulitzer Prize winners, three Turing Award winners.
