The Ransom Everglades calendar packs five dates into seven weeks, from January 8 to February 26. Each one concerns a different piece, and a file that misses any of them waits: the school does not review an application until every required item is in.

The interview comes first

Interviews start in September, and the school strongly recommends booking early because slots fill quickly. A family that applies in December may find few options left in January. For the Upper School, the applicant statement must be written before the interview, so the order is: application, statement, interview.

Testing within the dates

The ISEE can be taken once per season, autumn from August to November or winter in December and January. A student who sits it in the autumn keeps a winter attempt available before the deadline. The SSAT offers several formats, at home in the United States, at a Prometric centre or on paper. Scores count only when sent directly by the testing organisation, so the school must be named as a recipient. The Snapshot, a separate online assessment of about 25 minutes, is best done well before January 15.

Aid on its own track

For families applying for aid, the Clarity application is due January 10, two days after the admission application. Documents then follow on Clarity's own schedule: the most recent pay stubs on submission, year-end pay stubs from January 1, and W-2 forms from January 31. Aid decisions arrive with the admission letter on February 26.
