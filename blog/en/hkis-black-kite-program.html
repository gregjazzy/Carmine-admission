The Black Kite Program

Most of HKIS's admission rules concern families who pay full fees. The Black Kite Program is the exception. Founded in 2020 and funded entirely by donors, it opens HKIS to students from local Hong Kong families who show exceptional academic talent and significant financial need.

What it covers

  • Full tuition and fees every year.
  • The many costs of attending, including transport to and from school, meals and educational supplies.
  • Required technology, such as a laptop and other computing resources.

Once awarded, support is guaranteed for six years, from Grade 7 to graduation, provided the student makes acceptable academic progress and takes part fully in school life.

The application

  1. Register interest through the online inquiry form, which leads to the full application on the OpenApply portal.
  2. Complete the Black Kite application, with a Family Questionnaire and a Student Questionnaire.
  3. Upload supporting documents once the application has been reviewed, including identification for the student and family.
  4. Attend a pre-entry assessment and an interview.

Applications for 2027-28 are closed. The period for 2028-29 entry opens in the spring of 2027, for students then in Primary 5 or Grade 5 who will enter Grade 7 in August 2028.

The real challenge

A strong student at a local Hong Kong school may have excellent results and still find HKIS's English-medium, discussion-based classes a large step. The program requires the ability to complete all coursework in English. Building writing and speaking in English over the year before applying makes the difference between a promising application and a convincing one.

How I help

For seven years, I have worked with around fifteen families a year on applications to very selective schools. For Black Kite candidates, I focus on English writing and the interview, and on helping the student present their achievements and ambitions clearly.
