HKIS: the Black Kite Program

Hong Kong International School, Tai Tam. Photo Underwaterbuffalo, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

International schools in Asia November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Founded in 2020, HKIS's Black Kite Program is fully donor-supported. It supports students of exceptional academic talent from local Hong Kong families attending local schools.

What it covers

Full tuition and fees and associated costs, transport, meals, materials, for six years, through graduation, subject to good standing.

Who can apply

A Hong Kong resident in good standing at a local school, never previously enrolled at HKIS, entering Grade 7, meeting HKIS's academic entrance requirements, able to complete all coursework in English, with significant financial need and no other aid. Applications for 2028-2029 open in spring 2027.

Preparing a Black Kite application

Black Kite requires passing HKIS admission. I prepare the student for the assessment and file, in English.
