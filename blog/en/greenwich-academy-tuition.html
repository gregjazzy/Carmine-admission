Greenwich Academy publishes a single tuition figure per section of the school, and it includes more than many schools' tuition does: lunch, snacks, supplies, class trips and associated activities are all covered. That makes comparison with other schools easier, as long as families compare like with like.

I go through these figures with families considering the school. Here they are for 2026-27.

Payment

To hold a place, families return the enrollment contract with a $6,000 deposit, non-refundable and credited towards tuition. The first semester's tuition is due on May 1. Once a student is enrolled, families can choose among several payment schedules in the Veracross billing system.

For a girl entering in Pre-Connecting and staying through Group XII, the published 2026-27 rates add up to about $798,000 over fourteen years, before any increases. The figure is worth having in mind when a family weighs the commitment, and when it decides whether to apply for aid.

Comparing like with like

Because lunch, snacks, supplies, class trips and their activities are inside the Greenwich Academy figure, a school with a lower headline tuition can end up costing the same once those items are added back. When a family compares schools, the useful exercise is to list, for each one, what the tuition covers and what is billed on top, then compare the totals. The step from Group VIII to Group IX, about $3,750 a year, is the largest jump in the Greenwich Academy scale and is worth anticipating for a girl moving up to the Upper School.

Need-based aid, decided separately from admission, has its own deadline of December 15; the financial aid article sets out the rules.
