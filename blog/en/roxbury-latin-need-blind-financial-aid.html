Roxbury Latin: need-blind aid

Boston from the Charles River; Roxbury Latin is in West Roxbury, within the city. Photo King of Hearts, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

US independent schools November 2026 3 min read

Greg MitteletteFounder of Carmine Admission. 1,100 students supported over 14 years, with admissions to Stanford, Columbia, Oxford, EPFL and Bocconi.

Book a conversation

Roxbury Latin admits students without regard to family means, and meets the full demonstrated need of every admitted boy.

How it works

To protect this, the application asks no questions about aid. Aid depends on need only, never merit, and there are no loans. The Clarity application opens September 1, due January 15. Average family contributions range from about $1,200 for incomes under $75,000 to about $22,500 above $350,000. Additional aid also covers school trips.

Admission is all that matters

Since income plays no part, everything rests on the file. I prepare your son for the test and interview.
